# A29O

A29O is an oceanic mining world located within the [[Andromeda System]], primarily controlled and operated by the [[Hell Hosts]] due to its immense deposits of:

[[Axionium]]

One of the most valuable and strategically important resources used throughout modern military and industrial technology.

Although much of the planet is covered by deep oceans and violent storm systems, massive mining operations have been established across the surface and beneath the seas to extract [[Axionium]] from the planet’s crust and abyssal trench networks.

Unlike the heavily fortified military worlds of [[Voridinus II]] and [[Coria-6ZNH]], A29O functions primarily as an industrial resource world supporting Hell Hosts infrastructure, fleet production, and weapons manufacturing.

## Environment

A29O is dominated by:
- endless dark oceans
- violent electrical storms
- deep abyssal trenches
- isolated mining platforms
- underwater extraction facilities
- unstable ocean currents
- fog-covered industrial sectors

Only a small number of rocky island chains and artificial platforms rise above the ocean surface.

Most large-scale operations occur either:
- beneath the ocean
- within floating industrial complexes
- or inside massive reinforced drilling stations anchored to the seabed.

## [[Axionium]] Deposits

The planet contains some of the largest known reserves of [[Axionium]] within the region.

[[Axionium]] extracted from A29O is heavily used in:
- advanced military systems
- fleet technology
- weapons production
- energy infrastructure
- experimental technologies

Because of its strategic importance, the planet is heavily protected by Hell Hosts security forces and orbital patrol fleets.

Unauthorized mining or smuggling operations are treated as severe military offenses.

## Infrastructure

Major facilities on A29O include:
- oceanic mining platforms
- underwater drilling complexes
- refinery stations
- cargo transport hubs
- industrial docking zones
- armored storm-resistant settlements

Massive transport ships constantly move between A29O and other Hell Hosts-controlled worlds, particularly [[Voridinus II]].

## Hazards

Despite its industrial importance, A29O is considered extremely dangerous due to:
- violent superstorms
- equipment collapse beneath ocean pressure
- unstable trench systems
- massive sea predators
- mining accidents
- communication failures during deep-sea operations

Several underwater sectors have reportedly been abandoned after unexplained disappearances and structural failures.

## Reputation

A29O is known throughout the region as:
- one of the largest Axionium-producing worlds
- a critical industrial planet for the [[Hell Hosts]]
- an extremely dangerous oceanic mining world
- a harsh environment dominated by storms and industrial extraction

Among workers stationed there, A29O is often described as:
> “A planet trying to drown its own industry.”
