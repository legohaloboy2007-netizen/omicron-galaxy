# Act 16 — Hell Host–Angel Sword War

**Era:** [[Power Unbound (Era 3)]]
**Previous:** [[Act 15 — Blood’s Ascension]]
**Next:** [[Act 17 — Cabin Gathering]]

## Overview

The **Hell Host–Angel Sword War** was one of the largest conventional conflicts of the [[Power Unbound (Era 3)|Power Unbound]] era, fought between the [[Hell Hosts]] (with the aid of [[Free World Order]]) and the [[Angel Swords]] after years of increasing hostility between the two powers.

The conflict was driven largely by the growing search for [[Element 95]] weapons, artifacts, technologies, and knowledge.

The Angel Swords believed Element 95 represented an immense source of power and sought methods of controlling and weaponizing it. Their search repeatedly brought them into conflict with the Hell Hosts, whose own history had become increasingly connected to Element 95 and the anomalous regions surrounding it.

Neither faction trusted the other with such power.

Both feared what would happen if the other gained the advantage first.

Years of smaller confrontations, competing expeditions, and military escalation eventually developed into open war.

Unlike previous limited engagements, both factions committed everything available to them.

Entire fleets fought across space.

Armies invaded fortified worlds.

Titans, armored vehicles, aircraft, robots, artillery, elite soldiers, and enormous capital ships were deployed throughout the conflict.

Two worlds became particularly important:

[[Joshua]] and [[Genesis]].

The Battle of Joshua demonstrated the extraordinary difficulty of invading Angel Sword territory.

The later campaign around Genesis became the largest confrontation of the war.

Despite fierce resistance, the Hell Hosts eventually overwhelmed the Angel Swords through sustained military pressure, industrial strength, and the ability to continually reinforce their forces.

Recognizing that remaining concentrated would eventually lead to their destruction, the surviving Angel Swords abandoned conventional resistance and scattered across the galaxy.

The Hell Hosts won the war.

The Angel Swords survived it.

---

# Sub-Acts

## 16.1 — Arms Race

The search for [[Element 95]] becomes increasingly dangerous.

The [[Angel Swords]] have spent years searching the [[Farlands]], [[Voidlands]], and other anomalous regions for weapons, artifacts, technologies, and knowledge capable of helping them understand and control Element 95.

They know the substance is extraordinarily powerful.

They also know the [[Hell Hosts]] have become increasingly connected to the same forces.

Neither faction believes the other can safely be allowed to gain control of that power.

To the Angel Swords, a Hell Host breakthrough involving Element 95 could create an enemy they would never again be capable of challenging.

To the Hell Hosts, the idea of the Angel Swords successfully weaponizing something as unstable as Element 95 represents an unacceptable threat.

Smaller confrontations have already occurred.

The [[Act 10 — Desert Operation|Desert Operation]] demonstrated that both factions are willing to fight over information connected to the Farlands.

Eventually, these encounters stop being isolated incidents.

Both militaries begin preparing for the possibility of full-scale conflict.

Each believes the other is becoming too dangerous to ignore.

War becomes increasingly difficult to avoid.

Eventually, neither side attempts to avoid it.

---

## 16.2 — Total War

Open conflict erupts between the Hell Hosts and Angel Swords.

What follows is not a limited campaign.

Both factions commit everything.

Hell Host fleets engage Angel Sword naval formations across multiple systems while planetary armies fight over strategic worlds, military installations, supply routes, and fortified positions.

The Hell Hosts deploy forces from across their enormous military structure.

[[Doom Corps]] formations spearhead major ground offensives.

[[Shock & Awe Division]] personnel fight aboard ships and orbital installations.

The [[Powered Armour Division]] is deployed against heavily fortified Angel Sword positions.

The [[Aviation Division]] contests planetary airspace.

Artillery formations bombard defensive networks.

Robotic forces reinforce humanoid soldiers across numerous battlefields.

Armored vehicles and mechanized formations fight alongside infantry.

Strategic war machines are deployed when conventional forces prove insufficient.

The Angel Swords respond with the full strength of their own empire.

Their disciplined armies defend sacred territory while elite warriors launch counterattacks against Hell Host formations.

Angel Sword fleets engage Hell Host naval forces directly.

Fortified worlds become enormous battlefields.

Neither side expects a short war.

Neither intends to surrender the advantage.

The arms race has become total war.

---

## 16.3 — Battle of Joshua

[[Joshua]] becomes one of the first major centerpiece worlds of the conflict.

The Hell Hosts launch a massive offensive against the planet, expecting heavy resistance.

They receive it.

Joshua's defenses transform the invasion into a prolonged combined-arms campaign.

Hell Host infantry and armored formations push against heavily defended Angel Sword positions while artillery and aircraft attempt to break defensive lines.

The Angel Swords use the planet's fortifications to slow the invasion and force the Hell Hosts into increasingly destructive battles.

Hell Host strategic war machines, including Titans, are eventually committed against positions that conventional forces struggle to overcome.

Above the planet, another battle develops.

Hell Host fleets engage Angel Sword warships and orbital defenses while fighter formations battle for control of the space surrounding Joshua.

[[Champion]] becomes heavily involved in the naval campaign, coordinating Hell Host fleet movements and support for the forces fighting below.

The Angel Swords inflict significant losses.

Joshua proves that even the overwhelming military strength of the Hell Hosts cannot make an invasion of an Angel Sword core world easy.

The Hell Hosts eventually overpower the major Angel Sword defenses on Joshua.

But the battle changes the expectations of both factions.

The Angel Swords now understand the scale of the force being committed against them.

The Hell Hosts understand exactly how costly destroying the Angel Swords may become.

The war continues.

---

## 16.4 — The Crusade

The Angel Swords refuse to remain defensive.

As Hell Host forces continue their campaigns, Angel Sword commanders launch counteroffensives across multiple theaters.

Their response becomes a crusade against the faction they increasingly view as their greatest military rival.

Angel Sword forces strike Hell Host positions, fleets, military formations, and strategic routes wherever opportunities appear.

Elite personnel become increasingly important as the conventional war intensifies.

[[Phantom]] fights directly against Hell Host forces, bringing his speed, phase abilities, and personal hatred of the faction into the wider conflict.

[[Rapid]] participates in Angel Sword operations where his teleportation and shock-based abilities make him particularly dangerous in close combat.

[[Simulacrum]], whose combat doctrine was specifically developed around countering Hell Host methods, becomes especially valuable during the war.

[[Catalyst]] provides another unconventional threat through his matter restructuring abilities and combat experience.

The Angel Swords prove repeatedly that losing territory does not mean losing their ability to fight.

Hell Host victories are answered with counterattacks.

Fleet losses are answered with raids.

Ground defeats are answered with renewed offensives elsewhere.

The war spreads.

Individual systems change hands or become contested.

Entire military formations are destroyed and replaced.

Neither faction can end the conflict through a single victory.

The Hell Hosts begin preparing for a much larger campaign.

Their target is [[Genesis]].

But reaching it requires breaking the Angel Swords in space first.

---

## 16.5 — War in Heaven

The naval war becomes one of the largest space conflicts of the era.

Hell Host fleets begin a sustained campaign intended to break the Angel Sword naval network and open a route toward [[Genesis]].

The Angel Swords commit their remaining major fleet formations to stopping them.

Battles erupt across multiple systems.

Capital ships exchange fire across enormous distances.

Fighter formations swarm around larger vessels.

Boarding teams attack damaged ships.

Fleet Guard personnel fight through breached decks and contested hangars.

Warships are destroyed in numbers rarely seen outside the largest conflicts in modern galactic history.

The debris of previous engagements becomes part of later battlefields.

[[Champion]], commanding from the [[HH Commonwealth]], becomes one of the central Hell Host commanders of the naval campaign.

Rather than fighting as an individual soldier, Champion is forced to command on the scale of entire fleets.

Ships must be moved.

Reinforcements must be protected.

Damaged formations must be withdrawn.

Planetary offensives require orbital support.

Supply routes must remain open.

The Angel Swords fight relentlessly to prevent the Hell Hosts from concentrating their forces around Genesis.

For a time, the space surrounding Angel Sword territory becomes a constantly shifting battlefield.

The Hell Hosts lose ships.

The Angel Swords lose ships.

Both sides continue sending more.

Gradually, however, the balance begins to change.

The Hell Hosts can replace losses that the Angel Swords increasingly cannot.

Their enormous military infrastructure allows damaged formations to be reinforced while new vessels and personnel continue entering the war.

Angel Sword fleets remain dangerous, but every major loss becomes harder to recover from.

The naval network protecting Genesis begins to collapse.

The road to the heart of the Angel Swords opens.

The war in heaven gives way to the battle for Genesis.

---

## 16.6 — Genesis

The Hell Hosts launch their largest offensive of the war against [[Genesis]].

The Angel Swords understand what the battle represents.

Genesis is not simply another battlefield.

It is one of the central worlds of their civilization and a symbol of the empire they have built.

They commit everything available to its defense.

The Hell Hosts do the same.

The battle develops simultaneously across space, atmosphere, and ground.

Hell Host fleets engage the remaining Angel Sword naval forces.

Fighters and attack craft fill the space between capital ships.

Boarding operations erupt aboard damaged vessels.

Orbital defenses fire upon approaching Hell Host formations.

On the surface, Doom Corps soldiers fight alongside armored formations, robots, Powered Armour units, artillery, and other Hell Host forces.

Angel Sword armies defend fortified cities, military installations, religious strongholds, and strategic positions across the world.

Elite Angel Sword warriors fight alongside conventional troops.

Hell Host Titans are deployed against defenses designed to withstand ordinary armored assaults.

The scale of the battle becomes enormous.

Neither faction holds back.

The Angel Swords continue fighting even as their defensive network begins to collapse.

Individual Hell Host formations suffer devastating losses.

Angel Sword counterattacks repeatedly slow the advance.

But the wider balance of the war has already shifted.

Every Angel Sword formation destroyed on Genesis represents military strength they increasingly cannot replace.

The Hell Hosts continue bringing reinforcements into the campaign.

More soldiers arrive.

More machines arrive.

More ships arrive.

The Angel Swords can destroy individual formations.

They cannot destroy the system supporting them.

Eventually, Hell Host forces overpower the major Angel Sword resistance.

The battle for Genesis breaks the Angel Swords' ability to continue fighting the war as a concentrated conventional military power.

But it does not destroy them.

The surviving Angel Sword leadership makes a different decision.

If remaining together means annihilation, they will no longer remain together.

---

## 16.7 — Scattered Swords

The Angel Swords begin abandoning the conventional war.

Remaining fleets separate into smaller formations.

Military units evacuate threatened positions.

Surviving commanders disappear from known operational areas.

Soldiers, warriors, relic hunters, and other Angel Sword personnel scatter throughout the galaxy.

Some retreat to distant territories.

Others disappear into isolated systems.

Some continue operating in small groups.

The empire that once confronted the Hell Hosts directly becomes fragmented and increasingly difficult to locate.

The strategy denies the Hell Hosts the final battle they expected.

The Hell Hosts have won.

The Angel Swords can no longer challenge them through the concentrated military strength they possessed at the beginning of the war.

But destroying the organization entirely becomes another problem.

A fleet can be defeated.

A fortress can be captured.

An army can be surrounded.

An enemy scattered across an entire galaxy cannot be destroyed so easily.

The Angel Swords survive by refusing to provide the Hell Hosts with another decisive target.

Their crusade is broken.

Their military power is severely weakened.

Their forces are scattered.

But the Angel Swords are not extinct.

The Hell Hosts emerge as the dominant victor of the conflict.

For [[Predator]] and Hell Host command, however, the result remains incomplete.

They have defeated one of their greatest military rivals.

They have not eliminated them.

Across the galaxy, surviving Angel Sword forces begin disappearing into the shadows.

The war ends.

The hunt does not.

# Characters

## [[Predator]]

As Supreme Commander of the [[Hell Hosts]], Predator oversees the wider war against the Angel Swords.

Rather than personally determining every battlefield, Predator is responsible for directing an interstellar military campaign involving enormous numbers of personnel, ships, and resources.

The war becomes one of the largest tests of the military organization the Hell Hosts have built.

## [[Champion]]

Champion plays a major role in the naval conflict.

As one of the Hell Hosts' most important fleet commanders, he participates heavily in the Battle of Joshua and later becomes central to the campaign that opens the route toward Genesis.

During **War in Heaven**, Champion commands from the [[HH Commonwealth]] as Hell Host and Angel Sword fleets fight across multiple systems.

## [[Phantom]]

Phantom fights against the Hell Hosts during the war.

His existing hatred of the faction makes the conflict deeply personal, while his abilities make him an extremely dangerous opponent during direct engagements.

## [[Rapid]]

Rapid participates in Angel Sword operations throughout the conflict.

His teleportation and shock-based abilities make him particularly dangerous during close-range engagements.

## [[Simulacrum]]

The Hell Host–Angel Sword War provides one of the largest applications of Simulacrum's Anti-Hell Host combat doctrine.

His understanding and imitation of Hell Host tactics allows Angel Sword forces to counter methods that would otherwise be unfamiliar to many of their soldiers.

## [[Catalyst]]

Milo participates in the wider Angel Sword war effort.

His matter restructuring abilities make him one of several unconventional Angel Sword combatants deployed against Hell Host forces.

# Deaths and Casualties

The Hell Host–Angel Sword War results in enormous military losses on both sides.

Numerous soldiers, pilots, ship crews, robotic personnel, and other combatants are killed throughout the conflict.

Multiple vessels are destroyed during the naval campaigns.

Planetary battles cause extensive destruction to military infrastructure and contested regions.

Exact casualty figures remain undocumented.

No major named character is confirmed to have died during the war.

# Lore Introduced

## Element 95 Arms Race

The war demonstrates how the search for [[Element 95]] has begun influencing conventional galactic politics and warfare.

Element 95 is no longer simply an anomalous substance encountered in distant regions.

The possibility that a major military power could learn to control or weaponize it becomes enough to destabilize relations between entire civilizations.

Neither the Hell Hosts nor Angel Swords are willing to allow the other to gain an overwhelming advantage.

That fear becomes one of the primary causes of the war.

## Angel Sword Dispersal

Following their defeat, the Angel Swords fundamentally change how they survive.

Rather than maintaining all of their military power in concentrated and easily identifiable formations, surviving forces scatter throughout the galaxy.

This prevents the Hell Hosts from completely eliminating them after the war.

The Angel Swords remain dangerous.

They are simply no longer gathered in one place.

# Consequences

The [[Hell Hosts]] emerge victorious from one of the largest wars of the [[Power Unbound (Era 3)|Power Unbound]] era.

Their victory demonstrates the extraordinary scale their military has reached.

Even the heavily fortified Angel Sword worlds and their disciplined armies cannot withstand the Hell Hosts indefinitely when the faction commits its full military and industrial strength.

The victory comes at significant cost.

Ships are lost.

Soldiers are killed.

Entire formations suffer severe casualties.

The Angel Swords, however, suffer the greater strategic defeat.

Their ability to operate as a concentrated military empire is broken.

Rather than face complete destruction, surviving forces scatter across the galaxy.

The result leaves the Hell Hosts in an unusual position.

They have won the war.

There is no remaining conventional Angel Sword military capable of immediately continuing it.

But there is also no final army to destroy.

No single fleet to defeat.

No single fortress whose capture will eliminate the faction.

The Angel Swords survive through dispersal.

Their search for power is interrupted, but not necessarily ended.

And the knowledge that began the conflict remains somewhere in the galaxy.

[[Element 95]] still exists.

Neither side has solved the danger it represents.
