# Act 17 — Cabin Gathering

**Era:** [[Power Unbound (Era 3)]]
**Previous:** [[Act 16 — Hell Hosts vs Angel Swords]]
**Next:** [[Act 18 — Calypso's Ascension]]

## Overview

Following the defeat and dispersal of the [[Angel Swords]], members of the [[Hell Hosts]] and [[Free World Order]] gather at a remote cabin to celebrate their victory and the alliance that made it possible.

With [[Onyx]] still missing, [[Ultimatum]] attends as the acting leader of the Free World Order, joining Hell Host personnel including [[Predator]], [[Champion]], [[Forsaken]], [[Ranger]], and several other soldiers from both factions.

For one night, the people who had once fought against each other put aside their history. Weapons are lowered, helmets come off, drinks are passed around, and soldiers from both factions spend the evening talking, drinking, and remembering those who could not be there.

Predator eventually raises a toast to the alliance and the people who fought alongside them, while also reflecting on the absence of [[Azure Gale]].

The gathering does not formally unite the Hell Hosts and Free World Order, nor does it erase the conflicts between them. Instead, it represents a rare moment of peace between two former enemies who have survived another war together.

After the enormous battles of the Hell Host–Angel Sword War, the Cabin Gathering serves as a quieter moment in the [[Power Unbound (Era 3)|Power Unbound]] era, focusing on the soldiers themselves rather than the wars surrounding them.
