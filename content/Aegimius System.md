# Aegimius System

The Aegimius System is a mysterious and partially unexplored star system located within the [[Core Systems]] of the [[Omicron Galaxy]]. Unlike the heavily industrialized trade regions of the [[Prioxis System]], Aegimius is known for its dangerous expeditions, unexplored worlds, ancient anomalies, and hidden resources.

The system contains forty known planets, many of which remain largely uncharted due to hostile environments, unstable conditions, or unexplained phenomena. Eleven of these worlds are believed to contain immense untapped wealth, ancient technologies, or anomalous properties capable of altering biological and physical systems.

Over the centuries, the Aegimius System has attracted explorers, corporations, scientists, mercenaries, and government expeditions searching for power, knowledge, and resources. Numerous disappearances and classified incidents have also been reported throughout the system, causing many factions to treat the region with caution.

The system later became heavily associated with Ascension Events and anomalous discoveries connected to figures such as [[Robert Anderson]].

## Known For
- Ancient anomalies and unexplored worlds
- Dangerous expeditions
- Untapped resources and hidden wealth
- Rumors of reality-altering phenomena
- Classified scientific research
- Ascension-related discoveries
