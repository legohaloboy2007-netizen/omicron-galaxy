# Aeonhollow

## The Capital World of the Reality Weavers

Aeonhollow is the capital world of the enigmatic civilization known as:

The Reality Weavers

Located deep within the upper thresholds of the [[Voidlands]], Aeonhollow is considered one of the most structurally impossible worlds ever encountered.

The planet does not obey conventional geometry, architecture, or spatial law.

Entire cities fold inward endlessly while towers spiral:
- upward
- sideways
- downward
- and through themselves simultaneously

Despite this, movement through the world feels strangely natural to most visitors, as though the environment continuously rearranges itself to accommodate perception.

Many researchers believe Aeonhollow does not exist fully within normal reality.

## The Reality Weavers

The Reality Weavers are an ancient civilization devoted to:
- geometric manipulation
- dimensional architecture
- Void mathematics
- reality restructuring
- causal engineering

Unlike cults worshipping the [[The Witness]], the Weavers view reality itself as:
a structure to be rewritten.

Their society revolves around the belief that:
existence is merely an unstable pattern.

The Weavers seek to perfect it.

## Environment

Aeonhollow is a world of:
- recursive megacities
- impossible geometric structures
- shifting pathways
- gravity-defying architecture
- floating districts
- endless mirrored corridors
- spatial recursion zones

Observers frequently experience:
- changing horizons
- impossible depth perception
- repeating locations
- contradictory movement
- and temporary loss of directional awareness

Yet strangely:
the planet rarely feels hostile.

Instead, many visitors describe the sensation as:
unnervingly accommodating.

## Adaptive Spatiality

One of Aeonhollow’s most disturbing properties is its apparent ability to:
adapt itself to perception.

Corridors may:
- shorten
- expand
- redirect
- or rearrange

depending on:
- thought
- emotional state
- memory
- expectation
- or unknown Void-based variables

Two individuals traveling together may remember entirely different routes through the same structure.

## The Lattice Wells

The planet’s primary power source consists of massive geometric singularities known as:

Lattice Wells

These structures appear as:
- suspended mathematical formations
- impossible crystalline geometries
- rotating dimensional lattices
- gravity-defying energy cores

The Wells generate:
- spatial energy
- dimensional stabilization
- reality restructuring fields
- gravitational manipulation

Many scholars believe the Wells function by:
directly interacting with the underlying framework of existence itself.

## The Nexus Conclave

At the center of Aeonhollow lies:

The Nexus Conclave

The ruling seat of the Reality Weavers.

The Conclave exists within an immense recursive structure where:
- rooms overlap infinitely
- council chambers exist in multiple locations simultaneously
- time progression becomes inconsistent
- and architecture reshapes itself continuously

The leaders of the Weavers govern from within these impossible halls.

Very few outsiders have ever been permitted entry.

## Inhabitants

### High Weavers
The ruling intellectual elite of Aeonhollow.

Masters of:
- dimensional geometry
- Void mathematics
- spatial manipulation
- pattern restructuring

Many High Weavers appear partially detached from conventional physical reality.

---

### Pattern Scribes
Scholars and record-keepers responsible for mapping:
- probability flows
- dimensional instability
- geometric anomalies
- causal patterns

Their writings are considered nearly incomprehensible to outsiders.

---

### Null Architects
Engineers responsible for constructing and maintaining Aeonhollow’s impossible infrastructure.

Null Architects manipulate:
- gravity
- geometry
- dimensional layering
- and reality-binding structures

Some are rumored to build entire districts through thought-guided mathematics alone.

## Relationship to the Voidlands

Unlike most civilizations operating within the [[Voidlands]], the Reality Weavers are not fully consumed by chaos.

Instead:
they appear adapted to it.

Some believe the Weavers discovered methods of:
coexisting with Void Laws
rather than resisting them.

This has made them one of the most mysterious powers within the region.

## Psychological Effects

Extended exposure to Aeonhollow frequently causes:
- spatial disorientation
- recursive dreams
- memory overlap
- geometric hallucinations
- altered perception of time
- existential instability

Some visitors permanently lose the ability to perceive normal architecture correctly after leaving the world.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Aeonhollow is known as:
- the capital of the Reality Weavers
- one of the most impossible worlds in existence
- a city where geometry itself is alive
- a place where reality rearranges around thought

Among Voidlands explorers, one phrase is commonly repeated:

> “Aeonhollow does not trap you.  
> It convinces you there was nowhere else to go.”
