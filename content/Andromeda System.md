# Andromeda System

The Andromeda System is one of the most heavily militarized and strategically important regions within the [[Core Systems]] of the [[Omicron Galaxy]]. Known for its enormous fleet production capabilities, fortified worlds, industrial infrastructure, and military influence, the system became the foundation of several major galactic powers throughout history.

Unlike the civilian-focused [[Prioxis System]] or the mysterious [[Aegimius System]], the Andromeda System is dominated by military expansion, industrial production, and strategic control. Massive shipyards, orbital defense platforms, weapons factories, and fleet command centers can be found throughout the system.

The region later became heavily associated with the rise of the [[Hell Hosts]], with worlds such as [[Voridinus II]] serving as major command and operational centers. Due to its strategic importance, the system has remained one of the most defended and politically influential territories within known space.

The Andromeda System is also home to important scientific discoveries, including the ocean world [[A29O]], where the radioactive energy source known as Axionium was first identified.

## Known For
- Massive fleet production and shipyards
- Military infrastructure and industrial power
- Strategic importance across the galaxy
- Heavy faction presence and defense systems
- Hell Host military operations
- Discovery of Axionium
