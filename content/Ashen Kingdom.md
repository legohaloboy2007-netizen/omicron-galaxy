# Ashen Kingdom  
  
The Ashen Kingdom, commonly referred to as the Devil’s Hand, is a volcanic region located within the outer sectors of the [[Omicron Galaxy]]. Known for its burning skies, collapsing terrain, lava oceans, and constant meteor activity, the region is considered one of the most hostile environments in known space.  
  
Unlike many unified territories, the Ashen Kingdom originally consisted of scattered worlds operating independently, with most settlements focused on mining, military survival, and industrial production. Constant volcanic activity and violent ash storms made large-scale civilisation difficult for centuries.  
  
Following the ascension of [[Blood]], the region became increasingly unified under his rule. Now known as the Ashen Lord, Blood transformed the once fractured worlds into a growing kingdom built around survival, strength, and domination. Since his ascension, reports of intensified volcanic activity, mutated creatures, and unusual energy storms have spread throughout the region.  
  
Despite its brutal conditions, the Ashen Kingdom contains enormous mineral wealth and some of the most resource-rich worlds within the galaxy.
