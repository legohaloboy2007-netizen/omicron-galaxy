# Ashens

**Classification:** Sapient biological species  
**Origin:** [[Vexar Prime]]  
**Primary Region:** [[Ashen Kingdom]]  
**Distribution:** Ashen Kingdom and surrounding territories; smaller populations throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]]  
**Status:** Extant  

Ashens are a sapient humanoid species originating from [[Vexar Prime]] and are the dominant native species associated with the [[Ashen Kingdom]].

Ashens evolved within an environment shaped heavily by volcanic activity, frequent ashfall, extreme temperatures, mineral-rich landscapes, and atmospheric conditions considered hazardous to many other humanoid species.

These conditions produced a species exceptionally tolerant of heat, airborne particulates, and numerous environmental contaminants.

Although closely associated with the Ashen Kingdom, Ashens are a species rather than a political identity. Ashen populations exist throughout the Omicron Galaxy, and individuals may belong to factions and cultures entirely separate from the Kingdom.

## Appearance

Ashens possess a broadly humanoid body structure and are physically similar in proportion to humans.

Their skin commonly ranges from charcoal-grey and dark slate to lighter ash-grey, brown-grey, and occasionally warmer copper or reddish-grey tones.

Skin colour varies significantly between populations.

One of the most distinctive Ashen features is the presence of subtle mineral-like patterns beneath portions of the skin. These commonly appear around the arms, shoulders, neck, back, and face.

The patterns can resemble thin veins, cracks, or branching lines and vary considerably between individuals.

They are not literal cracks in the skin.

Depending upon genetics and environmental conditions, these markings may appear dark, reddish, copper-like, or faintly luminous under extreme heat.

Ashen eyes are adapted to environments containing smoke, ash, airborne dust, and intense volcanic light. Common colours include amber, copper, dark brown, red-brown, gold, and black.

Hair colours vary widely, although black, dark brown, grey, deep red, and copper tones are particularly common.

## Heat Adaptation

Ashens possess exceptional resistance to high environmental temperatures.

Their circulatory system distributes heat efficiently throughout the body, while specialised biological processes allow excess heat to be released considerably more effectively than in humans.

Ashens can comfortably operate in temperatures that would cause severe heat exhaustion in many other humanoid species.

Their resistance does not make them immune to fire.

Direct exposure to flame, molten material, or sufficiently extreme temperatures can still cause severe injury or death.

## Temperature Regulation

Ashen bodies maintain a slightly higher average internal temperature than humans.

Their skin commonly feels noticeably warm to the touch.

Blood vessels located close to the surface can expand rapidly when the body needs to release excess heat.

This process can make some of the natural patterns beneath Ashen skin become considerably more visible.

During intense physical exertion or extreme environmental heat, these markings may take on a faint reddish or amber appearance.

The effect has sometimes been incorrectly described as Ashens "glowing."

## Respiratory System

One of the most significant Ashen adaptations is their respiratory system.

Ashen lungs and airways are highly resistant to airborne particulates.

Natural filtration structures throughout their respiratory tract prevent large quantities of ash, dust, and other material from reaching vulnerable lung tissue.

Their bodies are also significantly more resistant than humans to several gases commonly associated with volcanic environments.

This resistance is not universal.

Ashens can still be poisoned by sufficiently toxic atmospheres and require respiratory equipment in environments containing dangerous concentrations of certain gases.

## Eyes

Ashen eyes possess protective adaptations against ash, smoke, dust, and intense light.

Their tear composition helps remove small particles from the surface of the eye, while an additional transparent protective membrane can temporarily cover the eye during severe environmental conditions.

This membrane is normally difficult to notice.

During heavy ashstorms, however, it can protect the eye while maintaining limited vision.

## Skin

Ashen skin is generally thicker and more resistant to heat, abrasion, and environmental irritation than human skin.

Natural oils and protective compounds reduce damage caused by ash and dry atmospheric conditions.

Ashens nevertheless sweat and require hydration.

Their biological adaptations improve heat tolerance rather than eliminating the dangers associated with dehydration or extreme temperatures.

## Cold

Ashens generally tolerate cold environments poorly.

Their physiology is designed to release heat efficiently, creating significant disadvantages in low-temperature environments.

Prolonged exposure to cold can rapidly reduce physical performance.

Ashens travelling through worlds such as [[Frostheim]] frequently require heated clothing and specialised environmental equipment.

Long-term Ashen residents of colder planets often rely heavily upon climate-controlled buildings and personal heating systems.

## Physical Characteristics

Ashens possess strong skeletal structures and dense connective tissue adapted to physically demanding environments.

Their bodies are particularly resistant to repetitive strain, minor impact injuries, and environmental wear.

They are not universally stronger than humans or Glacians.

Individual physical ability varies according to genetics, health, training, occupation, and augmentation.

## Lifespan

Ashen lifespans are broadly comparable to those of other humanoid species and have increased considerably through modern medicine.

Life expectancy varies significantly between populations.

Historically, dangerous working conditions, volcanic disasters, industrial accidents, and respiratory exposure reduced average lifespan throughout many early Ashen societies.

Modern technology has dramatically reduced these risks.

## Vexar Prime

[[Vexar Prime]] is the ancestral homeworld of the Ashen species.

The planet is characterised by extensive volcanic activity, enormous mountain systems, ash-covered landscapes, mineral-rich regions, and extreme environmental conditions.

Ashen civilisation developed around these conditions rather than in spite of them.

Volcanic regions provided enormous quantities of valuable minerals and geothermal energy, contributing heavily to the development of early Ashen industry.

Vexar Prime remains the political and cultural heart of the [[Ashen Kingdom]].

## The Ashen Kingdom

The [[Ashen Kingdom]] is the largest civilisation historically associated with Ashens.

Its worlds contain major population centres, industrial regions, mining operations, military installations, transportation networks, and civilian settlements.

Ashens form the cultural and historical foundation of the Kingdom, although other species also live within its territory.

Likewise, Ashens living elsewhere in the Omicron Galaxy are not automatically subjects of the Ashen Kingdom.

## Culture

No single culture represents every Ashen.

Different worlds, regions, cities, social groups, and historical periods have produced considerable cultural diversity.

However, craftsmanship and skilled labour hold considerable historical significance throughout many Ashen societies.

Metalworking, engineering, mining, construction, machinery, and other practical disciplines became deeply important during the development of civilisation on Vexar Prime.

This history remains visible within modern Ashen culture even where traditional industries are no longer economically dominant.

Objects built to survive generations are often highly respected.

## Industry

Ashen civilisation possesses a long history of heavy industry.

The abundant mineral resources of Ashen territory encouraged the development of mining, metallurgy, manufacturing, engineering, energy production, and large-scale construction.

Modern Ashen worlds contain some of the largest industrial facilities within their region of the Omicron Galaxy.

Large railway networks are particularly common on several Ashen worlds, where they transport enormous quantities of minerals, industrial equipment, manufactured goods, and passengers between settlements.

## Clothing

Traditional Ashen clothing developed around heat, ashfall, and industrial environments.

Breathable materials, protective coverings, eye protection, scarves, masks, and garments capable of preventing ash accumulation became common throughout many cultures.

Modern Ashens living within controlled environments wear the same enormous variety of clothing found among other advanced species.

Protective clothing remains particularly important within industrial and volcanic regions.

## Relations with Other Species

Ashens interact extensively with humans, [[Glacians]], [[Androids]], [[Elysarians]], and other species throughout the Omicron Galaxy.

Environmental differences are particularly noticeable between Ashens and Glacians.

Temperatures comfortable to an Ashen may be unpleasant or dangerous to a Glacian, while environments preferred by Glacians can require Ashens to use thermal protection.

Mixed-species settlements commonly use localised climate systems to accommodate these differences.

Despite the extreme environmental contrast between their ancestral territories, Ashens and Glacians are not inherently enemies.

Relations between individuals and governments depend upon politics, history, and culture rather than biology.

## Ashens Outside the Kingdom

Ashen communities exist throughout the Omicron Galaxy.

Many work within engineering, industry, mining, construction, research, military service, trade, and numerous other professions, although Ashens are by no means limited to these fields.

Ashens born outside the Kingdom may possess little cultural connection to Vexar Prime.

Others maintain strong family, religious, linguistic, or cultural ties to their ancestral homeland.

## Identity

Ashen identity is not synonymous with loyalty to the [[Ashen Kingdom]].

An Ashen may belong to the [[Hell Hosts]], live within the [[Core Systems]], operate independently within the [[Outer Rim]], or consider another civilisation their home.

The Ashen species existed long before the current political structure of the Kingdom.

As with humans and Glacians, species, culture, nationality, and political allegiance remain separate concepts.
