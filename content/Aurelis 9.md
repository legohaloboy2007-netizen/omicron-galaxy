# Aurelis 9

Aurelis 9 is a mineral-rich terrestrial world located within the [[Aegimius System]], widely regarded as one of the most economically valuable planets in the region due to its immense natural wealth and stable environment. Unlike many heavily industrialized mining worlds throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]], Aurelis 9 is known for its calm atmosphere, vast golden supercontinents, and shallow reflective oceans that give the planet its distinctive appearance from orbit.

Beneath the planet’s peaceful surface lies an enormous concentration of rare metals, crystalline compounds, and valuable industrial minerals. These resources attracted prospectors, corporations, mining guilds, and exploratory fleets shortly after the Aegimius System was charted.

Although large-scale settlements exist across the planet, much of Aurelis 9 remains relatively untouched compared to more populated systems. Mining operations are carefully distributed across the continents, allowing enormous portions of the world to remain visually pristine despite its industrial importance.

Due to the immense wealth hidden beneath its surface, Aurelis 9 became a major destination for corporations, private contractors, smugglers, and treasure hunters seeking fortune within the largely unexplored [[Aegimius System]]. Rumors persist of undiscovered caverns, ancient geological formations, and buried precursor materials deep beneath the planet’s crust.

The world is often viewed as one of the most stable and visually beautiful planets within the Aegimius frontier, though competition over mining rights and resource control has occasionally led to sabotage operations, black market activity, and corporate disputes.
