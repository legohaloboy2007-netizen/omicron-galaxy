# Axionium

## Overview

Axionium is a highly valuable high-energy resource used throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]] as one of the primary foundations of advanced technology, interstellar infrastructure, and military power.

Unlike anomalous substances such as [[Element 95]] or [[Deatherium]], Axionium is considered stable under controlled conditions and has become essential to:
- power generation
- spacecraft systems
- advanced weaponry
- industrial manufacturing
- planetary infrastructure
- and experimental military technologies

Its immense energy density and adaptability have made it one of the most economically important materials in known space.

Entire systems have fought wars over access to major Axionium reserves.

## Nature

Axionium is an energy-reactive crystalline mineral capable of storing and transferring extraordinary amounts of power.

The material naturally absorbs ambient energy and can be refined into:
- fuel cells
- plasma systems
- reactor cores
- energy conduits
- and high-capacity power matrices

Unlike conventional reactor fuels, Axionium demonstrates:
- extremely high energy efficiency
- low degradation rates
- stable long-term output
- and strong compatibility with advanced technological systems

Because of these properties, Axionium became one of the driving forces behind rapid interstellar expansion during the modern era.

## Physical Appearance

Raw Axionium commonly appears as:
- glowing blue crystalline ore
- metallic-veined mineral clusters
- or luminous energy-reactive deposits

Refined forms often emit:
- soft blue-white light
- electrical discharge patterns
- low-frequency energy resonance
- and thermal fluctuations during heavy energy transfer

Large deposits can occasionally destabilize local electromagnetic systems.

## Discovery & Expansion

The exact origin of Axionium discovery remains disputed, though early records suggest it became widely utilized during the expansion of major modern civilizations.

As demand increased, numerous systems developed large-scale Axionium mining operations.

The material rapidly became essential for:
- interstellar travel
- military fleets
- planetary defense systems
- industrial economies
- and advanced weapons development

Today, Axionium is considered one of the most strategically important resources in the galaxy.

## Industrial Applications

Axionium is utilized in:
- starship reactors
- planetary power grids
- orbital defense systems
- military-grade energy weapons
- shield generators
- advanced manufacturing systems
- and experimental propulsion technologies

Its versatility allows it to function across both civilian and military infrastructure.

Many advanced civilizations rely on Axionium-based systems for daily operation.

## Military Use

Numerous factions utilize Axionium in:
- plasma weapon systems
- high-output reactor cores
- powered armor
- orbital artillery
- fleet propulsion
- and advanced combat technologies

The substance is particularly favored by:
- [[Hell Hosts]]
- major industrial powers
- corporate factions
- and military research divisions

Axionium-powered weapons are capable of generating devastating energy output when properly stabilized.

## The Focus Connection

Axionium serves as one of the core components used in:

[[The Focus]]

When combined with [[Neurothium]], Axionium provides the immense energy required to accelerate neural processing and perception enhancement systems.

Within Focus technology:
- Axionium powers the reaction
- Neurothium amplifies neurological activity

This combination allows users to enter heightened combat states commonly referred to as:

“The Zone.”

## Mining Worlds

Several worlds throughout the galaxy are heavily dependent on Axionium extraction.

One of the most significant known mining planets is:

[[A29O]]

An oceanic industrial world rich in deep-sea Axionium reserves.

Control over major Axionium deposits is considered strategically critical.

## Risks

Though generally stable under controlled conditions, Axionium can become dangerous when:
- improperly refined
- overcharged
- exposed to anomalous fields
- or damaged during reactor operation

Catastrophic Axionium failures may result in:
- reactor meltdowns
- plasma explosions
- electromagnetic collapse
- or localized atmospheric destruction

Despite this, the material remains significantly safer than most anomalous substances.

## Economic Importance

Axionium is one of the most traded and contested materials in known space.

Major powers frequently compete over:
- mining rights
- trade routes
- processing facilities
- and deep-space extraction zones

Entire economies depend on stable Axionium supply chains.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Axionium is known as:
- the fuel of interstellar civilization
- the backbone of modern technology
- and one of the most valuable energy resources ever discovered

Among industrial workers and military engineers, one phrase is commonly repeated:

> “No Axionium.  
> No civilization.”
