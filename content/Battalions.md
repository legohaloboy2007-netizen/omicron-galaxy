# Hell Host Battalions

The [[Hell Hosts]] maintain thousands of military formations spread across numerous branches, divisions, fleets, planetary garrisons, and specialist commands.

Among these are highly specialized battalions designed for environments, enemies, technologies, and missions that conventional military formations are poorly equipped to handle.

Some have become famous throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]] due to major campaigns, unusual equipment, exceptional battlefield records, famous commanders, or the highly specific nature of their operations.

This page documents several of the most notable Hell Host battalions.

It does not represent the complete Hell Host order of battle.

# Doom Corps and Eradication Formations

## 660th Flame Battalion "The Hell Igniters"

**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Division:** [[Eradication Division]]  
**Specialty:** Incendiary Warfare / Close-Quarters Clearing

The **660th Flame Battalion**, commonly known as the **Hell Igniters**, is one of the most recognizable incendiary formations within the Hell Hosts.

Despite its equipment, the battalion does not belong to the [[Fire Division]]. Instead, it operates as part of the Doom Corps' [[Eradication Division]].

Hell Igniters specialize in environments where conventional infantry weapons are poorly suited to completely clearing hostile positions. Bunkers, tunnels, enclosed facilities, fortified structures, biological infestations, and dense urban environments are common operational zones.

The battalion is also deployed during larger Eradication operations where widespread destruction of hostile biological material or contaminated environments has been authorized.

## 1000th Scuba Battalion "1000 Leagues Under the Sea"

**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Specialty:** Underwater Warfare / Submerged Operations

The **1000th Scuba Battalion** is a specialist aquatic combat formation designed for operations beneath oceans and other large bodies of liquid.

Its personnel receive training in underwater navigation, submerged combat, pressure hazards, underwater demolition, hostile aquatic environments, and assaults against submerged installations.

The battalion's equipment is extensively sealed and modified for underwater use.

Despite its somewhat humorous nickname, **1000 Leagues Under the Sea**, the battalion is heavily equipped due to the extraordinary hazards encountered during deep-water military operations.

## 324th Ice Battalion "Arctic Legion"

**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Specialty:** Extreme Cold Warfare

The **324th Ice Battalion**, better known as the **Arctic Legion**, specializes in warfare across frozen planets and other extreme low-temperature environments.

Its personnel operate across glaciers, frozen oceans, cryogenic facilities, blizzards, polar wastelands, and worlds where conventional military equipment rapidly becomes unreliable.

Arctic Legion armor incorporates advanced thermal regulation, environmental sealing, anti-freezing systems, and emergency life-support equipment.

Older records claimed that Arctic Legion suits could function at temperatures approaching **-1,000°C**. This figure is physically impossible and is now generally regarded as either an erroneous historical specification or an exaggerated description of the armor's cryogenic resistance.

The actual operational limits of modern Arctic Legion equipment vary by suit configuration.

### Typical Equipment

- 94CAR
- CR-556
- 365 SPAR
- HB245 Incendiary Grenade
- H2O Hydro-Thermal Grenade
- Extreme-environment survival equipment

# Hunter, Reconnaissance, and Covert Formations

## 612th Hunters Battalion

**Nickname:** The Reapers Division  
**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Specialty:** HVT Pursuit / Reconnaissance / Target Elimination

The **612th Hunters Battalion** is a specialist pursuit formation trained to locate and eliminate high-value targets across an enormous range of environments.

Hunters may pursue targets through cities, wilderness, hostile planets, active battlefields, or territory far behind enemy lines.

Rather than overwhelming targets through conventional military force, the battalion emphasizes tracking, patience, reconnaissance, infiltration, and precision elimination.

Notable personnel have included [[Longstride|Kira Rhenford]] and [[Redline|Alec Vandrix]].

## 004th Shadow Prime Battalion "Night Phantoms"

**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Specialty:** Infiltration / Sabotage / Assassination / Deep Operations

The **004th Shadow Prime Battalion**, commonly known as the **Night Phantoms**, is an elite covert operations formation specializing in missions deep inside hostile territory.

Where conventional Hell Host forces overwhelm enemy positions, the Night Phantoms are intended to bypass those positions entirely.

Typical operations include:

- Deep infiltration
- Sabotage
- Covert reconnaissance
- Intelligence recovery
- Strategic asset destruction
- Targeted assassination
- Communications disruption
- Hostile facility penetration
- Prisoner recovery
- Preparation for larger military assaults

Night Phantom teams generally operate in small groups rather than as massed infantry.

### Nanite Camouflage

One of the defining technologies associated with the 004th is its **nanite-assisted camouflage system**.

Specialized armor can adapt its surface properties to reduce visual, thermal, and sensor signatures.

It does not provide true invisibility.

Movement, damage, specialist detection systems, environmental conditions, and close observation can still expose an operator.

The technology makes a skilled infiltrator considerably harder to find.

It does not make a careless one invisible.

### Doctrine

The Night Phantoms prioritize avoiding unnecessary combat.

Every firefight creates noise.

Every missing patrol creates evidence.

Every alarm gives the enemy time to react.

If discovered, however, Night Phantom personnel are expected to respond rapidly and violently enough to regain control of the situation.

Their unofficial philosophy is simple:

> **"If they know we were there, something went wrong."**

There are exceptions.

Sometimes the mission specifically requires the enemy to know exactly who destroyed it.

# Shock and Assault Formations

## 892nd Galactic Drop Shock Troopers "Helljumpers"

**Abbreviation:** GDST  
**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Specialty:** Orbital Assault / Rapid Insertion

The **892nd Galactic Drop Shock Troopers**, better known as the **Helljumpers**, are among the most recognizable specialist assault formations within the Hell Hosts.

GDST personnel are deployed directly from spacecraft and orbital positions into active combat zones, allowing them to bypass conventional defensive lines and establish a foothold before larger forces arrive.

Their armor is significantly reinforced to survive high-speed insertion and immediate combat following deployment.

The Helljumpers are rarely encountered compared with conventional Hell Host infantry, but their reputation is widespread among enemy forces.

The term **Galactic** refers to their ability to deploy across distant operational theatres with supporting Hell Host fleets. Individual troopers are not literally launched between galaxies.

### Typical Equipment

- Orbital insertion systems
- Reinforced combat armor
- Jetpacks
- 558 Cycler Sniper Rifle
- Atrius IV
- C74 LPG
- 569 GLS

[[Skyfall|Selena Rhenford]] frequently supports GDST deployments through the [[18th Skyborne Wing]], while [[Hardfall|Marcus Hale]] serves directly within the 892nd.

## 43rd Heavy Assault Battalion "Warhounds"

**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Specialty:** Heavy Assault / Urban Warfare / Line-Breaking  
**Commander:** [[Tyrant|Thomas Powell]]

The **43rd Heavy Assault Battalion**, commonly known as the **Warhounds**, specializes in sustained frontline combat and the destruction of heavily defended enemy positions.

Commanded by [[Tyrant|Thomas Powell]], the battalion is deployed when conventional infantry assaults are expected to encounter intense resistance and withdrawing immediately after first contact is not an option.

The Warhounds operate according to a simple principle:

> **Gain ground. Hold it. Keep moving.**

Rather than relying on rapid raids or precision attacks, the 43rd applies continuous pressure until enemy defensive lines begin to collapse.

It specializes in:

- Heavy frontline combat
- Urban warfare
- Fortified position clearance
- Trench assaults
- Industrial combat
- Line-breaking
- Mechanized infantry advances
- Prolonged engagements

The battalion maintains extensive heavy weapons and logistical support, allowing it to remain operational during engagements that would force lighter formations to rotate.

Tyrant favors aggressive operations but rejects pointless aggression. Positions are attacked because capturing them serves an operational purpose, not because attacking them looks impressive.

Once the 43rd establishes a foothold, removing it becomes considerably more difficult.

Warhounds are not expected to attack without thought.

They are expected to **bite down and refuse to let go.**

## 403rd Tactical Assault Battalion "Stormbreakers"

**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Specialty:** Tactical Assault / Environmental Disruption / Area Control  
**Commander:** [[Hammerfall|Kassandra Virek]]

The **403rd Tactical Assault Battalion**, commonly known as the **Stormbreakers**, specializes in manipulating battlefield conditions in support of aggressive infantry operations.

Rather than simply attacking an enemy defensive position, the 403rd attempts to make that position increasingly difficult to defend.

Its doctrine is summarized by a simple principle:

> **If the battlefield favors the enemy, change the battlefield.**

Stormbreaker operations can involve:

- Dense obscurants
- Sensor disruption
- Area denial
- Alternative breaching routes
- Electronic countermeasures
- Environmental manipulation
- Hazardous-environment warfare
- Fortified position assault

Once enemy observation and defensive coordination have been disrupted, the battalion's assault elements rapidly exploit the resulting confusion.

[[Haze|Nyra Voss]] serves within the 403rd as an environmental disruption specialist, using obscurants, hazardous-environment equipment, visibility disruption, and area-denial systems.

The formation is commanded by [[Hammerfall|Kassandra Virek]].

Older records occasionally refer to Hammerfall as Captain of the **403rd Assault Division**, terminology originating from an earlier organizational period that remains informally used by some veterans.

Hammerfall determines where pressure must be applied.

Personnel such as Haze ensure the enemy cannot respond under favorable conditions.

## 702nd Strategic Response Battalion "First Answer"

**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Fleet Attachment:** [[Fleet One]]  
**Specialty:** Strategic Response / Combined-Arms Operations  
**Commander:** [[Champion]]

The **702nd Strategic Response Battalion**, commonly known as **First Answer**, is an elite Doom Corps formation attached to [[Fleet One]] and commanded by [[Champion]].

Unlike specialist battalions built around one environment or method of warfare, the 702nd is deliberately designed around **adaptability**.

It provides Fleet One with a highly capable ground formation that can rapidly respond to critical military situations without requiring a different specialist battalion for every possible mission.

Operations can include:

- Critical objective seizure
- Emergency reinforcement
- Counterattacks
- Ship-to-surface operations
- Installation assaults
- Defensive operations
- Recovery missions
- Combined-arms warfare
- Landing-zone establishment

The battalion is not intended to outperform every specialist formation at its own specialty.

Its strength is remaining highly capable across many of them.

### Fleet One

The 702nd maintains a close operational relationship with [[Fleet One]] and frequently stages aboard its vessels.

The [[HH Commonwealth]], flagship of Fleet One, commonly functions as a command and deployment hub for 702nd operations.

Champion simultaneously serves as Admiral of Fleet One, Captain of the Commonwealth, and commander of the 702nd.

Routine battalion administration and field coordination are handled by subordinate command staff, allowing Champion to retain his wider responsibilities.

His position nevertheless gives the battalion unusually direct integration with Fleet One's command structure.

The nickname **First Answer** reflects the battalion's purpose.

When Fleet One encounters a developing crisis requiring ground forces immediately, the 702nd is one of its first answers.

## 117th Voidborne Assault Battalion "Hullbreakers"

**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Specialty:** Boarding Warfare / Zero-Gravity Combat

The **117th Voidborne Assault Battalion**, known as the **Hullbreakers**, specializes in capturing hostile spacecraft, orbital stations, shipyards, and other installations where conventional planetary infantry doctrine becomes ineffective.

Personnel train extensively in zero-gravity movement, artificial-gravity failure, vacuum exposure, compartment clearing, hull breaching, and fighting within narrow spacecraft interiors.

Hullbreaker operations can begin outside an enemy vessel.

Assault teams may cross open space using maneuvering systems, breach directly through an external hull, or enter through hangars and docking systems before securing internal compartments.

Their equipment is designed to function regardless of whether the target vessel still possesses atmosphere or gravity.

Hullbreakers are frequently deployed when destroying an enemy spacecraft would be easier than capturing it, but whatever is aboard is considered too valuable to lose.

## 217th Biohazard Response Battalion "Gravewatch"

**Operational Designation:** Biohazard Response Unit (BRU)  
**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Command:** Hazard Operations  
**Specialty:** Outbreak Containment / Infected Elimination / Biohazard Response  
**Commander:** Watcher  
**First Deployment:** [[Valcara Prime]] — Incident Day 01  
**Motto:** *"We go in, so others can live."*

The **217th Biohazard Response Battalion**, better known as **Gravewatch** or simply **BRU**, is a specialist Hell Host formation deployed during severe biological emergencies.

Gravewatch operates in environments where disease, parasites, hostile organisms, biological weapons, or other transmissible threats have rendered conventional military operations dangerously ineffective.

Its primary objective is not simply to destroy infected targets.

It is to **contain the outbreak, recover those who can still be saved, and prevent the threat from spreading beyond the affected region.**

The arrival of Gravewatch is generally considered an indication that a biological incident has escalated beyond conventional emergency response.

---

### Operational Role

Gravewatch is responsible for:

- Outbreak containment
- Infected elimination
- Quarantine enforcement
- Civilian extraction
- Survivor recovery
- Sample recovery
- Biological threat identification
- Contaminated facility clearance
- Countermeasure support
- Establishment of containment zones
- Prevention of cross-regional contamination
- Recovery of infected personnel where treatment remains possible

The battalion may operate within cities, military installations, research facilities, spacecraft, colonies, underground complexes, or entire planetary quarantine zones.

During extreme outbreaks, Gravewatch can be deployed for prolonged operations with minimal contact with uncontaminated personnel.

---

### Containment Doctrine

The 217th operates according to a strict containment hierarchy.

The first priority is determining the boundaries of the outbreak.

The second is preventing those boundaries from expanding.

Only then can personnel begin systematic recovery, elimination, investigation, and decontamination operations.

Gravewatch teams establish controlled zones around contaminated areas, commonly separating territory into progressively more dangerous sectors.

Movement between these zones is heavily regulated.

Personnel leaving contaminated environments undergo extensive screening and decontamination regardless of whether they display symptoms.

No assumption is made that an apparently healthy individual is uninfected.

---

### Infected Elimination

When infected organisms become actively hostile or recovery is considered impossible, Gravewatch personnel are authorized to eliminate them.

Different outbreaks require different methods.

Some infected targets can be killed conventionally.

Others require destruction of specific organs, parasites, biological structures, or infected tissue.

Certain organisms may continue spreading contamination after death.

Gravewatch therefore places significant emphasis on understanding **how an infected target must be killed**, rather than simply determining whether enough ammunition has been fired into it.

Recovered biological samples are frequently transferred to the [[Research Division]] and [[Medical Corps]] for further study.

---

### Civilian Extraction

Despite its intimidating appearance and reputation, Gravewatch is fundamentally a **life-preservation formation**.

Its personnel routinely enter quarantine zones specifically to recover civilians and isolated military personnel who cannot escape independently.

Extraction teams identify survivors, perform preliminary medical screening, establish temporary safe corridors, and escort recovered individuals toward controlled decontamination sites.

The battalion's motto reflects this responsibility:

> **"We go in, so others can live."**

Gravewatch personnel knowingly enter environments everyone else is being ordered to evacuate.

---

### Biohazard Equipment

BRU personnel use heavily sealed combat armor designed to reduce exposure to infectious material and hazardous biological environments.

Standard equipment may include:

- Biosealed respirators
- Sealed hazard armor
- Independent breathing systems
- Biological scanners
- Medical diagnostic equipment
- Decontamination systems
- Thermal and ultraviolet equipment
- Sample containment units
- Emergency medical kits
- Breaching equipment
- Incendiary equipment where authorized
- Conventional Hell Host weapons

Armor is designed not only to protect the wearer but also to prevent contaminated personnel from carrying biological material outside an affected zone.

A damaged environmental seal is therefore treated as a serious incident even when the soldier wearing it appears unharmed.

---

### BRU Teams

Gravewatch commonly deploys specialized response teams containing personnel with complementary roles.

A typical team may include:

**Commander**  
Coordinates the response team, evaluates containment priorities, and maintains communication with wider Gravewatch command.

**Breacher**  
Specializes in close-quarters operations, forced entry, infected clearing, and access to sealed or obstructed areas.

**Marksman**  
Provides precision fire against dangerous infected targets and identifies biological weak points from greater distances.

**Medic**  
Specializes in trauma treatment, infection assessment, emergency stabilization, and the recovery of exposed personnel.

**Rifleman**  
Provides adaptable frontline combat capability and protection for specialist team members.

**Technical Specialist**  
Operates biological scanners, drones, containment systems, environmental sensors, and other specialist equipment.

Team composition changes according to the nature of the outbreak.

---

### Watcher

The 217th is commanded by an officer known by the callsign **Watcher**.

Watcher is a veteran of multiple major containment events and specializes in coordinating operations within densely populated and urban environments.

Known for remaining calm during rapidly deteriorating situations, Watcher places considerable emphasis on maintaining containment discipline even when conventional military instincts would encourage immediate pursuit or attack.

Within Gravewatch, hesitation can allow an outbreak to spread.

Recklessness can do exactly the same thing.

Watcher expects personnel to understand the difference.

Further information regarding Watcher's identity and service history is restricted.

---

### Valcara Prime

The first major deployment of Gravewatch occurred during the [[Valcara Prime]] incident.

BRU personnel began deploying on **Incident Day 01** as the scale of the biological emergency became apparent.

The situation deteriorated rapidly.

Large areas were placed under quarantine as infection spread through populated regions, forcing Gravewatch teams to conduct simultaneous containment, infected-elimination, civilian-extraction, and sample-recovery operations.

The Valcara deployment became one of the defining events in Gravewatch history and heavily influenced later Hell Host biohazard doctrine.

Many of the procedures now considered standard BRU practice were developed, modified, or proven during the crisis.

For Gravewatch personnel, Valcara Prime remains a reminder of how quickly a localized biological incident can become a planetary catastrophe.

---

### Relationship with the Medical Corps

Gravewatch works extensively with the [[Medical Corps]] during biological emergencies.

The distinction between the organizations is primarily operational.

The Medical Corps focuses upon:

- Diagnosis
- Treatment
- Medical evacuation
- Countermeasure deployment
- Patient care

Gravewatch focuses upon:

- Entering contaminated territory
- Securing quarantine zones
- Recovering survivors
- Eliminating hostile infected
- Preventing further transmission

Neither can effectively handle a major outbreak alone.

---

### Relationship with the Research Division

The [[Research Division]] studies recovered organisms, samples, pathogens, parasites, and other biological material in an attempt to understand the threat and develop countermeasures.

Gravewatch personnel frequently provide those samples.

The relationship is therefore straightforward:

**Gravewatch contains it.**

**Research figures out what it is.**

Preferably in that order.

---

### Relationship with the Eradication Division

Gravewatch and the [[Eradication Division]] represent very different stages of Hell Host biological-threat doctrine.

Gravewatch exists to contain an outbreak while preserving as many lives as possible.

Eradication formations are deployed when command determines that a contaminated area or hostile biological presence must be destroyed outright.

Gravewatch personnel therefore generally attempt containment, evacuation, and recovery before widespread eradication is considered.

There are incidents where this is impossible.

Those incidents tend to be remembered.

---

### Reputation

Gravewatch possesses a grim reputation throughout the Hell Hosts.

This is not because its personnel are feared.

It is because of what their arrival means.

Seeing ordinary Doom Corps formations indicates a battle.

Seeing the [[518th Radiation Waste Battalion|Radwalkers]] indicates contamination.

Seeing Gravewatch means somebody believes the contamination can **spread**.

Despite this reputation, the 217th is respected for repeatedly entering environments from which everyone else is being evacuated.

Its personnel operate between infected populations and the worlds beyond them.

Among BRU personnel, another phrase has consequently become closely associated with the formation:

> **"We are the shield between humanity and extinction."**

## 808th Anomalous Containment Battalion "Lockkeepers"

**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Specialty:** Anomalous Containment / Emergency Response

The **808th Anomalous Containment Battalion**, known as the **Lockkeepers**, responds to anomalous threats that cannot be handled safely by conventional military forces.

Its missions can involve:

- Escaped anomalous entities
- Unstable artifacts
- Reality-distorting phenomena
- Unknown energy events
- Compromised research facilities
- Dangerous ancient technology
- Element 95 incidents

The Lockkeepers are **not researchers**.

Scientific investigation remains the responsibility of organizations such as the [[Research Division]].

The 808th exists for situations where whatever was being researched has stopped cooperating.

Personnel use modular containment equipment because no single system is effective against every anomaly.

Physical barriers, energy fields, remote systems, environmental isolation, specialized sensors, and heavy weapons may all be employed depending upon the threat.

Destroying an anomaly is not automatically considered preferable to containment.

Some cannot be reliably destroyed.

Others become considerably worse when somebody tries.

The first objective is therefore to isolate the threat, establish what it can do, and prevent the incident from expanding.

# Experimental Warfare Formations

## 120th Nanite Fusion Battalion "Grey Swarm"

**Specialty:** Nanite-Assisted Warfare  
**Status:** Restricted

The **120th Nanite Fusion Battalion**, known as **Grey Swarm**, fields experimental nanotechnology integrated into infantry equipment.

Nanite systems can assist with armor maintenance, battlefield repair, medical stabilization, reconnaissance, and autonomous drone operations.

The technology remains unpredictable under certain battlefield conditions, resulting in heavy restrictions surrounding Grey Swarm deployments.

Many of its operations remain classified.

---

## 999th Experimental Weapons Battalion "Shoved Under the Rug"

**Specialty:** Experimental Weapons / Prototype Warfare  
**Status:** Highly Restricted

The **999th Experimental Weapons Battalion** exists to field-test equipment considered too unstable, dangerous, unconventional, or poorly understood for ordinary Hell Host formations.

Its unofficial nickname, **Shoved Under the Rug**, reflects its reputation as the place where military projects go when nobody else wants responsibility for them.

Personnel may encounter prototype weapons, experimental armor, augmentation systems, anomalous technology, and equipment whose long-term effects remain uncertain.

Despite the danger, service within the 999th can be prestigious among weapons specialists and experimental personnel.

[[Null|Kael Virek]] serves within the battalion as part of **Project Null**.

### Typical Equipment

There is no standard loadout.

Equipment depends entirely upon whatever unfortunate idea is currently being tested.

# Information Warfare

## 771st Psychological Warfare Battalion "Whispers"

**Specialty:** Psychological Warfare / Information Operations

The **771st Psychological Warfare Battalion**, known as **Whispers**, specializes in damaging enemy cohesion without necessarily engaging them directly.

Operations include propaganda, deception, misinformation, psychological manipulation, false battlefield signals, holo-projected deception, and targeted morale campaigns.

Whispers personnel work alongside intelligence and conventional military formations to create confusion before and during major operations.

One of the battalion's most famous campaigns targeted the Free World Order's **7th Army**, saturating its information environment with false reports and contradictory intelligence before a major engagement.

The resulting confusion severely disrupted the formation before conventional fighting began.

### Typical Equipment

- Holo-projectors
- Communications interception systems
- Sonic systems
- Electronic warfare equipment
- Battlefield deception technology

# Organization

These battalions represent only a small fraction of the Hell Host military.

Thousands of additional numbered formations exist throughout the [[Doom Corps]], [[Fleet Guard]], [[Aviation Division]], [[Fire Division]], [[Spectre Division]], planetary defense forces, armored formations, specialist commands, and other branches.

Some are permanent formations with histories spanning centuries.

Others are created, reorganized, merged, disbanded, or rebuilt as military requirements change.

Battalion numbers do not necessarily indicate chronological creation or current strength. A formation may retain its number even after its purpose, command structure, equipment, or organizational affiliation changes significantly.

This has occasionally produced historical records referring to formations by classifications no longer considered accurate.

Battalion nicknames are similarly inconsistent.

Some are officially approved honorifics.

Others originate from soldiers, enemy forces, famous campaigns, jokes, or incidents that eventually became inseparable from the formation.

The enormous variety of Hell Host battalions reflects the environments in which the faction operates.

A military spanning numerous planets cannot assume every war will be fought on familiar ground.

Some enemies hide beneath oceans.

Others hide underground.

Some fight from orbit.

Some use machines.

Some spread through biological infection.

And occasionally, the Hell Hosts encounter something for which no existing military doctrine was designed.

When that happens, another specialist formation may eventually be created.

The Hell Hosts do not maintain thousands of battalions because every one is required in every war.

They maintain them because somewhere in the [[Omicron Galaxy]], eventually, somebody is going to need one.
