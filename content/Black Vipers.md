# Black Vipers

The Black Vipers are a ruthless cartel-style criminal syndicate operating throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]], feared for their brutality, territorial control, and violent enforcement tactics. Unlike more covert criminal organizations such as the [[Mirage Collective]], the Black Vipers maintain power openly through intimidation, fear, and overwhelming force.

The syndicate controls numerous smuggling routes, black market sectors, narcotics operations, weapons trades, and criminal territories across frontier systems and Outer Rim colonies. Entire settlements and trade corridors are rumoured to operate under Black Viper influence, with local populations often forced to obey cartel rule in exchange for protection or survival.

The Black Vipers are infamous for their savage retaliation against betrayal, disobedience, or interference. Public executions, mass intimidation campaigns, and large-scale gang wars are commonly associated with the faction. Their symbol — a black serpent — has become one of the most feared criminal markings throughout the galaxy.

Unlike traditional military powers, the Black Vipers function through regional crime lords, enforcement crews, smuggling captains, and heavily armed syndicate cells. Internal competition between Viper leaders is common, though all ultimately answer to the upper leadership of the cartel.

The organization is heavily involved in narcotics trafficking, illegal weapons distribution, extortion, piracy, bounty operations, and mercenary contracts. Their influence is particularly strong in lawless territories where governments and military powers struggle to maintain control.

Black Viper operatives are commonly identified by dark tactical armour, serpent insignias, venom-inspired weaponry, and neon green markings associated with the cartel’s identity.
