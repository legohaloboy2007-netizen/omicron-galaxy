# Black Wolf

## Overview

**Real Name:** Unknown

**Age:** 28

**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]

**Unit:** [[The Wolf Unit]]

**Rank:** Lieutenant Commander

Black Wolf is one-third of the:
[[Wolf Unit]]

and serves as the primary assault specialist and field leader of the trio.

Direct, intimidating, and relentless, Black Wolf is often the first member of the Wolves that enemies encounter.

Unfortunately for them, that is usually the beginning of the end.

Among military personnel, Black Wolf is known for his aggressive tactics, unwavering loyalty, and near-instinctive synchronization with:
[[White Wolf]]

Many soldiers describe the pair as:

> "Two bodies using one plan."

---

## Appearance

Black Wolf stands approximately:

**6'1"**

with a broad, athletic build.

### Notable Features

- Dark hair
- Amber eyes
- Scar across right eyebrow
- Sharp jawline
- Permanently annoyed expression

His appearance alone is often enough to discourage conversation.

Most people assume he is angry.

Most of the time, he simply looks that way.

### Equipment

Black Wolf typically wears:

- Dark tactical combat gear
- Wolf Unit insignia
- Reinforced combat armor
- Hooded combat cloak
- Multiple knife sheaths

His equipment is heavily worn from years of active operations.

Unlike many soldiers, he rarely replaces gear unless absolutely necessary.

---

## Personality

Black Wolf is:

- Cold
- Direct
- Loyal
- Intimidating
- Highly protective

He rarely wastes words and has little patience for unnecessary conversation.

To strangers, he can appear hostile.

To enemies, he usually is.

Despite this reputation, Black Wolf possesses a quieter side that is only visible to those he genuinely trusts.

### Loyalty

Black Wolf values loyalty above nearly everything else.

Once someone earns his trust, he remains fiercely protective of them.

Few people ever reach that point.

---

## Combat Style

Black Wolf specializes in:

- Close-quarters combat
- Aggressive assaults
- Target pressure
- Pursuit operations

He functions as the pressure point of the Wolf Unit.

While White Wolf eliminates targets from a distance, Black Wolf forces them into mistakes.

### Weapons

#### [[94-CAR Carbine Assault Rifle]]

His primary firearm.

Reliable, versatile, and effective across a wide range of combat situations.

---

#### Combat Knives

Black Wolf strongly prefers knife combat whenever possible.

His speed, aggression, and precision make him exceptionally dangerous at close range.

Many opponents never survive long enough to force him onto his rifle.

---

## Relationships

### [[White Wolf]]

Black Wolf's twin sister.

The most important person in his life.

The pair possess an almost supernatural level of synchronization, often communicating through:

- Gestures
- Eye contact
- Unspoken cues

Many soldiers believe the twins possess their own language.

Whether this is true remains unknown.

### Protective Instinct

Black Wolf's protectiveness toward White Wolf borders on obsession.

If she is seriously harmed, Black Wolf becomes extraordinarily difficult to control.

Several classified incidents suggest he has pursued attackers long after objectives were completed.

---

### [[Grey Wolf]]

Black Wolf views Grey Wolf as both a mentor and father figure.

Although he rarely expresses it openly, Grey Wolf is one of the few individuals whose advice he genuinely values.

---

### [[Predator]]

Predator respects Black Wolf's effectiveness and reliability.

Although their personalities differ significantly, Predator considers the Wolf Unit one of Hell Hosts' most dependable assets.

---

### [[Destiny]]

The two maintain a professional relationship built on mutual respect.

Neither actively seeks the other's company, but both recognize the other's abilities.

---

## Habits

- Cracks his knuckles before operations
- Leans against walls instead of sitting
- Finishes White Wolf's sentences
- Constantly scans rooms for threats
- Rarely sleeps more than necessary

---

## Off-Duty Life

When not deployed, Black Wolf prefers simple activities.

Most commonly:

- Sitting on a couch
- Watching terrible movies
- Drinking coffee
- Existing quietly alongside trusted company

He rarely seeks conversation.

Presence is usually enough.

---

## Likes

- Rainstorms
- Heavy metal music
- Competitive activities
- Knife throwing
- Strong coffee
- Quiet company
- Winning

---

## Dislikes

- Crowded social events
- Unnecessary attention
- Bureaucracy
- Losing
- Threats against his family

---

## Hidden Talents

### Cooking

To the surprise of nearly everyone outside the Wolf Unit, Black Wolf is an exceptional cook.

Very few people believe this until they experience it firsthand.

White Wolf and Grey Wolf appear completely unsurprised.

---

## Reputation

### Within Hell Hosts

Black Wolf is respected as:

- Reliable
- Dangerous
- Loyal
- Extremely effective

Most soldiers avoid provoking him.

Not because he is cruel.

Because everyone knows how protective he is of those he cares about.

---

### Outside Hell Hosts

Enemy personnel frequently describe Black Wolf as:

- Aggressive
- Unrelenting
- Impossible to intimidate

Many reports identify him as the most visible member of the Wolf Unit.

Unfortunately, focusing on Black Wolf often means failing to notice White Wolf.
