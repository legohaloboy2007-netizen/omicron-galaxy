# Blackjack

**Real Name:** Adrian Cole  
**Codename:** Blackjack  
**Species:** [[Humans|Human]]  
**Gender:** Male  
**Faction:** [[Free World Order]]  
**Role:** Precision Rifleman / Combat Analyst  
**Status:** Active  

Adrian Cole, better known by his codename **Blackjack**, is a precision rifleman and combat specialist serving within the [[Free World Order]].

Blackjack specialises in identifying and exploiting weaknesses in heavily armoured personnel, machines, creatures, vehicles, and other difficult targets.

Unlike conventional snipers, Blackjack typically operates considerably closer to active combat. He combines an advanced targeting system with a powerful precision battle rifle, allowing him to analyse opponents while fighting alongside other Free World personnel.

His reputation is built around calculated risk.

Blackjack rarely receives absolute certainty from his equipment.

He simply knows when the odds are good enough.

## Personality

Blackjack is confident, observant, sarcastic, and unusually comfortable taking calculated risks.

He approaches both combat and everyday situations through probability.

Blackjack understands that very few decisions offer complete certainty and has little interest in waiting indefinitely for perfect conditions.

Instead, he gathers as much information as possible, determines the likely outcomes, and makes his decision.

This attitude can make him appear reckless.

He generally isn't.

Blackjack distinguishes heavily between a calculated risk and a stupid one.

He is also considerably more sociable than several of the Free World Order's other specialists and is known for dry humour during otherwise dangerous situations.

Blackjack has a habit of using gambling terminology when discussing combat, sometimes deliberately irritating personnel around him.

He rarely claims that something is guaranteed.

He prefers **good odds**.

## Free World Order

Blackjack serves as a specialist combatant within the [[Free World Order]].

His primary role involves engaging targets that conventional infantry struggle to damage effectively.

Rather than overwhelming these targets through sheer firepower, Blackjack studies them.

Armour construction, movement patterns, previous damage, exposed mechanisms, biological structure, weapon placement, and environmental conditions can all provide opportunities.

This makes Blackjack particularly useful when Free World personnel encounter unfamiliar enemies.

He may not immediately know how to kill something.

His job is to figure it out.

## Targeting System

Blackjack's defining piece of equipment is an advanced targeting and combat-analysis system integrated into his helmet.

The system combines information gathered through optical sensors, thermal imaging, rangefinding equipment, battlefield intelligence, previous combat records, and direct observation.

As Blackjack observes a target, the system attempts to identify potential vulnerabilities.

These may include:

- Weak or damaged armour plating
- Gaps between armour sections
- Mechanical joints
- Weapon components
- Shield generators
- Power systems
- Sensory equipment
- Exposed biological structures
- Existing wounds
- Structural weaknesses in vehicles or machinery

Potential targets are assigned varying levels of confidence.

The system does not guarantee that an identified weakness will produce the desired result.

Incomplete information can result in inaccurate assessments, particularly when analysing unfamiliar technology, anomalous entities, or previously undocumented species.

Blackjack therefore treats the system as an adviser rather than an answer.

He makes the final shot.

## Combat Analysis

Blackjack's effectiveness does not come entirely from technology.

He possesses extensive knowledge of weapons, armour, anatomy, machinery, and battlefield behaviour.

If his targeting system becomes damaged or unavailable, he remains capable of identifying weaknesses manually.

Blackjack frequently observes an opponent for several moments before committing to a particularly difficult shot.

Against known targets, this process can be almost instantaneous.

Against unfamiliar opponents, his assessment becomes progressively more accurate as the engagement continues.

Consequently, an enemy that fails to eliminate Blackjack early may find him increasingly dangerous as the battle progresses.

## Battle Rifle

Blackjack's signature weapon is a powerful precision battle rifle designed to bridge the gap between a conventional infantry rifle and a dedicated sniper weapon.

The rifle fires a high-powered ballistic cartridge capable of engaging armoured personnel and other resilient targets while remaining practical during mobile combat.

It is primarily operated semi-automatically.

Its comparatively heavy recoil and ammunition prevent it from functioning as a conventional assault rifle, but Blackjack's experience allows him to maintain accurate fire at a considerably faster rate than would normally be expected from a precision weapon.

The rifle can accept specialised ammunition when required, although Blackjack frequently uses conventional ballistic rounds.

For Blackjack, specialised ammunition is useful.

Shot placement is more important.

## Combat Style

Blackjack prefers medium-to-long-range engagements.

He moves with friendly forces rather than remaining permanently positioned far behind them, allowing him to react rapidly when unexpected threats appear.

During an engagement, Blackjack identifies whichever hostile target is causing the greatest difficulty and begins analysing it.

He may deliberately fire several shots intended primarily to observe how armour, shields, or defensive systems react.

Once a vulnerability is identified, he attempts to exploit it before the enemy can compensate.

Blackjack is particularly effective when cooperating with heavier Free World personnel.

While soldiers such as [[Slaughter]] draw attention and apply overwhelming firepower, Blackjack can use the resulting distraction to identify and attack specific vulnerabilities.

## Calculated Risk

Blackjack's codename originates from his tendency to think in terms of probability and calculated risk.

He does not require certainty before acting.

A vulnerability identified with extremely high confidence is an obvious target.

A vulnerability identified with moderate confidence becomes a judgement call.

A potentially catastrophic weakness identified with low confidence becomes a gamble.

Blackjack has built much of his reputation upon knowing which gambles are worth taking.

He has also lost enough of them to understand that luck should never be mistaken for skill.

## Equipment

Blackjack's armour is lighter than equipment used by dedicated heavy infantry but provides sufficient protection for conventional frontline combat.

His helmet contains the majority of his specialised systems, including his targeting suite, rangefinder, thermal imaging, optical magnification, environmental sensors, and battlefield data connection.

He additionally carries a sidearm, combat knife, medical equipment, spare ammunition, and compact maintenance equipment for his rifle and targeting systems.

Blackjack is particularly protective of his helmet.

Damage to its targeting suite does not render him combat ineffective, but considerably reduces the amount of information available to him.

## Relationship with Killshot

Blackjack and [[Killshot]] are both exceptional marksmen, although their battlefield roles differ substantially.

Killshot specialises in extreme precision, concealment, reconnaissance, and elimination of targets from long range.

Blackjack operates closer to conventional combat and focuses upon analysing difficult opponents for exploitable weaknesses.

The distinction has occasionally produced friendly competition between the two.

Blackjack has never seriously claimed to be the superior sniper.

He has, however, argued that hitting the smallest possible target is considerably easier once someone has told you **which small target actually matters**.

Killshot reportedly disagrees.

## Relationship with Roulette

Blackjack is acquainted with [[Roulette]], a member of [[The Bounties Legion]].

The exact nature and history of their relationship remain largely undocumented.

Their respective codenames have made the association a recurring source of jokes among personnel familiar with both individuals.

Blackjack and Roulette appear comfortable around one another and have interacted outside strictly hostile circumstances, although neither has publicly clarified the extent of their relationship.

Whatever exists between them has yet to become particularly significant within the wider histories of their respective factions.

## Reputation

Blackjack is respected within the Free World Order as a reliable specialist against targets that initially appear difficult or impossible to defeat through conventional means.

His reputation differs from that of soldiers known primarily for overwhelming combat ability.

Blackjack does not need to be the strongest person on a battlefield.

He needs enough time to determine where strength stops mattering.

When an unfamiliar enemy appears, Free World personnel have learned that keeping Blackjack alive long enough to study it can dramatically alter the course of an engagement.

## Trivia

Blackjack frequently uses gambling terminology during combat.

Whether this behaviour existed before he adopted the codename or developed afterward is disputed by personnel who know him.

He has occasionally been accused of deliberately exaggerating the habit because he finds everyone else's irritation entertaining.

Blackjack refuses to clarify the matter.
