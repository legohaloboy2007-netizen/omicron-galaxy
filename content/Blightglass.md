# Blightglass

## Overview

Blightglass is a semi-organic crystalline corruption material formed through prolonged exposure to:

[[Element 95]]

The substance is primarily associated with:
- the [[Farlands]]
- corrupted ecosystems
- abandoned outbreak zones
- and regions affected by advanced reality contamination

Unlike naturally stable crystals, Blightglass behaves as both:
- mineral growth
- and partially living corruption structure

The material is infamous for:
- spreading through contaminated terrain
- reacting to nearby consciousness
- causing hallucinations
- and altering surrounding biological environments

Across the galaxy, Blightglass is considered one of the most recognizable signs of severe Element 95 corruption.

## Nature

Blightglass forms when Element 95 contamination saturates:
- planetary crust
- biological matter
- structures
- or atmospheric systems

Over time, the corruption crystallizes into aggressive semi-organic growths capable of:
- spreading through surfaces
- infecting ecosystems
- and reacting to nearby living organisms

Researchers remain uncertain whether Blightglass is:
- alive
- partially conscious
- or simply reacting to surrounding neurological activity

The material frequently behaves unpredictably under observation.

## Physical Appearance

Blightglass commonly appears as:
- jagged crimson-purple crystal growth
- pulsing blackened shards
- or semi-transparent infected crystal formations

The material often emits:
- faint internal glow
- low-frequency resonance
- heatless vapor
- and biological movement beneath the crystal surface

Certain formations appear:
wet,
breathing,
or slowly expanding over time.

Large Blightglass forests may resemble:
infected crystal jungles overtaking entire landscapes.

## Formation

Blightglass typically develops in:
- severe Element 95 outbreak zones
- corrupted ruins
- contaminated ecosystems
- and abandoned Farlands settlements

The growth process accelerates in regions containing:
- high emotional activity
- death
- consciousness-linked anomalies
- or Veil instability

Some scientists believe the crystal feeds on:
psychological activity itself.

## Properties

Blightglass possesses several highly dangerous anomalous characteristics.

### Corruption Growth
The material slowly spreads across:
- terrain
- structures
- machinery
- and organic matter

---

### Consciousness Reactivity
Blightglass appears to react to:
- nearby thoughts
- emotional states
- fear responses
- and psychological instability

---

### Hallucinogenic Influence
Extended exposure frequently causes:
- visual hallucinations
- paranoia
- auditory distortion
- memory instability
- and Veil-linked perception anomalies

---

### Biological Integration
The material may partially fuse with:
- flesh
- nervous systems
- and surrounding organic tissue

---

### Environmental Mutation
Large formations can alter entire ecosystems and atmospheric conditions.

## Farlands Ecosystems

Blightglass has become one of the defining materials of the:
[[Farlands]]

Entire regions are now covered by:
- crystal forests
- infected plains
- pulsing cave systems
- and corrupted structures consumed by Blightglass growth

Many native Farlands organisms have adapted to:
- feed on
- inhabit
- or spread the crystal

Certain predators camouflage themselves within Blightglass terrain.

## Relationship to The Veil

Blightglass demonstrates strong association with:
[[The Veil]]

High-density formations frequently produce:
- Veil whispers
- hallucinations
- consciousness overlap
- and reality instability

Some exposed individuals report:
seeing movement within the crystal,
or hearing voices originating from inside the formations themselves.

In rare cases, entire Blightglass fields appear to react collectively to nearby observers.

## Biological Effects

Direct contact with Blightglass may result in:
- skin crystallization
- neurological corruption
- memory fragmentation
- emotional instability
- and progressive mutation

Advanced exposure may eventually lead to:
- partial assimilation into corruption growth
- loss of identity
- or complete psychological collapse

Some victims continue moving after full crystallization.

## Industrial Interest

Despite its danger, certain factions attempt to study or weaponize Blightglass.

Known interested groups include:
- [[Obsidian Veil]]
- corruption cults
- rogue researchers
- and black-market anomaly traders

Potential applications include:
- psychological warfare
- corruption weaponry
- biological experimentation
- and Veil synchronization research

Most experiments end catastrophically.

## Containment Difficulty

Blightglass is extremely difficult to contain due to its:
- growth behavior
- psychological influence
- and environmental adaptability

Standard containment methods often fail once growth reaches critical density.

Destroyed formations may:
- regrow
- spread through microscopic residue
- or reappear unexpectedly nearby

Some contaminated facilities remain abandoned centuries after initial outbreaks.

## Common Theories

The true nature of Blightglass remains unknown.

Popular theories suggest:
- it is crystallized Element 95 corruption
- a partially living extension of [[The Veil]]
- or a physical manifestation of consciousness destabilization

Some researchers fear large Blightglass networks may function as:
a distributed intelligence.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Blightglass is known as:
- the crystal corruption of the Farlands
- one of the clearest signs of Element 95 contamination
- and one of the most psychologically dangerous materials ever discovered

Among Farlands survivors, one warning is commonly repeated:

> “If the crystals begin growing toward you,  
> run.”
