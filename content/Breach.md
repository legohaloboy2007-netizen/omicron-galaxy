# Breach

> "He doesn't fire the cannon. He tells it where to end things."

## Overview

**Rylan Voss**, known by the callsign **Breach**, is a Hell Host forward siege operative serving within the [[Doom Corps]] and the [[188th Siege Battalion|188th Siege Battalion "Iron Casters"]].

Operating ahead of conventional artillery positions, Breach specializes in reconnaissance, target designation, artillery coordination, and forward operations within areas that are often already under heavy bombardment.

His job places him directly between Hell Host infantry and some of the faction's most destructive conventional weapons.

Breach identifies the target.

He confirms the coordinates.

Then he gets everyone who matters out of the way.

## Appearance

Rylan wears reinforced Doom Corps combat armour configured for extended operations within active siege zones.

His equipment prioritizes protection from:

- Fragmentation
- Blast pressure
- Structural debris
- Smoke and airborne contaminants
- Prolonged battlefield exposure

His helmet incorporates an enlarged multi-optic observation system used for long-range identification, ranging, environmental scanning, and target designation.

Breach frequently operates in environments where conventional visibility is poor.

Smoke, dust, fire, collapsing structures, and artillery impacts are normal working conditions.

His equipment is designed accordingly.

# Personality

Breach is quiet, focused, disciplined, and emotionally reserved.

He rarely speaks unnecessarily during operations and generally prefers precise communication over conversation.

Unlike some Doom Corps soldiers, Rylan does not particularly enjoy destruction.

He simply understands it extremely well.

Years spent directing artillery onto occupied positions have made him acutely aware of what heavy weapons actually do once fired.

He does not celebrate successful bombardments.

He confirms the objective.

Checks for survivors.

Then moves on.

This emotional distance is partly professional discipline and partly the result of prolonged exposure to some of the most destructive conventional warfare conducted by the Hell Hosts.

---

# The Iron Casters

The **188th Siege Battalion**, commonly known as the **Iron Casters**, is a Doom Corps formation specializing in heavy artillery and siege warfare.

The unit supports Hell Host operations involving:

- Fortified enemy positions
- Large defensive complexes
- Entrenched military formations
- Urban strongholds
- Heavy armour concentrations
- Area denial
- Counter-battery operations
- Battlefield preparation

Iron Caster artillery can destroy targets far beyond direct visual range.

That capability creates another problem:

Someone still needs to tell them exactly where to shoot.

Forward operatives such as Breach solve that problem.

---

# Forward Siege Operations

Breach frequently operates significantly ahead of the primary Iron Caster artillery line.

His responsibilities include:

1. Entering or observing the target area.
2. Identifying hostile positions.
3. Confirming friendly and civilian locations.
4. Designating priority targets.
5. Transmitting accurate targeting information.
6. Coordinating the strike window.
7. Confirming impact and damage.
8. Redirecting fire when necessary.

This often requires Rylan to remain dangerously close to the area being targeted.

Artillery calculations are extremely precise.

Battlefields are not.

Enemy movement, damaged communications, weather, debris, electronic warfare, or simple human error can turn a safe observation position into an impact zone very quickly.

Breach has survived enough close calls to treat every fire mission seriously.

---

# Artillery Designation

Breach carries a specialized laser designation and targeting system connected to Hell Host battlefield networks.

When possible, target information is transmitted through [[Nexus|Samuel Ardyn]] and other battlefield-control systems before reaching artillery command.

This allows multiple sources of intelligence to be compared before a strike is authorized.

Breach can designate:

- Fortifications
- Vehicles
- Artillery positions
- Defensive lines
- Communications systems
- Enemy concentrations
- Structural weak points
- Reinforcement routes

He is particularly skilled at identifying targets whose destruction will cause wider defensive collapse.

Sometimes destroying one wall is more useful than destroying the entire building.

# Siege Integration

## TAC — Tributary Artillery Cannon

The **TAC**, or **Tributary Artillery Cannon**, is one of the heavy artillery systems supported by Iron Caster forward teams.

Mounted aboard large mobile or floating platforms, the TAC is capable of delivering extremely powerful long-range bombardment.

Different configurations can support conventional siege ammunition and specialized payloads.

Breach frequently provides forward targeting data for TAC batteries during large operations.

---

## RAC — Rotary Artillery Cannon

The **RAC**, or **Rotary Artillery Cannon**, is a heavy rapid-fire artillery platform designed for sustained suppression and area control.

Rather than relying entirely on individual high-yield impacts, RAC systems can maintain repeated fire against enemy positions.

They are particularly effective when suppressing:

- Reinforcement corridors
- Defensive formations
- Vehicle concentrations
- Enemy artillery
- Large exposed positions

Breach coordinates fire patterns to minimize unnecessary ammunition expenditure and reduce the risk to advancing Hell Host forces.

---

# Relationship with Nexus

[[Nexus|Samuel Ardyn]] is one of Breach's most valuable operational partners.

Nexus provides battlefield telemetry, drone surveillance, movement tracking, and broader tactical information.

Breach provides direct confirmation from the ground.

The distinction matters.

A drone can identify what appears to be an enemy position.

Breach can determine whether it actually needs several tonnes of artillery dropped onto it.

During coordinated operations, Nexus may continuously update Rylan with:

- Enemy movements
- Friendly positions
- Changing firing windows
- New targets
- Artillery availability

Breach then makes the final forward assessment.

---

# Relationship with Hammerfall

[[Hammerfall|Kassandra Virek]] frequently benefits from Iron Caster support during major assaults.

Their roles naturally complement one another.

Breach destroys or weakens the defensive structure.

Hammerfall exploits the opening.

Rylan has occasionally remained close enough to designated targets to provide Hammerfall's forces with immediate post-impact intelligence.

Their basic operational relationship is straightforward:

> **Breach:** "You break the ground."

> **Hammerfall:** "I finish what's left."

---

# The Weight of the Role

Breach understands exactly what happens when he transmits firing coordinates.

That knowledge has affected him considerably.

Forward observers do not experience artillery as distant dots on a tactical display.

Rylan sees the target beforehand.

He sees the impact.

And he frequently enters the area afterward.

The difference between a successful fire mission and a catastrophic mistake may be a handful of coordinates.

Because of this, Breach compulsively verifies targeting information whenever circumstances allow.

He would rather delay a strike for several seconds than spend the rest of his life knowing he directed it onto the wrong position.

---

# Combat

Although primarily an artillery coordinator, Breach is fully trained for direct combat.

Forward siege teams regularly operate without immediate support and must be capable of defending themselves while establishing observation positions.

Rylan specializes in:

- Reconnaissance
- Close-quarters combat
- Defensive shooting
- Infiltration
- Target acquisition
- Demolition
- Battlefield navigation

His goal is usually not prolonged engagement.

If Breach is exchanging sustained gunfire with the enemy, something has already gone wrong.

# Operational Reputation

Breach has earned considerable trust within the Iron Casters because of his consistency and accuracy.

Artillery crews rarely see the targets they engage.

They therefore depend heavily on personnel such as Rylan.

A mistake made by a rifleman may cost one shot.

A mistake made by Breach can redirect an entire artillery battery.

He treats that responsibility accordingly.

Despite the scale of destruction associated with his role, Rylan does not seek recognition for it.

As far as he is concerned, nobody should remember the person holding the designator.

They should remember that the artillery landed where it was supposed to.

# House Voss

Rylan belongs to **House Voss**, one of the established Houses represented within the Hell Hosts.

Like other major Houses, the Voss name is shared across a wider lineage rather than necessarily indicating immediate family relationships between every individual carrying it.

Several members of House Voss have served across different periods of Hell Host history.

Rylan himself rarely places much importance on the name while deployed.

An artillery shell does not care who designated the target.
