# Bulwark

> [!info] Character Profile  
> **Real Name:** Gideon Vale  
> **Codename:** Bulwark  
> **Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]  
> **Division:** [[Doom Corps]]  
> **Rank/Position:** Commander of the Doom Corps  
> **Status:** Active  
> **Species:** Human  
> **Affiliation:** Hell Host High Command  
> **Known For:** Building the modern Doom Corps into one of the most powerful military divisions in the [[Omicron Galaxy]]

---

## Overview

**Bulwark**, real name **Gideon Vale**, is the commander of the [[Doom Corps]] and one of the most respected military figures within the [[Hell Hosts]].

Unlike many legendary Hell Host figures, Bulwark is not famous because of overwhelming power, theatrical violence, or battlefield spectacle. His reputation comes from discipline, service, memory, and the soldiers he has shaped across decades of command.

To most of the galaxy, Bulwark is the officer responsible for turning the Doom Corps into the backbone of Hell Host ground warfare.

To Doom Corps soldiers, he is something far more personal.

He is the commander who remembers names.

The officer who attends memorials.

The veteran who built an army without forgetting the people inside it.

A common saying among Doom Corps personnel states:

> **“Predator built the Hell Hosts. Bulwark built the Doom Corps.”**

---

## Personality

Bulwark is calm, disciplined, patient, and quietly commanding.

He rarely raises his voice, and he does not need to. His presence alone is often enough to make soldiers correct their posture, officers review their reports, and recruits stop speaking.

He is not cold, but he is controlled.

Bulwark does not waste words on empty speeches or dramatic threats. When he speaks, his words are direct, measured, and difficult to ignore.

Despite his stern reputation, Bulwark is deeply compassionate toward the soldiers under his command. He understands that the Doom Corps is not merely a military machine, but a living institution made of frightened recruits, exhausted veterans, grieving friends, and officers carrying the weight of impossible orders.

He does not encourage weakness.

He does not tolerate carelessness.

But he does understand people.

This combination has made him one of the few Hell Host commanders who is respected almost universally by both senior officers and ordinary soldiers.

---

## Role Within the Hell Hosts

Bulwark serves as the commander of the [[Doom Corps]], the largest ground-combat division within the Hell Hosts.

His responsibilities include:

- Overseeing Doom Corps battalions
    
- Maintaining combat readiness
    
- Setting recruitment standards
    
- Developing division doctrine
    
- Supervising battalion commanders
    
- Reviewing casualty reports
    
- Approving major deployments
    
- Preserving battalion traditions
    
- Advising High Command on ground warfare
    
- Ensuring the Doom Corps remains disciplined, unified, and effective
    

Although ultimate authority rests with [[Predator]], Bulwark holds direct responsibility for the Doom Corps itself.

He is trusted to maintain the division without constant oversight.

This allows commanders such as [[Champion]] to focus on their own fleets, battalions, and battlefield operations without needing to manage the entire Corps.

---

## Command Style

Bulwark leads through standards rather than intimidation.

He believes that discipline is not created by fear, but by repetition, trust, and responsibility.

His inspections are feared throughout the Doom Corps, not because he screams at soldiers, but because he notices everything.

A damaged strap.

An unclean barrel.

A missing name from a casualty report.

A recruit pretending not to be injured.

An officer who has stopped checking on their soldiers.

Bulwark believes leadership is not proven by giving orders from above, but by knowing what those orders cost below.

He expects officers under his command to understand their soldiers as people, not numbers on a deployment sheet.

A battalion may be large, but Bulwark believes it is still made of individuals.

That belief defines his command.

---

## The Soldier’s Commander

Bulwark’s reputation among Doom Corps soldiers is unusual.

He is respected as a commander, but also regarded as someone who genuinely understands the burden of service.

Stories about him are common throughout the division.

Some soldiers claim he visited them in hospital after campaigns.

Others say he remembered the name of a fallen squadmate years after their death.

Many battalion commanders credit him with shaping their understanding of leadership.

Unlike more theatrical figures within the Hell Hosts, Bulwark’s legend is built from small acts repeated over decades.

He attends memorials whenever possible.

He reviews casualty reports personally.

He speaks with wounded soldiers.

He remembers destroyed battalions.

He knows that soldiers do not need to be treated like heroes.

They need to know they mattered.

---

## Relationship with Predator

Bulwark and [[Predator]] share a relationship built on long-standing professional respect.

Predator commands the Hell Hosts as a whole, while Bulwark commands the Doom Corps.

Their conversations are often brief, direct, and efficient. Predator does not need to explain ground warfare to Bulwark, and Bulwark does not waste Predator’s time with unnecessary reports.

Predator trusts Bulwark because the Doom Corps functions.

Bulwark respects Predator because he understands the scale of the burden carried by supreme command.

Unlike many officers, Bulwark is not intimidated by Predator’s authority. He obeys the chain of command, but he is willing to speak honestly when Doom Corps lives are at stake.

Predator is one of the few people within High Command who may still refer to Bulwark by his real name.

---

## Relationship with Azure Gale

Bulwark and [[Azure Gale]] represent two different forms of military intelligence.

Azure Gale thinks in outcomes, deception, fleet movements, and probability.

Bulwark thinks in soldiers, battalions, casualties, morale, and the cost of holding ground.

Their views sometimes clash, but the respect between them is strong.

Azure may see the most efficient path to victory.

Bulwark ensures that the path can actually be walked by the soldiers expected to take it.

Azure’s humour contrasts sharply with Bulwark’s dry seriousness, though Bulwark is not above giving quiet, cutting replies when provoked.

Together, they form one of the most reliable strategic partnerships within Hell Host High Command.

---

## Relationship with Champion

Bulwark holds great respect for [[Champion]], commander of the [[702nd Battalion]] and Admiral of [[Fleet One]].

Champion represents the kind of officer Bulwark values most: capable, courageous, disciplined, and willing to lead from the front.

Although Champion has his own authority and responsibilities, Bulwark remains one of the few figures whose opinion Champion takes extremely seriously.

Their relationship is defined by mutual respect rather than rivalry.

Champion wins battles.

Bulwark builds the soldiers who fight them.

---

## Relationship with Destiny

Bulwark has a close, almost familial bond with [[Destiny]].

Although Destiny is the daughter of [[Predator]], Bulwark refuses to treat her as untouchable or exempt from discipline. He holds her to the same standards as other soldiers, and in some cases even higher ones.

This is one of the reasons Destiny respects him.

To her, Bulwark is not merely another officer.

He is something closer to an uncle.

He is one of the few people who can correct her without making it feel political, and one of the few who can offer advice without treating her like a symbol of her father’s legacy.

Bulwark sees Destiny’s potential clearly, but he is also aware of the pressure placed upon her by her name, her bloodline, and her position within the Hell Hosts.

When needed, he offers guidance without ceremony.

A quiet conversation.

A cup of tea.

A reminder that she is still allowed to be human.

---

## Relationship with Red Wind

Bulwark has a quiet protective concern for [[Red Wind]].

He recognises her strength, loyalty, and value as a soldier, but he also understands that something within her remains deeply unresolved.

Red Wind does not always seek formal help, and Bulwark does not force it upon her.

Instead, he gives her space.

His office has become one of the few places where Red Wind can simply exist without being expected to perform, obey, or explain herself perfectly.

At times, she may sit curled up in his office, legs pulled against her chest, ranting about her day, complaining about other soldiers, or speaking quietly about fragments of memory she cannot fully understand.

Bulwark listens.

Sometimes he answers.

Sometimes he lets silence do the work.

He does not treat Red Wind as broken.

He treats her as a soldier who deserves somewhere safe to breathe.

---

## Appearance

Bulwark usually wears modified Doom Corps armour based on standard division colours.

His armour is not ornate, ceremonial, or excessively decorated.

Instead, it appears old, reinforced, repaired, and heavily used.

Common features include:

- Matte-black combat plating
    
- Deep red Doom Corps markings
    
- Blood-red visor
    
- Reinforced chest armour
    
- Campaign-worn shoulder plates
    
- Minimal decoration
    
- Practical battlefield modifications
    
- Subtle commander markings
    
- Signs of long-term use and repair
    

Unlike officers who display rank through extravagance, Bulwark’s authority comes from restraint.

His armour looks like it has survived decades of service because it has.

---

## Equipment

Bulwark does not rely on exotic weapons or theatrical equipment.

He prefers reliable standard-issue Hell Host weaponry, maintaining the belief that a commander of soldiers should understand the weapons those soldiers carry.

His usual equipment may include:

- [[CR-556]]
    
- [[P2PSP]]
    
- Combat knife
    
- Command interface system
    
- Tactical communications suite
    
- Doom Corps officer armour
    
- Battalion command uplink
    

Bulwark’s greatest weapon is not his rifle.

It is the Doom Corps itself.

---

## Office

Bulwark’s office is not luxurious.

It is practical, heavily used, and filled with reminders of the Corps he commands.

Common items include:

- Campaign maps
    
- Battalion banners
    
- Casualty reports
    
- Old helmets
    
- Service records
    
- Memorial plaques
    
- Tactical displays
    
- A worn desk
    
- A battered chair
    
- A kettle or drink station
    
- Shelves filled with documents rather than trophies
    

Despite its stern appearance, the office has become an unexpectedly safe place for certain soldiers.

It is one of the few rooms in High Command where silence is not uncomfortable.

---

## Philosophy

Bulwark believes the Doom Corps exists to protect, endure, and finish what others cannot.

He does not glorify death.

He does not romanticise sacrifice.

He does not believe soldiers should be wasted for symbolism.

To Bulwark, every soldier under his command is a life entrusted to him by the Corps.

Some will die.

That is unavoidable.

But none should die because their commanders were careless, vain, or unprepared.

His philosophy is simple:

> **A battalion is not measured by how many soldiers it has. It is measured by how many come home.**

This belief shapes his leadership, his doctrine, and his refusal to treat soldiers as disposable.

---

## Reputation

### Within the Doom Corps

Bulwark is regarded with immense respect by Doom Corps soldiers.

He is stern, demanding, and almost impossible to impress, but his standards are trusted because they are consistent.

Soldiers may fear disappointing him, but few believe he would abandon them.

### Within High Command

Among Hell Host leadership, Bulwark is considered one of the most reliable officers in service.

He is not flashy, ambitious, or politically manipulative.

He is trusted because he does his job.

### Among Enemies

To enemies of the Hell Hosts, Bulwark’s presence indicates that the Doom Corps is fully committed to an operation.

He is not known for unnecessary cruelty or reckless aggression.

He is feared because his campaigns are disciplined, organised, and extremely difficult to break.

---

## Legacy

Bulwark’s legacy is not defined by a single battle.

It is defined by the institution he built.

Every Doom Corps battalion shaped by his doctrine, every commander trained under his standards, and every soldier who survived because of those standards forms part of his legacy.

He is not remembered as the loudest commander in Hell Host history.

He is remembered as one of the most necessary.

Where Predator forged the Hell Hosts into a galactic power, Bulwark ensured its largest fighting force could endure the wars that followed.

## Trivia

- He is known to attend memorials without announcing his presence beforehand.
    
- His office has quietly become a safe place for certain soldiers, particularly those who struggle to speak openly elsewhere.
    
- He rarely discusses his own service record.
