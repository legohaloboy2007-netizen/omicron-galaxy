# Cindralis

Cindralis is a colossal volcanic urban world within the [[Ashen Kingdom]], infamous across the galaxy as:

The City of Evil

Unlike the fortress discipline of [[Grimhold]] or the industrial brutality of [[Vexar Prime]], Cindralis thrives on indulgence, corruption, ambition, and excess. The planet is almost entirely covered by sprawling megacities illuminated by crimson lights and rivers of molten rock flowing beneath endless streets.

On Cindralis:
everything has a price.

## Role Within the Ashen Kingdom

Cindralis functions as:
- pleasure world
- trade hub
- intelligence center
- black market capital
- financial nexus
- political underworld

The planet attracts:
- merchants
- nobles
- spies
- criminals
- information brokers
- mercenary groups
- corrupt officials
- syndicates loyal to the Ashen Kingdom

Much of the kingdom’s hidden economy flows through Cindralis.

## Environment

Cindralis is a volcanic urban planet almost entirely consumed by massive cityscapes.

The world contains:
- endless industrial districts
- towering obsidian skyscrapers
- lava channels beneath the streets
- neon-lit markets
- elevated transit bridges
- ash-filled skies
- dense urban sectors stretching across continents

Rivers of molten rock flow both beneath and through the cities, powering enormous sections of the planet while also serving as punishment zones and industrial energy sources.

The atmosphere is:
- toxic
- ash-filled
- sulfuric
- permanently illuminated by volcanic fire

## The Scarlet Spire

At the center of the planet stands:

The Scarlet Spire

A colossal palace-district where the Ashen elite indulge in excess beyond imagination.

The district contains:
- noble courts
- elite pleasure houses
- luxury towers
- political gathering halls
- private syndicate sectors

On Cindralis, status is often measured through wealth, influence, and survival.

## The Black Silk Markets

The Black Silk Markets form the largest illegal trade network on the planet.

Within these endless maze-like districts:
- illegal technology is sold
- forbidden information is traded
- weapons change hands
- identities disappear
- contracts are purchased
- mercenary deals are brokered

The markets operate under one rule:

> No questions. No laws.

## The House of Whispers

The House of Whispers is the intelligence and espionage center of Cindralis.

Here:
- spies trade secrets
- informants exchange information
- political manipulation is orchestrated
- assassinations are arranged
- loyalty is bought and sold

Within the Ashen Kingdom, information is considered one of the most valuable weapons of all.

## The Lava Veins

Massive molten channels known as:

The Lava Veins

flow beneath and throughout the planet’s urban sectors.

These volcanic systems provide:
- industrial power
- heat
- energy distribution
- waste disposal
- punishment systems

Many lower districts are built directly above exposed magma flows.

## The Pits of Pleasure

The Pits of Pleasure are infamous entertainment sectors where morality holds little meaning.

These districts contain:
- combat arenas
- gambling dens
- pleasure halls
- narcotic markets
- underground theaters
- illegal experimentation zones

Desire rules absolutely within the pits.

Everything can be bought—
for the right price.

## The Ashen Exchange

The Ashen Exchange serves as the financial heart of Cindralis.

Major syndicates, merchant houses, and banking factions control enormous economic networks throughout the Ashen Kingdom from within the Exchange sectors.

Debt on Cindralis is considered permanent.

## Society & Culture

The culture of Cindralis revolves around:
- ambition
- corruption
- indulgence
- manipulation
- survival through influence

Unlike the militarized discipline of other Ashen worlds, power on Cindralis is often gained through:
- wealth
- secrets
- blackmail
- political maneuvering
- criminal influence

Weakness is exploited instantly.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Cindralis is known as:
- the City of Evil
- the black market heart of the [[Ashen Kingdom]]
- one of the most dangerous urban worlds in known space
- a planet where corruption flows as freely as lava

One Ashen proverb is especially associated with the planet:

> “In Cindralis, temptation wears a crown and sin pays the taxes.”
