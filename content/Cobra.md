# Cobra

**Real Name:** Unknown  
**Codename:** Cobra  
**Species:** [[Humans|Human]]  
**Gender:** Male  
**Height:** 6'0"  
**Faction:** [[Free World Order]]  
**Role:** Stealth Operative / Infiltration Specialist  
**Status:** Active  

Cobra is a high-ranking stealth operative and infiltration specialist serving within the [[Free World Order]].

Known for conducting operations deep within hostile territory, Cobra rapidly advanced through the ranks of the Free World Order due to his effectiveness during infiltration, sabotage, reconnaissance, and covert combat missions.

His reputation eventually spread beyond the Free World Order itself.

Among [[Hell Hosts]] personnel, Cobra has become an infamous name associated with breached security, compromised facilities, missing intelligence, and attacks carried out from within supposedly secure positions.

## Personality

Cobra is patient, calculating, and highly disciplined.

Unlike personnel who thrive during open warfare, Cobra prefers engagements in which the enemy never receives an opportunity to properly confront him.

He is comfortable operating independently for extended periods and can remain concealed within hostile territory while waiting for an opportunity to complete an objective.

Cobra rarely takes unnecessary risks simply to eliminate enemy personnel.

For him, remaining undetected and completing the mission usually takes priority over accumulating kills.

When violence becomes necessary, however, he attempts to end encounters quickly and quietly.

## Free World Order

Cobra initially served in lower-ranking operational roles within the Free World Order but gained rank unusually quickly.

His advancement was primarily driven by his performance during covert operations and his ability to complete missions within heavily defended hostile territory.

Over time, Cobra became one of the faction's recognised infiltration specialists.

His assignments commonly involve intelligence gathering, sabotage, reconnaissance, assassination, disruption of enemy infrastructure, and infiltration of secured installations.

He may operate independently or alongside small specialised teams depending upon the objective.

## Stealth Operations

Cobra specialises in entering locations without alerting the personnel defending them.

He studies patrol routes, surveillance systems, environmental conditions, access points, security procedures, and communication networks before committing to an infiltration whenever possible.

Rather than relying exclusively upon technology, Cobra is extensively trained in conventional stealth techniques.

He understands concealment, silent movement, observation, timing, infiltration routes, and the behaviour of personnel responsible for guarding secure facilities.

His equipment enhances these abilities.

It does not replace them.

## Active Camouflage

Cobra's armour incorporates an advanced active camouflage system capable of significantly reducing his visual profile.

Adaptive exterior systems reproduce aspects of the surrounding environment across the armour, allowing Cobra to become extremely difficult to detect visually.

The system does not provide true invisibility.

Rapid movement, physical contact, environmental debris, rain, smoke, damage, specialised sensors, and close observation can expose distortions produced by the camouflage.

Cobra therefore uses the technology carefully rather than treating it as guaranteed concealment.

Unlike [[Killshot]], who primarily uses active camouflage to conceal firing positions and movement between them, Cobra's system is configured specifically around infiltration and close-range stealth operations.

## Thermal Vision

Cobra's helmet incorporates thermal and multispectral imaging systems.

These systems allow him to detect heat signatures in darkness, through smoke, and under other conditions where conventional vision becomes unreliable.

Thermal imaging is particularly useful when observing patrols or locating personnel within poorly illuminated environments.

It does not allow Cobra to see freely through every material or structure.

Instead, it forms one component of a wider sensor package incorporated into his helmet.

## Combat

Cobra attempts to avoid prolonged direct engagements.

When combat becomes unavoidable, he specialises in rapidly eliminating isolated personnel before an alarm can be raised.

His weapons are selected accordingly.

Suppressed firearms allow him to engage targets while reducing the sound and visible signature of gunfire, while knives provide an option for extremely close-range encounters where maintaining concealment is essential.

Cobra is capable of conventional combat but loses many of his greatest advantages during large open engagements.

Whenever possible, he controls when and where an encounter occurs.

## Weapons

Cobra primarily carries suppressed ballistic firearms.

His exact loadout changes depending upon the requirements of an operation, with compact weapons generally preferred when infiltrating enclosed facilities.

He also carries multiple combat knives for utility and close-range use.

Rather than carrying excessive weaponry, Cobra favours equipment that can be transported quietly without significantly interfering with his movement.

## Armour

Cobra wears specialised lightweight Free World combat armour designed around stealth and mobility.

The armour provides less protection than equipment worn by heavy personnel such as [[Slaughter]], but substantially reduces weight and interference during infiltration.

Its systems include active camouflage, thermal and multispectral optics, environmental monitoring, communications equipment, and other tools useful during covert operations.

External components are designed to minimise noise while moving.

## Reputation Among Hell Hosts

Cobra has become particularly infamous among Hell Host personnel who have encountered the aftermath of his operations.

His reputation does not come from overwhelming firepower.

It comes from uncertainty.

Facilities targeted by Cobra may discover compromised surveillance systems, disabled communications, missing intelligence, sabotaged equipment, or eliminated personnel before defenders realise an infiltration has occurred.

As reports accumulated, his codename became increasingly familiar among Hell Host security personnel.

Some installations have developed procedures specifically intended to counter active camouflage and covert infiltration following encounters with Free World operatives such as Cobra.

## Reputation

Within the Free World Order, Cobra is respected for his reliability and rapid advancement through the organisation.

His career demonstrates that rank within the faction can be earned through specialised operational performance rather than conventional battlefield command.

Outside the Free World Order, Cobra is considerably less celebrated.

Among those who have been targeted by his operations, his reputation is considerably darker.

Cobra's greatest advantage is rarely that his enemies cannot fight him.

It is that they frequently do not realise he is there until his objective has already been completed.
