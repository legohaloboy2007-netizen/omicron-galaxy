# Era 5 — Collapse Incarnate

The Collapse Incarnate Era marked the beginning of the galaxy’s descent into full existential crisis, as ancient powers, forgotten horrors, and reality-altering forces returned to known space. Unlike the exploration-driven discoveries of the [[Pareidolia]] Era, this period was defined by collapse, resurgence, and the awakening of threats capable of destroying entire civilizations.

The era began with the unexpected return of [[Onyx]], whose survival shocked both the [[Hell Hosts]] and the wider galaxy following the events of the [[Rainfall Execution]]. His reappearance destabilized political tensions across numerous systems and reignited old conflicts once thought finished.

At the same time, long-forgotten organizations and myths began resurfacing throughout the galaxy. The [[Crimson Order]] emerged from legend once more, while sightings of [[The Hunters]] increased across corruption zones, anomaly regions, and frontier systems. Many civilizations viewed these returns as signs that the galaxy was entering a far darker age.

As instability spread, exploration beyond the known boundaries of the [[Farlands]] and [[Voidlands]] led to the discovery of two catastrophic cosmic regions: [[The Veil]] and [[The Bleeding Abyss]]. These regions represented entirely new stages of cosmic corruption and existential danger.

The emergence of [[The Veil]] introduced the galaxy to higher forms of Element 95 consciousness linked to [[The Witness]], while the awakening of [[The Forsaken Monarch]] within [[The Bleeding Abyss]] unleashed violent [[Deatherium]]-based collapse across nearby systems.

Meanwhile, the [[Obsidian Veil]] — long responsible for countless experiments and hidden manipulations — finally began collapsing under the weight of its own actions. Internal fractures, cosmic exposure, and escalating conflicts led to the eventual downfall of much of the organization.

During this era, the [[Hell Hosts]] also gained direct access to advanced technologies connected to [[The Forge]], dramatically increasing their military and technological capabilities as the galaxy spiraled further toward collapse.

By the end of the era, the Omicron Galaxy stood on the edge of complete transformation, with reality itself beginning to fracture beneath the weight of corruption, ascension, and cosmic war.
