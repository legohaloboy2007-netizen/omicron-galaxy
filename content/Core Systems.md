# Core Systems

The Core Systems are the most populated, developed, and politically influential regions within the [[Omicron Galaxy]]. Located closer to the galactic center, the systems contain major trade routes, industrial worlds, corporate headquarters, military strongholds, and civilian population centers.

Unlike the unstable frontier regions found in the [[Outer Rim]], the Core Systems are heavily charted and densely connected through major transport lanes. Many of the galaxy’s largest factions, corporations, and governments maintain a presence within the region.

Despite their stability and economic power, the Core Systems are far from peaceful. Political tension, criminal syndicates, corruption, and growing militarisation continue to spread throughout many worlds.

The Core Systems are also home to several major systems of importance, including the [[Prioxis System]], [[Aegimius System]], and [[Andromeda System]].

## Known For
- Dense population centers
- Major trade routes and industries
- Corporate and political influence
- Advanced infrastructure and technology
- Increasing faction tension and militarisation
