# Coria-6ZNH

Coria-6ZNH is a heavily fortified military world located within the [[Andromeda System]] and serves as the secondary operational planet of the [[Hell Hosts]]. While [[Voridinus II]] functions as the faction’s primary command and defense world, Coria-6ZNH operates as one of the main residential, deployment, and logistical hubs for Hell Hosts personnel.

The planet is infamous for its hellish appearance.

From orbit, Coria-6ZNH appears as a glowing orange world covered in volcanic plains, scorched mountain ranges, deep fissures, and ash-filled storm systems. Rivers of magma and unstable tectonic zones stretch across much of the surface, giving the planet an appearance often compared to ancient depictions of hell itself.

Despite its hostile appearance, Coria-6ZNH is considered relatively safe by Hell Hosts standards.

The planet supports extensive fortified military settlements protected against:
- seismic instability
- volcanic eruptions
- dangerous native wildlife
- extreme environmental conditions

Large armored barracks complexes, industrial facilities, and deployment sectors are spread across stable regions of the planet, housing vast numbers of Hell Hosts soldiers and personnel.

## Environment

Coria-6ZNH is dominated by:
- volcanic terrain
- ash-covered plains
- lava rivers
- unstable tectonic regions
- glowing canyon systems
- dense orange atmospheric haze
- constant geothermal activity

Frequent seismic events and volcanic eruptions are common across the planet, though most military infrastructure is heavily reinforced to withstand environmental disasters.

The world’s skies are often filled with:
- ash storms
- volcanic lightning
- industrial smoke
- orange atmospheric glow

At night, massive magma fields illuminate large portions of the surface.

## Wildlife

Although considered habitable, Coria-6ZNH contains several dangerous native species adapted to its volcanic ecosystem.

Hell Hosts personnel stationed on the planet are trained extensively in:
- environmental survival
- volcanic hazard response
- hostile wildlife encounters

Many regions outside fortified zones remain largely unexplored due to unstable terrain and predatory lifeforms.

## Role Within Hell Hosts

Coria-6ZNH functions as:
- a major military housing world
- troop deployment center
- logistics and supply hub
- training location
- secondary defensive stronghold

The planet maintains constant transport movement between itself and [[Voridinus II]], supporting fleet operations and military campaigns across the galaxy.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Coria-6ZNH is known for:
- its terrifying hell-like appearance
- massive fortified military compounds
- extreme environmental conditions
- volcanic landscapes visible from orbit
- being one of the primary worlds controlled by the [[Hell Hosts]]

Despite its appearance, many Hell Hosts soldiers consider Coria-6ZNH strangely beautiful in its own brutal way.
