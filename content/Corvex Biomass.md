# Corvex Biomass

## Overview

Corvex Biomass is a living corruption-based organic material heavily associated with:

[[Element 95]]

and the infected ecosystems of the:
[[Farlands]]

Unlike crystalline corruption materials such as [[Blightglass]], Corvex Biomass behaves as a fully adaptive biological growth capable of:
- spreading aggressively
- assimilating organic matter
- mutating surrounding ecosystems
- and restructuring environments into living corruption zones

The substance is regarded as one of the most dangerous biological anomalies in known existence due to its:
- rapid adaptation
- regenerative capability
- and unpredictable mutation behavior

Entire regions of the Farlands have been overtaken by Corvex growth.

## Nature

Corvex Biomass is a semi-sentient organic corruption substance created through prolonged:
- Element 95 saturation
- biological mutation
- and ecosystem destabilization

The material behaves similarly to:
- fungal networks
- parasitic tissue
- and adaptive organic colonies

However, Corvex Biomass demonstrates levels of environmental responsiveness far beyond normal biological systems.

The growth appears capable of:
- reacting to threats
- restructuring itself
- and evolving new biological forms rapidly

Researchers remain uncertain whether the biomass functions as:
- a distributed organism
- a corruption ecosystem
- or a primitive hive intelligence

## Physical Appearance

Corvex Biomass commonly appears as:
- pulsating flesh-like growth
- black-red organic tissue
- infected root systems
- or massive living corruption colonies

The material frequently displays:
- exposed vein-like structures
- moving tendrils
- eye-like growths
- organic breathing motion
- and pulsing bioluminescent corruption veins

Large infestations may resemble:
living forests of flesh and corrupted tissue.

Some Corvex zones continue growing continuously for centuries.

## Formation

Corvex Biomass develops when severe Element 95 contamination infects:
- biological ecosystems
- organic lifeforms
- decomposing matter
- and planetary environments

The corruption gradually restructures surrounding biology into:
adaptive living corruption tissue.

Growth accelerates dramatically in areas containing:
- death
- emotional instability
- heavy mutation
- and Veil-linked anomalies

Some outbreak zones appear to evolve independently over time.

## Properties

Corvex Biomass possesses numerous dangerous characteristics.

### Adaptive Mutation
The biomass rapidly evolves in response to:
- environmental threats
- damage
- weapons
- and containment attempts

---

### Biological Assimilation
Organic matter absorbed into the biomass may be:
- consumed
- mutated
- or integrated into the larger growth network

---

### Regenerative Growth
Destroyed sections frequently regenerate unless fully eradicated.

---

### Ecosystem Conversion
The material gradually transforms surrounding terrain into:
living corruption environments.

---

### Consciousness Influence
Certain large Corvex colonies may react to:
- fear
- stress
- aggression
- and nearby thought patterns

## Corrupted Organisms

Corvex Biomass frequently produces:
- mutated predators
- parasitic organisms
- corruption swarms
- and adaptive bioforms

Some creatures appear partially grown directly from the biomass itself.

Others originate from:
infected wildlife,
humans,
or corrupted experimental subjects.

Many retain little resemblance to their original forms.

## Farlands Infestation Zones

Corvex Biomass has consumed enormous portions of:
the [[Farlands]]

Major infestation regions include:
- abandoned settlements
- underground corruption tunnels
- flesh-plains
- mutation pits
- and biological hive zones

Entire ecosystems now function as:
living corruption networks interconnected through Corvex growth.

## Relationship to Blightglass

Corvex Biomass frequently spreads alongside:

[[Blightglass]]

The two materials often coexist within heavily corrupted regions.

Generally:
- Blightglass dominates crystalline environments
- Corvex Biomass dominates organic ecosystems

In some regions, the two appear partially fused together.

These hybrid zones are considered exceptionally dangerous.

## Relationship to The Veil

Corvex Biomass demonstrates strong interaction with:
[[The Veil]]

Large infestations may produce:
- hallucinations
- psychic whispers
- perception distortion
- and consciousness instability

Some survivors claim massive Corvex colonies exhibit:
collective awareness.

Whether this is true remains unknown.

## Military Threat

Corvex infestations are considered catastrophic containment threats.

Standard military responses often fail due to:
- rapid mutation
- regenerative growth
- and environmental spread

Fire,
orbital bombardment,
and full quarantine procedures are commonly required to contain major outbreaks.

Several worlds have been abandoned entirely following uncontrolled infestation.

## Research & Experimentation

Numerous groups have attempted to study or weaponize Corvex Biomass.

Known interested factions include:
- [[Obsidian Veil]]
- Farlands cults
- rogue bio-engineers
- and black-market anomaly laboratories

Potential applications include:
- adaptive bio-weapons
- regenerative tissue systems
- living military organisms
- and corruption-based warfare

Nearly all major experiments have resulted in containment disaster.

## Common Theories

The true nature of Corvex Biomass remains unknown.

Popular theories suggest:
- it is a biological extension of Element 95
- a living mutation ecosystem
- or corrupted evolutionary acceleration beyond natural limits

Some researchers fear the biomass is:
attempting to evolve something larger.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Corvex Biomass is known as:
- the living corruption of the Farlands
- one of the most dangerous biological anomalies ever encountered
- and a substance capable of consuming entire ecosystems

Among Farlands survivors, one warning is commonly repeated:

> “If the ground starts breathing,  
> you’re already too late.”
