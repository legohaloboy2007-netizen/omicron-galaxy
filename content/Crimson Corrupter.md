# The Crimson Corrupter

> "I said I could stop it. I didn't say I knew what would happen after."

## Overview

**The Crimson Corrupter** is a young member of [[The Crimson Order]] whose abilities revolve around spatial distortion and the temporary corruption of anomalous powers.

At only twenty-seven years old, he is unusually young compared to many of the Order's known members.

His inclusion within the Crimson Order appears to be based less on experience and more on potential.

Corrupter possesses abilities powerful enough to interfere with the anomalous capabilities of other beings, but his control remains incomplete.

Unlike senior members such as [[Crimson Creator|Aethros]], [[Crimson Preserver|Ava]], [[Crimson Shadow|Helios]], and [[Crimson Crusader|Stanislav]], Corrupter is still learning what his powers are capable of.

This makes him dangerous in a very different way.

Not because he always knows what he is doing.

Because sometimes he does not.

## Personality

Corrupter is considerably less composed than the older members of the Crimson Order.

He is intelligent and capable, but lacks centuries or millennia of experience.

He is still learning how to interpret his own abilities and frequently approaches situations with a mixture of confidence and uncertainty.

Corrupter is aware that the Order contains beings vastly more experienced than himself.

This does not always make him cautious.

If anything, it sometimes creates pressure to prove that he belongs there.

He dislikes being treated as fragile or inexperienced and may take unnecessary risks when he believes others are underestimating him.

Despite this, Corrupter is not reckless by nature.

He simply has not yet learned where the true limits of his power lie.

---

# Role Within the Crimson Order

Corrupter's role within the Order appears to revolve around **disruption**.

Where other members may create, preserve, track, or adapt, Corrupter interferes.

His abilities are particularly useful against hostile anomalous beings whose powers would otherwise overwhelm conventional resistance.

Rather than defeating an ability through superior strength, Corrupter can temporarily cause it to behave incorrectly.

This makes him extremely valuable against opponents who rely heavily on supernatural or anomalous capabilities.

However, his inconsistent control means senior members of the Order remain cautious when deploying him against especially unstable phenomena.

---

# Abilities

## Portal Creation

Corrupter can create portals using gestures or movements of his hands.

These portals can connect separate points in space and allow matter, energy, or individuals to pass between them.

Known uses include:

- Rapid movement
- Redirecting attacks
- Escaping dangerous environments
- Transporting allies
- Repositioning opponents
- Bypassing physical barriers

The maximum range of his portals is unknown.

Corrupter's portal control is reliable at short and moderate distances but becomes increasingly difficult over greater ranges.

Poor concentration can produce unstable portal placement or premature collapse.

---

## Power Corruption

Corrupter's defining ability allows him to temporarily interfere with another individual's anomalous powers.

The affected ability is not removed.

Instead, its behaviour becomes unstable or altered.

Possible effects include:

- Reduced accuracy
- Altered range
- Unexpected activation
- Delayed activation
- Reduced strength
- Excessive output
- Reversed targeting
- Inconsistent duration
- Distorted secondary effects

The exact result varies depending on the target and ability involved.

Corrupter cannot always predict what will happen.

---

## Temporary Ability Destabilization

When Corrupter targets a particularly powerful ability, he may not be capable of fully corrupting it.

Instead, he can introduce temporary instability.

This may force the target to concentrate harder, use the ability less frequently, or risk losing control.

Extremely powerful cosmic or ancient entities may resist the effect entirely.

---

## Spatial Distortion

Corrupter's portal abilities occasionally produce localized spatial abnormalities.

These may include:

- Warped distances
- Brief directional changes
- Short-lived spatial fractures
- Objects appearing slightly displaced
- Unstable edges between locations

These effects are usually accidental.

Their frequency decreases as Corrupter's control improves.

---

# Limitations

## Incomplete Control

Corrupter does not fully understand his powers.

He can intentionally activate them, but predicting the exact result remains difficult in complex situations.

This is particularly true when corrupting unfamiliar anomalous abilities.

---

## Unpredictable Corruption

Power corruption does not always produce the outcome Corrupter wants.

Attempting to weaken an ability may instead alter it.

Attempting to disrupt one effect may accidentally strengthen another.

This makes indiscriminate use dangerous.

---

## Concentration

Portal creation requires concentration.

Multiple portals, long distances, or unstable environments significantly increase the difficulty.

If his concentration is interrupted, portals may collapse unexpectedly.

---

## Resistance

Some entities possess enough anomalous stability to resist corruption.

Others may adapt to it over time.

Corrupter therefore cannot rely entirely on this ability against every opponent.

---

## Feedback

Interfering with extremely powerful abilities can cause feedback.

Possible consequences include:

- Headaches
- Disorientation
- Temporary loss of spatial awareness
- Uncontrolled portal formation
- Physical exhaustion
- Brief interference with Corrupter's own powers

Severe feedback can leave him temporarily unable to use his abilities at all.

---

# Relationship with Aethros

[[Crimson Creator|Aethros]] appears to have taken a particular interest in Corrupter's development.

The reason is unknown.

Corrupter's ability to interfere with anomalous powers may represent an unusually rare phenomenon even by Crimson Order standards.

Aethros has not publicly explained why Corrupter was accepted into the Order at such a young age.

Some believe Aethros sees potential in him.

Others believe he sees danger.

The two possibilities are not mutually exclusive.

---

# Relationship with Ava

[[Crimson Preserver|Ava]] is one of the members most likely to intervene when Corrupter loses control.

Her temporal abilities allow her to slow or contain unstable effects before they become catastrophic.

This has made her one of the safest individuals for Corrupter to train around.

Ava tends to treat him with considerably more patience than some of the Order's other members.

---

# Relationship with Helios

[[Crimson Shadow|Helios]] frequently serves as a difficult training opponent for Corrupter.

Their abilities interact unpredictably.

Corrupter attempts to destabilize Helios's adaptations.

Helios attempts to adapt to the corruption.

These encounters provide valuable information for both of them.

They also occasionally destroy large amounts of surrounding architecture.

---

# Relationship with Stanislav

[[Crimson Crusader|Stanislav]] generally treats Corrupter more like an inexperienced operative than a cosmic entity.

He values control, repetition, and discipline.

Corrupter finds this frustrating.

It is probably good for him.

---

# Potential

The Crimson Order appears to consider Corrupter's abilities unusually significant.

If he eventually gains complete control, he may become capable of deliberately rewriting how anomalous powers function for short periods.

Such an ability could make him one of the Order's most effective counters against other powerful entities.

It could also make him one of its most dangerous members.

For now, Corrupter remains unpredictable.
