# **Aethros** 

> "Creation is not power. Creation is responsibility."

## Overview

**G. Ghoziarian**, more commonly known as **The Creator** or **The Crimson Creator**, is the mysterious leader of [[The Crimson Order]] and one of the most powerful known entities associated with the early history of the Omicron Galaxy.

His true age, species, origin, and nature are unknown.

Legends surrounding Ghoziarian are ancient enough that some cultures claim he existed before the formation of the galaxy itself.

Others go further.

Certain religious traditions, fringe historical records, and Crimson Order doctrines suggest that Ghoziarian may have participated in the creation of the universe or in events surrounding its earliest formation.

No reliable evidence has ever confirmed this claim.

What is known is considerably more concerning.

Ghoziarian possesses abilities far beyond those of almost any conventional biological being and has demonstrated control over matter, space, and anomalous forces at levels modern civilizations struggle to understand.

Despite this, he is not believed to be truly invincible.

He can be injured, resisted, and theoretically defeated.

Doing so would require extraordinary power.

## Appearance

Ghoziarian possesses a broadly humanoid form and is approximately 6'1" in height.

Beyond this, reliable descriptions vary.

Accounts of his appearance differ significantly across historical periods, leading some researchers to question whether Ghoziarian maintains a permanent physical body at all.

The imagery most commonly associated with him includes crimson coloration, dark ceremonial armour or robes, and visual phenomena surrounding his presence.

Whether these descriptions represent his true appearance or symbolic interpretations remains unknown.

---

## Personality

Despite the enormous power associated with him, Ghoziarian is not typically described as impulsive or needlessly destructive.

He is patient, controlled, and difficult to provoke.

His behaviour often suggests an individual operating with an understanding of history and causality far beyond that of ordinary civilizations.

Ghoziarian rarely explains his actions fully.

When he does speak, he often discusses events in terms of consequence, balance, creation, destruction, and inevitability.

This has contributed heavily to the perception that he sees himself as something fundamentally separate from ordinary life.

Whether this belief comes from arrogance or genuine knowledge remains disputed.

---

# The Crimson Order

Ghoziarian is the leader of [[The Crimson Order]], a secretive collection of exceptionally powerful individuals whose origins may be linked to the earliest periods of universal history.

The Order is not structured like a conventional military or government.

Its members are united less by territory or species and more by their power, knowledge, and connection to ancient events.

Some members appear to possess abilities comparable to Ascended beings.

Others may be considerably older.

The exact purpose of the Order remains unclear.

Ghoziarian appears to hold authority over its members, although the nature of this authority is poorly understood.

Whether they follow him through loyalty, ideology, fear, or shared origin is unknown.

---

# Abilities

## Matter Creation

Ghoziarian possesses the ability to generate physical matter through mechanisms that remain completely unexplained.

This is not believed to be ordinary material manipulation.

Recorded accounts suggest that Ghoziarian can produce matter where none previously existed.

If accurate, this ability violates several fundamental principles of conventional physics.

The limits of this power are unknown.

It remains unclear whether Ghoziarian is truly creating matter from nothing or drawing it from an inaccessible source or dimension.

---

## Spatial Translocation

Ghoziarian can relocate himself across large distances without conventional transportation.

Observers commonly describe this as teleportation.

The maximum range and restrictions of this ability remain unknown.

---

## Ability Replication

One of Ghoziarian's most dangerous known abilities is his apparent capacity to replicate anomalous powers used by other individuals.

The copied ability may manifest at a greater intensity than that demonstrated by its original user.

However, the exact mechanics remain poorly understood.

It is unknown whether Ghoziarian permanently retains copied abilities or reproduces them temporarily through some form of adaptive anomalous process.

---

## Overwhelming Physical and Anomalous Power

Numerous historical accounts describe Ghoziarian defeating extremely powerful anomalous or Ascended entities with minimal effort.

Some myths claim that beings worshipped as gods have been destroyed by simple gestures from him.

These stories cannot be independently verified.

However, encounters involving Ghoziarian consistently indicate that his physical and anomalous capabilities greatly exceed those of ordinary species.

---

# Mortality

Despite the mythology surrounding him, Ghoziarian is not believed to be absolutely immortal or indestructible.

Historical evidence suggests that sufficiently powerful forces can injure or weaken him.

Defeating him would likely require prolonged confrontation, overwhelming force, or the involvement of another entity operating on a comparable level.

This vulnerability is important.

The title **Creator** does not make Ghoziarian omnipotent.

---

# The Creator Myth

Numerous civilizations possess myths describing a crimson figure present during the earliest stages of existence.

Some versions describe the entity shaping matter.

Others describe it fighting beings that existed before conventional life.

Several later cultures identified this figure with Ghoziarian.

The Crimson Order itself has never publicly confirmed whether these stories are accurate.

As a result, one of the largest unanswered questions surrounding Ghoziarian remains simple:

Did he witness the beginning of the universe?

Or did he help cause it?

---

# Relationship with Gods

Ghoziarian has occasionally been described as a **god-killer**.

The term is misleading.

"God" is not a scientific classification within the Omicron Galaxy and has been applied to Ascended beings, cosmic entities, anomalous intelligences, ancient rulers, and powerful extraterrestrial life.

Several legends claim Ghoziarian has destroyed entities considered divine by their respective civilizations.

Whether these stories are literal or symbolic remains disputed.

Ghoziarian himself does not appear to use the title of god.
