# Helios

> "You only have one advantage over me. I have not learned it yet."

## Overview

**Helios**, known as **The Crimson Shadow**, is a senior member of [[The Crimson Order]] and one of its most dangerous combatants.

His true origin, species, and age remain unknown.

Within the Crimson Order, Helios appears to serve as a response to threats that cannot be ignored.

Where [[Crimson Creator|Aethros]] is associated with creation and [[Crimson Preserver|Ava]] with preservation, Helios is associated with **adaptation**.

His abilities allow him to alter his own anomalous state in response to the powers, strengths, and weaknesses of those he faces.

This makes him increasingly dangerous during prolonged combat.

Helios does not perfectly copy his enemies.

He becomes capable of answering them.

## Appearance

Helios possesses a humanoid male form approximately 5'10" in height.

Unlike some members of the Crimson Order, his appearance is generally practical rather than ceremonial.

He is often depicted wearing dark armour with crimson detailing, designed more for combat than display.

The exact appearance of his armour has varied across different sightings.

Some reports describe its surface subtly changing during combat, particularly when Helios begins adapting to an opponent.

Whether this represents a physical transformation of the armour, an extension of his anomalous abilities, or simple visual distortion remains unknown.

His face is rarely described in reliable accounts.

---

## Personality

Helios is direct, controlled, and highly confident.

He is considerably less philosophical than Aethros and less introspective than Ava.

He tends to view problems in simple terms:

Identify the threat.

Understand it.

Adapt.

Eliminate it.

Despite his reputation, Helios is not mindlessly aggressive.

He is capable of patience and will often observe an opponent before committing fully to combat.

The reason is practical.

Information is one of his greatest weapons.

Every technique he sees gives him more to work with.

Every attack he survives teaches him something.

Helios is therefore at his most vulnerable during the beginning of an encounter and at his most dangerous once he understands what he is fighting.

He rarely wastes time intimidating opponents verbally.

When he does speak during combat, it is usually because he has noticed something important.

---

# The Crimson Shadow

The title **Crimson Shadow** refers primarily to Helios's adaptive nature.

His abilities allow him to become increasingly similar to the threat opposing him without becoming a literal copy.

Helios reflects strengths, develops counters, and alters his capabilities until the advantage begins to shift in his favour.

This has led to an old description associated with the Crimson Order:

**Every great power casts a shadow.**

Helios is often believed to be that shadow.

The exact origin of the title remains unknown.

---

# Role Within the Crimson Order

Helios appears to serve as one of the Order's primary combat-oriented members.

He is most commonly associated with:

- Direct confrontation
- Elimination of extreme threats
- Protection of Crimson Order interests
- Containment of anomalous entities
- Pursuit of hostile cosmic beings
- Intervention when conventional resistance has failed

He is not believed to command the Crimson Order.

That authority remains with [[Crimson Creator|Aethros]].

However, when an event requires direct and overwhelming force, Helios is one of the individuals most likely to be deployed.

His presence generally indicates that the Order considers the threat significant.

# Abilities

## Adaptive Resonance

Helios's defining ability is **Adaptive Resonance**.

This allows him to alter his own anomalous state in response to abilities used by another individual.

The adaptation is not instantaneous.

Helios must first observe, experience, or survive an ability before adapting effectively to it.

The longer an encounter continues, the more accurately he can respond.

Possible adaptations include:

- Increasing physical strength
- Improving durability
- Developing resistance to specific forms of energy
- Adjusting reaction speed
- Compensating for unusual movement
- Detecting repeated teleportation patterns
- Replicating limited aspects of anomalous powers
- Developing temporary counter-effects

Adaptive Resonance does not grant Helios a perfect duplicate of another person's abilities.

Instead, it creates whatever response best allows him to continue fighting them.

---

## Limited Power Replication

In some circumstances, Helios can temporarily reproduce aspects of another individual's anomalous abilities.

These copies are usually incomplete.

A replicated ability may be weaker, less precise, or function differently from the original.

The copied effect is generally temporary and fades once Helios is no longer exposed to the individual or force responsible.

This ability is considered an extension of Adaptive Resonance rather than true power theft.

---

## Immense Physical Strength

Helios possesses extreme physical strength even before adaptation occurs.

He is capable of overpowering most conventional biological opponents and damaging heavily reinforced materials without assistance.

His strength can increase further during combat when facing physically superior enemies.

The upper limit of this adaptation remains unknown.

---

## Enhanced Durability

Helios can survive physical trauma that would kill ordinary biological life.

His durability also appears to increase in response to repeated exposure.

An attack that injures him severely the first time may be significantly less effective when repeated.

This does not make him invulnerable.

Sufficiently powerful attacks can still injure or kill him.

---

## Spatial Translocation

Helios is capable of instantaneous or near-instantaneous relocation.

This is commonly described as teleportation.

He frequently uses this ability aggressively in combat, repositioning around opponents rather than simply using it as transportation.

The maximum range remains unknown.

---

## Combat Adaptation

Helios's abilities also affect the way he fights.

He rapidly adjusts his combat style after observing an opponent.

This includes:

- Changing attack patterns
- Altering timing
- Identifying repeated habits
- Exploiting defensive weaknesses
- Adjusting movement to counter unfamiliar fighting styles

This effect appears to be partly anomalous and partly the result of extensive combat experience.

---

# Limitations

Despite his extreme power, Helios has several important weaknesses.

## Initial Vulnerability

Helios is most vulnerable at the beginning of an encounter.

Abilities he has never experienced may bypass his existing defences entirely.

A sufficiently powerful or unexpected attack can injure him before adaptation occurs.

---

## Adaptation Requires Exposure

Helios cannot automatically counter abilities he has never witnessed.

He must first gather information.

This may require him to deliberately allow an opponent to demonstrate their abilities.

In extreme cases, this is dangerous.

---

## Overwhelming Force

Adaptation does not guarantee survival.

An attack capable of destroying Helios before his abilities can respond remains effective.

This is one of the few reliable methods theorized for defeating him.

---

## Conflicting Adaptations

Facing multiple radically different threats simultaneously can strain Helios's adaptive abilities.

Adapting against one opponent may reduce his effectiveness against another.

The more conflicting conditions he is forced to handle, the less efficiently his abilities respond.

---

## Temporary Replication

Abilities copied through Adaptive Resonance do not appear to remain permanently.

Once the relevant threat is removed or sufficient time passes, Helios gradually returns to his normal state.

---

# Combat Behaviour

Helios rarely begins combat at maximum aggression.

He prefers to test opponents first.

Early exchanges are often deliberately controlled.

He observes reaction speed, defensive behaviour, anomalous output, physical strength, and psychological tendencies.

Once enough information has been gathered, his approach changes rapidly.

Helios becomes more aggressive as the opponent becomes more predictable.

This has created a simple rule among individuals familiar with his abilities:

**The longer the fight lasts, the worse your chances become.**

---

# Relationship with Aethros

Helios recognizes [[Crimson Creator|Aethros]] as the leader of the Crimson Order.

Their exact relationship is unclear.

Unlike Ava, Helios is not currently known to be related to Aethros by blood.

He appears to respect Aethros primarily because of his power, experience, and authority.

Whether this loyalty is personal, ideological, or simply practical remains unknown.

Aethros appears to trust Helios with situations where overwhelming combat force may be required.

---

# Relationship with Ava

Helios and [[Crimson Preserver|Ava]] possess radically different approaches.

Ava attempts to preserve.

Helios attempts to overcome.

This difference occasionally creates disagreement.

Ava may favour containment, withdrawal, or preservation of endangered individuals.

Helios is more likely to remove a threat entirely if he believes it will remain dangerous.

Despite this, the two appear to cooperate effectively.

Ava can slow or restrict an opponent.

Helios can then adapt to it.

This combination makes them exceptionally dangerous when operating together.

---

# Reputation

Helios is one of the few Crimson Order members known primarily through combat accounts rather than mythology.

Stories surrounding him frequently involve extremely powerful opponents who initially appeared to hold the advantage.

Several accounts describe Helios being wounded early in an encounter before gradually becoming increasingly resistant to the same attacks.

Because many of these records originate from damaged or ancient sources, the exact accuracy of the stories cannot be confirmed.

The pattern is consistent enough to be taken seriously.
