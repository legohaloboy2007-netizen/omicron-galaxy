# Cryomede

Cryomede is a frozen oceanic world located within the [[Aegimius System]], known for its endless glaciers, violent ice storms, and hidden subglacial oceans buried beneath miles of frozen crust. Although the planet appears completely lifeless from orbit, vast liquid oceans and ancient geological networks exist deep below the ice.

The surface of Cryomede is dominated by colossal frozen wastelands, fractured ice shelves, and permanent blizzard systems capable of swallowing entire expeditions. Temperatures across much of the planet remain lethally low, forcing most research stations and exploration outposts to operate beneath the surface or within heavily reinforced thermal facilities.

Beneath the frozen crust lies an enormous interconnected ocean system warmed by geothermal activity deep within the planet’s core. These hidden oceans contain strange ecosystems, rare mineral deposits, and unexplored abyssal regions still largely untouched by modern civilization.

Cryomede became a major target for scientific expeditions and deep-drilling operations following the discovery of unusual energy readings and ancient structures trapped beneath the ice. Several exploration teams have vanished while attempting to access deeper subglacial regions.

The world remains one of the least explored planets within the [[Aegimius System]], largely due to its extreme conditions and the immense difficulty of navigating beneath the frozen crust.
