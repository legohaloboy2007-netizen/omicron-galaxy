Overview

Michael Vandrix, known throughout the [[Hell Hosts]] as Deadeye, is a Commander within the [[Doom Corps]] and one of the most feared precision shooters of [[House Vandrix]].

A member of the [[722nd Sniper Recon Battalion]], better known as Ghostwatch, Deadeye built his reputation through patience, battlefield calm, impossible shots, and a level of observation that borders on unnerving.

Unlike his elder brother [[Warden]], whose reputation comes from command presence and battlefield discipline, Deadeye became known through silence.

He rarely speaks during operations.

He rarely misses either.

Within the Hell Hosts, Deadeye is not viewed as a loud hero or theatrical killer. He is regarded as a reliable long-range specialist whose presence can decide whether a squad survives an impossible advance or walks into a kill zone.

A common internal command note states:

«“If Deadeye has a target, consider it already dealt with.”»

---

House Vandrix

[[House Vandrix]] is one of the old military families whose history predates the modern Hell Hosts.

Where Warden represents the family’s command discipline, Deadeye represents its precision.

He carries the Vandrix name differently from his elder brother. He does not command through speeches, inspections, or visible authority. Instead, he upholds the family reputation through patience, restraint, and results.

House Vandrix is associated with:

- Discipline
- Duty
- Reliability
- Command
- Tradition
- Service
- Battlefield endurance
- Professionalism under pressure

Deadeye embodies these traits through stillness rather than force.

If Warden is the line refusing to break, Deadeye is the shot fired before the line ever needs to move.

Family

Deadeye is one of the Vandrix siblings.

Known members of House Vandrix include:

- [[Warden]] — Brother
- [[Doom Vandrix]] — Sister
- [[Hope Vandrix]] — Younger sibling
- [[War Vandrix]] — Younger sibling
- [[Red Vandrix]] — Younger sibling

As one of the older Vandrix siblings, Michael carries both pride and pressure from the family name.

He is loyal to his siblings, though he is rarely openly sentimental. His care usually appears through training, watchfulness, criticism, and quiet protection rather than affection.

To younger members of House Vandrix, Deadeye can seem harsh.

He trains them strictly.

Corrects them bluntly.

Refuses to praise them too early.

Then privately tells others when they have improved.

Personality

Deadeye is quiet, disciplined, dry-humoured, highly observant, and difficult to distract.

He rarely speaks unless he has something worth saying. During operations, his voice over comms is usually brief, calm, and precise.

He dislikes wasted movement, unnecessary noise, rushed decisions, and people who mistake confidence for competence.

Common traits include:

- Quiet
- Dry sense of humour
- Highly observant
- Discipline-driven
- Loyal to family
- Strict with standards
- Patient under pressure
- Difficult to surprise
- Dislikes wasted movement
- Operates best alone, but strikes hardest with a team

Deadeye is not emotionless.

He simply does not believe every thought needs to be spoken.

---

Biggest Flaw

Deadeye’s greatest flaw is rare hesitation.

Not often.

Not enough to ruin his reputation.

But often enough to matter.

There are moments where his finger rests on the trigger and he waits a fraction too long. A fraction of a second can be nothing in ordinary life.

For a sniper, it can be everything.

Deadeye does not hesitate because he doubts his aim.

He hesitates because he understands exactly what one shot changes.

At extreme range, killing can become silent. There is no struggle, no shouted warning, no clash of blades, and no final expression seen clearly with the naked eye.

There is only a target in the scope.

A breath.

A decision.

A result.

Deadeye has fired enough shots to know that precision does not make death weightless.

That knowledge makes him better.

It also makes him human.

---

Personal Habit

Deadeye counts things.

Not obsessively, but constantly.

He counts:

- Footsteps
- Breaths
- Shots fired
- Patrol rotations
- Seconds between movements
- Wind changes
- Reload timings
- Heartbeat rhythms
- Pauses before someone lies

This habit makes him unnervingly perceptive.

He often notices details others overlook entirely.

When asked how he knew something was wrong, his answer is usually frustratingly simple.

«“You breathed wrong.”»

722nd Sniper Recon Battalion

Deadeye serves within the [[722nd Sniper Recon Battalion]], better known as Ghostwatch.

Ghostwatch specialises in:

- Sniper reconnaissance
- Long-range overwatch
- Counter-sniper operations
- Target observation
- Priority target elimination
- Battlefield mapping
- Forward intelligence
- Precision support during assaults
- Extraction overwatch
- Silent route clearing

The battalion’s role is not merely to shoot from a distance.

Ghostwatch observes, identifies, predicts, and removes threats before the wider force is forced to react.

By the time enemies realise Ghostwatch is present, the mission is often already decided.

---

Combat Style

Deadeye prefers ending fights before enemies realise one has begun.

His combat style is built around patience, observation, positioning, and precision.

He specialises in:

- Long-range eliminations
- Overwatch command
- Counter-sniper hunting
- Precision support during assaults
- Covering extraction routes
- Neutralising officers and specialists
- Eliminating threats to advancing units
- Protecting squads from unseen angles

Deadeye does not rush.

He waits until the shot matters.

Where other soldiers measure a battle through noise, movement, and pressure, Deadeye measures it through stillness.

A shift in posture.

A change in breathing.

A commander stepping into view.

A sniper giving away one lens flare.

That is enough.

Weapons

M138G Sniper Energy Rifle

The [[M138G Sniper Energy Rifle]] is Deadeye’s primary long-range weapon.

It is a high-calibre energy rifle built for extreme-range precision and high-value target elimination.

In Deadeye’s hands, the weapon is used less like a firearm and more like a battlefield correction tool.

If something threatens the formation, he removes it.

---

CR-556 Combat Rifle

The [[CR-556 Combat Rifle]] serves as Deadeye’s reliable secondary battlefield rifle.

He uses it during closer engagements, repositioning, defensive firefights, and situations where the M138G is impractical.

Deadeye prefers not to be forced into standard combat ranges, but he is fully capable when it happens.

---

VX-6 Heavy Revolver

The [[VX-6 Heavy Revolver]] is Deadeye’s sidearm.

High stopping power, heavy recoil, and deadly accuracy make it dangerous in skilled hands.

Deadeye uses the weapon sparingly, but effectively.

Some Ghostwatch soldiers joke that if Deadeye has drawn the VX-6, someone allowed the situation to become “impolite.”

---

Favourite Drink

Deadeye regularly drinks [[Pulsar]], the Blackstar energy drink popular among pilots, mechanics, students, and exhausted military personnel.

For Deadeye, Pulsar is less a casual drink and more a survival tool during long overwatch periods.

Medical staff dislike how often he drinks it.

Warden dislikes it even more.

Deadeye continues drinking it anyway.

---

Relationship with Warden

[[Warden]] is Deadeye’s elder brother and one of the most important figures in [[House Vandrix]].

Deadeye respects Warden deeply, though the two rarely have long emotional conversations.

Their relationship is built on discipline, trust, and shared family expectation rather than open sentiment.

A typical conversation between them may involve only a few words.

That does not mean the bond is weak.

It means both understand the other without needing much explanation.

Warden trusts Deadeye’s judgement in matters of reconnaissance, overwatch, and precision support. Deadeye respects Warden’s ability to hold collapsing fronts together through discipline and command.

Warden holds the line.

Deadeye watches over it.

---

Relationship with Doom

[[Doom Vandrix]] and Deadeye share a strong mutual understanding through action rather than words.

Where Doom represents direct force, Deadeye represents precision.

Their methods differ, but they complement one another well.

Doom advances.

Deadeye clears the path.

Their relationship is less about conversation and more about trust in the field. Each understands that the other will do what is necessary when the mission begins.

---

Relationship with Striker

[[Striker]] is one of Deadeye’s closest operational partners within the [[722nd Sniper Recon Battalion]].

A former member of the [[Free Order Rebellion]], Striker brings a more aggressive and improvisational style to sniper reconnaissance. Unlike Deadeye’s patient precision, Striker is more mobile, instinctive, and willing to create opportunities rather than wait for them.

The two often clash over method.

Deadeye thinks Striker moves too much.

Striker thinks Deadeye waits too long.

Despite this, their battlefield trust is exceptional.

Striker is one of the few people capable of recognising when Deadeye hesitates, and one of the few who knows how to bring him back without making it worse.

Sometimes all he has to say is:

«“Breathe, Mike.”»

Deadeye waits for the perfect shot.

Striker makes the shot perfect.

Together, they are among Ghostwatch’s most dangerous pairs.

---

Relationship with Younger Vandrix Members

Deadeye is strict when training younger members of House Vandrix.

He does not believe the family name should protect them from criticism.

If anything, he believes it should expose them to higher standards.

He is harsh during drills, unforgiving of repeated mistakes, and especially critical of wasted motion or careless weapon handling.

However, his criticism is not cruelty.

Deadeye trains them hard because he knows battlefield mistakes become funerals.

Though he rarely praises them directly, he is known to quietly acknowledge their progress to others.

Younger Vandrix soldiers often spend years trying to earn one clear compliment from him.

When it finally comes, they remember it.

---

Relationship with Ghostwatch

Within Ghostwatch, Deadeye is respected as one of the battalion’s most reliable precision specialists.

He is not the loudest member of the unit and does not seek command attention, but his record speaks for him.

Ghostwatch soldiers know that Deadeye’s silence usually means he is working.

They also know that when he speaks, they should listen.

Among his battalion, one joking phrase has followed him for years:

«“Forty-six confirmed. Forty-seven fired. Don’t ask.”»

Whether the story behind the saying is true, exaggerated, or deliberately misreported depends entirely on who is telling it.

Deadeye refuses to clarify.

---

Reputation

Among Troops

To Hell Host troops, Deadeye represents safety from unseen threats.

If he is assigned to overwatch, soldiers move with more confidence.

A common belief among troops states:

«“If Deadeye is watching, you’re safer.”»

Among Officers

Among officers, Deadeye is valued for reliability, patience, and precision support.

He is not always easy to work with, particularly for commanders who rush decisions or disregard reconnaissance data.

However, few question his results.

Among Enemies

Among enemies, Deadeye is a nightmare.

Movement does not guarantee survival.

Stillness does not guarantee safety.

A common warning associated with him states:

«“If you stop moving, you die. If you keep moving, you still might.”»

---

Legacy

Deadeye’s reputation is built on restraint rather than spectacle.

He is not remembered for charging enemy lines or delivering speeches before battle. He is remembered for the shots that prevented disasters, the officers who never gave fatal orders because they were eliminated first, and the soldiers who survived because he was watching.

For House Vandrix, he represents precision within discipline.

For Ghostwatch, he represents the quiet standard every sniper hopes to reach.

And for the Doom Corps, he is a reminder that sometimes the most important soldier on the battlefield is the one nobody sees.
