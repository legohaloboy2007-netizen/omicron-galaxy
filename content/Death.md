# The Crimson Death

> "You were given time. You wasted it."

## Overview

**The Crimson Death** is one of the most feared members of [[The Crimson Order]] and is widely regarded as one of its cruelest.

Their true name, species, and identity remain unknown.

Standing approximately 7'2" in height, Crimson Death possesses an imposing humanoid form and is most commonly associated with execution, punishment, and the elimination of enemies considered beyond negotiation.

Unlike many members of the Order, Crimson Death is not known for elaborate displays of power.

Their abilities are comparatively simple.

Teleportation.

Invisibility.

Used properly, they are more than enough.

Crimson Death does not need to overpower an enemy openly.

They only need to reach them.

## Appearance

Crimson Death possesses an unusually tall humanoid form, standing approximately 7'2".

Very little reliable information exists regarding their face or true physical appearance.

Most witnesses encounter them under poor conditions, often briefly and during violent confrontations.

Their equipment is typically described as dark, heavy, and minimalistic, with limited crimson detailing.

Unlike more ceremonial members of the Order, Crimson Death appears designed almost entirely around intimidation and functionality.

The lack of identifying features has made it difficult to determine whether Crimson Death is human, altered human, or something else entirely.

---

## Personality

Crimson Death is considered one of the cruelest members of the Crimson Order.

They are not known for mercy.

Unlike [[Crimson Arsonist|Joseph]], whose destructive tendencies are often accompanied by humour, Crimson Death displays little visible enjoyment or theatricality.

Their cruelty is quieter.

More deliberate.

Crimson Death is willing to prolong fear, isolate targets, and exploit uncertainty before striking.

They appear to understand intimidation extremely well and frequently use invisibility and teleportation to destabilize opponents psychologically before direct engagement.

They rarely speak more than necessary.

When they do, it is usually to make their intentions clear.

---

# The Crimson Death

The title **Crimson Death** appears to reflect their role within the Order rather than any literal control over death itself.

Crimson Death is most frequently associated with:

- Execution of condemned targets
- Elimination of high-risk enemies
- Internal punishment
- Retrieval of individuals who betrayed the Order
- Silent infiltration
- Psychological warfare
- Assassination of protected targets

Their deployment usually indicates that the Order no longer considers negotiation useful.

---

# Role Within the Crimson Order

Crimson Death appears to function as one of the Order's primary executioners.

Where [[Crimson Crusader|Stanislav]] may pursue a target and [[Crimson Shadow|Helios]] may adapt to defeat one, Crimson Death is more often associated with the final stage.

The target has already been found.

The decision has already been made.

Crimson Death simply carries it out.

Because of this, even other members of the Crimson Order are believed to treat their involvement seriously.

---

# Abilities

## Teleportation

Crimson Death can relocate themselves instantaneously across short and potentially moderate distances.

They use teleportation primarily for ambushes and repositioning.

Known applications include:

- Appearing behind targets
- Bypassing physical barriers
- Escaping direct retaliation
- Entering sealed rooms
- Cutting off escape routes
- Repositioning during close combat

The maximum range remains unknown.

Crimson Death rarely uses teleportation theatrically.

Movements are typically efficient and difficult to predict.

---

## Invisibility

Crimson Death can render themselves visually undetectable.

This ability appears to conceal their physical form from ordinary sight.

Whether it also suppresses thermal signatures, energy emissions, sound, or other detection methods remains uncertain.

Crimson Death commonly combines invisibility with teleportation, creating encounters where targets cannot determine whether they are being followed, watched, or already surrounded.

This combination is considered one of their most dangerous advantages.

---

## Ambush Expertise

Although not strictly anomalous, Crimson Death possesses considerable skill in stalking and surprise attacks.

They frequently avoid prolonged direct combat when a faster and more efficient method is available.

Their preferred approach is to attack from a position of absolute advantage.

---

## Enhanced Physical Capability

Crimson Death possesses strength and durability exceeding ordinary human levels.

Their exact limits remain uncertain.

Given their height and build, even conventional physical attacks are extremely dangerous.

# Combat Style

Crimson Death fights with patience.

They rarely approach enemies directly unless intimidation is the objective.

More commonly, they disappear.

Then the environment becomes the weapon.

A door opens with nobody visible.

A light dies.

A target disappears from formation.

Movement is heard where nobody stands.

Then Crimson Death attacks.

Their combat style depends heavily on confusion and isolation.

Against groups, they attempt to separate individuals.

Against powerful opponents, they avoid predictable exchanges and repeatedly reposition until an opening appears.

---

# Psychological Warfare

Crimson Death appears to deliberately cultivate fear.

They understand that an invisible opponent is often more dangerous psychologically than physically.

Targets may spend minutes or hours waiting for an attack that never comes.

Crimson Death sometimes allows this fear to build before acting.

This behaviour has contributed heavily to their reputation for cruelty.

The objective is not always simply to kill.

Sometimes it is to make the target understand that escape was never possible.

---

# Cruelty

Crimson Death's reputation is not based solely on battlefield efficiency.

Multiple accounts describe behaviour considered unnecessarily severe even by Crimson Order standards.

These include deliberately prolonging pursuits, isolating targets from allies, and allowing condemned individuals to realize who has been sent after them before completing an execution.

The reasons for this behaviour remain unclear.

It may be personal preference.

It may be intimidation intended to discourage future betrayal.

It may simply be part of the role Crimson Death has chosen within the Order.

---

# Relationship with Aethros

[[Crimson Creator|Aethros]] appears to trust Crimson Death with assignments requiring absolute finality.

This indicates an unusually high level of confidence.

However, it remains unclear whether Aethros personally approves of Crimson Death's methods.

There is a difference between accepting someone as useful and agreeing with everything they do.

That distinction has never been publicly clarified.

---

# Relationship with Stanislav

[[Crimson Crusader|Stanislav]] and Crimson Death occasionally perform complementary roles.

Stanislav locates and tracks targets.

Crimson Death eliminates them.

The two appear effective together, although their approaches differ considerably.

Stanislav is methodical.

Crimson Death is predatory.

---

# Relationship with Helios

[[Crimson Shadow|Helios]] appears better suited to direct confrontation than Crimson Death.

As a result, the two are often deployed under different circumstances.

Helios is sent when the Order expects resistance.

Crimson Death is sent when the Order expects escape.

---

# Reputation

Crimson Death is one of the few Crimson Order members whose name alone can alter how an enemy behaves.

Targets who learn that Crimson Death has been assigned to them frequently become paranoid, abandon safe locations, or make mistakes while attempting to flee.

This is often exactly what Crimson Death wants.

Their reputation therefore functions as part of their weaponry.

Fear creates movement.

Movement creates mistakes.

Mistakes create openings.
