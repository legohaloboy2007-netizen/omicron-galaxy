# Deatherium

## Overview

Deatherium is an extremely rare anomalous substance associated with:
- death
- decay
- abyssal corruption
- and catastrophic biological collapse

Unlike [[Element 95]], which destabilizes reality and consciousness, Deatherium primarily affects:
- life
- biological structure
- entropy
- and cellular degradation

The substance is widely feared throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]] due to its ability to corrupt living organisms into violent, unstable, death-linked forms.

Many civilizations consider Deatherium more immediately lethal than Element 95.

Where Element 95 transforms reality—

Deatherium consumes life.

## Nature

Deatherium is a dense black-red crystalline substance often found in:
- abyssal regions
- collapsed ecosystems
- deep volcanic anomalies
- contaminated ruins
- and death-saturated environments

The material emits:
- low thermal radiation
- unstable energy pulses
- and biological decay fields

Organic matter exposed to Deatherium rapidly deteriorates, mutates, or becomes violently unstable.

Unlike conventional toxins or radiation, Deatherium appears to actively spread through:
- biological systems
- bloodstream contamination
- organic absorption
- and prolonged environmental exposure

Some researchers describe the substance as:
“living entropy.”

## Physical Appearance

Deatherium commonly appears as:
- dark crystalline growth
- blackened metallic ore
- pulsating red-veined formations
- or molten abyssal residue

The material often emits:
- faint crimson light
- heatless vapor
- distorted electromagnetic interference
- and low-frequency resonance detectable only at close range

In highly concentrated regions, nearby environments may begin:
- decaying rapidly
- losing biological stability
- or suffering spontaneous ecosystem collapse

## Biological Effects

Exposure to Deatherium causes severe physiological damage.

Known symptoms include:
- necrotic tissue corruption
- organ degradation
- skeletal mutation
- blood crystallization
- neurological collapse
- violent aggression
- and eventual biological restructuring

In advanced stages, victims may become:
- partially undead
- hyper-aggressive
- physically unstable
- or permanently linked to Deatherium corruption fields

Many affected organisms lose normal pain response entirely.

## Deatherium Corruption

Unlike the adaptive corruption of [[Element 95]], Deatherium corruption is:
- destructive
- degenerative
- violent
- and heavily associated with death itself

Regions contaminated by Deatherium often become:
- barren wastelands
- corpse-filled zones
- biological dead regions
- or unstable predator ecosystems

Some corrupted organisms continue functioning long after conventional biological death.

## The Bleeding Abyss Connection

Deatherium is strongly associated with:

[[The Bleeding Abyss]]

Ancient records suggest the substance naturally forms within regions experiencing:
- massive death events
- abyssal instability
- dimensional collapse
- and biological corruption

The Bleeding Abyss contains some of the highest known concentrations of Deatherium in existence.

Many explorers entering the region never return physically intact.

## Ascension & Mutation

While significantly rarer than Element 95 Ascension, Deatherium exposure has produced catastrophic transformations in certain individuals.

The most infamous example is:

[[Blood]]

Following exposure to:
- extreme volcanic conditions
- anomalous energy
- and Deatherium-linked corruption

Blood underwent a violent Ascension tied to:
- fire
- ash
- destruction
- and abyssal mutation

Unlike Veil-linked Ascensions, Deatherium transformations are typically:
- physically brutal
- psychologically unstable
- and overwhelmingly destructive

## Environmental Effects

Deatherium contamination frequently causes:
- dying vegetation
- atmospheric discoloration
- blackened terrain
- biological collapse
- crimson storm activity
- and ecosystem mutation

Long-term contamination zones may become:
completely uninhabitable.

Some regions continue decaying centuries after initial exposure.

## Known Forms

### Raw Deatherium
Naturally occurring crystalline deposits found in abyssal and corrupted regions.

---

### Molten Deatherium
Highly unstable liquid state commonly associated with volcanic contamination and catastrophic mutation events.

---

### Abyssal Residue
Fine particulate contamination capable of spreading through atmosphere and biological systems.

---

### Refined Deatherium
Weaponized or experimentally stabilized forms utilized by certain factions and rogue researchers.

## Modern Research

Research into Deatherium is heavily restricted throughout most civilized systems.

Known groups studying the substance include:
- [[Obsidian Veil]]
- abyssal cults
- black-market bio-weapon researchers
- and certain extremist military divisions

Most governments classify Deatherium experimentation as:
existentially dangerous.

## Common Theories

The true origin of Deatherium remains unknown.

Popular theories suggest it may be:
- condensed death energy
- a byproduct of dimensional collapse
- corruption originating from [[The Bleeding Abyss]]
- failed biological creation matter
- or an opposing force to [[Element 95]]

Some ancient records imply the substance may predate known biological life entirely.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Deatherium is known as:
- the substance of decay
- the corruption of death itself
- one of the deadliest anomalous materials ever discovered
- and the primary corruption source of the [[The Bleeding Abyss|Bleeding Abyss]]

Among abyssal survivors, one warning is commonly repeated:

> “Element 95 changes you.  
> Deatherium leaves nothing left to change.”
