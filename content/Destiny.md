# Destiny

## Overview

**Real Name:** Victoria Grant

Destiny is a Battalion Captain within the:
[[Hell Hosts]]

and commander of the:
612th Hunters Battalion
within the:
Doom Corps — Reapers Division.

Known for her unmatched stealth capabilities, precision combat methods, and psychological resilience, Destiny rapidly became one of the most effective covert operatives in Hell Host history.

Despite being the daughter of:
[[Predator]]

she refused all favoritism and earned her reputation entirely through performance.

Following her kidnapping and eventual Ascension, Destiny became one of the most mysterious and dangerous individuals in the galaxy.

## Appearance

Destiny stands approximately:
5’8"

with a thin but athletic build shaped by years of military conditioning and infiltration work.

### Notable Features
- Blonde hair (usually tied back)
- Green eyes
- Scar across left eye
- Pale complexion
- Calm, unreadable expression

Unlike Predator’s heavy command armor, Destiny’s combat suit is:
- sleek
- lightweight
- stealth-focused
- and heavily specialized for infiltration operations.

The armor features:
- adaptive cloaking systems
- lightweight tactical plating
- silent movement systems
- and integrated recon technology.

Among enemy forces, Destiny is often described as:
“a ghost.”

## Personality

Destiny is:
- quiet
- intelligent
- independent
- and highly disciplined.

She speaks very little during operations and rarely wastes words unnecessarily.

Although highly capable socially when required, Destiny generally prefers isolation and small trusted circles over large groups.

She values:
- competence
- trust
- efficiency
- and loyalty.

### Emotional State

Long-term military isolation and covert warfare gradually caused Destiny to become emotionally distant even before her Ascension.

She became increasingly:
- withdrawn
- difficult to emotionally reach
- and heavily mission-focused.

Following her kidnapping and exposure to:
[[The Veil]]

this emotional distance intensified dramatically.

## Combat Style

Destiny specializes in:
- reconnaissance
- deep infiltration
- stealth warfare
- HVT elimination
- and long-range precision combat.

Unlike many Hell Host commanders, Destiny avoids unnecessary destruction whenever possible.

Her combat philosophy revolves around:
speed,
precision,
and remaining unseen.

### Common Equipment
- [[Y205 SSMG]]
- [[333 HCS High-Calibre Sniper]]
- [[PX20 Silenced Pistol]]

### Abilities

#### Invisibility
An advanced active camouflage system capable of rendering Destiny nearly invisible during combat operations.

---

#### AI Guardian Shield
An adaptive emergency force-field system that automatically activates when lethal danger is detected.

## History

Victoria Grant was born to:
[[Predator]]

during the early years of growing galactic instability.

Much of her childhood was shaped by:
- military environments
- long absences from her father
- and constant exposure to warfare.

Although Predator cared deeply for her, his responsibilities often prevented him from being fully present in her life.

This created emotional distance between them from a young age.

---

Destiny later joined the:
[[Hell Hosts]]

where she quickly gained attention due to:
- exceptional combat results
- stealth specialization
- and unmatched mission efficiency.

Despite widespread assumptions that her success came from family lineage, Destiny actively rejected special treatment and insisted on advancing entirely through merit.

She eventually rose to command the:
612th Hunters Battalion.

## Disappearance & Kidnapping

During a high-risk operation, Destiny disappeared and was listed:
MIA for 7 months.

It was eventually discovered that she had been captured by:
[[Obsidian Veil]]

due to:
- her lineage
- and a unique interaction between her biology and:
[[Element 95]]

At some point during captivity, Destiny was exposed to:
- The Veil
- Element 95
- and eventually:
[[The Witness]]

The experience fundamentally altered her psychologically and physically.

## Ascension

Destiny’s Ascension did not fully erase her humanity.

Instead, her consciousness became linked to:
[[The Witness]]

creating an unstable internal conflict between:
- Victoria Grant
- and the growing influence of the Veil.

### The Remaining Humanity
Part of Destiny still:
- remembers her previous life
- cares about people she once trusted
- and attempts to resist losing herself completely.

---

### The Witness Influence
Another side of her became:
- colder
- detached
- increasingly cosmic in perspective
- and emotionally disconnected from ordinary humanity.

This internal fracture makes Destiny one of the most psychologically unstable Ascended individuals known.

## Relationships

### [[Predator]]
Destiny’s relationship with her father is deeply complicated.

Although the two became closer through military service, emotional distance always remained between them.

Destiny understands Predator genuinely tried to save her following her kidnapping.

However, part of her still blames him emotionally for failing to protect her.

Predator carries immense guilt over what happened to her.

---

### [[Blood]]
Blood and Destiny maintained a close relationship for many years within the:
[[Hell Hosts]]

Although their bond never became romantic, the two trusted each other deeply.

Following Blood’s Ascension, Destiny became increasingly emotionally isolated.

---

### [[Azure Gale]]
Destiny respects Azure Gale’s abilities but often found his personality exhausting.

Their interactions were frequently filled with sarcasm and tension, though mutual trust still existed between them.

## Reputation

### Within Hell Hosts
Destiny is viewed as:
- legendary
- efficient
- and deeply intimidating.

Many soldiers consider her one of the most effective covert operatives ever produced by the:
[[Hell Hosts]]

---

### Outside Factions
Enemy forces often describe Destiny as:
- impossible to track
- psychologically disturbing
- and terrifyingly precise.

Rumors surrounding her activities became even more extreme following her Ascension.

## Psychological State

Before Ascension, Destiny already struggled with:
- emotional isolation
- pressure
- and internal exhaustion.

After her transformation, these issues intensified into:
- fractured identity
- dissociation
- emotional instability
- and existential confusion.

Despite this, fragments of Victoria Grant still remain beneath the corruption.

That remaining humanity is what makes Destiny both:
tragic
and terrifying.
