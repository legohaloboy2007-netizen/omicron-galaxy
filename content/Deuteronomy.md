# Deuteronomy

Deuteronomy is one of the most mysterious and least understood worlds controlled by the [[Angel Swords]]. Very little verified information exists regarding the planet, and most available records are fragmented, classified, or entirely contradictory.

The surface of Deuteronomy is believed to consist almost entirely of a vast viscous black liquid covering the entire planet.

No confirmed stable landmasses have ever been documented.

## Environment

The black substance covering Deuteronomy displays highly abnormal properties.

Reports suggest the liquid:
- absorbs nearly all light
- produces minimal reflection
- swallows solid matter rapidly
- behaves inconsistently under observation
- may possess non-natural composition

Anything unfortunate enough to make direct contact with the surface is rarely recovered.

Ships, probes, and exploration equipment sent too close to the liquid frequently disappear without trace.

Some reports suggest objects swallowed by the substance continue transmitting signals briefly after submersion before abruptly ceasing entirely.

## Atmospheric Anomalies

One of the most unusual properties of Deuteronomy is the severe disruption of transverse waves within the atmosphere.

The planet interferes heavily with:
- communication systems
- sensor arrays
- electromagnetic transmission
- long-range scanning
- visual observation

Signals entering the atmosphere often become:
- distorted
- delayed
- duplicated
- or completely erased

Because of this, accurate planetary mapping has proven nearly impossible.

## Exploration Attempts

Very few expeditions to Deuteronomy have succeeded.

Most missions suffer from:
- navigation failure
- communication loss
- equipment malfunction
- disappearance of personnel
- unexplained psychological effects

The [[Angel Swords]] heavily restrict access to all known approach routes surrounding the planet.

The reasons for this remain unknown.

## Theories

Due to the lack of reliable information, numerous theories surround Deuteronomy.

Some believe:
- the black liquid is alive
- the planet is an ancient containment world
- the surface conceals structures beneath the liquid
- the planet possesses dimensional properties
- Deuteronomy is connected to forgotten Angel Sword history

No theory has ever been confirmed.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Deuteronomy is regarded as:
- one of the least understood planets in known space
- a forbidden Angel Sword world
- a planet associated with disappearances and anomalies
- a world many believe should remain unexplored

Even among the [[Angel Swords]], discussion of Deuteronomy is rare and heavily restricted.
