# The Crimson Devil

> "Taking life is easy. Making it is the part nobody warns you about."

## Overview

**The Crimson Devil** is a young member of [[The Crimson Order]] whose abilities revolve around the manipulation of life itself.

His original name is unknown.

Unlike many ancient members of the Order whose histories have simply been lost to time, the Crimson Devil possesses almost no memory of his existence before joining the faction.

He does not remember where he came from.

He does not remember his family.

He does not remember his original name.

As far as he is concerned, his life begins with the Crimson Order.

Despite his title and intimidating appearance, the Crimson Devil's abilities are not exclusively destructive.

He can drain biological life from living organisms with frightening efficiency, but he also possesses the far more difficult ability to create or restore living matter.

This unusual contradiction makes him one of the more poorly understood members of the Order.

He carries a large scythe as his signature weapon.

Nobody appears surprised by this.

## Appearance

The Crimson Devil possesses a young humanoid male form approximately 5'9" in height.

His appearance is commonly associated with dark clothing or armour incorporating crimson elements consistent with the wider Crimson Order.

His most recognizable piece of equipment is a large scythe carried during combat.

Although visually reminiscent of ancient agricultural and ceremonial weapons, the scythe is fully functional and has been adapted for combat.

Whether the weapon possesses anomalous properties of its own remains uncertain.

Despite his title, there is no evidence that the Crimson Devil is literally connected to any religious interpretation of a devil or demonic entity.

The title appears primarily derived from his abilities, weapon, and reputation.

---

## Personality

The Crimson Devil is considerably younger and less experienced than many senior members of the Crimson Order.

His missing memories have heavily influenced his personality.

He rarely discusses his life before the Order because, from his perspective, there is almost nothing to discuss.

This has left him with an unusual relationship with identity.

Others can describe where they were born, who raised them, or what they were before becoming what they are.

The Crimson Devil cannot.

Although this occasionally frustrates him, he does not spend every waking moment obsessing over the past.

He has gradually accepted the Crimson Order as the closest thing he possesses to a home.

His personality is generally reserved, dry, and occasionally morbid.

He is aware of how ridiculous the combination of the title **Crimson Devil**, a scythe, and life-draining powers appears.

He has stopped attempting to convince people that the image was not entirely intentional.

---

# The Crimson Devil

The title **Crimson Devil** appears to originate primarily from the destructive side of his abilities.

Witnesses have described living organisms weakening rapidly in his presence when he deliberately activates his life-draining power.

Combined with his scythe and association with the Crimson Order, the resulting imagery quickly produced the title.

However, the name only describes half of what he can do.

The same anomalous force that allows him to remove life can, under considerably more difficult conditions, be used to create it.

This has led some members of the Order to question whether **Devil** is actually an accurate description of his role.

The Crimson Devil does not particularly care.

# Abilities

## Life Drain

The Crimson Devil can extract biological vitality from living organisms.

The exact nature of the energy being removed remains poorly understood.

Victims exposed to the effect may experience:

- Sudden weakness
- Loss of coordination
- Rapid exhaustion
- Reduced body temperature
- Cellular degradation
- Loss of consciousness
- Death during prolonged exposure

The process can occur through direct physical contact, although experienced use may allow limited extraction across short distances.

The Crimson Devil can control the intensity of the effect.

He does not need to kill a target.

---

## Life Absorption

Energy extracted through Life Drain can temporarily strengthen the Crimson Devil.

Potential effects include:

- Increased stamina
- Accelerated recovery
- Improved physical strength
- Reduced fatigue
- Limited healing of injuries

He cannot store unlimited amounts of absorbed biological energy.

Excessive absorption becomes difficult to control and may produce physical instability.

---

## Life Creation

The Crimson Devil possesses the extraordinary ability to generate living biological material.

This process is considerably more difficult than destroying or draining existing life.

Simple biological structures can be produced more easily.

Complex organisms require dramatically greater concentration, energy, and time.

Creating a fully developed sapient individual is believed to be either impossible for him or far beyond his current capabilities.

His ability is significantly less powerful and less refined than the matter creation demonstrated by [[Crimson Creator|Aethros]].

Where Aethros appears capable of creating matter with relative ease, the Crimson Devil must carefully construct biological systems.

Creating life is not instantaneous.

And creating something that can remain alive is considerably harder than merely creating living tissue.

---

## Biological Restoration

The Crimson Devil can redirect life energy into damaged organisms.

This allows him to assist natural healing or stabilize severe biological injuries.

The effect is different from the green flame used by [[Crimson Arsonist|Joseph]].

Joseph accelerates healing through his anomalous flame.

The Crimson Devil appears to directly supply biological vitality.

This can potentially restore damaged tissue, but cannot casually reverse death.

---

## Teleportation

Like several other members of the Crimson Order, the Crimson Devil can relocate himself through anomalous spatial displacement.

He frequently combines teleportation with close-range combat.

This allows him to appear suddenly near an opponent, attack with his scythe, and withdraw before conventional retaliation.

The maximum range of this ability remains unknown.

---

# The Scythe

The Crimson Devil carries a large scythe as his primary weapon.

The design is intentionally intimidating but remains practical enough for combat due to the Crimson Devil's enhanced abilities and teleportation.

He commonly uses the weapon for:

- Sweeping close-range attacks
- Pulling opponents off balance
- Controlling distance
- Disarming weapons
- Redirecting attacks
- Channeling his anomalous abilities through physical contact

Life Drain appears easier to apply when the scythe makes contact with a target.

Whether this occurs because the weapon actively channels his power or simply provides a physical medium remains unclear.

The weapon's true origin is unknown.

---

# Relationship with Life

The Crimson Devil possesses an unusual perspective toward living organisms.

His powers make life appear simultaneously incredibly fragile and extraordinarily complicated.

Removing life can take seconds.

Creating it can require hours.

This imbalance appears to have affected him psychologically.

He has occasionally expressed frustration that destruction requires almost no effort while genuine creation demands extraordinary precision.

This has also made him surprisingly cautious about using his abilities indiscriminately.

He understands better than most people how difficult life is to replace.

---

# Relationship with Aethros

[[Crimson Creator|Aethros]] appears particularly interested in the Crimson Devil's ability to create living material.

The two possess superficially similar abilities but operate on radically different scales.

Aethros can create matter.

The Crimson Devil specializes specifically in living biological systems.

It remains unknown whether Aethros taught him this ability, awakened it, or merely recognized that the potential already existed.

The Crimson Devil occasionally trains under Aethros when attempting more complex acts of biological creation.

Progress is slow.

Aethros appears to consider this normal.

---

# Relationship with Ava

The Crimson Devil shares an unusual connection with [[Crimson Preserver|Ava]] because both suffer from severe memory loss relating to their lives outside the Crimson Order.

Their circumstances are not necessarily identical.

Ava remembers portions of an extremely long existence and recognizes Aethros as her father.

The Crimson Devil possesses almost nothing from before his arrival within the Order.

This shared absence of identity has nevertheless created a degree of understanding between them.

Neither needs to explain what it feels like to know that part of your own history is missing.

---

# Relationship with Joseph

[[Crimson Arsonist|Joseph]] finds the Crimson Devil's appearance extremely entertaining.

The Crimson Devil does not.

Joseph has repeatedly made jokes concerning the scythe.

The Crimson Devil has repeatedly threatened to remove Joseph's remaining lifespan.

Neither appears particularly serious.

Their abilities can work effectively together during emergencies, as Joseph's green flame and the Devil's biological restoration provide two entirely different methods of treating injured allies.

---

# Combat Style

The Crimson Devil is most dangerous at close range.

Teleportation allows him to rapidly close distance before attacking with his scythe.

Physical contact gives him the opportunity to use Life Drain directly, forcing opponents to choose between engaging him and allowing him to weaken them.

He frequently uses short teleportation bursts to attack from unusual directions.

Unlike [[Crimson Shadow|Helios]], he does not attempt to prolong fights unnecessarily.

The Crimson Devil generally prefers ending an engagement quickly before an opponent can adapt to his abilities.

---

# Limitations

## Life Creation Requires Time

Creating living matter is enormously more difficult than destroying it.

Even relatively simple organisms require concentration.

Complex organisms may require extended periods of uninterrupted work.

---

## Biological Complexity

The more complicated the organism, the harder creation becomes.

A functioning collection of cells is dramatically easier than a complete animal.

A complete animal is dramatically easier than a sapient organism.

---

## Energy Requirements

Creating or restoring life consumes large amounts of the Crimson Devil's own energy.

Absorbed vitality can partially compensate for this cost, but he cannot create indefinitely.

---

## Life Drain Resistance

Certain species, synthetic organisms, anomalous entities, and Ascended beings may resist his ability.

Machines without genuine biological systems are generally difficult or impossible to affect through conventional Life Drain.

---

## Death Is Not Easily Reversed

The Crimson Devil cannot simply revive anyone who dies.

Once biological death progresses beyond a certain point, reconstructing the body does not necessarily restore the original consciousness.

The distinction between creating a functioning organism and restoring the individual who once inhabited it remains poorly understood.

The Crimson Devil considers this one of the most important limitations of his powers.

---

# Memory Loss

The Crimson Devil remembers almost nothing from before joining the Crimson Order.

Even his original name has been lost.

It remains unknown whether this memory loss resulted from:

- Trauma
- Deliberate removal
- Anomalous transformation
- Damage caused by his own powers
- Intervention by another entity
- Something that occurred before the Order discovered him

Unlike Ava, the Crimson Devil possesses very few fragments from which to reconstruct his past.

For now, **The Crimson Devil** is the only identity he knows with certainty.

---

# Reputation

Outsiders frequently assume the Crimson Devil is among the more openly malevolent members of the Order.

His title does little to discourage this interpretation.

Neither does the scythe.

Nor does the ability to drain life from living organisms.

Those who spend significant time around him generally discover a more complicated individual.

The Crimson Devil understands destruction extremely well.

That is precisely why he has developed such an interest in creation.
