# Diablo

**Real Name:** William Rogers  
**Codename:** Diablo  
**Species:** [[Humans|Human]]  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** 29  
**Height:** 6'0"  
**Faction:** [[Free World Order]]  
**Rank:** Commander  
**Role:** Division Commander  
**Status:** Active  

William Rogers, better known by his codename **Diablo**, is a senior military commander within the [[Free World Order]].

Diablo commands an entire Free World military division and has developed a reputation for leading some of the organisation's most violent conventional combat operations.

Unlike commanders primarily associated with strategy from behind the front lines, Diablo frequently operates close to active combat and is known for personally participating in major engagements alongside the forces under his command.

His campaigns have resulted in considerable casualties on both sides, earning him a reputation as one of the Free World Order's most aggressive battlefield commanders.

## Personality

Diablo is aggressive, determined, and exceptionally comfortable operating within chaotic combat environments.

He possesses little hesitation once an engagement has begun and is willing to continue applying pressure against an enemy long after more cautious commanders might withdraw or reorganise.

This does not make him mindlessly violent.

Diablo understands military strategy and is capable of coordinating large numbers of personnel, vehicles, aircraft, and supporting assets across major battlefields.

His brutality is most apparent in how he applies those resources.

When Diablo commits his division to an engagement, he generally intends to overwhelm the opposing force rather than engage in prolonged cautious manoeuvring.

Despite his reputation, he maintains the discipline necessary to command a major military formation.

## Free World Order

Diablo is one of the [[Free World Order]]'s senior military officers and operates under the wider authority of [[Onyx]].

His position gives him command over an entire division of Free World personnel and its associated military resources.

The exact organisation and designation of Diablo's division has varied throughout its operational history.

Within the Free World Order, Diablo is particularly associated with large-scale ground warfare and high-intensity military operations.

Where specialised personnel may be deployed for infiltration, reconnaissance, sabotage, or precision strikes, Diablo is most effective when substantial conventional forces are required.

## Reputation

Diablo has earned a particularly violent reputation among both allied and enemy personnel.

Numerous operations under his command have resulted in extensive battlefield destruction and heavy casualties.

The old expression that **"wherever Diablo walks, blood follows"** became associated with him as accounts of his campaigns spread.

Among enemy forces, his presence can indicate that the Free World Order expects an engagement to escalate significantly.

Within his own division, opinions of Diablo vary.

Some personnel respect his willingness to enter dangerous combat alongside them and his determination to complete an objective.

Others consider his methods excessively aggressive and believe his operations expose personnel to unnecessary risk.

Diablo himself rarely appears concerned with his wider reputation.

## Leadership

Diablo leads from close to the battlefield.

Although the scale of divisional command prevents him from personally participating in every engagement, he prefers maintaining direct awareness of combat conditions rather than relying entirely upon information passed through several layers of command.

His leadership style emphasises momentum, aggression, and rapid exploitation of weaknesses within enemy positions.

Once an enemy formation begins to collapse, Diablo frequently attempts to prevent it from recovering by maintaining continuous pressure.

This approach can produce decisive victories.

It can also result in extremely costly engagements when an opponent refuses to break.

## Combat

Diablo is an experienced frontline combatant in addition to his responsibilities as a commander.

He is proficient with numerous conventional ballistic weapons and can adapt his equipment according to the requirements of an operation.

Unlike some notable soldiers who become closely associated with a single specialised weapon, Diablo maintains familiarity with a broad range of standard military equipment.

His combat style reflects his command philosophy.

He favours direct pressure, rapid movement between positions, heavy firepower, and aggressive engagement of hostile forces.

## Equipment

Diablo has access to the standard and specialised equipment available to senior Free World Order personnel.

His exact loadout varies according to mission requirements.

Ballistic firearms remain his primary weapons.

As a division commander, he also has access to command, communication, tactical coordination, and battlefield intelligence systems allowing him to direct forces while operating near active combat.

## Relationship with Onyx

Diablo serves under [[Onyx]], leader of the Free World Order.

The two occupy significantly different roles within the organisation.

Onyx is responsible for the broader leadership and direction of the Free World Order, while Diablo's authority is primarily military and operational.

Onyx's willingness to place an entire division under Diablo's command demonstrates considerable trust in his ability to conduct major military operations.

Diablo remains loyal to the Free World Order and its leadership.

## Hell Hosts

Diablo has fought forces associated with [[Hell Hosts]] during conflicts between the two factions.

His aggressive battlefield doctrine has made him particularly dangerous during direct military confrontations.

Hell Host intelligence identifies Diablo primarily as a conventional military threat rather than an infiltrator, assassin, or anomalous combatant.

His danger comes from both his individual combat ability and the substantial military force he is capable of commanding.

## Legacy

Diablo has become one of the Free World Order's most recognisable battlefield commanders.

His reputation is inseparable from the violent engagements he has led and the casualties associated with them.

While [[Onyx]] represents the leadership of the Free World Order as a whole, Diablo represents one of its most direct expressions of military force.

When his division is committed to a battlefield, the Free World Order is rarely intending to fight quietly.
