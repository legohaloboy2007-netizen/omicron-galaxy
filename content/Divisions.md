# Hell Hosts Divisions

The Hell Hosts military is organised into several specialised divisions, each responsible for a distinct role within the faction's military infrastructure.

Together, these divisions allow the Hell Hosts to project military power across the Omicron Galaxy, supporting everything from planetary invasions and large-scale fleet warfare to scientific research, internal security, and logistical support.

Each division maintains its own command structure, traditions, specialist personnel, and operational doctrine.

---

# Combat Divisions

## [[Doom Corps]]

The primary combat division and backbone of the Hell Hosts military.

Responsible for:

- Frontline infantry
- Planetary invasions
- Urban warfare
- Occupation operations
- Reconnaissance battalions
- Heavy assault operations

---

## [[Eradication Division]]

A specialised robotic assault division designed to overwhelm enemy positions through brute force and advanced autonomous warfare.

Responsible for:

- Combat robots
- Flamethrower units
- Heavy robotic assaults
- Objective destruction
- Hazard eradication
- High-risk combat operations

---

## [[Aviation Division]]

Responsible for all atmospheric and aerospace flight operations throughout the Hell Hosts military.

Responsible for:

- Fighter pilots
- Bomber crews
- Dropship pilots
- Transport aircraft
- Close air support
- Pilot training

Exceptional pilots may eventually earn assignment aboard one of the Hell Hosts' major fleets.

---

## [[Fire Division]]

Originally composed of veteran soldiers from both the **Free Order Rebellion** and the **Terrorism Control Agency**, the Fire Division was reorganised following the formation of the Hell Hosts.

Rather than maintaining another standard infantry force, these experienced soldiers were retrained as specialists in artillery, siege warfare, and heavy fire support.

Responsible for:

- Long-range artillery
- Siege warfare
- Defensive emplacements
- Rocket batteries
- Orbital fire coordination
- Heavy fire support

### Standard Equipment

**R96 Rotary Artillery Cannon (RAC)**

A rapid-fire artillery platform featuring four rotating barrels capable of sustained bombardment over large areas.

**445 Tributary Artillery Cannon (TAC)**

A heavy artillery platform designed for mobile siege warfare.

Equipped with a hover-track propulsion system, allowing it to traverse rivers, marshlands, shallow water, and difficult terrain with ease.

Features two operational modes:

- **Siege Mode** — Maximum destructive power against fortifications.
- **Gas Mode** — Deploys specialised chemical or suppression payloads across enemy positions.

The TAC delivers significantly greater destructive power than the R96, though at the cost of reduced mobility and a shorter effective range.

# Security & Protection Divisions

## [[Spectre Division]]

The military police and internal security division of the Hell Hosts.

Originally composed of repurposed robots from the FOR–TCA War, the division now consists of both robotic and human personnel.

Responsible for:

- Military policing
- Internal investigations
- Prison security
- Base security
- Law enforcement
- Military discipline

---

## [[Shock & Awe Division]]

An elite protection division responsible for safeguarding the Hell Hosts' highest-ranking personnel and most valuable strategic assets.

Responsible for:

- VIP protection
- Strategic asset security
- Diplomatic escorts
- Emergency response
- High-value convoy protection

Only the most exceptional personnel are selected to join the division.

---

# Support & Logistics Divisions

## [[RAM Division]]

**RAM (Robotic Assault Machines)** is responsible for operating and maintaining the largest robotic machinery used throughout the Hell Hosts military.

Responsible for:

- Heavy cargo transport
- Battlefield engineering
- Defensive machines
- Industrial support
- Heavy robotic assault platforms

---

## [[Research Division]]

The scientific and technological division of the Hell Hosts.

Responsible for researching, developing, and testing new military technologies, anomalous materials, experimental weaponry, and advanced scientific discoveries.

Responsible for:

- Element 95 research
- Axionium technology
- Experimental weapon development
- Reverse engineering
- Medical research
- Ascension studies
- Prototype equipment

---

# Notes

Every member of the Hell Hosts military belongs to one of these divisions.

Large military fleets, such as [[Fleet One]], are composed of personnel drawn from multiple divisions depending on operational requirements.

Each division possesses its own command structure, operational doctrine, traditions, insignia, and specialist units.
