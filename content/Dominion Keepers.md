# Dominion Keepers

The Dominion Keepers are one of the four dominant Void Civilizations inhabiting the [[Voidlands]], known for their colossal military infrastructure, authoritarian rule, and relentless pursuit of order through absolute control. Among the Void Civilizations, they possess the largest standing military force and are widely regarded as the most openly expansionist power within the region.

The civilization is centered around the massive citadel-world of [[Kron Vhaz'kar]], a fortress construct so immense it rivals the size of a small star. Built from black-gold voidsteel and surrounded by colossal defensive structures, the world serves as the political and military heart of the Dominion Keepers.

Unlike the philosophical isolation of [[The Anchorless Caste]] or the abstract pursuits of the [[Reality Weavers]], the Dominion Keepers believe the Void must be controlled, organized, and defended through strength. To them, chaos and instability are weaknesses that threaten the survival of existence itself.

The civilization is ruled by the Overlord Council, a governing body composed of ancient military rulers, war architects, and Void strategists. Under their command, the Dominion Keepers maintain enormous fleets, genetically engineered war-beasts, and highly disciplined armies equipped with advanced Void weaponry.

Their primary military-industrial world, [[Dreadmarch Bastion]], functions as a colossal foundry capable of producing armies, cloning battalions, and biomechanical war creatures at an industrial scale. Entire fleets can reportedly be prepared for war within days.

Dominion military doctrine revolves around overwhelming force, territorial control, and the rapid elimination of threats before instability can spread. Their elite soldiers, known as Dominion Void-Lancers, are considered among the deadliest military units within the Voidlands.

Despite their brutal methods, some civilizations within the Voidlands tolerate the Dominion Keepers due to their role in containing dangerous dimensional fractures and hostile anomalies.
