Overview

Alexia Vandrix, known across the [[Doom Corps]] as Doom, is a frontline Battalion Captain of the [[188th Siege Assault Battalion]], better known as the Iron Casters.

A member of [[House Vandrix]], Doom built her reputation independently through combat performance, battlefield reliability, and relentless assault command rather than family prestige.

She is not known for speeches.

She is not known for diplomacy.

She is known for advancing when others hesitate.

Where her elder brother [[Warden]] became respected for holding failing fronts together, and [[Deadeye]] became feared for impossible precision, Doom earned her name by breaking enemy positions through direct force.

Enemies coined the name first.

Hell Hosts simply kept it.

A common phrase associated with her states:

«“Where others fall back, Doom holds. Where others hesitate, Doom advances.”»

---

House Vandrix

[[House Vandrix]] is one of the old military families whose history predates the modern Hell Hosts.

The family is associated with discipline, duty, service, reliability, and battlefield endurance.

Doom represents one of the family’s most aggressive expressions of these values.

She does not interpret discipline as caution.

To Doom, discipline means advancing when ordered, holding formation under fire, and completing the breach no matter how terrifying the approach becomes.

House Vandrix carries a powerful reputation within the Hell Hosts, but Doom refuses to rely on that reputation.

She believes the Vandrix name should be earned repeatedly, not inherited passively.

To Doom, family legacy is not protection.

It is pressure.

A belief often associated with her states:

«“Vandrix is not a shield. It is a standard.”»

---

Family

Doom is one of the Vandrix siblings.

Known members of House Vandrix include:

- [[Warden]] — Brother
- [[Deadeye]] — Brother
- [[Hope Vandrix]] — Younger sibling
- [[War Vandrix]] — Younger sibling
- [[Red Vandrix]] — Younger sibling

Among her siblings, Doom is known for being direct, blunt, and difficult to soften once she has made up her mind.

She is fiercely loyal to her family, but not indulgent.

If anything, she is often harsher toward those carrying the Vandrix name because she refuses to let them become entitled.

Doom does not believe her siblings should be respected because they are Vandrix.

She believes they should be respected because they prove they deserve it.

---

Personality

Doom is quiet, disciplined, blunt, dependable, and intimidatingly focused.

She is not warm in the obvious sense.

She does not comfort easily, does not soften bad news, and does not waste time dressing up criticism. Her words are usually brief, direct, and difficult to misinterpret.

Common traits include:

- Quiet
- Blunt
- Highly disciplined
- Dependable
- Protective of subordinates
- Intolerant of cowardice
- Aggressive under pressure
- Strict with family members
- Leads from the front
- Secretly generous off-duty
- Difficult to overrule once committed

Doom does care about her soldiers.

She simply does not show it through warmth.

She shows it by going first.

---

Secret Generosity

Off-duty, Doom is quietly more generous than most people realise.

She rarely announces it and almost never accepts thanks.

Known examples include:

- Replacing damaged equipment for soldiers without taking credit
- Sending better supplies to exhausted squads
- Covering costs for repairs or medical needs
- Quietly helping younger troops adjust after brutal deployments
- Making sure wounded soldiers are not forgotten by command paperwork

If confronted, Doom usually denies involvement or changes the subject.

To her, generosity does not need witnesses.

A soldier once claimed Doom paid for half his squad’s replacement gear after a failed breach.

When asked about it, Doom reportedly replied:

«“Then they should stop losing gear.”»

The replacement gear arrived anyway.

---

Biggest Flaw

Doom struggles to stop once committed.

This is the most dangerous part of her command style.

Once she has begun an assault, she finds withdrawal extremely difficult to accept. To Doom, stopping mid-push can feel like betraying the soldiers who have already fallen getting that far.

This flaw has won battles.

It has also nearly cost lives.

Her refusal to retreat is not simple recklessness. Doom understands risk, casualties, and battlefield cost. But when she believes momentum is the only thing keeping her soldiers alive, she will push harder than most commanders would dare.

This sometimes places her in direct disagreement with more cautious leaders such as [[Warden]].

Where Warden may see a necessary withdrawal, Doom may see one final chance to break the enemy line.

Neither is always wrong.

That is what makes their disagreements dangerous.

---

Leadership Style

Doom leads from the front.

She does not send soldiers into breaches she would not enter herself.

Her command style is built around pressure, momentum, and presence. She believes that in assault warfare, hesitation spreads faster than fear, and soldiers need to see their commander moving forward with them.

Doom expects her troops to be disciplined, aggressive, and prepared for close combat.

Under her command, soldiers are expected to:

- Advance under fire
- Maintain formation during breaches
- Clear fortified interiors
- Keep momentum through resistance
- Protect wounded comrades without stalling the assault
- Follow orders quickly
- Hold captured ground
- Avoid unnecessary hesitation
- Trust the soldier beside them
- Finish the push once committed

She does not give orders to be heard.

She gives them to be done.

188th Siege Assault Battalion

The [[188th Siege Assault Battalion]], better known as the Iron Casters, is a [[Doom Corps]] siege-assault battalion commanded by Doom.

The Iron Casters specialise in fortress breaking, breach warfare, urban assault, and siege follow-through.

They are not a pure artillery formation.

Instead, they are the troops sent in after artillery, orbital strikes, armour, or heavy weapons crack open enemy defences.

Their role is to enter the breach, clear resistance, secure the objective, and hold the position until reinforcements or occupation forces arrive.

The Iron Casters specialise in:

- Siege-assault operations
- Fortress clearing
- Urban warfare
- Breach follow-through
- Counteroffensives
- Stronghold entry
- Close-quarters assault
- Defended corridor fighting
- Capturing fortified interiors
- Holding newly opened breaches

A common description of the battalion states:

«“The Iron Casters enter after the walls crack.”»

---

Morvannis

The Iron Casters became legendary after their role in cracking the Angel Sword fortress on [[Morvannis]].

During the campaign, [[Fire Division]] bombardment using [[TAC Cannon|TAC]] and [[RAC Cannon|RAC]] artillery systems shattered major sections of the fortress defences. However, the bombardment alone did not secure victory.

Someone still had to enter.

The Iron Casters advanced through the damaged fortress lines, fighting through broken walls, collapsed corridors, fortified interiors, and surviving Angel Sword defenders.

Their assault helped secure the fortress and cemented the battalion’s reputation as one of the Doom Corps’ premier siege-assault formations.

The walls broke first.

The Iron Casters ended what remained inside.

---

Combat Role

Doom specialises in aggressive battlefield control and frontline assault command.

Her combat role includes:

- Shock infantry pushes
- Urban breach operations
- Fortress assaults
- Counteroffensives
- Battalion morale stabilisation
- Close-quarters command
- Breach route control
- Assault momentum preservation
- Frontline presence
- Siege follow-through

Unlike Warden, Doom does not primarily stabilise a line through command from behind it.

She stabilises it by moving forward until everyone else follows.

Unlike Deadeye, she does not seek distant certainty.

She prefers close certainty.

If Doom is present, the battle is likely about to become direct.

---

Combat Style

Doom’s combat style is controlled aggression.

She is not careless.

She is not chaotic.

She is not a berserker.

She advances because the enemy position must break, and she refuses to let fear or hesitation steal momentum from the assault.

Doom fights best in:

- Breaches
- Streets
- Fortified interiors
- Collapsed structures
- Defensive corridors
- Frontline pushes
- Chaotic close-range engagements

She is especially effective when soldiers around her begin to waver.

Her presence turns fear into motion.

If Doom advances, her troops advance.

If Doom holds, the line holds.

Weapons

94-CAR Assault Rifle

The [[94-CAR Assault Rifle]] is Doom’s primary battlefield weapon.

Reliable, adaptable, and effective across multiple combat ranges, the 94-CAR suits her role as a frontline assault commander.

She favours it because it performs consistently during chaotic pushes, urban fighting, and breach operations.

---

P2PSP Particle Sidearm

The [[P2PSP Particle Sidearm]] is Doom’s secondary weapon.

Compact and high-penetration, it is designed for armour-piercing sidearm use and close-quarters lethality.

Doom uses it when fighting in confined environments, during weapon transitions, or when the battle collapses into close-range chaos.

---

Pre-Assault Habit

Before an assault, Doom personally checks her first magazine.

Every time.

It is a small ritual, but one her soldiers know well.

She does not do it because she distrusts armourers or supply crews. She does it because the first shots matter, and because she believes commanders should take personal responsibility before asking others to follow them into danger.

When possible, Doom also walks the breach route herself before sending troops through it.

If the route is too dangerous to inspect safely, she often becomes the first through it.

This habit has earned fierce loyalty from her troops.

They know Doom will not ask them to enter a place she is unwilling to face herself.

---

Relationship with Warden

Doom and [[Warden]] share deep mutual respect, but frequently disagree on method.

Warden values discipline through restraint, stability, and holding ground.

Doom values discipline through momentum, pressure, and forward movement.

Their disagreements are rarely emotional, but they can be intense.

Warden may order a withdrawal to preserve the battalion.

Doom may argue that one more push could break the enemy entirely.

Both are capable commanders.

Both have been right.

Both have been wrong.

This tension defines much of their relationship.

Despite disagreements, Doom respects Warden’s experience and understands why soldiers trust him.

Warden respects Doom’s courage and effectiveness, even when her refusal to stop concerns him.

He holds the line.

She breaks it.

---

Relationship with Deadeye

Doom and [[Deadeye]] understand one another through action more than conversation.

Deadeye’s precision and Doom’s aggression complement each other naturally.

During operations, Deadeye clears threats from distance while Doom advances through the breach. She trusts him to remove targets she may never see, and he trusts her to keep moving once the way is opened.

Their relationship is quiet, practical, and deeply reliable.

Doom rarely asks Deadeye if a path is clear.

If he has not warned her, she assumes it is.

That is trust by Vandrix standards.

---

Relationship with Younger Vandrix Members

Doom is especially strict toward younger members of [[House Vandrix]].

She refuses to allow the family name to become an excuse for entitlement, arrogance, or laziness.

Younger Vandrix soldiers are often judged more harshly by Doom than ordinary recruits, not because she dislikes them, but because she believes they carry a name that must be earned every day.

She teaches them that legacy is not armour.

It will not stop a bullet.

It will not win a battle.

It will not excuse failure.

To Doom, the only thing the Vandrix name gives someone is a greater responsibility to prove they deserve it.

---

Relationship with the Iron Casters

Doom’s relationship with the [[188th Siege Assault Battalion]] is built on trust forged under extreme pressure.

The Iron Casters do not follow her because she is pleasant.

They follow her because she goes first.

Her soldiers know she is demanding, blunt, and often difficult, but they also know she will not abandon them in the breach.

Among the Iron Casters, Doom’s presence means the assault is not over.

Even when morale collapses, her troops often rally simply because she is still moving.

A common saying within the battalion states:

«“If Doom is still standing, the breach is still open.”»

---

Reputation

Among Troops

Among Doom Corps soldiers, Doom is respected as a commander who leads from the front and protects subordinates fiercely.

She is not easy to serve under, but her troops trust her.

A common belief among soldiers states:

«“If Doom is here, we’re pushing forward.”»

Among Officers

Among officers, Doom is known as difficult to overrule once committed.

Her battlefield judgement is respected, but her refusal to stop can frustrate commanders who prefer cautious withdrawals or slower planning.

She is often described as dangerous, effective, and stubborn.

Among Enemies

Among enemies, Doom’s reputation is simple.

By the time they know she has arrived, it is usually too late.

Her presence means the breach will be tested.

Then widened.

Then taken.

---

Legacy

Doom’s legacy is built around assault, momentum, and refusal.

She is not the commander sent to negotiate.

She is not the commander sent to wait.

She is the commander sent when the wall has cracked and someone must step through first.

For [[House Vandrix]], Doom represents disciplined aggression.

For the [[Doom Corps]], she represents the truth that sometimes the line is not protected by standing still.

Sometimes it is protected by moving forward before the enemy can recover.
