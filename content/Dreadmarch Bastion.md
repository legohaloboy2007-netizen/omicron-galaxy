# Dreadmarch Bastion

## The Military Foundry World of the Dominion Keepers

Dreadmarch Bastion is the primary military-industrial world of the [[Dominion Keepers]] and one of the most heavily weaponized forge-planets within the [[Voidlands]].

Where [[Kron Vhaz’kar]] serves as the seat of command and dominion, Dreadmarch Bastion exists for one purpose only:

War.

The entire world functions as a colossal production engine dedicated to the creation of:
- armies
- war-beasts
- Void weaponry
- armored fleets
- cloning legions
- and siege constructs

Within the Dominion Keepers, Dreadmarch is often referred to simply as:

The Forge of Conquest.

## Environment

Dreadmarch Bastion is a massive industrial nightmare covered by:
- endless forge-citadels
- cloning complexes
- volcanic foundries
- war-factories
- ash-choked wastelands
- colossal military shipyards
- beast containment pits
- and fortress manufactorums

The skies are permanently obscured by:
- industrial smoke
- forge-fire
- gravitational storms
- and atmospheric ash clouds

The planet never truly sleeps.

Entire continents glow from continuous weapons production and industrial activity.

## Purpose

Dreadmarch Bastion exists as the primary military production center of the Dominion Keepers.

The world manufactures:
- Voidsteel armor
- heavy warships
- siege engines
- biological war-beasts
- cloning legions
- dimensional artillery
- anti-Void weapon systems

Most Dominion military campaigns originate from Dreadmarch production sectors.

## The Creature-Forges

One of the planet’s most feared features are the:

Creature-Forges

Immense bio-industrial complexes where engineered war organisms are created through:
- accelerated growth systems
- genetic reconstruction
- Void adaptation
- biological weapon engineering
- and neural imprint conditioning

The Creature-Forges are capable of producing:
entire armies within hours.

Known creations include:
- siege beasts
- Void-adapted predators
- armored bio-titans
- assault organisms
- and partially synthetic war entities

Many civilizations consider the Forges morally abhorrent.

The Dominion Keepers consider them necessary.

## Cloning Vats

Beneath the forge districts lie colossal:

Cloning Vats

These systems mass-produce:
- infantry
- support crews
- military specialists
- and genetically optimized soldiers

Unlike mindless drones, many cloned troops retain:
- tactical intelligence
- combat memory imprinting
- and partial individuality

The Keepers believe loyalty can be manufactured as efficiently as steel.

## Titan Pits

Massive containment sectors known as:

Titan Pits

hold the largest war-beasts created by the Dominion.

These creatures are engineered for:
- fortress assault
- Void warfare
- planetary invasion
- dimensional breach combat

Some Titans rival small cities in size.

The Pits are guarded constantly due to repeated containment failures.

## Dominion Void-Lancers

Dreadmarch Bastion is best known for producing the elite military forces called:

Dominion Void-Lancers

The Void-Lancers are among the most feared troops within the Voidlands.

They specialize in:
- rapid breach warfare
- anti-anomaly combat
- dimensional assault
- elite spearhead operations

Their equipment includes:
- Voidsteel war armor
- dimensional lances
- gravity disruptors
- phase shields
- and neural synchronization systems

Void-Lancers are deployed only during critical campaigns.

## The March Engines

Gigantic industrial constructs known as:

March Engines

move continuously across portions of the planet’s surface.

These mobile forge-citadels:
- manufacture weapons while advancing
- process raw material extraction
- deploy military forces directly
- and reshape terrain for industrial expansion

Some March Engines are centuries old.

## Internal Hierarchy

Dreadmarch Bastion operates under rigid military-industrial doctrine.

Its ruling structure consists of:
- Forge Overseers
- Beastmasters
- War Architects
- Legion Marshals
- and Dominion Commanders

Failure within production sectors is punished severely.

Efficiency is considered sacred.

## Relationship to the Voidlands

The Dominion Keepers use Dreadmarch Bastion to maintain military superiority over:
- rogue Void entities
- cult factions
- instability zones
- and rival civilizations within the [[Voidlands]]

Many believe the world is preparing for:
a future war on a scale not yet seen.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Dreadmarch Bastion is known as:
- the military forge-world of the Dominion Keepers
- one of the largest war-production planets in existence
- a world where armies are manufactured like machinery
- the birthplace of the Void-Lancers

Among Dominion military personnel, one phrase is repeated constantly:

> “Steel wins battles.  
> Production wins eternity.”
