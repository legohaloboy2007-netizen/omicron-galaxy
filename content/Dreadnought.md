# Dreadnought

> "I don't ask if we can win. I decide that we will."

## Overview

**Adrian Virek**, known by the callsign **Dreadnought**, is a senior Hell Host naval commander serving as Admiral of [[Fleet 3]].

A veteran of large-scale orbital warfare, Adrian is responsible for the strategic deployment, coordination, and combat effectiveness of one of the Hell Hosts' major fleet formations.

Dreadnought is known for aggressive fleet doctrine, overwhelming force concentration, and a willingness to commit enormous military resources when he believes decisive victory is achievable.

He does not approach warfare cautiously.

He approaches it deliberately.

Once Adrian commits Fleet 3 to an engagement, he intends to end the conflict on terms favourable to the Hell Hosts.

## Appearance

Adrian is a tall, heavily built human male with dark hair and a visibly weathered appearance.

He typically wears reinforced fleet-command armour rather than a conventional naval uniform.

The armour combines command-system integration with enough protection for operations aboard threatened capital ships or contested orbital installations.

Its white, black, and crimson markings identify him as a senior Fleet 3 officer.

Adrian's command equipment is built around:

- Fleet communications
- Tactical displays
- Orbital tracking
- Ship coordination
- Command authorization
- Emergency combat protection

Dreadnought appears more comfortable on a command deck than in ceremonial settings.

---

## Personality

Adrian is commanding, decisive, aggressive, and uncompromising.

He possesses enormous confidence in his own strategic ability.

This confidence is not entirely arrogance.

His record gives him reasons to trust his judgement.

Dreadnought believes hesitation at fleet scale can be catastrophic.

A delayed strike allows enemy ships to reposition.

A missed opportunity allows reinforcements to arrive.

A weak response encourages further resistance.

Because of this, Adrian prefers decisive action once sufficient information exists.

He can be ruthless when necessary, particularly against enemy military forces he believes will continue fighting unless completely broken.

However, he is not careless.

Adrian's aggression is usually calculated.

He does not throw ships away simply to appear powerful.

He wants total victory because partial victory often means fighting the same war twice.

# Fleet 3

Dreadnought commands **Fleet 3**, one of the Hell Hosts' major naval formations.

Fleet 3 is built for large-scale strategic warfare and may participate in:

- System invasions
- Orbital superiority operations
- Planetary assault support
- Fleet interception
- Capital-ship engagements
- Blockades
- Strategic reinforcement
- High-value target destruction

The fleet contains a mixture of:

- Capital ships
- Cruisers
- Destroyers
- Carriers
- Escort vessels

Adrian's responsibility is not simply commanding individual vessels.

He commands the entire formation as one strategic instrument.

# Rise to Admiral

Adrian rose through Hell Host naval command through a combination of ambition, battlefield performance, and strategic intelligence.

He demonstrated particular skill during large fleet engagements where rapidly changing conditions required immediate decisions.

By the age of thirty-eight, he had been given command of Fleet 3 following the **Korvus Crusade**.

His performance during the campaign established him as one of the Hell Hosts' most important naval commanders.

Since then, Fleet 3 has participated in numerous major operations across multiple systems.

---

# The Korvus Crusade

The **Korvus Crusade** became one of the defining campaigns of Adrian's career.

Although the full history of the conflict remains to be expanded, the campaign involved prolonged fleet warfare and major strategic pressure against Hell Host forces.

Adrian's performance during the conflict demonstrated his ability to coordinate large formations under extreme pressure.

His actions contributed heavily to the eventual Hell Host victory.

Following the campaign, he was promoted to command Fleet 3.

Dreadnought rarely discusses Korvus casually.

The campaign appears to have shaped much of his later command philosophy.

---

# Fleet Doctrine

Adrian believes fleet warfare is decided by concentration of force and destruction of enemy options.

His doctrine revolves around:

- Overwhelming local superiority
- Rapid target prioritization
- Command disruption
- Aggressive positioning
- Maintaining initiative
- Cutting reinforcement routes
- Eliminating enemy retreat options

He rarely attempts to destroy every hostile ship individually.

Instead, he attacks the structure holding the enemy fleet together.

Command vessels.

Carriers.

Supply ships.

Communications hubs.

Orbital infrastructure.

Remove enough of those, and the rest of the fleet becomes significantly easier to defeat.

---

# Orbital Warfare

Dreadnought is highly experienced in fighting across planetary orbital space.

These operations may involve:

- Orbital defence platforms
- Planetary weapons
- Station assaults
- Carrier battles
- Atmospheric support
- Civilian orbital infrastructure
- Ground-force coordination

Adrian understands that orbital warfare cannot be separated entirely from what happens on the planet below.

Destroy the wrong infrastructure and ground forces may lose critical support.

Fail to destroy enough and the invasion may never reach the surface.

Fleet 3 therefore operates in close coordination with Hell Host ground command during major planetary campaigns.

---

# Strategic Deployment

One of Adrian's greatest strengths is knowing **where** Fleet 3 needs to be before battle begins.

He constantly considers:

- Enemy fleet locations
- Reinforcement times
- Fuel requirements
- Orbital geography
- Jump routes
- Supply corridors
- Friendly fleet availability

A fleet arriving several hours too late may be strategically useless.

Dreadnought treats positioning as seriously as firepower.

---

# Planetary Assault Oversight

Fleet 3 frequently supports Hell Host planetary invasions.

Dreadnought may oversee:

- Orbital fire support
- Transport deployment
- Fighter cover
- Landing-zone suppression
- Reinforcement corridors
- Enemy fleet interception

Adrian does not directly command ground formations, but his decisions can determine whether those formations ever reach the battlefield.

He therefore maintains strong coordination with senior ground officers.

---

# High-Value Target Authorization

As Admiral of Fleet 3, Adrian possesses authority to approve the destruction of strategically significant enemy assets.

These may include:

- Capital ships
- Orbital stations
- Military shipyards
- Command facilities
- Strategic weapons platforms

Dreadnought takes these decisions seriously.

Large-scale destruction may be tactically easy and strategically disastrous.

He asks whether destroying something improves the war.

If the answer is yes, he rarely hesitates.

# Command Style

Dreadnought commands through clear intent and aggressive tempo.

He expects captains beneath him to understand the strategic objective rather than wait for instructions covering every detail.

This allows Fleet 3 to react quickly during chaotic engagements.

However, Adrian has little tolerance for hesitation once orders are given.

He believes decentralized execution only works if everyone understands when action is required.

Fleet 3 officers learn quickly:

If Dreadnought says **move**, he already expects the fleet to be moving.

# Personal Combat Ability

Dreadnought is physically capable and trained in standard Hell Host combat doctrine.

However, his true threat is strategic rather than personal.

An enemy does not need to fear Adrian because he carries a rifle.

They need to fear him because he can command dozens of warships to focus fire on the same location.

His **Extreme** threat classification reflects command authority as much as individual capability.
