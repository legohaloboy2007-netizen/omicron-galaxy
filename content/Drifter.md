# Drifter

> "Assignment is temporary. Results aren't."

## Overview

**Kael Rourke**, known by the callsign **Drifter**, is a Hell Host reconnaissance and unconventional warfare specialist serving within the [[Doom Corps]].

Unlike most Doom Corps personnel, Kael possesses no permanent unit assignment.

Instead, he operates through rotational deployments, temporary attachments, and independent missions requiring extended survival behind hostile lines.

Drifter specializes in deep reconnaissance, solo infiltration, long-duration operations, survival and evasion, and unconventional combat.

His reputation is unusual.

Command considers him highly effective.

Command also considers him extremely difficult to manage.

Kael rarely approaches the same problem twice in the same way and frequently abandons established plans when battlefield conditions change.

The results have repeatedly justified allowing him to continue.

For some officers, that is precisely the problem.

## Appearance

Kael is a tall, heavily conditioned human male equipped for extended independent operations.

He typically wears adaptable black Doom Corps combat armour modified heavily around mobility, survival, concealment, and equipment capacity.

Unlike standardized unit configurations, Drifter's armour changes frequently depending upon assignment.

Common features include:

- Modular storage
- Environmental protection
- Tactical visor systems
- Noise reduction
- Lightweight armour plating
- Reconnaissance equipment
- Adaptive camouflage technology

Kael's helmet uses a dark enclosed visor and minimal external identification.

His equipment rarely remains factory-standard for long.

If something can be made more useful, Kael usually modifies it.

---

## Personality

Drifter is independent, sarcastic, adaptable, and deceptively relaxed.

He rarely appears hurried.

Even during dangerous situations, Kael often behaves as though he has significantly more time than everyone around him.

This attitude occasionally irritates personnel unfamiliar with him.

Underneath the relaxed exterior, however, Drifter is highly calculating.

He constantly evaluates:

- Escape routes
- Terrain
- Enemy movement
- Available equipment
- Environmental hazards
- Alternative objectives

Kael dislikes rigid thinking more than he dislikes authority itself.

He follows orders.

What he does not always follow is the original method.

If circumstances change, Drifter expects the plan to change with them.

His philosophy can be summarized simply:

**Complete the objective, not the briefing.**

---

# Unassigned Operative

Drifter's lack of permanent assignment is deliberate.

Earlier attempts to keep Kael inside conventional formations produced mixed results.

His combat performance remained excellent.

His compatibility with rigid command structures did not.

Eventually, Doom Corps command began deploying him through temporary attachments instead.

Kael may operate alongside one unit for several weeks before being reassigned to an entirely different theatre.

Other missions are conducted completely alone.

This arrangement allows Hell Host command to place Drifter wherever his unusual skill set is most useful without forcing him permanently into a structure that restricts his effectiveness.

Kael strongly prefers the arrangement.

---

# Rotational Deployment

Drifter can be attached temporarily to:

- Reconnaissance formations
- Assault units
- Special operations teams
- Expeditionary forces
- Search-and-recovery operations
- Counter-insurgency missions
- Frontier deployments

His role changes depending upon circumstances.

On one operation he may scout an enemy position.

On another he may spend days behind hostile lines disrupting logistics.

On another he may simply survive somewhere long enough to determine what happened to everyone sent before him.

This operational flexibility is one of Kael's greatest strengths.

---

# Deep Reconnaissance

Drifter is highly experienced in operating far beyond conventional Hell Host support.

His reconnaissance missions may last days or weeks.

During these operations, Kael gathers information concerning:

- Enemy installations
- Troop movements
- Supply routes
- Defensive systems
- Terrain
- Patrol behaviour
- Potential infiltration routes

Unlike conventional recon teams, Drifter frequently works alone.

This reduces his operational signature but places significantly greater pressure on self-sufficiency.

---

# Long-Duration Operations

Kael is trained to survive for extended periods without resupply.

He carefully manages:

- Food
- Water
- Ammunition
- Power
- Medical supplies
- Equipment wear

Drifter avoids unnecessary engagements during these missions.

Every bullet fired creates noise.

Every injury consumes medical supplies.

Every damaged component becomes another problem he must solve alone.

Survival is not separate from the mission.

It is part of it.

---

# Survival and Evasion

Kael possesses extensive training in survival and escape techniques.

His skills include:

- Improvised shelter
- Navigation
- Field medicine
- Resource conservation
- Tracking avoidance
- Environmental camouflage
- Pursuit disruption
- Improvised equipment repair

Drifter is particularly difficult to track because he rarely maintains predictable routes.

If pursued, he intentionally changes:

- Direction
- Travel speed
- Terrain type
- Movement schedule

He does not simply hide his trail.

He attempts to make the trail meaningless.

---

# Unconventional Warfare

Drifter frequently uses methods outside standard infantry doctrine.

These may include:

- Sabotage
- Ambushes
- Equipment theft
- Misdirection
- Improvised traps
- Supply disruption
- Enemy equipment reuse
- Communication interference

Kael prefers exploiting whatever is available rather than relying exclusively upon his assigned loadout.

An enemy vehicle is transportation.

An abandoned weapon is ammunition.

A hostile communication network is intelligence.

Drifter views the battlefield as a collection of resources.

Some simply belong to the enemy temporarily.

---

# Adaptive Combat

Kael is trained across multiple engagement styles rather than specializing entirely in one.

He can transition between:

- Long-range precision fire
- Mid-range rifle combat
- Infiltration
- Close-range engagement
- Survival combat

Drifter's greatest strength is not mastery of a single method.

It is recognizing when that method should change.

---

# Combat Style

Kael avoids fighting fairly whenever possible.

He does not interpret this as dishonourable.

He interprets it as sensible.

Drifter prefers:

- Surprise
- Terrain advantage
- Concealment
- Distance
- Controlled withdrawal
- Re-engagement from another position

If an enemy has prepared for the way Kael fought them previously, he will simply fight differently.

Predictability gets people killed.

- Reliability

He considers standard configurations suggestions.

Drifter rarely carries it when mobility matters more than range.

---

Drifter carries a standard combat knife for close-range defence and survival work.

# Adaptive Cloak System

Kael's armour contains a limited-use adaptive camouflage system.

The cloak can significantly reduce his visual profile for short periods.

It is primarily used during:

- Infiltration
- Observation
- Emergency evasion
- Crossing exposed terrain

The system is not permanent invisibility.

Power consumption, movement, environmental conditions, and sensor technology all reduce its effectiveness.

Kael therefore treats the cloak as another tool rather than relying upon it.

---

# Tactical Visor

Drifter's helmet incorporates a configurable tactical HUD.

Depending upon assignment, it may provide:

- Night vision
- Thermal imaging
- Navigation
- Rangefinding
- Environmental data
- Target tracking

Kael deliberately keeps automated guidance limited.

He prefers equipment that provides information without attempting to make decisions for him.

# Relationship with Haze

Drifter works surprisingly well with [[Haze|Nyra Voss]].

Their methods complement one another.

Haze deliberately manipulates battlefield conditions.

Kael adapts to whatever those conditions become.

Smoke, obscured routes, and environmental confusion provide Drifter with additional opportunities for unconventional movement.

Their trust is highly situational but effective.

Kael once summarized their partnership:

> "You plan everything. I improvise everything."

Haze did not consider this criticism.

---

# Relationship with Vantage

[[Vantage|Victor Rhenford]] occasionally provides Drifter with long-range overwatch.

The arrangement is efficient.

Vantage establishes observation and firing positions while Kael moves through the environment below.

Their operational philosophies differ considerably.

Victor prefers preparation and observation.

Kael prefers movement and adaptation.

Drifter has repeatedly told Vantage:

> "You stay still too long."

Victor has repeatedly ignored him.

---

# Relationship with Nexus

[[Nexus|Samuel Ardyn]] finds Drifter professionally irritating.

Kael's movement patterns are inconsistent, his routes change unexpectedly, and his communication may become intermittent during deep operations.

This makes him unusually difficult to integrate into predictive battlefield models.

Nexus prefers knowing where every friendly operative is.

Drifter considers remaining in one predictable location dangerous.

Kael once told Samuel:

> "You can't map someone who doesn't stay in one place."

Nexus reportedly replied that this was not how maps worked.

---

# Relationship with Command

Hell Host command has a complicated relationship with Drifter.

He is highly effective.

He is also difficult to incorporate into conventional structures.

Kael has ignored recommended routes, abandoned planned positions, modified objectives in the field, and returned through extraction points nobody expected him to use.

Yet his missions repeatedly succeed.

As one command officer reportedly summarized:

> "He gets results. That's the problem."

Drifter is therefore granted more operational freedom than most personnel of comparable rank.

That freedom is conditional.

If Kael stops producing results, command will stop tolerating his methods.

He understands this perfectly.

---

# Callsign

The callsign **Drifter** originated from Kael's habit of moving between assignments, units, and operational theatres without establishing a permanent place within any of them.

The name also reflects his battlefield behaviour.

Kael rarely remains in one location longer than necessary.

He moves.

Observes.

Adapts.

Leaves.

His own interpretation is simpler:

> "I don't stay in one place, and neither do the dead."

# Reputation

Within the Doom Corps, Drifter has developed a reputation as the operative assigned when nobody is entirely sure what the mission will look like by the time somebody reaches it.

He is difficult to categorize.

Reconnaissance personnel consider him too aggressive.

Assault troops consider him too evasive.

Command considers him unreliable.

His mission record disagrees.

Kael seems perfectly comfortable remaining difficult to define.
