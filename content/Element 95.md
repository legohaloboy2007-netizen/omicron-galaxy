# Element 95

## Overview

Element 95 is an anomalous reality-reactive substance considered one of the most dangerous discoveries in the history of the [[Omicron Galaxy]].

Unlike conventional matter, Element 95 exhibits properties that violate known physical laws, destabilize dimensional boundaries, alter biological life, and interact directly with consciousness itself.

Exposure to Element 95 has been linked to:
- planetary corruption
- dimensional instability
- Ascension Events
- mass mutation
- Veil manifestations
- psychological deterioration
- and large-scale reality collapse

Entire worlds have fallen following uncontrolled exposure to the substance.

Among scientists, soldiers, cults, and ancient civilizations alike, Element 95 is regarded not merely as a dangerous material—

But as something fundamentally incompatible with reality itself.

## Nature

Element 95 does not behave like a conventional element.

Though often classified as a mineral, crystalline substance, or energy-reactive compound, no universal scientific explanation has successfully defined its true nature.

The substance appears to exist simultaneously as:
- matter
- energy
- dimensional instability
- and partially reactive consciousness

Element 95 has demonstrated the ability to:
- alter surrounding environments
- react to nearby thought and emotion
- corrupt living organisms
- weaken dimensional boundaries
- and reshape physical reality itself

The substance frequently behaves unpredictably under observation.

Different samples may:
- change form
- emit impossible energy readings
- display biological growth
- or exhibit completely contradictory properties.

## Discovery

Ancient records from the [[The Elysarian Dominion|Elysarian Dominion]] suggest Element 95 was encountered thousands of years before the modern era during the beginning of the:

[[Ancient War Against Element 95]]

At the time, entire systems experienced:
- mutations
- dimensional tears
- disappearing settlements
- corrupted storms
- and reality instability

The Dominion ultimately succeeded in containing many outbreaks and sealing corrupted regions, though much of their research was intentionally hidden afterward.

In the modern era, Element 95 resurfaced following exploration into:
- the [[Farlands]]
- the [[Voidlands]]
- and unstable regions connected to [[The Veil]]

Its rediscovery permanently altered galactic history.

## Corruption

Prolonged exposure to Element 95 results in progressive corruption affecting:
- biology
- psychology
- perception
- and surrounding reality

Known corruption symptoms include:
- crystalline growth
- tissue mutation
- hallucinations
- emotional instability
- memory fragmentation
- violent behavior
- consciousness distortion
- and physical restructuring

Environmental exposure may also produce:
- shifting terrain
- gravity distortion
- atmospheric instability
- living organic structures
- and dimensional fractures

On heavily contaminated worlds, corruption can spread across entire ecosystems.

## The Veil Connection

Element 95 is heavily associated with:
[[The Veil]]

Many researchers believe the substance weakens the separation between normal reality and Veil-linked dimensions or consciousness states.

High concentrations of Element 95 frequently result in:
- Veil manifestations
- psychic anomalies
- consciousness overlap
- hallucinations
- and contact with entities linked to [[The Witness]]

Some corrupted individuals report:
hearing voices,
seeing impossible structures,
or perceiving realities beyond normal existence.

## Ascension Events

Element 95 is one of the primary known causes of:

[[Ascension Events]]

Most exposure results in death, mutation, or complete psychological collapse.

However, in extremely rare cases, individuals survive the process and undergo catastrophic transformation beyond normal biological limits.

These Ascensions often grant:
- immense physical power
- altered perception
- anomalous abilities
- partial connection to Veil consciousness
- and reality-altering influence

Known Ascended individuals linked to Element 95 include:
- [[Destiny]]
- [[Scyther]]

Ancient Elysarian records suggest controlled Ascension experimentation may have existed thousands of years ago through specialized chambers and containment systems.

## Known Forms

### Raw Element 95
Highly unstable naturally occurring corruption deposits.

---

### Refined 95
Processed forms utilized in experimentation, containment research, and weapon development.

---

### Dormant 95
Inactive or minimally reactive deposits capable of sudden reactivation.

---

### Veil-Linked 95
Extremely dangerous forms displaying strong consciousness-reactive behavior and direct association with [[The Veil]].

## Major Outbreak Zones

Known regions heavily affected by Element 95 include:
- [[Farlands]]
- [[Voidlands]]
- [[Vraelith 95]]
- [[Nyxaris]]
- [[Velkanar]]

Ancient records suggest many additional outbreak zones were intentionally erased from galactic archives.

## Modern Research

Despite the danger, numerous factions continue researching Element 95.

Known groups include:
- [[Obsidian Veil]]
- rogue scientific organizations
- cult factions
- black-market anomaly researchers
- and certain classified military divisions

Most large-scale experimentation eventually results in containment failure.

## Common Theories

The true origin of Element 95 remains unknown.

Popular theories include:
- an extradimensional substance
- a remnant of pre-universal reality
- a living cosmic organism
- a failed form of creation itself
- or a force connected directly to [[The Witness]]

No theory has been confirmed.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Element 95 is known as:
- the source of the Farlands corruption
- the catalyst of Ascension
- the foundation of modern Veil disasters
- and one of the greatest existential threats in known existence

Among Farlands survivors, one warning is repeated constantly:

> “The corruption does not spread through space.  
> It spreads through reality.”
