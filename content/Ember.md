# Ember Solis

## Overview

**Real Name:** Ember Solis

Ember Solis serves as the Captain of the Ashen Guard within the:

[[Ashen Kingdom]]

and is entrusted with the personal protection of the [[Blood|Ashen Lord]].

Widely regarded as one of the kingdom's finest warriors, Ember has spent her entire life defending the Ashen Kingdom and its people. Though often perceived as cold and intimidating, those who know her understand that her unwavering discipline is rooted not in arrogance, but in an immense sense of responsibility.

Among the Ashen Guard, her name has become synonymous with loyalty.

Few individuals have earned Blood's trust as completely as Ember.

Their relationship stretches back to childhood, long before Blood became the Ashen Lord, and many within the kingdom quietly regard them as family despite sharing no blood.

---

## Appearance

Ember stands approximately:

**5'10"**

with a tall, athletic build developed through decades of combat training.

### Notable Features

- Long white hair
- Amber-orange glowing eyes
- Ash-grey complexion
- Calm, unreadable expression
- Exceptional posture

Ember rarely displays emotion publicly.

Her neutral expression has become so well known that many soldiers believe she is incapable of smiling.

This assumption is incorrect.

She simply smiles very rarely.

### Armor

As Captain of the Ashen Guard, Ember wears ceremonial armor forged exclusively for the kingdom's royal protectors.

Her armor features:

- Dark volcanic steel
- Gold detailing
- Ember-forged plating
- Royal Ashen insignia
- Flowing ceremonial robes

Although elegant in appearance, every component is designed for active combat.

Nothing she wears exists purely for decoration.

---

## Personality

Ember is:

- Disciplined
- Reserved
- Loyal
- Observant
- Protective
- Quietly compassionate

She rarely wastes words and prefers actions over lengthy discussion.

Many first impressions paint her as cold or unapproachable.

Those who know her understand that she simply struggles to express affection openly.

Instead, she demonstrates care through reliability.

If Ember promises something, it happens.

### Sense of Humor

Although exceptionally serious, Ember possesses an unexpectedly dry sense of humor.

On the rare occasions she smiles, those nearby often remember the moment for years afterwards.

Within the Ashen Guard, it has become something of a joke that witnessing Ember laugh is considered good luck.

---

## History

Ember was born on:

[[Malgor]]

a volcanic mining world within the Ashen Kingdom.

Growing up among miners and craftsmen taught her discipline, patience, and resilience from an early age.

During childhood she became close friends with a young traveler named:

[[Blood]]

whose family frequently visited Malgor during their travels.

Although their lives eventually separated, they remained important figures in each other's memories.

Years later, following Blood's Ascension as the new Ashen Lord, the two unexpectedly reunited.

While the kingdom welcomed its new ruler, Ember found herself conflicted.

She respected the Ashen Lord completely.

Yet part of her quietly missed the friend she had once known.

Despite these feelings, she accepted her new responsibility without hesitation.

Following years of distinguished military service, Ember was appointed Captain of the Ashen Guard, becoming the foremost protector of the Ashen Lord and the royal household.

---

## Combat Style

Ember specializes in defensive warfare and elite close-quarters combat.

Unlike many warriors who overwhelm opponents through aggression alone, Ember controls battles through precision, positioning, and relentless pressure.

Every movement is deliberate.

Every strike serves a purpose.

Many duelists report feeling as though the battlefield itself slowly closes around them.

### Weapon

#### Ashen Spear

Ember wields an ancient spear forged within the volcanic forges of the Ashen Kingdom.

Dark metal runs throughout its shaft, broken by glowing lava-like fractures that radiate constant heat.

The weapon symbolizes both the strength and endurance of the Ashen people.

It has accompanied Ember throughout most of her military career.

---

## Relationships

### [[Blood]]

Ember's oldest and closest friend.

The two share a sibling-like bond formed long before Blood became the Ashen Lord.

Although Ember respects his authority completely, she refuses to treat him as anything other than the person she grew up alongside.

She remains one of the very few individuals who addresses him simply as:

**Blood**

rather than by title.

More than anything else, Ember wants to protect the person beneath the crown.

---

### [[Calypso]]

Ember deeply distrusts the Queen of the Ice Kingdom.

Partly because of the long-standing rivalry between their kingdoms.

Partly because she believes Calypso represents a danger to Blood.

Whether that concern is justified remains uncertain.

---

### [[Hell Hosts]]

Ember maintains a respectful but cautious opinion of Hell Hosts personnel.

Having spent her entire life within the Ashen Kingdom, she has had relatively little interaction with the organization.

While she acknowledges their accomplishments, her loyalty has always belonged first and foremost to her own people.

---

## Hobbies & Interests

### Weapons Training

Even outside official duties, Ember continues refining her skills.

She believes mastery is something that must be maintained rather than achieved.

---

### Watching the Night Sky

Late at night, Ember can often be found overlooking the volcanic landscape in complete silence.

Few people know this has become one of her favorite ways to relax.

---

### Annoying Blood

One of Ember's favorite pastimes.

Despite her otherwise serious demeanor, she quietly enjoys teasing Blood whenever the opportunity presents itself.

She rarely admits this.

---

## Habits

- Regularly inspects palace security routes.
- Stands rather than sits whenever possible.
- Automatically positions herself between Blood and unfamiliar visitors.
- Straightens Blood's cloak or armor before official ceremonies without thinking.
- Rarely removes her spear from within arm's reach.

## Flaws

### Self-Sacrifice

Ember has dedicated so much of her life to protecting Blood that she struggles to imagine a purpose beyond that responsibility.

She consistently places her own wellbeing beneath the safety of others.

Many fear she would willingly sacrifice herself without hesitation if it meant ensuring Blood survived.

---

## Reputation

### Within the Ashen Kingdom

Ember is regarded as one of the kingdom's greatest protectors.

Soldiers admire her discipline.

Citizens respect her devotion.

Children, surprisingly, seem entirely comfortable around her.

---

### Outside the Ashen Kingdom

Foreign diplomats often find Ember intimidating.

Enemy commanders recognize her as an exceptionally dangerous opponent capable of ending battles before they properly begin.
