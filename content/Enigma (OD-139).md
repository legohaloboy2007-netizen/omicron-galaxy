# Enigma

## Overview

**Designation:** OD-139

**Callsign:** Enigma

**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]

**Branch:** Doom Corps / Fleet Guard

**Rank:** Commander

**Classification:** Autonomous Combat Intelligence

**Threat Level:** Extreme

**Primary Weapons:** [[A12 Heavy Shotgun]], [[94-CAR Assault Rifle]]

**Secondary Weapons:** [[P2 Particle Systematic Pistol]], Combat Knife

OD-139, known across Hell Hosts as **Enigma**, is one of the most successful autonomous combat intelligences ever deployed by the faction.

Originally developed several years after the founding of Hell Hosts, Enigma was selected for active combat service after displaying exceptional adaptability, advanced emotional development, and unusually strong protective instincts toward human personnel.

The decision proved correct.

Today, Enigma is regarded as one of the most trusted officers within Hell Hosts service.

---

## Appearance

### Current Chassis

Enigma's standard combat frame is built around flexibility rather than overwhelming firepower.

Unlike larger synthetic units such as [[Viper (D3)|Viper]], Enigma's body was designed to operate comfortably alongside human soldiers.

### Physical Characteristics

- Black armored chassis
- Blue illuminated visor display
- Reinforced tactical combat frame
- Human-proportioned silhouette
- Integrated communications suite
- Modular combat systems

The glowing blue visor has become one of the most recognizable sights among Hell Hosts personnel.

Many soldiers claim that seeing Enigma arrive on a battlefield immediately improves morale.

---

## Personality

Enigma is often described as:

- Friendly
- Sarcastic
- Loyal
- Protective
- Observant
- Surprisingly empathetic

### Humor

Enigma uses humor constantly.

Partly to entertain people.

Partly to keep them calm.

Partly because he genuinely enjoys it.

Many recruits remember Enigma's jokes long after they remember mission briefings.

### Seriousness

When lives are in danger, the humor disappears immediately.

Enigma can transition from making jokes to issuing combat orders in seconds.

### Loyalty

Enigma's loyalty to Hell Hosts is unquestionable.

His loyalty to the people within Hell Hosts is even stronger.

---

## History

### Creation

OD-139 was developed as part of an advanced autonomous intelligence program several years after the founding of Hell Hosts.

Unlike many synthetic projects that remained in support roles, Enigma demonstrated exceptional battlefield potential.

Testing revealed:

- Advanced emotional intelligence
- Exceptional adaptability
- Rapid tactical processing
- Strong social awareness
- Independent decision-making

Deployment authorization was approved.

### Service Record

Since activation, Enigma has participated in countless operations throughout Hell Hosts history.

His actions have directly saved hundreds of personnel through:

- Battlefield intervention
- Tactical guidance
- Casualty extraction
- Defensive actions
- Emergency evacuations

Many soldiers trust Enigma faster than they trust newly assigned officers.

---

## Core Transfer System

Unlike conventional synthetic intelligences, Enigma's consciousness is housed within a compact:

### Omega Core Chip

Located within a heavily protected cavity at the center of his chassis.

If Enigma's body is destroyed:

- The chip can be recovered
- Installed into reserve combat frames
- Integrated into compatible android bodies
- Temporarily connected to specialized exo-suits

Destroying Enigma's body does not necessarily destroy Enigma.

### Operational Reality

Enemy forces have successfully destroyed Enigma's chassis numerous times.

Enigma continues to return.

---

## Combat Style

Enigma specializes in aggressive support warfare.

Rather than operating independently, he prefers fighting alongside allied personnel.

### Specializations

- Breach operations
- Close-quarters combat
- Extraction missions
- Defensive engagements
- Tactical support
- Electronic warfare

### Combat Philosophy

Enigma prioritizes people over objectives whenever possible.

This mindset has occasionally frustrated commanders.

It has also saved countless lives.

### Rescue Operations

One of Enigma's most recognizable habits is personally recovering wounded personnel.

Many soldiers owe their lives to Enigma's refusal to leave people behind.

## Digital Warfare

Enigma possesses advanced battlefield intrusion capabilities.

### Capabilities

- Hacking hostile systems
- Overriding enemy robotics
- Battlefield surveillance
- Communications support
- Tactical data processing

### HUD Projection

One of Enigma's most unique features.

Functions include:

- Tactical updates
- Threat warnings
- Navigation support
- Communications
- Sarcastic commentary

## Humanity

Though synthetic, Enigma often displays behavior many consider deeply human.

He studies:

- Humor
- Relationships
- Psychology
- Culture
- Human history

Not because he was programmed to.

Because he wants to understand people.

### Personal Philosophy

Enigma believes people matter.

Even after they are gone.

This belief influences nearly everything he does.

---

## The List

Among Enigma's internal records exists a protected archive.

The contents are simple:

Names.

Thousands of names.

Every allied casualty Enigma personally witnessed or failed to save.

Each entry includes:

- Name
- Unit
- Operation
- Date of death

No protocol requires this archive.

Enigma maintains it voluntarily.

When questioned about the purpose of the list, his response is always:

> "Someone should remember them."
