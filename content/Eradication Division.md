# Eradication Division

> [!info] Division Profile  
> **Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]  
> **Type:** Robotic Assault / Extermination Division  
> **Status:** Active  
> **Primary Role:** Total target elimination and high-risk assault  
> **Secondary Roles:** Hazard eradication, siege breaking, target hunting, anti-anomaly combat, demolition, battlefield sterilisation  
> **Composition:** Robotic combat units supported by human technical and command personnel  
> **Motto:** _“If it exists, if it is a target, if it resists, it will be eradicated.”_  
> **Operational Status:** Restricted Deployment  
> **Common Colours:** Blood Red, Matte Black, Gunmetal, Hellfire Orange  
> **Insignia:** Red skull surrounded by angular eradication markings

---

## Overview

The **Eradication Division** is one of the most destructive specialised branches of the [[Hell Hosts]] military.

Unlike the [[Doom Corps]], which serves as the primary ground-combat backbone of the faction, Eradication is not designed to hold territory, police populations, or conduct routine military operations.

It exists for one purpose:

> **To completely destroy designated resistance.**

Eradication forces are deployed when conventional assault is considered inefficient, excessively dangerous, or incapable of eliminating a threat quickly enough.

Their targets may include:

- Heavily fortified military installations
    
- Entrenched enemy formations
    
- Contaminated combat zones
    
- Rogue war machines
    
- Hostile artificial intelligences
    
- Biological hazards
    
- Anomalous entities
    
- Ascended threats
    
- Enemy command structures
    
- Locations marked for complete destruction
    

The division is composed overwhelmingly of purpose-built robotic combat platforms known collectively as:

> **ER Units**

These machines are designed without fear, exhaustion, hesitation, or concern for personal survival.

Where conventional infantry may be suppressed, exhausted, or forced into retreat, Eradication machines continue advancing until:

- Their target is destroyed
    
- They are ordered to stop
    
- Or their chassis is physically incapable of further combat
    

Their defining doctrine is simple:

> **“If it exists, if it is a target, if it resists, it will be eradicated.”**

---

# Purpose

The Eradication Division is not deployed merely to win battles.

It is deployed to **end them**.

Its primary responsibilities include:

- Extermination operations
    
- Heavy robotic assault
    
- Fortification destruction
    
- Hazard removal
    
- Incendiary warfare
    
- Anti-armour operations
    
- High-risk objective destruction
    
- Hostile facility sterilisation
    
- Demolition
    
- Hunter-killer missions
    
- Extreme-environment operations
    
- Anti-anomaly operations
    
- Support against threats unsuitable for ordinary infantry
    

Eradication forces are particularly valuable in environments considered too dangerous for organic soldiers.

---

# Deployment Doctrine

The deployment of Eradication forces represents a significant escalation in Hell Host military operations.

They are not generally used for ordinary patrols, occupation, civilian security, or standard infantry engagements.

Instead, Eradication units are introduced when command determines that a target must be:

- Destroyed completely
    
- Cleared rapidly
    
- Neutralised without concern for machine casualties
    
- Assaulted under extreme environmental conditions
    
- Denied any realistic opportunity to regroup
    

A typical Eradication deployment may occur after Doom Corps units have already:

- Identified the primary objective
    
- Established friendly lines
    
- Evacuated civilians
    
- Cleared non-designated personnel
    
- Marked zones for destruction
    

Once the operational area is cleared, Eradication is released.

---

# Restricted Deployment

Despite their reputation, Eradication units are **not permitted unrestricted use**.

Hell Host Command maintains strict rules concerning their deployment near:

- Civilian populations
    
- Allied forces
    
- Medical facilities
    
- Evacuation corridors
    
- Politically sensitive locations
    
- Unstable anomalous environments
    
- Critical infrastructure intended for later capture
    

Lower-tier ER units may be authorised relatively quickly during major battles.

Higher-tier machines require increasingly senior approval.

The deployment of units such as:

- [[ER-13 Null]]
    
- [[ER-14 Overlord]]
    
- [[ER-XV Extinction]]
    

may require authorisation from extremely senior Hell Host leadership.

Some ER-XV deployments require direct approval from [[Predator]] or equivalent supreme command authority.

---

# Deployment Warning

Before major Eradication operations, all friendly personnel within the designated combat zone receive an automated warning.

The warning begins with:

**Three low electronic tones.**

This is followed by:

> **“Eradication deployment authorised. All non-designated personnel evacuate the operational area.”**

Veteran Hell Host soldiers recognise the warning immediately.

Enemy forces have also learned what it means.

On some battlefields, the sound alone has caused hostile personnel to abandon defensive positions before the first Eradication unit arrived.

---

# Composition

The fighting strength of the Eradication Division consists almost entirely of robotic units.

These machines are supported by organic personnel responsible for:

- Command oversight
    
- Programming
    
- Logistics
    
- Maintenance
    
- Weapons engineering
    
- Battlefield recovery
    
- Tactical analysis
    
- Artificial intelligence monitoring
    
- Transport
    
- Research coordination
    

Humans do not normally fight directly as conventional Eradication infantry.

The machines are the infantry.

---

# Artificial Intelligence

ER units vary significantly in intelligence.

Not every Eradication machine is a fully sapient artificial intelligence.

Most standard ER units operate using advanced tactical combat intelligence capable of:

- Target identification
    
- Squad coordination
    
- Tactical adaptation
    
- Pathfinding
    
- Threat prioritisation
    
- Weapon selection
    
- Limited independent decision-making
    

Higher ER classes may possess significantly more sophisticated systems.

This does **not** necessarily make them people in the same sense as [[Enigma]].

Enigma is a fully developed autonomous intelligence with emotional development, independent personality, and recognised personhood.

Most Eradication units are weapons.

The distinction is considered extremely important.

---

# ER Classification System

Eradication machines are categorised through the **ER Classification System**.

The numerical designation does not simply indicate increasing strength.

Instead, each class represents a different battlefield purpose.

An ER-7 is not a replacement for an ER-1.

An ER-1 is still vastly more suitable for mass deployment.

Each machine exists because it solves a particular military problem.

# ER-1 “Scorcher”

> [!danger] Standard Eradicator  
> **Role:** General assault / incendiary infantry  
> **Production:** Mass-produced  
> **Estimated Quantity:** Millions  
> **Threat Level:** Moderate individually / Extreme in mass deployment

The **ER-1 Scorcher** is the standard combat machine of the Eradication Division.

ER-1s are manufactured in enormous numbers and form the bulk of most Eradication deployments.

They are:

- Durable
    
- Relatively inexpensive
    
- Easy to repair
    
- Simple to manufacture
    
- Capable of operating in large coordinated formations
    

Their preferred weapons include:

- [[569 Grenade-Launching Shotgun]]
    
- [[543 Ultra Flamethrower]]
    
- [[DTH-9 Incinerator Shotgun]]
    
- [[EXE-12 Eradicator Rifle]]
    
- [[HB245 Incendiary Grenade]]
    
- Explosive charges
    
- Incendiary grenades
    

Scorchers are particularly infamous for combining firearms, explosives, and incendiary weapons during assaults.

A battlefield containing thousands of advancing ER-1 units is one of the most recognisable sights associated with the division.

---

# ER-2 “Ripper”

> [!danger] Rapid Assault Eradicator  
> **Role:** Pursuit / flanking / rapid breach  
> **Protection:** Light  
> **Mobility:** Extremely High

The **ER-2 Ripper** is a lighter, faster derivative of the ER-1.

It sacrifices armour and durability for:

- Speed
    
- Agility
    
- Reaction time
    
- Pursuit capability
    

ER-2s specialise in:

- Chasing retreating forces
    
- Rapid flanking attacks
    
- Urban assault
    
- Interior clearing
    
- Fast breaches
    
- Overrunning lightly defended positions
    

Their primary weakness is reduced protection.

They can be destroyed more easily than most other Eradication classes.

Reaching them is the difficult part.

Some ER-2 units are capable of dropping into a quadrupedal running posture during high-speed pursuit.

This behaviour is not commonly appreciated by whoever is being pursued.

---

# ER-3 “Bulwark”

> [!danger] Heavy Eradicator  
> **Role:** Suppression / frontal advance  
> **Protection:** Heavy  
> **Mobility:** Low

The **ER-3 Bulwark** is a heavily armoured version of the standard Eradication chassis.

Its purpose is simple:

> **Advance through fire that would stop lighter machines.**

ER-3s commonly move at the front of Eradication formations, absorbing enemy fire while smaller units advance behind them.

Common weapons include:

- [[71 High-Calibre Machine Gun]]
    
- [[39TG Semi-Automatic Grenade Launcher]]
    
- [[REDSTORM Missile Launcher]]
    
- [[GRV-7 Mauler]]
    
- Heavy shotguns
    
- Heavy suppression weapons
    

An ER-3 may continue fighting after sustaining damage that would completely disable ordinary infantry.

---

# ER-4 “Butcher”

> [!danger] Close-Quarters Eradicator  
> **Role:** Interior assault / bunker clearance  
> **Identifier:** Red skull painted across helmet

The **ER-4 Butcher** is one of the most feared conventional Eradication machines.

Larger than the first three ER classes, Butchers are designed for close-range combat inside:

- Trenches
    
- Ships
    
- Bunkers
    
- Underground facilities
    
- Industrial complexes
    
- Urban interiors
    

ER-4s are immediately recognisable by a painted:

> **Red skull**

across the front of their helmets.

Common weapons include:

- [[ION-47 Breaker]]
    
- [[DTH-9 Incinerator Shotgun]]
    
- [[Atrius IV]]
    
- Heavy breaching weapons
    
- Ion-disruption ammunition
    

ER-4s are notorious for using Ion weaponry at distances considered dangerously close by organic soldiers.

The machines do not care about thermal shock, recoil trauma, or electromagnetic backlash in the same way a human operator would.

If an ER-4 has entered the building, the battle has usually already gone very badly for the defenders.

# ER-5 “Longshot”

> [!danger] Heavy Marksman Eradicator  
> **Role:** Long-range elimination / anti-material combat

The **ER-5 Longshot** is a specialised long-range Eradication platform.

Rather than focusing solely on personnel, ER-5 units destroy:

- Officers
    
- Light vehicles
    
- Communications arrays
    
- Firing positions
    
- Weapon emplacements
    
- Fortified windows
    
- Defensive equipment
    

Common weapons include:

- [[558 Cycler Sniper Rifle]]
    
- [[47T Heavy Ballistic Sniper Rifle]]
    
- [[89X Rupture Explosive Rifle]]
    

ER-5 systems are capable of calculating penetration paths through structures before firing.

Targets may be eliminated through:

- Walls
    
- Defensive barriers
    
- Light vehicle armour
    
- Reinforced firing positions

## 47T Field Story

An old Hell Host field report claims that a round from the [[47T Heavy Ballistic Sniper Rifle]] once:

1. Penetrated the outer armour of a light tank
    
2. Passed through the driver's position
    
3. Exited the opposite side
    
4. Embedded itself inside a nearby concrete structure
    

Whether every part of the story is true remains debated.

The 47T remains powerful enough that few volunteers have expressed interest in testing it.

# ER-6 “Martyr”

> [!danger] Demolition Eradicator  
> **Role:** Self-detonation / fortification destruction

The **ER-6 Martyr** is effectively a mobile demolition system.

ER-6 units infiltrate or charge enemy positions before deliberately detonating an internal explosive payload.

Different payload modules include:

- Anti-fortification
    
- Incendiary
    
- EMP
    
- Anti-shield
    
- Fragmentation
    
- Heavy demolition
    

ER-6 units are rarely deployed near organic Hell Host personnel.

Their use is generally reserved for areas already designated for destruction.

Because the ER-6 is robotic, Hell Host doctrine does not classify its destruction as a suicide attack.

The machine is considered expendable ordnance with legs.

# ER-7 “Goliath”

> [!danger] Heavy Weapons Eradicator  
> **Height:** Approximately 8 feet  
> **Role:** Heavy firepower / anti-armour / assault support

The **ER-7 Goliath** is the largest routinely deployed Eradication infantry platform.

Standing approximately:

**8 feet tall**

the Goliath carries weapons that would be physically impractical for ordinary Hell Host soldiers.

Common systems include:

- [[GRV-7 Mauler]]
    
- [[ION-88 Headsman]]
    
- [[REDSTORM Missile Launcher]]
    
- Heavy rotary cannons
    
- Portable plasma weapons
    
- Oversized anti-armour rifles
    

Some ER-7 weapon systems are nearly the size of a standard infantry soldier.

Goliaths are slow compared with lighter ER units.

Their purpose is not mobility.

Their purpose is to bring vehicle-scale firepower into an infantry formation.

> [!danger] Infiltration Eradicator  
> **Role:** Sabotage / stealth demolition

The **ER-8 Wraith** approaches eradication differently.

It does not attack the front gate.

It enters before the battle begins.

ER-8 units employ:

- Low-signature armour
    
- Reduced-noise actuators
    
- Electronic camouflage
    
- Advanced sensor suppression
    
- Covert communications
    

Primary targets include:

- Ammunition stores
    
- Shield generators
    
- Anti-air networks
    
- Fuel depots
    
- Communications arrays
    
- Command centres
    
- Power systems
    

A typical ER-8 operation may involve silently entering an enemy installation hours before a larger Eradication assault.

Common weapons include:

- [[AX-0 Silence]]
    
- Compact explosives
    
- High-yield demolition charges
    
- Suppressed penetrator weapons
    

The first visible sign of an ER-8 attack is frequently the destruction of whatever the enemy needed most.

ER-9 units commonly work alongside:

[[Research Division]]

# ER-10 “Shepherd”

> [!danger] Command Eradicator  
> **Role:** Battlefield coordination  
> **Rarity:** Uncommon

The **ER-10 Shepherd** is a specialised command machine.

Rather than maximising direct firepower, Shepherds coordinate large numbers of lesser ER units.

An ER-10 may dynamically manage:

- Target distribution
    
- Attack routes
    
- Formation changes
    
- Ammunition priorities
    
- Reinforcement movement
    
- Threat assessment
    
- Recovery operations
    

The destruction of an ER-10 does **not** shut down nearby Eradication machines.

ER units remain independently functional.

However, their overall coordination usually becomes less efficient.

Shepherd units are recognisable by a smooth, featureless black faceplate containing a thin horizontal red light.

---

# ER-11 “Harvester”

> [!danger] Recovery Eradicator  
> **Role:** Battlefield salvage / machine recovery

The **ER-11 Harvester** operates behind advancing Eradication formations.

Its task is not primarily to destroy enemies.

It recovers the Division itself.

Harvesters retrieve:

- Damaged ER units
    
- Intelligence cores
    
- Weapons
    
- Armour components
    
- Specialist systems
    
- Experimental equipment
    

They are also capable of crude battlefield repairs.

An ER machine missing an arm may receive a replacement taken from another destroyed chassis.

Some ER units have effectively been destroyed multiple times across their operational histories but repeatedly returned to service because enough of their core systems remained intact.

# ER-12 “Revenant”

> [!danger] Hunter-Killer Eradicator  
> **Role:** Individual target pursuit  
> **Rarity:** Rare

The **ER-12 Revenant** is not normally assigned to battlefields.

It is assigned to:

> **People.**

Or specific entities.

Targets may include:

- Enemy commanders
    
- Terrorists
    
- War criminals
    
- Rogue Ascended
    
- Dangerous creatures
    
- Escaped high-risk prisoners

Once assigned, an ER-12 can pursue a target for:

- Weeks
    
- Months

Changes tactics.

Studies routines.

Running from an ER-12 does not automatically end the mission.

It merely extends it.

# ER-13 “Null”

> [!danger] Anti-Anomaly Eradicator  
> **Role:** Anomalous / Ascended threat suppression

The **ER-13 Null** was developed for threats that conventional weapons struggle to kill.

Potential targets include:

- Ascended entities
    
- Element 95 organisms
    
- Dimensional anomalies
    
- Reality-altered creatures
    
- Unusual energy beings
    
- Other extreme anomalous threats
    

Where the ER-9 is designed to **survive** anomalous environments, the ER-13 is designed to:

> **Fight what lives inside them.**

ER-13 systems vary depending on operational requirements.

Common equipment may include:

- [[VXR-13 Null Lance]]
    
- Experimental suppression systems
    
- Anti-anomaly ammunition
    
- Prototype energy weaponry
    

ER-13 deployments are heavily restricted.

---

# ER-14 “Overlord”

> [!danger] Superheavy Eradicator  
> **Role:** Mobile fortress / extreme assault  
> **Height:** Approximately 10–12 feet  
> **Rarity:** Extremely Rare

The **ER-14 Overlord** is the largest standardised Eradication platform.

Standing significantly taller than the ER-7, an Overlord operates somewhere between:

- Infantry
    
- Heavy weapons platform
    
- Armoured vehicle
    

Its armour is sufficient for smaller ER units to advance behind it during assaults.

An Eradication formation built around an Overlord may include:

- ER-1s advancing behind its armour
    
- ER-2s operating along its flanks
    
- ER-3s reinforcing frontal pressure
    
- ER-7s providing heavy fire support
    
- ER-10s coordinating the formation
    

ER-14 deployment usually indicates that command expects exceptional resistance.

---

# ER-XV “Extinction”

> [!danger] Experimental Classification  
> **Designation:** ER-XV  
> **Standardised Chassis:** None  
> **Standardised Weapons:** None  
> **Rarity:** Exceptional  
> **Authorisation:** Highest-level restricted

The **ER-XV Extinction** designation does not refer to a single robot model.

ER-XV is a classification given to experimental Eradication platforms incorporating technologies considered:

- Too unstable
    
- Too expensive
    
- Too powerful
    
- Too poorly understood
    
- Too dangerous
    
- Or too specialised
    

for conventional mass production.

No two ER-XV units are guaranteed to be alike.

- Reverse-engineered alien systems
    
- Prototype shielding
    
- Experimental energy sources
    
- Anomalous materials
    
- Exotic armour
    
- Advanced artificial intelligence
    
- Experimental teleportation
    
- Dimensional technology
    

Some ER-XV machines combine several systems.

---

## ER-XV Weapons

There is no standard ER-XV arsenal.

One machine might use:

- Element 95 energy projection
    

Another might carry:

- Axionium weaponry
    

Another may possess:

- Forge-derived particle technology
    

Another may use a weapon that has never been approved for use anywhere else in the Hell Host military.

Some ER-XV units have reportedly been tested once and immediately placed into sealed storage.

ER-XV does not necessarily mean:

> **More powerful than ER-14.**

It means:

> **Experimental. Unstandardised. Restricted.**

A common saying regarding the classification states:

> **“If XV has arrived, whatever we're fighting has already gone too far.”**

Eradication does not operate a completely isolated arsenal.

Robotic units may use modified versions of weapons also found elsewhere in the Hell Host military.

- Target
    
- Environment
    
- Expected resistance
    
- Machine class
    
- Available supply

[[660th Flame Battalion]]

## Atrius IV

The [[Atrius IV]], often shortened to **A-IV**, is a heavy pulse rifle.

Rather than producing rapid fire, the weapon charges before releasing a powerful concentrated energy pulse.

Its output makes it particularly useful against:

- Armour
    
- Shields
    
- Fortified personnel
    
- Heavy robotic targets
    

---

## 47T Heavy Ballistic Sniper Rifle

The [[47T Heavy Ballistic Sniper Rifle]] is an extreme anti-material weapon capable of penetrating heavy armour.

It remains one of the most powerful conventional ballistic precision weapons available to Hell Host forces.

---

## 558 Cycler Sniper Rifle

The [[558 Cycler Sniper Rifle]] uses an electromagnetic acceleration chamber.

The weapon requires time to charge before firing.

During this process, successive acceleration systems increase projectile velocity before release.

The longer firing cycle makes it unsuitable for ordinary close combat.

For an ER-5 capable of waiting motionlessly for hours, this is rarely considered a problem.

# ION-47 “Breaker”

The **ION-47 Breaker** is one of the primary Ion weapons used by the Eradication Division.

It fires concentrated ionised energy designed to disrupt:

- Powered armour
    
- Shields
    
- Electronics
    
- Vehicles
    
- Robotic systems
    

The Breaker possesses three primary firing modes.

### Pulse

Rapid lower-energy Ion fire.

### Breaker

A charged anti-armour and anti-shield shot.

### Surge

A short-range discharge releasing most of the weapon's capacitor at once.

Surge Mode produces enough thermal and electromagnetic backlash that it is considered unsafe for ordinary organic infantry.

ER-4 Butchers use it anyway.

Frequently at close range.

---

# ION-88 “Headsman”

The **ION-88 Headsman** is the heavy counterpart to the Breaker.

Designed primarily for ER-7 Goliaths, the weapon produces an enormous concentrated Ion discharge capable of damaging:

- Heavy armour
    
- Fortifications
    
- Energy shields
    
- Large robotic targets
    

The weapon requires significant cooling after firing.

An organic operator would struggle with both recoil and thermal output.

---

# GRV-7 “Mauler”

# AX-0 “Silence”

# HX-9 “Cremator”

The **HX-9 Cremator** is a purpose-built heavy incendiary system.

A common Doom Corps warning regarding these units states:

> **“If you see a Scorcher's tanks, don't shoot the tanks.”**

# VXR-13 “Null Lance”

Not every Null Lance is identical.

Different versions may incorporate:

- Axionium
    
- Element 95
    
- Experimental energy systems
    
- Anomalous materials
    
- Reverse-engineered technology

# Recovery Doctrine

Hell Hosts considers damaged Eradication machines recoverable military assets whenever possible.

This responsibility falls largely to:

[[ER-11 Harvester]]

units.

Important recovered components include:

- Combat cores
    
- Memory systems
    
- Targeting systems
    
- Weapons
    
- Experimental materials
    
- Unique chassis components
    

A machine does not necessarily leave service because its body was destroyed.

If the core survives, it may be:

- Repaired
    
- Reinstalled
    
- Transferred
    
- Reconstructed
    

Some veteran ER units have served through multiple chassis.

---

# Relationship with Doom Corps

The [[Doom Corps]] and Eradication Division fulfil very different roles.

Doom Corps:

- Captures territory
    
- Holds positions
    
- Protects strategic locations
    
- Conducts general ground warfare
    
- Works around civilians
    
- Maintains long-term battlefield presence
    

Eradication:

- Destroys designated targets
    
- Clears fortified zones
    
- Performs extreme assaults
    
- Eliminates hazardous threats
    
- Operates where preservation is secondary to destruction
    

Doom Corps personnel generally respect Eradication units.

They also prefer not to be standing near the target when they arrive.

---

# Relationship with Research Division

The [[Research Division]] maintains one of the closest working relationships with Eradication.

Research personnel develop and test:

- Experimental weaponry
    
- New robotic systems
    
- Anomalous countermeasures
    
- Element 95 technology
    
- Axionium systems
    
- Prototype armour
    
- Artificial intelligence
    
- ER-XV projects
    

Eradication provides a battlefield environment in which those systems can be tested under actual combat conditions.

This arrangement has produced some of the most powerful weapons in Hell Host service.

It has also produced several projects everyone involved would prefer not to discuss.

---

# Relationship with Spectre Division

The relationship between the [[Spectre Division]] and Eradication is occasionally uncomfortable.

Spectre specialises in:

- Law enforcement
    
- Arrest
    
- Investigation
    
- Prison security
    
- Internal discipline
    

Eradication specialises in:

> **Not those things.**

Spectre personnel are often responsible for determining whether a target must be captured.

If capture is no longer required, Eradication may become involved.

---

# Relationship with Fire Division

The [[Fire Division]] frequently supports Eradication operations through:

- Artillery preparation
    
- Siege bombardment
    
- Defensive suppression
    
- Long-range target destruction
    
- Fire coordination
    

Fire Division destroys the battlefield from distance.

Eradication enters what remains.

Combined deployment of both divisions is considered a severe escalation.

---

# Human Personnel

Although the frontline machines receive most of the attention, Eradication could not function without its organic support personnel.

These include:

- Robotics engineers
    
- Artificial intelligence specialists
    
- Weapons technicians
    
- Logistics officers
    
- Armourers
    
- Command personnel
    
- Recovery crews
    
- Software engineers
    
- Research liaisons
    

Many develop unusually dark senses of humour.

Working around several million heavily armed machines apparently has that effect.

---

# Machine Culture

Most Eradication robots possess no true social culture.

However, long-serving machines may accumulate:

- Repair markings
    
- Operation identifiers
    
- Chassis replacement records
    
- Enemy kill markings
    
- Unit insignia
    
- Maintenance nicknames
    

Ground crews occasionally name individual machines.

The machines generally do not care.

The technicians do.

---

# Psychological Warfare

Eradication deployment has considerable psychological impact.

Enemy forces have learned to recognise:

- Red optical systems
    
- Blood-red skull markings
    
- Eradication warning tones
    
- ER-4 helmet skulls
    
- Heavy robotic footsteps
    
- Incendiary weapon signatures
    

Some enemy formations have surrendered immediately after confirming Eradication deployment.

Whether surrender prevents Eradication engagement depends on:

- Mission orders
    
- Target classification
    
- Command authorisation
    
- Whether the surrender occurred before or after eradication status was confirmed
    

This uncertainty contributes heavily to the division's reputation.

---

# Visual Identity

Eradication equipment typically uses:

**Primary:** Blood Red  
**Secondary:** Matte Black  
**Accent:** Gunmetal  
**Lighting:** Hellfire Orange / Red

Common visual features include:

- Red optics
    
- Skull insignia
    
- Heavy black armour
    
- Exposed mechanical systems
    
- Glowing weapon components
    
- Red target markings
    

The [[ER-4 Butcher]] is particularly recognisable due to the red skull painted across its helmet.

---

# Insignia

The Eradication Division insignia consists of a stylised skull surrounded by angular markings associated with:

- Containment
    
- Destruction
    
- Extermination
    

The symbol is rarely displayed subtly.

When Eradication arrives, concealment is generally no longer part of the plan.

---

# Reputation

## Among Hell Host Personnel

Eradication is regarded as:

- Effective
    
- Terrifying
    
- Extremely reliable
    
- Excessive
    
- Necessary under the correct circumstances
    

Many soldiers are grateful the machines are on their side.

Most also prefer maintaining a respectable distance.

---

## Among Enemy Forces

Few Hell Host formations possess the same psychological reputation.

The appearance of Eradication units commonly indicates that:

- The objective is no longer being negotiated over
    
- The area may soon become unsafe
    
- Reinforcements are unlikely to change the outcome
    
- Hell Host Command has stopped prioritising capture
    

The sight of an ER-14 or ER-XV can dramatically alter enemy morale before combat begins.

---

# Division Doctrine

The Eradication Division does not celebrate cruelty.

It does not require hatred.

Its machines do not need either.

They simply receive:

- A target
    
- An operational boundary
    
- An authorisation
    

And then execute the order.

The Division's defining principle remains:

> **“If it exists, if it is a target, if it resists, it will be eradicated.”**
