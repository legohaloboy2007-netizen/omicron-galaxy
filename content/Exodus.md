# Exodus

Exodus is one of the primary inhabited worlds of the [[Angel Swords]] and is widely regarded as the most beautiful and peaceful planet under their control. Unlike [[Genesis]], which serves as the militarized capital and industrial heart of the faction, Exodus exists as a civilian sanctuary world where the majority of Angel Sword citizens live when not serving in military roles.

Across the galaxy, Exodus is commonly known as:

The Garden of Eden

due to its near-perfect natural environment and extraordinary ecological stability.

## Environment

Exodus is considered one of the most naturally hospitable planets in known space.

The world is defined by:
- endless green landscapes
- crystal-clear freshwater oceans
- enormous forests and plains
- perfect atmospheric conditions
- stable climates across most regions
- thriving ecosystems untouched by pollution or industrial decay

Remarkably, the planet contains:
- no salt water
- no poisonous plants
- no naturally toxic environments
- no apex predators or hostile wildlife

All known life on Exodus exists within an unusually balanced ecosystem.

The planet’s oceans, lakes, and rivers are entirely freshwater, contributing heavily to the world’s reputation as a paradise.

## Society

Exodus serves as the primary civilian population center of the [[Angel Swords]].

Many citizens stationed on:
- [[Genesis]]
- military fleets
- defense worlds
- expeditionary forces

eventually return to Exodus following periods of service.

The planet is heavily associated with:
- family life
- peace
- cultural tradition
- education
- spiritual reflection
- recovery from war

For many Angel Sword citizens, Exodus represents the ideal future their civilization seeks to protect.

## Architecture

Settlements on Exodus are designed to coexist with the planet’s natural beauty rather than dominate it.

Common architectural features include:
- elegant white structures
- open sanctuaries
- riverside cities
- elevated walkways
- massive gardens
- luminous ceremonial halls
- naturally integrated construction

Unlike the harsh military atmosphere of [[Genesis]], Exodus feels calm, open, and harmonious.

## Protection & Security

Despite its peaceful nature, Exodus remains heavily protected by the Angel Swords.

Orbital defense fleets, planetary shield systems, and hidden military installations protect the world from external threats.

The Angel Swords consider Exodus too valuable to lose.

Many soldiers view defending the planet as a sacred responsibility.

## Reputation

Throughout the galaxy, Exodus is known as:
- the Garden of Eden
- one of the most beautiful worlds in existence
- the civilian heart of the [[Angel Swords]]
- a symbol of peace protected through strength
- a paradise untouched by the chaos consuming much of the galaxy

To the Angel Swords, Exodus represents what civilization is meant to become when war finally ends.
