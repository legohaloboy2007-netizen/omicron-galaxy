# Farlands

The Farlands are a heavily corrupted frontier region located beyond the [[Outer Rim]], marking the beginning of the galaxy’s descent into instability and cosmic corruption. Once a sparse but survivable collection of frontier systems, the region was permanently transformed following a catastrophic Element 95 containment rupture that scattered the substance across dozens of worlds.

Since the incident, the Farlands have become a spreading wound within the [[Omicron Galaxy]], where entire ecosystems, planets, and lifeforms have been violently reshaped by Element 95 corruption. Matter itself behaves unpredictably throughout the region, resulting in mutated wildlife, living terrain, crystallized storms, and hostile anomaly zones.

Despite the danger, the Farlands remain active due to the presence of abandoned technology, lost research facilities, ancient ruins, and valuable corrupted resources. Numerous factions, cults, scavengers, and military groups continue to enter the region searching for power, knowledge, or answers surrounding the origins of Element 95.

Travel throughout the Farlands is extremely dangerous. Ships entering the region require reinforced hulls, anti-corruption shielding, and strict quarantine protocols. Even heavily protected crews frequently report hallucinations, temporal distortion, distorted signals, and whisper-like transmissions believed to originate from corrupted environments themselves.

The Farlands also serve as the final known boundary before the mysterious [[Voidlands]], separated by a silent anomaly zone known as the Gate of the Edge.
