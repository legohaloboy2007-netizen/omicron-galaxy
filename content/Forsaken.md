## Overview

**Real Name:** Silas Mercer

**Callsign:** Forsaken

**Age:** 39

**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]

**Rank:** Platoon Captain / Lieutenant

**Role:** Black Operations Commander

**Unit Size:** Three-Man Covert Strike Cell

**Threat Level:** Extreme

Silas Mercer, known throughout the galaxy as **Forsaken**, commands one of the most effective black operations units within the Hell Hosts.

Specializing in:

- Infiltration
- Extraction
- Assassination
- Covert Warfare
- Intelligence Recovery
- Deniable Operations

Forsaken is routinely assigned missions too sensitive for conventional military command structures.

Where official records end, his operations usually begin.

---

## Appearance

Forsaken stands approximately:

**6'1"**

with a lean, athletic build.

Unlike many elite Hell Hosts personnel, he avoids anything that draws attention.

### Features

- Short dark brown hair
- Grey beginning to form around the temples
- Grey eyes
- Weathered appearance
- Controlled posture

His eyes are often described as difficult to read.

Many people believe he is staring directly at them.

More often than not, he is already thinking three steps ahead.

### Armor

Forsaken's equipment is designed entirely around practicality.

His armor favors:

- Mobility
- Stealth
- Protection
- Utility

Rather than intimidation.

Unlike many famous Hell Hosts figures, Forsaken does not stand out in a crowd.

That is intentional.

---

## Personality

Forsaken is:

- Quiet
- Disciplined
- Observant
- Controlled
- Emotionally Guarded

He speaks only when necessary.

When he does, people listen.

Many junior officers have remarked that Forsaken's calm voice is more intimidating than shouting.

### The Man Behind The Reputation

Many people assume Forsaken is cold.

The reality is more complicated.

He cares deeply about the people under his command.

He simply struggles to express it.

Years of black operations have taught him that attachment can become a liability.

As a result, he often keeps people at arm's length.

---

## Leadership Style

Forsaken leads through certainty.

His teams operate with minimal communication.

Orders are clear.

Objectives are simple.

Mistakes are rare.

Many larger units have reported completing mission briefings only to discover Forsaken's team has already completed the operation.

### Trust

Forsaken places immense value on competence.

Once trust is earned, it is rarely lost.

---

## Combat Style

Forsaken specializes in:

- Close-quarters combat
- Silent elimination
- Infiltration
- Urban warfare
- Rapid room clearing
- Target extraction

He is dangerous at all ranges.

Most people fear him up close.

### Preferred Weapons

- Suppressed rifles
- Pistols
- Combat knives
- Covert equipment

When stealth fails, Forsaken transitions into direct action with terrifying efficiency.

---

## The Name "Forsaken"

Many theories exist regarding the origin of the name.

Some believe it refers to morality abandoned during black operations.

Others believe it refers to a mission where command abandoned him.

Neither explanation is entirely correct.

Both contain pieces of the truth.

### Operation Black Winter

Years earlier, Silas Mercer led a six-man covert team deep into Angel Swords territory.

The operation was compromised.

One by one, his team died.

For seven days, Forsaken fought alone behind enemy lines.

When recovery forces finally arrived, he was the only survivor.

Something changed during those seven days.

The man who returned was not quite the same as the man who left.

## Relationships

### [[Predator]]

Predator values results.

Few people deliver results as consistently as Forsaken.

Their relationship is built upon professional trust and mutual respect.

Predator rarely questions Forsaken's methods.

Forsaken rarely gives him a reason to.

---

### [[Azure Gale]]

Azure Gale appreciates discretion.

Forsaken appreciates efficiency.

The two possess very different personalities, yet work together remarkably well.

Both trust each other with highly classified operations.

---

### [[Champion]]

Champion and Forsaken represent opposite sides of military leadership.

Champion leads openly.

Forsaken operates from the shadows.

Despite this difference, both respect one another greatly.

Forsaken trusts Champion more than most people realize.

---

### [[Destiny]]

One of the few people who consistently treats him as a person rather than an asset.

Forsaken often finds this surprisingly difficult to respond to.

Despite his reserved nature, he quietly appreciates her company.

---

### [[Ranger]]

Ranger and Forsaken frequently challenge each other.

Their approaches to operations differ significantly.

Ranger favors speed and instinct.

Forsaken favors patience and preparation.

Their disagreements are frequent.

Their respect is absolute.

---

## Off-Duty Life

Contrary to popular belief, Forsaken does not spend his free time preparing for missions.

He actively separates himself from operations whenever possible.

### Long Walks

Forsaken spends significant time walking alone through cities, settlements, and colony worlds.

Not for tactical reasons.

Simply because he enjoys it.

---

### Cafés

One of his favorite habits.

Whenever possible, he visits places where nobody recognizes him.

For a few hours:

He is not Forsaken.

He is simply Silas.

---

## Hobbies

### Reading

Forsaken reads extensively.

His preferred subjects include:

- History
- Philosophy
- Crime Fiction
- Biographies

He avoids military literature whenever possible.

He has lived enough military history already.

---

### Chess

A long-standing hobby.

Forsaken enjoys strategy, patience, and prediction.

Initially developed as mission preparation.

- Rooftops

Long before he actually needs them.

### Isolation

Forsaken pushes people away.

Not because he dislikes them.

Because he believes it protects them.
