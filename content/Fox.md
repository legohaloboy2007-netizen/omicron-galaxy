# Fox

> *"If there's a plan... I'll find a better one."*

## Profile

**Real Name:** Jace Riven  
**Callsign:** Fox  
**Age:** 32  
**Status:** Active  
**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]  
**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Specialization:** Irregular Operations / Reconnaissance / Disruption  
**Rank:** Corporal  
**Threat Level:** High  
**Primary Role:** Infiltration / Disruption / Hit-and-Run Warfare  
**Primary Weapon:** VX-9 Compact SMG  
**Secondary Weapon:** PX-12 Pistol

Jace Riven, better known by his callsign **Fox**, is a Hell Host irregular operations specialist known for infiltration, disruption, improvisation, and unconventional battlefield tactics.

Fox specializes in entering hostile territory, disrupting enemy operations, and disappearing before opposing forces can organize an effective response.

Unlike personnel who rely upon carefully prepared plans and predictable operational procedures, Fox deliberately embraces uncertainty.

He adapts quickly when missions change, exploits unexpected opportunities, and frequently abandons established plans when he believes a more effective option has presented itself.

This has made him difficult to command.

It has also made him extremely useful.

---

## Appearance

Fox typically wears lightweight Hell Host combat armor configured for mobility and prolonged infiltration.

His equipment sacrifices some of the protection available to heavier Doom Corps personnel in exchange for reduced weight, quieter movement, and greater freedom of motion.

His helmet incorporates reconnaissance and communications equipment while obscuring his face during operations.

Fox commonly carries a scarf, cloak, or other environmental covering depending on the location of deployment.

His equipment is intentionally modular.

Fox rarely knows exactly what he will need when an operation begins.

He prefers to be prepared to improvise.

---

## Irregular Operations

Fox operates within Hell Host irregular warfare and disruption operations.

His assignments frequently take place ahead of conventional forces or inside territory already controlled by the enemy.

Typical objectives include:

- Infiltration
- Sabotage
- Communications disruption
- Reconnaissance
- Hit-and-run attacks
- Intelligence recovery
- Urban warfare
- Misdirection
- Target marking
- Infrastructure disruption
- Supporting larger assaults from behind enemy lines

Fox is particularly effective when an enemy force depends heavily upon communication, coordination, and predictable defensive procedures.

Rather than confronting those defenses directly, he attempts to make them unreliable.

---

## Combat Style

Fox prioritizes mobility, confusion, and initiative.

He rarely remains in one location after attacking and avoids prolonged engagements unless doing so directly benefits the mission.

Fox frequently uses suppressed weapons, smoke, electronic interference, environmental hazards, and deliberately misleading activity to manipulate enemy responses.

An enemy searching for Fox may receive conflicting reports of his location, discover sabotaged equipment, lose communications, or redirect personnel toward an area he has already abandoned.

Fox does not need an enemy to believe he is invisible.

He only needs them to look in the wrong direction.

His preferred combat pattern involves striking unexpectedly, relocating immediately, and attacking again before opposing forces can determine what happened.

This makes Fox particularly dangerous in dense urban environments, industrial complexes, military installations, and other locations containing numerous possible routes of movement.

---

## Improvisation

Fox's defining characteristic is his ability to improvise under pressure.

He views mission plans as useful starting points rather than immutable instructions.

If the original plan remains effective, Fox follows it.

If circumstances change, he changes with them.

If he discovers something better, the original plan may cease to exist entirely.

Fox has been known to alter infiltration routes, manipulate enemy systems, create distractions not included in operational planning, and exploit environmental opportunities without requesting authorization.

This behavior has repeatedly frustrated commanding officers.

It has also repeatedly worked.

Fox distinguishes heavily between an **objective** and a **plan**.

In his view, the objective is what must be accomplished.

The plan is merely one proposed method of accomplishing it.

---

## Information Disruption

Fox frequently attempts to control what an enemy believes is happening rather than simply remaining undetected.

His operations may involve:

- Disabling communications
- Creating false alarms
- Manipulating surveillance systems
- Cutting power
- Producing misleading movement
- Sabotaging vehicles or equipment
- Using smoke to obscure routes
- Attacking from unexpected positions
- Drawing security forces away from an objective

These methods can create the impression that multiple Hell Host units are operating within an area when Fox is working alone.

They can also conceal the presence of larger Hell Host forces preparing to attack.

Fox considers confusion a weapon.

He uses it accordingly.

---

## Equipment

### VX-9 Compact SMG

Fox's primary weapon is a suppressed **VX-9 Compact SMG** configured for close-range engagements and infiltration operations.

Its compact profile allows Fox to maneuver easily through confined environments while maintaining sufficient firepower for rapid engagements.

Fox strongly prefers suppressors whenever mission conditions permit.

His objective is rarely to win a prolonged firefight.

It is to ensure one never develops.

### PX-12 Pistol

Fox carries a **PX-12 Pistol** as his standard sidearm.

Its compact size and reliability make it suitable for his operational style.

### Additional Equipment

Fox's equipment varies considerably between missions but commonly includes:

- Signal disruptor
- Smoke grenades
- Throwing blades
- Combat knife
- Compact breaching tools
- Reconnaissance equipment
- Utility tools
- Lightweight tactical rig

Fox frequently carries equipment for purposes other than those originally intended by its manufacturer.

---

## Personality

Fox is cunning, sarcastic, opportunistic, and difficult to predict.

He enjoys finding unconventional solutions and possesses little patience for procedures he considers unnecessary.

Despite this reputation, Fox is not incapable of discipline.

He understands military hierarchy and follows direct orders when necessary.

His conflicts with command generally concern **method**, not objective.

If ordered to retrieve intelligence from a hostile facility, Fox will retrieve it.

Whether he enters through the route specified during the briefing is considerably less certain.

His humor persists during combat and is frequently directed toward personnel with more rigid personalities.

Fox is especially fond of pointing out when an elaborate plan could have been replaced by a much simpler solution.

This habit has not universally endeared him to Hell Host command staff.

---

## Loyalty

Fox has occasionally been described as morally flexible and difficult to fully trust.

This reputation primarily originates from his willingness to disregard procedure rather than any established disloyalty to the Hell Hosts.

Fox is intensely committed to completing his assigned objective.

He simply places less importance on bureaucracy surrounding that objective.

His loyalty is generally described as being **to the mission before the method**.

This has occasionally resulted in disciplinary action.

It has also resulted in successful operations that would otherwise have failed.

For this reason, Fox continues to be deployed despite the headaches associated with doing so.

---

## No Special Abilities

Fox possesses no known anomalous or supernatural abilities.

His speed and agility are the result of physical conditioning, lightweight equipment, training, and an operational style designed around constant movement.

He cannot become invisible.

He cannot teleport.

He cannot predict the future.

He is simply extremely good at ensuring the enemy does not know where he is going to be next.

---

## Known Relationships

### [[Breach]]

Fox and [[Breach]] approach battlefield obstacles from almost completely opposite perspectives.

Breach specializes in destroying obstacles directly.

Fox generally prefers finding another way through.

Fox once summarized the difference:

> *"You break doors. I go around them."*

Despite their differing methods, Fox respects Breach's effectiveness and frequently takes advantage of the chaos created by siege operations.

---

### [[Anvil]]

Fox regularly jokes about [[Anvil]]'s slow and methodical approach to warfare.

Anvil rarely reacts.

Fox continues anyway.

During one operation, Fox remarked:

> *"You take your time. I'll take shortcuts."*

Anvil reportedly ignored him.

Fox considered this permission to continue.

---

### [[Aeon Unit]]

Fox is fascinated and occasionally irritated by [[Aeon Unit]]'s tendency to analyze battlefield possibilities before acting.

He frequently attempts to introduce unpredictable variables into Aeon's calculations, apparently for his own amusement.

Fox has asked Aeon:

> *"You ever not calculate everything?"*

Aeon's answer was:

> *"No."*

Fox reportedly laughed for considerably longer than the response justified.

Despite their differences, the two work effectively together. Aeon's ability to process enormous amounts of tactical information complements Fox's willingness to exploit opportunities that were not included in the original plan.

---

## Relationship with Command

Fox's relationship with Hell Host command personnel is complicated.

He is considered highly effective, particularly during missions requiring unconventional thinking.

He is also considered difficult to predict.

Some commanders avoid assigning Fox to operations requiring strict synchronization or rigid adherence to predetermined procedures.

Others specifically request him when they expect the original plan to collapse.

One assessment of Fox within Hell Host command records summarizes the issue:

> **USEFUL. NOT FULLY TRUSTED.**

Fox reportedly considered this a compliment.

---

## Reputation

Fox has developed a reputation for appearing where enemy forces least expect him.

Stories surrounding his operations occasionally exaggerate this into claims that Fox can bypass any security system or enter any location without detection.

These claims are untrue.

Fox has been detected.

He has been cornered.

His plans have failed.

What distinguishes him is his ability to adapt when they do.

Catching Fox making a mistake does not necessarily mean an enemy has caught Fox.

It may simply mean the operation has changed.

Among personnel familiar with his methods, one description has become particularly associated with him:

> *"You won't catch him. You'll realise he was already there."*

---

## Notes

- Fox possesses no anomalous abilities.
- He specializes in irregular operations and enemy disruption.
- He favors suppressed and lightweight weapons.
- Fox frequently modifies operational plans while already deployed.
- His unpredictability occasionally creates tension with command personnel.
- He is considered highly resourceful in unstable situations.
- Fox generally follows objectives more reliably than he follows plans.
- He is particularly effective during urban and infiltration operations.
