# Fractaline

## Overview

Fractaline is an extremely rare crystalline material found within unstable regions of the [[Voidlands]], particularly near:
- recursive anomaly zones
- geometric distortion fields
- dimensional fractures
- and Reality Weaver-controlled territories

The material is infamous for its bizarre relationship with:
- observation
- perception
- geometry
- and spatial consistency

Unlike conventional crystalline substances, Fractaline appears to change structure depending on:
- viewing angle
- observer position
- nearby consciousness
- and local dimensional conditions

Because of its unpredictable properties, Fractaline is heavily associated with:
the [[Reality Weavers]]

who utilize the material in advanced spatial manipulation technologies and impossible architectural systems.

## Nature

Fractaline is a semi-reactive geometric crystal displaying partially unstable dimensional characteristics.

The substance appears capable of:
- altering visible structure
- refracting space itself
- distorting perception
- and interacting with localized reality fields

In many cases, the crystal does not appear to maintain a single stable shape.

Observers frequently report:
- shifting angles
- recursive internal patterns
- impossible reflections
- and structures visible only from specific perspectives

Some researchers believe Fractaline exists simultaneously across multiple geometric states.

## Physical Appearance

Fractaline commonly appears as:
- translucent geometric crystal
- reflective angular formations
- or floating fragmented structures suspended slightly above surrounding surfaces

The material often displays:
- shifting internal light patterns
- impossible depth perception
- mirrored recursion
- and color variation depending on observation angle

Under certain conditions, Fractaline may appear:
- larger internally than externally
- physically incomplete
- or geometrically impossible

No two recorded samples appear completely identical.

## Discovery

Fractaline was first documented during early exploration of:
- recursive Void zones
- Reality Weaver territory
- and dimensional anomaly sectors

Initial expeditions described regions where:
- architecture changed while observed
- reflections behaved independently
- and geometric structures appeared alive

Fragments of Fractaline were later recovered from these environments.

Many researchers exposed to raw samples suffered:
- perceptual instability
- geometric hallucinations
- and severe spatial disorientation

Fractaline possesses numerous highly anomalous characteristics.

- perspective

## Applications

Fractaline is primarily utilized by:
[[Reality Weavers]]

Known applications include:
- recursive architecture
- geometric computation systems
- dimensional navigation
- spatial restructuring devices
- and advanced anomaly mapping

Reality Weaver structures containing Fractaline often display:
- shifting layouts
- adaptive pathways
- and impossible internal geometry

Some [[Anchorless]] navigators also utilize refined Fractaline for:
- Void navigation instruments
- tide mapping
- and anomaly route prediction

## The Reality Weaver Connection

Among the [[Reality Weavers]], Fractaline is considered:
sacred geometry made physical.

The civilization believes the material reveals:
the underlying mathematical structure of existence itself.

High-ranking Weavers frequently embed Fractaline into:
- ceremonial architecture
- cognitive chambers
- and dimensional observatories

Some advanced Weaver constructs appear almost entirely composed of living Fractaline frameworks.

## Risks

Fractaline exposure may cause:
- geometric hallucinations
- distorted spatial awareness
- recursive thought patterns
- visual instability
- dissociation
- and severe perceptual confusion

Extended exposure can permanently alter how individuals perceive:
- angles
- movement
- and physical structure

Some victims lose the ability to interpret normal geometry correctly.

## Mining & Containment

Fractaline deposits are extremely difficult to extract safely due to:
- unstable geometry
- shifting terrain
- recursive environmental effects
- and inconsistent crystal structure

Containment chambers frequently fail because the material may:
- alter shape internally
- distort container dimensions
- or phase partially outside measurable space

Standard industrial tools often malfunction near concentrated deposits.

## Relationship to the Voidlands

Fractaline is considered one of the most iconic naturally occurring materials associated with:
the [[Voidlands]]

Many scholars believe the crystal forms in locations where:
reality becomes mathematically unstable.

Some theories suggest Fractaline is not a mineral at all—
but crystallized spatial information.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Fractaline is known as:
- the crystal of impossible geometry
- the mirrored mineral
- one of the strangest materials ever discovered
- and the signature substance of the [[Reality Weavers]]

Among Voidlands navigators, one phrase is often repeated:

> “If the crystal changes shape when you blink,  
> stop looking at it.”
