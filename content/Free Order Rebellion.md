# Free Order Rebellion

The Free Order Rebellion, commonly known as FOR, was a large resistance movement formed to oppose the growing authoritarian rule of the [[Terrorism Control Agency]] during the early conflicts of the [[Omicron Galaxy]]. Comprised of rebels, defectors, mercenaries, civilians, and former military personnel, the organization became the primary force resisting TCA occupation and political control.

Founded under the leadership of [[Jordan Thompson]], the rebellion rapidly expanded across multiple systems as public opposition to the TCA intensified. Unlike the heavily structured military doctrine of the TCA, FOR relied on guerrilla warfare, sabotage, rapid strike operations, and civilian support networks to survive against a far larger enemy.

The rebellion became widely known for attacking TCA facilities, liberating occupied settlements, disrupting supply lines, and exposing classified operations carried out by the agency. Although viewed as freedom fighters by many civilians, the organization also employed brutal tactics during the war, including bombings, assassinations, and large-scale raids against TCA infrastructure.

As the conflict escalated, the war between FOR and the TCA devastated numerous worlds and pushed large portions of the galaxy into instability. The final stages of the conflict culminated during the events of [[Virex Prime]], where both factions suffered catastrophic losses.

Following the collapse of the war, surviving members of FOR and the TCA later contributed to the formation of the [[Hell Hosts]], permanently reshaping the balance of power within the galaxy.
