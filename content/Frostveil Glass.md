# Frostveil Glass

## Overview

Frostveil Glass is a rare translucent mineral native to the:

[[Ice Kingdom]]

and is renowned for its extraordinary thermal absorption and concealment properties.

Unlike conventional crystalline materials, Frostveil Glass naturally absorbs surrounding heat and light distortion, allowing it to:
- regulate temperature
- obscure detection
- reduce thermal signatures
- and stabilize frozen environments

The material became heavily integrated into:
- Ice Kingdom architecture
- stealth systems
- cryonic sanctuaries
- and defensive fortifications

Among Ice Kingdom civilizations, Frostveil Glass symbolizes:
silence,
stillness,
and concealment beneath the frozen world.

## Nature

Frostveil Glass is a highly refined cryonic mineral possessing unusual interactions with:
- thermal energy
- atmospheric moisture
- and light diffusion

The material naturally drains heat from surrounding environments while dispersing thermal signatures across its surface.

In large quantities, Frostveil Glass can create environments of:
unnatural silence and cold stability.

Researchers believe the material partially bends environmental energy around itself, producing:
- visual distortion
- muted reflections
- and reduced detectability

## Physical Appearance

Frostveil Glass commonly appears as:
- pale translucent crystal
- frosted semi-transparent mineral
- or smooth ice-like glass formations

The material reflects light softly and often appears:
- partially fogged
- blurred at the edges
- or slightly transparent beyond normal physical expectation

Under low temperatures, Frostveil Glass emits:
- faint silver-blue shimmer
- drifting frost particles
- and subtle internal glow patterns

Large structures made from the material can become nearly invisible during snowstorms or frozen atmospheric conditions.

## Discovery

Ancient Ice Kingdom records suggest Frostveil Glass was first discovered beneath the frozen caverns surrounding:
[[Northreach]]

The material rapidly became essential to:
- defensive architecture
- stealth infrastructure
- and preservation sanctuaries

Over centuries, Ice Kingdom engineers refined the mineral into one of their most iconic construction materials.

## Properties

Frostveil Glass possesses several highly unusual characteristics.

### Thermal Absorption
The material rapidly absorbs and disperses surrounding heat.

---

### Concealment Distortion
Frostveil Glass subtly distorts:
- visibility
- reflections
- and thermal detection systems

---

### Environmental Stabilization
Large formations can maintain stable cold environments for extended periods.

---

### Sound Dampening
The material naturally suppresses echoes and environmental noise.

---

### Cryonic Compatibility
Highly effective within:
- cryonic chambers
- frozen vaults
- and preservation systems

## Architectural Use

The Ice Kingdom extensively utilized Frostveil Glass in:
- fortress walls
- hidden sanctuaries
- observation towers
- archive vaults
- and frozen city structures

Buildings constructed from the material often appear:
- ghostlike
- partially concealed
- or blended into surrounding ice formations

Many abandoned Ice Kingdom ruins remain difficult to detect even from orbit.

## Stealth Technology

Frostveil Glass became highly valued for its:
stealth applications.

The material is capable of reducing:
- infrared detection
- thermal scans
- energy reflection
- and atmospheric visibility

Some Ice Kingdom scouts and guardians reportedly wore:
Frostveil-integrated armor
designed for movement across frozen battlefields undetected.

## Preservation Sanctuaries

Cryonic vaults and preservation chambers frequently incorporated Frostveil Glass to:
- isolate thermal fluctuation
- stabilize preserved environments
- and conceal sensitive locations

Certain hidden sanctuaries beneath the Ice Kingdom are believed to remain undiscovered due to the material’s concealment properties.

## Relationship to the Ice Kingdom

Within the:
[[Ice Kingdom]]

Frostveil Glass is associated with:
- secrecy
- endurance
- memory
- and isolation

Many ancient Ice Kingdom philosophies viewed concealment not as weakness—
but survival.

This belief heavily influenced the material’s widespread use throughout their civilization.

## Corruption Resistance

Frostveil Glass demonstrates moderate resistance to:
- environmental corruption
- thermal destabilization
- and atmospheric decay

However, exposure to:
[[Element 95]]

may cause the material to develop:
- corrupted frost growths
- unstable reflections
- or hallucination-inducing visual distortions

Some corrupted Frostveil ruins reportedly appear:
larger internally than externally.

## Modern Rarity

Pure Frostveil Glass is extremely difficult to acquire in the modern era.

Most surviving deposits remain:
- buried beneath frozen regions
- hidden inside ancient Ice Kingdom ruins
- or locked within abandoned preservation vaults

Modern stealth engineers and collectors highly value the material due to its unusual concealment capabilities.

## Common Theories

The exact formation process of Frostveil Glass remains uncertain.

Popular theories suggest:
- it formed naturally under extreme cryonic pressure
- it was engineered using lost Ice Kingdom technology
- or it partially exists between solid matter and frozen atmospheric state

Some researchers believe the material subtly bends perception itself.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Frostveil Glass is known as:
- the hidden crystal of the Ice Kingdom
- one of the most effective thermal-concealment materials ever discovered
- and a substance capable of making entire structures vanish into frozen landscapes

Among Ice Kingdom survivors, one phrase is commonly associated with the material:

> “The cold hides what the world should not find.”
