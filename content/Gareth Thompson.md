# Gareth Thompson

**Full Name:** Gareth Thompson  
**Known As:** Commissioner Thompson  
**Species:** [[Humans|Human]]  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** 46  
**Height:** 6'0"  
**Weight:** 88 kg  
**Faction:** [[Protection and Crime Enforcement]]  
**Rank:** Commissioner  
**Occupation:** Law Enforcement Officer  
**Relationship Status:** Married  
**Status:** Active  

**Gareth Thompson**, commonly addressed as **Commissioner Thompson**, is a senior officer within [[Protection and Crime Enforcement]] and a veteran of interstellar law enforcement.

Having spent much of his adult life dealing with organised crime, armed violence, civil disorder, and other threats to populated worlds, Thompson has developed an uncompromising approach to maintaining order.

He believes that laws which cannot be enforced are ultimately meaningless.

This philosophy has made him an effective commissioner.

It has also made him controversial.

## Appearance

Thompson is a heavily built human male standing approximately six feet tall and weighing around 88 kilograms.

He has light skin, brown eyes, and black hair normally maintained in a short crew cut.

Although still physically strong, Thompson's build has become somewhat heavier with age as his responsibilities have increasingly shifted from routine field operations toward senior command.

He nevertheless maintains sufficient physical conditioning to participate directly in operations when necessary.

## Personality

Thompson is cold, controlled, pragmatic, and highly disciplined.

Years of law-enforcement work have left him with little patience for intimidation, excuses, or unnecessary theatrics.

He tends to speak plainly and expects personnel beneath his authority to do the same.

Thompson rarely allows emotion to influence his behaviour while on duty.

This can make him appear detached even during situations involving significant loss of life.

His apparent coldness does not necessarily originate from a lack of concern.

Thompson believes that a commissioner who becomes emotionally overwhelmed by every crisis will eventually become incapable of making difficult decisions.

Whether he has taken that philosophy too far is a matter of disagreement among those who work with him.

## Career

Thompson is a veteran officer with decades of experience in interstellar law enforcement.

He began his career considerably closer to ordinary policing and gradually progressed through PCE command structures.

His career has exposed him to numerous forms of criminal activity, including organised crime, armed offenders, trafficking networks, violent unrest, and threats extending beyond the capabilities of conventional local authorities.

These experiences gradually shaped Thompson's increasingly uncompromising philosophy toward law enforcement.

Eventually, he achieved the rank of Commissioner.

Despite his senior position, Thompson has not forgotten his earlier experience as an operational officer.

He has little patience for senior officials who issue commands without understanding what those commands require from personnel on the ground.

## Commissioner

As a Commissioner within PCE, Thompson possesses extensive authority over personnel and law-enforcement operations under his jurisdiction.

His responsibilities extend far beyond personally pursuing criminals.

He oversees deployments, major investigations, tactical responses, interdepartmental coordination, resource allocation, and operations involving threats too significant for ordinary patrol personnel.

Thompson's position frequently requires him to balance public safety, legal procedure, political pressure, and the immediate realities faced by officers in the field.

He has developed a reputation for prioritising results and public security when these interests come into conflict.

## Philosophy

Thompson believes strongly in the necessity of law and social order.

However, his interpretation of enforcement is considerably harsher than that of many PCE officers.

He is willing to authorise aggressive tactics when he believes conventional approaches would unnecessarily endanger civilians or personnel.

Thompson does not view force as inherently desirable.

He views it as a tool.

When negotiation works, he will negotiate.

When arrests can be conducted safely, he expects arrests.

When a dangerous situation can be contained without unnecessary casualties, containment is preferable.

But when Thompson determines that extreme force is genuinely necessary, he is willing to authorise it.

This willingness has made him respected by some officers and deeply uncomfortable to others.

## Extreme Methods

Thompson's reputation for extreme methods has followed him throughout the later stages of his career.

These methods do not mean that Thompson disregards law entirely.

He remains a law-enforcement officer rather than a military commander.

However, Thompson has repeatedly demonstrated a willingness to interpret the full extent of PCE authority aggressively during major emergencies.

He believes hesitation can kill just as effectively as recklessness.

Critics argue that this philosophy risks allowing exceptional measures to become increasingly normal.

Thompson's response is generally that people criticising his decisions rarely had to make them.

## Leadership

Thompson is demanding toward personnel beneath his authority.

He expects discipline, competence, and accountability.

Mistakes made through inexperience can be corrected.

Negligence is considerably less tolerated.

Thompson is particularly hostile toward officers who abuse their authority for personal reasons.

Whatever controversy surrounds his own methods, he considers corruption fundamentally incompatible with law enforcement.

An officer who accepts bribes, abuses civilians, falsifies evidence, or uses their position for personal gain should expect little protection from Thompson.

The badge, in his view, grants authority.

It does not grant immunity.

## Tactical Response

Thompson possesses the authority to approve or coordinate specialised PCE tactical deployments during high-risk incidents.

His relationship with tactical personnel is generally professional and direct.

He expects tactical commanders to provide honest assessments rather than simply agreeing with senior leadership.

If an operation is unsafe, Thompson wants to know.

If a plan is flawed, he expects someone to say so.

Once a final decision has been made, however, he expects it to be executed.

## P2 PSP

Thompson carries a **P2 Particle Systematic Pistol**, commonly abbreviated as the **P2 PSP**.

The weapon serves as his standard sidearm and has remained associated with him throughout much of his career.

Although his current position rarely requires routine frontline combat, Thompson continues to carry the weapon while on duty.

He remains proficient with it.

## Field Experience

Despite being considerably more valuable as a commander than as another officer carrying a weapon, Thompson is not entirely confined to administrative work.

During major incidents, he has occasionally appeared at active command sites and coordinated operations directly.

He generally avoids unnecessarily inserting himself into tactical situations.

Thompson has nothing to prove by kicking down a door himself.

If circumstances require him to draw his sidearm, something has probably already gone badly wrong.

## Marriage

Thompson is married.

He keeps his family life largely separate from his professional identity, and comparatively little information concerning his spouse is publicly associated with his career.

Personnel who know Thompson primarily through PCE occasionally find the existence of a private domestic life surprising given his notoriously cold professional demeanour.

Thompson appears to prefer keeping the two worlds separate.

## Reputation

Commissioner Thompson is regarded as an effective but controversial figure within PCE.

Supporters point toward his experience, decisiveness, resistance to corruption, and willingness to make difficult decisions during dangerous situations.

Critics question whether his increasingly aggressive approach to enforcement sometimes places too much emphasis on maintaining order at the expense of restraint.

Thompson himself appears largely uninterested in being universally liked.

His concern is whether the worlds under PCE protection remain safe enough for ordinary people to live on them.

He has spent decades enforcing the law.

The experience has not made him kinder.

It has made him certain that somebody has to.
