# Genesis

Genesis is the capital world and primary stronghold of the [[Angel Swords]], serving as the political, religious, industrial, and military center of the faction. It is one of the most sacred planets under Angel Sword control and is viewed by its people as the birthplace of their civilization’s modern identity.

Unlike the heavily industrial fortress worlds of the [[Hell Hosts]], Genesis combines military infrastructure with deeply spiritual architecture and cultural tradition.

The planet is inhabited exclusively by members of the Angel Swords.

## Judgment Day

Approximately one thousand years ago, Genesis suffered a catastrophic planetary event known as:

Judgment Day

The disaster ravaged the planet’s surface and resulted in the deaths of approximately:

87.66% of all life on Genesis.

The true cause of Judgment Day remains heavily debated within Angel Sword history.

Some believe it was:
- divine punishment
- an ancient superweapon
- a failed experiment
- a cosmic anomaly
- or the result of forgotten internal conflict

Large portions of the planet still bear the scars of the catastrophe.

Entire regions remain:
- burned
- abandoned
- irradiated
- structurally ruined
- or permanently uninhabitable

Despite the devastation, the [[Angel Swords]] rebuilt Genesis and transformed it into the heart of their civilization.

## Role Within the Angel Swords

Genesis functions as:
- the capital of the [[Angel Swords]]
- primary military manufacturing world
- central fleet construction hub
- religious and ceremonial center
- headquarters of Angel Sword leadership
- cultural homeworld of the faction

Much of the faction’s:
- weapons
- armor
- warships
- military systems
- and strategic infrastructure

are produced directly on Genesis.

## Environment

Genesis is a world of striking contrasts.

Parts of the planet contain:
- massive fortified sanctuaries
- cathedral-like megastructures
- towering citadels
- industrial forge districts
- military shipyards
- sacred ceremonial grounds

While other regions remain devastated remnants of Judgment Day:
- scorched wastelands
- collapsed cities
- shattered landscapes
- ruined industrial sectors
- massive impact zones

The atmosphere of Genesis is often described as:
- solemn
- disciplined
- reverent
- militarized

The memory of Judgment Day continues to shape [[Angel Sword]] culture even after a millennium.

## Architecture & Culture

Genesis is heavily defined by Angel Sword ideology and symbolism.

Structures throughout the planet commonly feature:
- white and gold metallic construction
- monumental statues
- towering spires
- ceremonial halls
- illuminated sanctuaries
- massive defensive walls

Military discipline and spiritual devotion are deeply intertwined within everyday life.

Many Angel Sword citizens view themselves not simply as soldiers—
but as guardians chosen to prevent another catastrophe like Judgment Day.

## Military Presence

Genesis is one of the most heavily defended worlds controlled by the [[Angel Swords]].

The planet maintains:
- massive orbital fleets
- planetary defense systems
- fortified military districts
- defensive satellite networks
- fleet deployment platforms
- standing military forces across multiple sectors

Despite its spiritual significance, Genesis remains fully prepared for large-scale war at all times.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Genesis is known as:
- the sacred capital of the [[Angel Swords]]
- a world scarred by ancient catastrophe
- one of the largest military-industrial planets in the region
- a symbol of survival and rebirth
- a heavily fortified religious stronghold

To the [[Angel Swords]], Genesis is more than a planet.

It is proof that even after near extinction—
their civilization endured.
