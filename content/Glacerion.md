# Glacerion

Glacerion is one of the largest industrial and resource worlds of the [[Ice Kingdom]], known throughout the northern sectors as:

The Spireworld

Unlike many frozen worlds dominated by wastelands and ruin, Glacerion is a thriving vertical civilization built directly into colossal glacial spires stretching kilometers into the sky.

The Ice Kingdom did not conquer Glacerion—

It built within it.

Across the planet, entire cities, forges, strongholds, and industrial complexes are carved directly into ancient ice formations linked together through elevated bridges, gravity elevators, and atmospheric transit platforms.

Where [[Frostheim]] commands and [[Northreach]] defends—

Glacerion provides.

## Environment

Glacerion is a world of:
- colossal ice spires
- endless glaciers
- frozen mountain ranges
- deep subglacial caverns
- perpetual winter
- high-altitude storms
- aurora-filled skies

Towering frozen structures dominate the horizon across nearly every continent, with some spires extending above cloud level.

Strong winds and freezing atmospheric conditions make surface travel difficult outside secured routes and transit systems.

## Spire Cities

The cities of Glacerion are constructed directly into the massive glacial towers rising from the frozen surface.

These vertical settlements contain:
- residential districts
- industrial sectors
- forge-citadels
- research facilities
- military barracks
- transit hubs
- elevated skyways

Many structures are suspended across enormous gaps between ice spires through reinforced bridges and gravity-assisted platforms.

At night, the frozen cities glow with blue and white illumination visible across the surrounding glaciers.

## Industrial Heart of the Ice Kingdom

Glacerion serves as one of the primary industrial centers of the Ice Kingdom.

The planet supports:
- military production
- fleet material refinement
- weapons manufacturing
- engineering development
- large-scale resource extraction

Much of the Ice Kingdom’s military infrastructure relies heavily on materials and systems produced on Glacerion.

## Frostfoundries

At the heart of Glacerion’s industrial sectors lie the:

Frostfoundries

Massive forge complexes responsible for producing:
- advanced armor
- weapons systems
- military components
- starship materials
- Frostguard equipment

Unlike conventional factories, Frostfoundries combine geothermal energy, cryonic engineering, and ancient Ice Kingdom technology.

Many forge sectors operate deep beneath the ice where geothermal heat powers enormous industrial chambers.

## Deep-Core Mining

Glacerion contains extensive resource deposits buried beneath kilometers of ancient ice.

Major extraction operations recover:
- Glacerite
- Cryosteel
- Bluefire Crystals
- geothermal energy reserves
- rare isotopes

Deep-core mining facilities stretch far below the surface into ancient frozen geological layers.

Some lower excavation zones remain unstable or only partially explored.

## Atmospheric Docks

Large portions of the Ice Kingdom’s fleet production and transport infrastructure operate from elevated atmospheric docks built high among the spires.

These launch platforms support:
- military deployment vessels
- cargo fleets
- patrol ships
- northern exploration craft

Many ports exist above the cloud layer itself.

## Strategic Importance

Glacerion is considered one of the most strategically important worlds in the Ice Kingdom because it:
- supplies military resources across the kingdom
- supports fleet construction and repair
- houses advanced engineering facilities
- produces Frostguard equipment
- powers major industrial operations
- supports northern expansion and defense

Without Glacerion, much of the Ice Kingdom’s infrastructure would struggle to function at full capacity.

## Population & Society

Unlike the solemn stillness of [[Frostheim]] or the militarized vigilance of [[Northreach]], Glacerion is constantly active.

Its population consists primarily of:
- engineers
- miners
- forge workers
- military manufacturers
- researchers
- industrial guilds
- Frostguard patrol forces

The people of Glacerion are known for resilience, discipline, and technical expertise.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Glacerion is known as:
- the Spireworld of the Ice Kingdom
- one of the largest frozen industrial worlds in known space
- the production heart of the northern kingdoms
- a civilization carved into eternal ice

To the Ice Kingdom, Glacerion represents strength built not through conquest—

But through endurance and creation.
