# Glacians

**Classification:** Sapient biological species  
**Origin:** [[Frostheim]]  
**Primary Region:** [[Ice Kingdom]]  
**Distribution:** Ice Kingdom and surrounding territories; smaller populations throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]]  
**Status:** Extant  

Glacians are a sapient humanoid species originating from [[Frostheim]] and are the dominant native species of the [[Ice Kingdom]].

Having evolved within extremely cold environments, Glacians possess numerous biological adaptations allowing them to survive temperatures that would rapidly incapacitate or kill most humans.

Although strongly associated with the Ice Kingdom, Glacians are a species rather than a political identity. Glacian communities and individuals can be found throughout other regions of the [[Omicron Galaxy]], and not every Glacian considers themselves a citizen of the Kingdom.

Glacians are physically similar enough to humans and several other humanoid species for their emotions and body language to be easily recognisable, although their appearance and internal biology contain several significant differences.

## Appearance

Glacians possess a broadly humanoid body structure with two arms, two legs, forward-facing eyes, and facial structures comparable to humans.

Their most immediately recognisable characteristic is their skin.

Glacian skin commonly ranges from extremely pale white and silver-grey to subtle shades of blue and blue-grey. Colouration varies considerably between individuals and populations.

Their eyes are particularly adapted to environments dominated by snow, ice, and reflected light. Common eye colours include pale blue, grey, silver, and other cold-toned shades.

Glacian hair varies in colour, although white, silver, grey, pale blond, and darker contrasting colours are common.

They generally possess slightly denser and heavier builds than humans of comparable height, reflecting adaptations required for heat retention and survival in hostile climates.

## Cold Adaptation

Glacian physiology is extraordinarily resistant to low temperatures.

Their circulatory system, metabolism, body tissues, and distribution of insulating biological material allow them to maintain internal temperatures in conditions considered dangerously cold to most humanoid species.

Blood flow can be selectively reduced in exposed extremities while preserving sufficient circulation to prevent tissue damage.

Their bodies are also capable of rapidly altering metabolic activity in response to environmental temperature.

As a result, temperatures requiring humans to wear specialised protective equipment may be considered comfortable by Glacians.

This resistance is not unlimited.

Extreme cold can still injure or kill a Glacian, particularly following prolonged exposure.

## Body Temperature

Glacians maintain a lower average internal body temperature than humans.

Their skin frequently feels noticeably cool to the touch even when the individual is completely healthy.

This has occasionally caused confusion among human medical personnel unfamiliar with Glacian physiology.

Medical equipment intended for humans must therefore be correctly calibrated when treating Glacian patients.

## Heat

The adaptations that allow Glacians to survive extreme cold create disadvantages in warmer environments.

Glacians are significantly more susceptible to overheating than humans and many other humanoid species.

Temperatures considered merely uncomfortable by humans can become physically exhausting to a Glacian after prolonged exposure.

Extremely hot environments may require specialised cooling equipment, climate-controlled clothing, hydration systems, or environmental suits.

Glacians living permanently on warmer worlds frequently adapt behaviourally by remaining indoors during hotter periods and maintaining considerably colder homes and workplaces.

## Eyesight

Glacian eyes are adapted to the unusual lighting conditions of frozen environments.

They possess excellent visual performance in low-light conditions and strong resistance to glare produced by snow and ice.

Their pupils can adjust rapidly between dark environments and highly reflective landscapes.

However, intense artificial lighting can sometimes cause discomfort.

Protective eyewear is common among Glacians travelling on exceptionally bright or hot worlds.

## Metabolism

Glacians possess a highly adaptable metabolism capable of producing considerable amounts of internal heat when necessary.

Food consumption can increase substantially during prolonged exposure to extreme cold or periods of intense physical activity.

Traditional Glacian cuisine therefore contains numerous high-energy foods originally developed to support survival in harsh environments.

Modern technology has reduced the necessity of these diets, although many remain culturally significant throughout the [[Ice Kingdom]].

## Physical Characteristics

Glacians generally possess dense musculature and strong skeletal structures.

These traits originally developed in response to Frostheim's environment and gravity, although variation between individuals remains considerable.

Glacians are not inherently stronger than every human or other humanoid species.

However, the average Glacian tends to possess excellent physical endurance in cold environments where other species rapidly lose performance.

## Lifespan

Glacian lifespans are broadly comparable to those of other advanced humanoid species, although modern medicine has significantly increased healthy life expectancy.

Exact lifespan varies according to genetics, living conditions, medical access, occupation, and planetary environment.

## Frostheim

[[Frostheim]] is regarded as the ancestral homeworld of the Glacian species.

The planet's extreme climate played a major role in Glacian evolution and remains deeply influential upon their cultures.

Frozen oceans, immense glaciers, snow-covered mountain ranges, seasonal storms, and long periods of extreme cold shaped early Glacian civilisation.

Despite these conditions, Frostheim developed into the political and cultural centre of the [[Ice Kingdom]].

Glacians often maintain a strong cultural connection to Frostheim even when their families have lived elsewhere for generations.

## The Ice Kingdom

The [[Ice Kingdom]] is the largest civilisation historically associated with the Glacian species.

Its territory contains numerous frozen worlds, colonies, military installations, mining settlements, and population centres.

Although Glacians form the foundation of the Kingdom's population and culture, the Ice Kingdom is not necessarily exclusively Glacian.

Likewise, Glacians living outside the Kingdom are not automatically subjects of its government.

Species and citizenship are considered separate identities.

## Culture

There is no single universal Glacian culture.

Thousands of years of settlement across different worlds have produced significant regional variation.

However, many Glacian societies traditionally place considerable importance upon community, preparation, hospitality, resilience, and cooperation.

These values are often associated with the conditions faced by early Glacian settlements, where survival during severe winters depended heavily upon cooperation between individuals and communities.

Offering shelter during dangerous weather remains an important tradition in many Glacian cultures.

Historically, refusing emergency shelter during a major storm could be considered an extremely serious social offence.

## Architecture

Traditional Glacian architecture is heavily influenced by the environments of the Ice Kingdom.

Settlements commonly incorporate extensive insulation, underground infrastructure, protected transport routes, enclosed public spaces, and structures designed to withstand enormous quantities of snow and ice.

Modern Glacian cities can be enormous.

Some extend beneath glaciers or into mountains, while others rise from frozen landscapes behind massive environmental barriers.

Ice itself is sometimes incorporated into architecture, although advanced structural materials remain responsible for most load-bearing construction.

## Clothing

Traditional Glacian clothing varies greatly between regions.

Because Glacians possess significant natural resistance to cold, their clothing does not always appear sufficiently protective by human standards.

A Glacian may comfortably wear relatively light clothing in temperatures where a human would require extensive thermal protection.

Heavy traditional clothing still exists, particularly in the most extreme environments and during severe weather.

Clothing also carries cultural, professional, regional, and political significance throughout the Ice Kingdom.

## Military Service

Glacians form a substantial portion of the [[Ice Kingdom]]'s military forces.

Their natural resistance to cold provides considerable advantages during operations on frozen worlds.

Glacian forces are particularly effective in environments where enemy soldiers depend heavily upon heating systems, insulated armour, and specialised equipment.

This environmental advantage has played an important role in the defence of Ice Kingdom territory throughout its history.

Glacians also serve within military organisations outside the Ice Kingdom.

## Relations with Other Species

Glacians interact extensively with humans, androids, Ashens, Elysarians, and numerous other inhabitants of the Omicron Galaxy.

Cultural misunderstandings occasionally arise from differences in environmental preference.

Glacian ships and buildings are frequently maintained at temperatures other species consider uncomfortably cold.

Conversely, Glacians often find human-controlled environments unnecessarily warm.

Mixed-species facilities generally compromise through localised climate systems.

## Glacians Outside the Ice Kingdom

Large Glacian communities exist outside their ancestral territory.

Some are descended from migrants, explorers, traders, soldiers, or settlers who left the Ice Kingdom generations earlier.

Others travel temporarily for employment, education, diplomacy, military service, research, or exploration.

Glacians living on warm planets frequently use specialised environmental technology to remain comfortable.

Over generations, some populations have become considerably more tolerant of warmer climates, although their fundamental cold-adapted physiology remains.

## Identity

Glacian identity varies considerably between individuals.

Some strongly identify with the Ice Kingdom and its traditions.

Others identify primarily with their homeworld, profession, community, or another political faction.

A Glacian may therefore be culturally Glacian without supporting the Ice Kingdom, or may consider themselves a loyal citizen of another civilisation entirely.

Their species predates the modern political structure of the Ice Kingdom and cannot be defined solely by it.
