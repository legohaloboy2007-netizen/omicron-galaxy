# Graveyard V19

Graveyard V19 is a supermassive black hole located at the center of the [[Omicron Galaxy]]. Surrounded by distorted systems, unstable gravitational fields, and dangerous anomaly zones, it is considered one of the most mysterious locations in the known galaxy.

The black hole has become the subject of countless myths, scientific studies, and lost expeditions. Strange signals, unexplained energy readings, and reports of missing fleets have caused many to believe that something exists beyond the event horizon.

Entire ships and crews have vanished near Graveyard V19 without explanation. Some believe the black hole predates the galaxy itself, while others claim it is connected to ancient myths such as the [[The Crimson Order]], [[The Veil]], or other unknown cosmic phenomena.

Most civilian and military vessels avoid the region entirely.

## Known For
- Being located at the center of the galaxy
- Dangerous gravitational distortions
- Missing expeditions and fleets
- Strange signals and anomaly readings
- Endless myths and conspiracy theories
