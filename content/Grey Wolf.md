# Grey Wolf

## Overview

**Real Name:** Unknown

**Age:** Late 40s

**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]

**Unit:** [[Wolf Unit|The Wolf Unit]]

**Rank:** Commander

Grey Wolf is the eldest member of the:
[[Wolf Unit]]

and serves as its tracker, hunter, and mentor.

Unlike:
[[Black Wolf]]
and
[[White Wolf]]

Grey Wolf is not biologically related to the twins.

Despite this, he is considered family by both and is widely regarded as the man who raised them.

Across the galaxy, Grey Wolf has become the subject of countless rumors, stories, and military reports.

Some describe him as a tracker.

Others describe him as a ghost.

Most simply describe him as:

> "The last person you want hunting you."

---

## Appearance

Grey Wolf appears older than most frontline soldiers.

Years of warfare, tracking, and survival have left visible marks upon him.

### Notable Features

- Grey hair
- Brown eyes
- Tired appearance
- Weathered features
- Calm expression

Unlike many commanders, Grey Wolf makes little effort to appear intimidating.

The effect is often the opposite.

### Clothing & Equipment

Grey Wolf commonly wears:

- Long dark hunter's coat
- Weathered combat armor
- Multiple knife sheaths
- Tactical survival equipment

Small crow feathers are worked into various sections of his equipment.

Whether symbolic or practical remains unclear.

### Distinguishing Feature

Personnel frequently report the same observation:

> There always seems to be a crow nearby.

No official explanation exists.

---

## Personality

Grey Wolf is:

- Observant
- Patient
- Protective
- Quietly caring
- Thoughtful

Many mistake him for being cold.

The reality is far different.

Grey Wolf simply spends more time observing than speaking.

He notices things other people miss.

### The Observer

Grey Wolf is often the first person to notice:

- Hidden injuries
- Exhaustion
- Stress
- Lack of sleep
- Missed meals

He rarely comments on these observations directly.

Instead, he quietly finds ways to help.

Many soldiers have received assistance from Grey Wolf without realizing it came from him.

---

## Combat Style

Grey Wolf specializes in:

- Tracking
- Psychological warfare
- Target pursuit
- Stealth operations
- Close-quarters combat

Unlike many hunters, Grey Wolf rarely rushes.

He prefers patience.

Targets often spend days believing they are safe.

Only later do they realize the hunt began long ago.

### Weapons

#### Throwing Knives

Grey Wolf possesses exceptional accuracy with throwing blades.

Many targets never see the attack that kills them.

---

#### Silenced Pistol

Used when stealth and precision are required.

---

#### Combat Knives

Like the rest of the Wolf Unit, Grey Wolf is highly proficient with bladed weapons.

---

## Abilities

### Eyes of the Hunter

Grey Wolf can perceive through the eyes of nearby crows and similar birds.

This allows him to gather information from extraordinary distances and angles.

---

### Hunter's Mark

Grey Wolf can place a mark upon a target.

Once marked, hiding becomes nearly impossible.

Many victims report strange feelings of being watched long before contact occurs.

---

### Enhanced Speed

Grey Wolf possesses extraordinary acceleration and close-range combat capability.

Combined with his tracking skills, this makes escape extremely difficult.

---

## History

Much of Grey Wolf's early history remains unknown.

At some point during his military career, he encountered two young survivors following a traumatic event.

Those survivors would later become:

- [[Black Wolf]]
- [[White Wolf]]

Rather than abandoning them, Grey Wolf took responsibility for their care and training.

Over the years he became:

- Mentor
- Teacher
- Guardian
- Family

The twins rarely speak about this period.

Grey Wolf never does.

---

## Relationships

### [[Black Wolf]]

Grey Wolf is one of the few people Black Wolf listens to without argument.

Their relationship resembles that of a father and son more than commander and subordinate.

Grey Wolf helped shape the hunter Black Wolf would eventually become.

---

### [[White Wolf]]

White Wolf views Grey Wolf as family.

He taught her many of the skills she uses today and remains one of the few individuals whose advice she genuinely values.

---

### [[Predator]]

Predator values Grey Wolf's effectiveness and experience.

Although their methods differ greatly, both respect the results the other achieves.

---

### [[Destiny]]

Destiny acknowledges Grey Wolf as one of Hell Hosts' most capable specialists.

The two rarely interact directly but maintain mutual professional respect.

---

### [[Red Wind]]

Red Wind finds Grey Wolf unsettling.

Not because he is threatening.

Because he often seems to know things he should not.

Grey Wolf remains entirely unaware of this.

---

## Habits

- Feeds birds regularly
- Watches the sky for long periods
- Stares into the distance while thinking
- Appears to know where everyone is
- Checks on people without announcing it
- Rarely asks for help

---

## Off-Duty Life

Grey Wolf prefers solitude.

When not deployed, he is often found:

- Sitting somewhere quiet
- Watching the sky
- Drinking coffee or whiskey
- Observing wildlife

Unlike many soldiers, he is rarely bored.

He can sit in silence for hours without issue.

---

## Likes

- Rain
- Long walks
- Wildlife
- Quiet environments
- Teaching
- Watching people improve
- Coffee
- Whiskey

---

## Dislikes

- Wastefulness
- Needless cruelty
- Arrogance
- Loud crowds
- Seeing people neglect themselves

---

## Flaws

### Self-Reliance

Grey Wolf rarely allows others to help him.

No matter how difficult the burden becomes, he insists on carrying it himself.

This often leaves him isolated.

Many people rely on Grey Wolf.

Very few realize Grey Wolf relies on no one.

---

## Teaching

One of Grey Wolf's greatest passions is teaching.

Although he rarely admits it openly, he enjoys:

- Passing on skills
- Sharing experience
- Watching others grow

This is especially true regarding:

[[Black Wolf]]
and
[[White Wolf]]

whose development remains one of the things he is most proud of.

He would never admit this aloud.

---

## Reputation

### Within Hell Hosts

Grey Wolf is respected as:

- Experienced
- Reliable
- Wise
- Extremely dangerous

Many younger soldiers seek his advice.

Most never realize they are doing so.

---

### Outside Hell Hosts

Enemy forces often describe Grey Wolf as:

- Untraceable
- Omnipresent
- Impossible to evade

Several intelligence reports describe him as:

> "The hunt itself."
