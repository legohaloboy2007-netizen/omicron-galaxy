# Havoc

**Real Name:** Unknown  
**Codename:** Havoc  
**Species:** [[Humans|Human]]  
**Gender:** Female  
**Height:** 5'9"  
**Faction:** [[Free World Order]]  
**Role:** Soldier  
**Status:** Active  

Havoc is a soldier serving within the [[Free World Order]] who has gained attention for her aggressive combat style, exceptional mobility, and proficiency with a lever-action shotgun.

Unlike many of the Free World Order's better-known operatives, Havoc does not currently hold a particularly senior position within the faction.

Several prominent Free World personnel have witnessed her capabilities in combat, and she is regarded as a soldier with considerable potential. However, she has yet to earn a position among the organisation's higher-ranking personnel.

## Personality

Havoc is energetic, determined, and ambitious.

She wants to prove that she is capable of operating alongside the Free World Order's more experienced soldiers, although this ambition can occasionally encourage her to take greater risks than necessary.

Despite this, Havoc is not motivated entirely by recognition.

She maintains a strong attachment to her family and carries a photograph of them whenever she is deployed.

The photograph is one of the few personal possessions she consistently carries into combat.

## Free World Order

Havoc serves primarily as a conventional combat soldier within the Free World Order.

She has participated in multiple military operations and has gradually attracted the attention of more senior personnel through her battlefield performance.

Her reputation within the organisation is still developing.

She has not yet achieved the standing held by personnel such as [[Diablo]], [[Slaughter]], or [[Killshot]], and she receives few of the privileges or responsibilities associated with senior Free World operatives.

For Havoc, this distinction is important.

She is still proving herself.

Whether she eventually rises into the upper ranks of the Free World Order remains dependent upon her future service and performance.

## Combat

Havoc specialises in aggressive close-range combat.

She relies heavily upon mobility, rapidly closing distance with enemy personnel before attacking with her shotgun.

Rather than remaining behind cover for prolonged periods, Havoc frequently moves between positions and attempts to attack opponents from unexpected directions.

Her mobility equipment makes this combat style particularly effective.

## Enhanced Mobility

Havoc's armour incorporates a specialised mobility-assistance system capable of producing brief periods of greatly increased movement speed.

The system uses powered armour assistance and enhanced movement control to allow Havoc to accelerate, reposition, and cross exposed areas considerably faster than an ordinary soldier.

It does not provide unlimited superhuman speed.

Extended use places significant strain on both the equipment and its wearer, preventing Havoc from maintaining maximum acceleration indefinitely.

The system is most effective in short bursts.

Havoc commonly uses it to cross gaps between cover, evade incoming fire, rapidly close distance, or reposition before an opponent can react.

Her extensive experience with the system allows her to perform movements that would be extremely difficult for personnel unfamiliar with the equipment.

## Lever-Action Shotgun

Havoc's signature weapon is a lever-action ballistic shotgun.

Although more modern weapon systems are widely available throughout the Omicron Galaxy, Havoc has developed considerable proficiency with the weapon and continues to favour it during combat.

The shotgun complements her mobility-focused fighting style.

Havoc frequently closes distance rapidly, fires at close range, cycles the weapon while moving, and immediately redirects toward another target.

She carries additional shells on her armour for rapid access.

The weapon's comparatively old-fashioned operating mechanism has become a recognisable part of Havoc's equipment.

## Family

Unlike many Free World personnel whose personal histories remain heavily classified or unknown, Havoc is known to maintain a connection with family outside of active military service.

She carries a photograph of her family with her during deployments.

The photograph is normally stored securely within her armour rather than displayed openly.

Havoc rarely discusses it with personnel she does not trust.

For all of her ambition within the Free World Order, the photograph serves as a reminder that her identity extends beyond her military service.

## Reputation

Havoc remains relatively unknown outside of the Free World Order compared with its senior operatives.

Within the faction, however, her name has begun circulating among personnel familiar with her combat record.

Some senior members believe she possesses the potential to advance considerably further within the organisation.

Others consider her talented but inexperienced, particularly compared with veterans who have survived years of major conflicts.

For the moment, Havoc remains somewhere between an ordinary soldier and a recognised Free World operative.

She has attracted attention.

She has not yet proven what she will become.
