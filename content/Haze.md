# Haze

> "You don't choke on the air. You choke because I changed it."

## Overview

**Nyra Voss**, known by the callsign **Haze**, is a Hell Host tactical support specialist serving within the [[Doom Corps]].

Assigned to the [[403rd Tactical Support Battalion]], Haze specializes in environmental disruption, visibility denial, toxic and gas warfare, infiltration support, and battlefield misdirection.

Her role is not primarily to destroy enemy forces directly.

Instead, she alters the conditions in which they fight.

Smoke.

Gas.

Obscured optics.

Broken sightlines.

False movement.

Contaminated routes.

Confused communications.

By the time hostile forces understand what Haze has changed, another Hell Host unit is usually already exploiting it.

## Appearance

Nyra is a young human woman with dark hair, dark eyes, and a lean build.

Outside combat, she appears considerably less intimidating than many Doom Corps personnel.

This changes quickly once she equips her field gear.

Haze typically wears dark tactical armour designed around chemical protection, mobility, and close-range support.

Her helmet includes:

- Respiratory protection
- Environmental filtration
- Enhanced optics
- Chemical detection
- Low-visibility targeting systems

Her armour frequently incorporates camouflage patterns suited to urban and industrial environments.

Nyra also carries multiple sealed canisters containing smoke, irritants, obscurants, and specialized battlefield chemicals.

---

## Personality

Haze is confident, observant, sharp-tongued, playful, and unpredictable.

She enjoys getting under people's skin.

Sometimes verbally.

Sometimes professionally.

Nyra possesses a flirtatious and teasing personality off duty and is usually comfortable provoking reactions from people simply because she finds them interesting.

This behaviour disappears quickly when operations begin.

Haze becomes intensely focused once deployed.

She understands that environmental disruption affects allies as easily as enemies if handled poorly.

Every gas deployment, smoke screen, or obscured route must therefore be controlled carefully.

Behind the jokes is a soldier who knows exactly what she is doing.

# The 403rd Tactical Support Battalion

Haze serves within the **403rd Tactical Support Battalion**, a Doom Corps formation specializing in battlefield support through environmental and tactical disruption.

The unit operates under the principle:

> **"We control what they can't see."**

Its responsibilities include:

- Smoke deployment
- Gas warfare
- Visibility denial
- Environmental manipulation
- Tactical misdirection
- Chemical defence
- Infiltration support
- Extraction support
- Area denial

The 403rd frequently operates alongside assault, reconnaissance, and special-purpose units.

Their presence is rarely the main event.

It is what makes the main event possible.

---

# Environmental Disruption

Haze specializes in altering local battlefield conditions.

Her goal is to make the environment work against hostile forces.

This may involve:

- Obscuring sightlines
- Filling corridors with smoke
- Denying access routes
- Disrupting thermal optics
- Forcing enemies from cover
- Separating hostile formations
- Creating artificial concealment

Nyra constantly considers:

- Wind direction
- Ventilation
- Air pressure
- Visibility
- Building layout
- Friendly positions
- Environmental hazards

Poor gas deployment can harm allies.

Haze treats this as unacceptable.

---

# Visibility Denial

One of Nyra's most common battlefield roles is controlling what enemy personnel can see.

She uses specialized smoke and obscurant systems capable of interfering with:

- Normal vision
- Thermal optics
- Infrared sensors
- Laser designation
- Automated targeting
- Drone cameras

Different environments require different compounds.

A smoke system that works outdoors may be useless inside a sealed industrial complex.

Haze adapts accordingly.

---

# Toxic and Gas Warfare

Nyra is extensively trained in the controlled use of toxic battlefield agents.

These may include:

- Irritant gas
- Disorientation agents
- Respiratory suppressants
- Sensor-obscuring compounds
- Temporary incapacitating agents

Hell Host doctrine requires strict control over deployment conditions.

Haze is trained to calculate spread and duration before release.

She strongly distinguishes between **controlled tactical chemical use** and indiscriminate contamination.

The battlefield must remain usable after the objective is complete.

---

# Area Denial

Haze can temporarily render specific routes extremely difficult or dangerous for hostile personnel to enter.

This allows her to:

- Seal corridors
- Control intersections
- Protect extraction routes
- Prevent reinforcement
- Redirect enemy movement
- Create temporary defensive boundaries

This ability becomes particularly useful during urban combat.

A single gas-filled corridor can force an entire enemy squad toward a route already covered by Hell Host weapons.

Nyra refers to this as:

**"Helping them choose."**

---

# Tactical Misdirection

Haze does not rely exclusively on chemical agents.

She is also skilled at using battlefield conditions to create false impressions.

This may involve:

- Smoke suggesting movement where none exists
- False thermal signatures
- Concealed unit movement
- Artificial noise
- Redirected sensor attention

An enemy does not need to be blinded completely.

Sometimes it is enough to make them look the wrong way.

---

# Infiltration Support

Haze frequently assists stealth-oriented personnel.

Her environmental systems can conceal movement or temporarily disable enemy observation.

This makes her particularly effective when supporting operators such as [[Whisper|Elias Virek]].

Whisper moves silently.

Haze makes sure nobody is looking in the correct direction.

The two approaches complement one another extremely well.

---

# Relationship with Whisper

Haze has operated alongside [[Whisper|Elias Virek]] during infiltration missions.

Their personalities could hardly be more different.

Elias speaks only when necessary.

Nyra occasionally speaks specifically because it is unnecessary.

Despite this, they work effectively together.

Haze can create concealment and misdirection while Whisper moves through hostile positions.

Nyra once summarized their working relationship:

> "You disappear. I make sure no one notices."

Whisper reportedly responded with a nod.

Haze considered this enthusiastic.

---

# Relationship with Hardfall

[[Hardfall|Marcus Hale]] and Haze are frequently involved in high-impact assault operations.

Their methods are almost complete opposites.

Hardfall enters loudly.

Haze controls what happens around him.

Smoke and gas can isolate defenders before Marcus breaches a position.

In return, Hardfall can remove threats that would prevent Haze from establishing environmental control.

Their conversations are considerably louder than their tactical planning needs to be.

Haze once described the arrangement as:

> "You make noise. I clean the air behind you."

Marcus argued that she was using the phrase **clean the air** incorrectly.

---

# Relationship with Nexus

[[Nexus|Samuel Ardyn]] frequently coordinates Haze's deployments through battlefield telemetry.

Samuel provides information including:

- Friendly locations
- Airflow predictions
- Enemy movement
- Drone observations
- Safe deployment areas

Haze then uses this information to control visibility and movement.

Nyra describes the relationship simply:

> "I cloud their vision. You control the battlefield."

Nexus has never corrected her.

---

# Relationship with Vantage

[[Vantage|Victor Rhenford]] occasionally provides overwatch during Haze's operations.

At first glance, filling a battlefield with smoke would appear extremely inconvenient for a sniper.

Haze uses specialized compounds and planned firing lanes to prevent this.

Vantage is often given:

- Clear observation corridors
- Sensor-compatible tracking data
- Predicted enemy exits

Enemies emerging from Nyra's smoke may therefore find themselves directly inside Victor's firing window.

Haze refers to the arrangement as:

> "I set the stage. You pull the trigger."

---

# Combat Style

Haze fights through control rather than raw aggression.

She constantly manipulates:

- Visibility
- Movement
- Access
- Positioning

Nyra prefers forcing enemies into disadvantageous situations before engaging them directly.

When firefights become unavoidable, she uses suppressed weapons and close-range mobility.

Her ideal engagement is one where the enemy never clearly understands how many Hell Host personnel are present.

## Toxic / Smoke Deployment System

Haze's defining equipment consists of multiple specialized deployment canisters.

These may contain:

- Smoke
- Obscurant compounds
- Irritants
- Incapacitating agents
- Sensor-disruption aerosols

Canisters can be thrown manually or deployed through specialized launch systems depending on mission requirements.

Nyra carefully labels all compounds.

She becomes extremely irritated when other personnel move them.

---

## Combat Knife

Haze carries a standard Hell Host combat knife.

---

## Enhanced Optic Visor

Nyra's visor is specifically designed to function in environments where ordinary vision becomes unreliable.

It includes:

- Thermal imaging
- Low-light systems
- Chemical detection
- Friendly identification
- Environmental mapping

The system allows Haze to operate effectively inside conditions she created herself.

---

# Protective Systems

Because Haze regularly operates around chemical agents, her armour contains extensive filtration and protection systems.

These include:

- Sealed respiratory equipment
- Skin protection
- Environmental monitoring
- Exposure alarms
- Emergency antidotes

Nyra is highly disciplined about chemical safety.

She has little sympathy for soldiers who remove protective equipment too early.

---

# Callsign

The name **Haze** originated from Nyra's specialization in smoke and environmental denial.

The callsign quickly became associated with her reputation for transforming clear battlefields into confusing, hostile environments.

Personnel familiar with her operations have developed a simple expectation.

When Haze deploys, visibility is about to become optional.

Wind, ventilation, pressure, and weather can reduce the effectiveness of her equipment.

## Friendly Risk

Poorly controlled deployment could affect allied personnel.

This requires constant situational awareness.

## Limited Heavy Firepower

Haze is not designed for direct engagements against heavily armoured enemies.

## Equipment Reliance

Many of her strongest battlefield abilities depend on specialized compounds and deployment systems.

# Reputation

Haze has become known within the Doom Corps as a soldier who makes otherwise straightforward environments increasingly unpleasant for the enemy.

Clear streets become blind zones.

Defensive corridors become unusable.

Snipers lose visibility.

Units lose contact.

Then Hell Host personnel begin moving through the confusion.

Nyra enjoys the psychological effect almost as much as the tactical one.

Almost.
