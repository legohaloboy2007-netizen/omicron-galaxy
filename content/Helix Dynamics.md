# Helix Dynamics

Helix Dynamics is a powerful biotechnology and scientific research corporation operating throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]], known for its advanced work in genetics, cybernetics, pharmaceuticals, biological engineering, and experimental augmentation technologies. Widely regarded as one of the most technologically advanced corporations in known space, Helix Dynamics maintains enormous influence across both civilian healthcare and classified scientific sectors.

Originally founded as a medical and pharmaceutical company, Helix Dynamics rapidly expanded into advanced biological research, cybernetic enhancement programs, and anomaly-based experimentation. Their medical technologies, surgical systems, and life-support innovations are used throughout much of the galaxy, making the corporation essential to countless civilian and military systems.

Despite its public reputation as a leader in scientific advancement, Helix Dynamics has become increasingly associated with dangerous experimentation, classified research facilities, and unethical augmentation projects. Numerous rumors and investigations have linked the corporation to biological outbreaks, illegal human experimentation, and anomaly-related incidents.

The corporation is heavily involved in research surrounding genetic mutation, biological adaptation, cybernetic enhancement, and corruption-resistant technologies designed for operations within hazardous regions such as the [[Farlands]]. Their scientists have also reportedly conducted highly classified studies involving [[Element 95]] and other anomalous materials.

Helix Dynamics facilities are known for their sterile architectural design, advanced containment systems, heavily secured laboratories, and private security divisions tasked with protecting corporate research.

Although criticized by governments and civilian organizations for its secrecy and morally questionable practices, Helix Dynamics continues to maintain enormous economic and scientific influence throughout the galaxy due to the unmatched effectiveness of its technologies.
