# Hell Hosts

The **Hell Hosts** are one of the most powerful military superpowers within the [[Omicron Galaxy]], possessing enormous armies, advanced fleets, highly specialized military divisions, powerful war machines, and extensive industrial infrastructure.

The faction was formed following the events of [[Virex Prime]], when surviving elements of the [[Terrorism Control Agency]] and [[Free Order Rebellion]] began cooperating after years of conflict. What began as an alliance born from survival eventually developed into a permanent military organization.

[[Predator|William Grant]] was not one of the original founders of the Hell Hosts. He joined the faction after its formation as an ordinary soldier and gradually rose through its ranks, eventually becoming one of the most important figures in its history and assuming leadership of the faction.

Under later Hell Host leadership, the faction expanded dramatically in military strength, territory, industry, research, and technological development. Its forces became capable of conducting warfare across entire systems, supported by enormous fleets, specialized battalions, advanced vehicles, [[Titans]], artificial intelligence, and heavily fortified worlds.

The Hell Hosts have fought several major conflicts throughout their history, including wars against the [[Free World Order]] and [[Angel Swords]]. They have also conducted operations within anomalous and poorly understood regions such as the [[Farlands]] and [[Voidlands]].

Although primarily a military organization, the Hell Hosts maintain extensive scientific, medical, engineering, exploration, containment, and research capabilities. Their increasing involvement with [[Element 95]], anomalous phenomena, ancient civilizations, and experimental technology has gradually pushed the faction beyond conventional warfare and into some of the most dangerous regions of the galaxy.
