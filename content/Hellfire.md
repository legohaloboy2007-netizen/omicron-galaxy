# Ryan

> "You don't need to start a fire when the world is willing to do it for you."

## Overview

**Ryan**, known as **The Crimson Hellfire**, was a powerful anomalous individual historically associated with [[The Crimson Order]].

Unlike many other known members, Ryan's exact status within the Order remains uncertain.

Several records identify him as a member, while others describe him only as an associate operating alongside the faction.

He disappeared from known activity before his position could ever be clearly established.

Ryan possessed abilities involving spatial translocation, environmental manipulation, and the use of anomalous incendiary chains.

He was eventually killed during an undocumented confrontation, making him one of the relatively few individuals associated with the Crimson Order known to have died permanently.

The exact circumstances of his death remain unclear.

## Appearance

Very few reliable descriptions of Ryan survive.

He possessed a humanoid male form approximately 5'11" in height.

Accounts commonly associate him with dark combat clothing or armour containing crimson markings, although it remains unclear whether these markings represented formal membership in the Crimson Order or merely affiliation with it.

Ryan was frequently observed carrying lengths of heavy chain.

These chains served as both weapons and a focus for his anomalous abilities.

---

## Personality

Ryan was aggressive, confident, and prone to solving problems through direct action.

Compared with many members of the Crimson Order, he displayed relatively little interest in ancient history, cosmic philosophy, or the wider mysteries surrounding the faction.

He preferred practical results.

Ryan also possessed a dangerous tendency to overestimate his own resilience.

His environmental abilities allowed him to transform battlefields dramatically, and repeated success appears to have made him increasingly willing to enter situations that should have been avoided.

Whether this ultimately contributed to his death is unknown.

---

# The Crimson Hellfire

Ryan's title originated from the unusual interaction between his chain weapon and his ability to manipulate environmental conditions.

Rather than possessing direct fire manipulation comparable to [[Crimson Arsonist|Joseph]], Ryan specialized in creating conditions in which fire became increasingly destructive.

Heat.

Dry air.

Electrical storms.

Violent wind.

Falling atmospheric pressure.

Once a battlefield began changing, Ryan's incendiary weaponry became increasingly dangerous.

The resulting destruction earned him the name **Crimson Hellfire**.

---

# Abilities

## Hellfire Chain

Ryan could manifest or release an anomalously empowered chain from his hands.

The chain behaved like a conventional physical object until it made contact with a living target.

Upon contact, the affected section immediately ignited.

The resulting flame was unusually difficult to extinguish while the chain remained attached.

Ryan commonly used the weapon to:

- Restrain opponents
- Pull targets toward him
- Disarm weapons
- Swing across environmental obstacles
- Strike enemies at moderate range
- Ignite targets
- Anchor himself during extreme environmental conditions

The exact origin of the chain is unknown.

---

## Environmental Manipulation

Ryan could temporarily influence local environmental and atmospheric conditions.

Known effects included:

- Increasing ambient temperature
- Reducing humidity
- Producing strong winds
- Creating localized storms
- Increasing electrical activity
- Altering atmospheric pressure
- Encouraging the spread of existing fires

His control was limited geographically and became increasingly difficult at larger scales.

Ryan could not casually control the weather of an entire planet.

Instead, he manipulated the immediate battlefield.

---

## Storm Generation

Under suitable atmospheric conditions, Ryan could create violent localized storms.

These events could produce strong winds, heavy rainfall, lightning, or rapid temperature shifts.

Interestingly, Ryan did not always use storms to extinguish fires.

Wind and lightning could instead be used to spread destruction across a much wider area.

---

## Teleportation

Ryan possessed the ability to relocate himself instantaneously.

He commonly used teleportation during combat to reposition around enemies or avoid attacks.

His spatial abilities appeared less refined than those demonstrated by older members of the Crimson Order.

Long-distance teleportation may have required considerably more effort.

---

## Environmental Synergy

Ryan's abilities became most dangerous when used together.

He could dry an environment, increase heat, generate strong wind, and then ignite a target or structure with his chain.

This allowed relatively small fires to become major hazards extremely quickly.

His abilities were therefore especially dangerous in:

- Forest environments
- Industrial facilities
- Fuel depots
- Dense urban areas
- Dry planetary regions
- Locations containing volatile materials

---

# Limitations

## Limited Scale

Ryan's environmental manipulation affected relatively localized areas.

Increasing the size of the affected region significantly increased physical strain.

---

## Environmental Dependence

Although Ryan could influence atmospheric conditions, existing environmental factors still mattered.

Creating a major storm inside an enclosed underground facility was significantly more difficult than manipulating an already unstable atmosphere.

---

## Concentration

Complex environmental changes required concentration.

Heavy injury or sensory disruption could cause conditions to return rapidly toward normal.

---

## Chain Dependence

Ryan's most dangerous incendiary ability depended heavily upon his chain.

Without it, he could still manipulate the environment and teleport, but his ability to directly ignite opponents was considerably reduced.

---

## Mortality

Ryan was powerful.

He was not immortal.

His eventual death demonstrated an important fact frequently overlooked when discussing the Crimson Order:

Association with the Order does not make an individual undefeatable.

---

# Relationship with the Crimson Order

Ryan's exact relationship with [[The Crimson Order]] remains disputed.

Some records explicitly identify him as **The Crimson Hellfire**, suggesting that he had been granted a Crimson title and was therefore accepted into the faction.

Other sources never list him among its formal membership.

One possibility is that Ryan operated as an associate or prospective member.

He may have worked alongside the Order without ever becoming part of its inner ranks.

His death occurred before the issue was ever publicly clarified.

[[Crimson Creator|Aethros]] has never commented on Ryan's status.

---

# Relationship with Joseph

Ryan and [[Crimson Arsonist|Joseph]] possessed obvious overlap in their abilities but approached fire very differently.

Joseph controlled flames directly.

Ryan controlled the conditions surrounding them.

When operating together, their abilities could become extremely destructive.

Ryan could transform the battlefield into an environment where combustion spread rapidly.

Joseph could then manipulate the resulting flames.

For obvious reasons, deploying the two together near populated infrastructure was considered undesirable.

---

# Death

Ryan is one of the few individuals associated with the Crimson Order confirmed to have died.

Very little reliable information survives regarding the event.

It is unknown:

- Who killed him
- Where he died
- Why the confrontation occurred
- Whether other members of the Crimson Order were present
- Whether his body was recovered
- Whether his death was connected to his uncertain status within the Order

What is known is that Ryan never returned.

No credible sightings of the Crimson Hellfire have been documented since.

Unlike many ancient figures whose deaths eventually become myths or disputed legends, the Crimson Order appears to regard Ryan's death as genuine.

---

# Legacy

Ryan was never among the most powerful members of the Crimson Order.

He was not an ancient cosmic figure.

He was not a creator, temporal manipulator, or adaptive entity.

His significance comes partly from that fact.

Ryan proved that individuals associated with the Crimson Order could die.

His death became a small but important contradiction to the mythology surrounding the faction.

For civilizations that regarded the Order as a collection of untouchable gods, the existence of a dead Crimson member raised an uncomfortable possibility.

If one could die, perhaps others could as well.
