# Shadow

> *"You don't need powers to become something dangerous."*

## Profile

**Real Name:** Ryan Taylor  
**Callsign:** Shadow  
**Operational Designation:** Hitman 0-7  
**Age:** 37  
**Gender:** Male  
**Species:** Human  
**Status:** Active  
**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]  
**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Formation:** Hitman Battalion  
**Rank:** Battalion Commander  
**Former Rank:** Corporal  
**Role:** Special Operations / Battalion Command  
**Known Abilities:** None  
**Primary Weapon:** [[94CAR]]  
**Secondary Weapon:** O2 PR  
**Relationship Status:** Single  

Ryan Taylor, better known as **Shadow**, is a Hell Host battalion commander and veteran soldier most commonly associated with the operational designation **Hitman 0-7**.

Unlike many of the Hell Hosts' most dangerous operatives, Shadow possesses no anomalous abilities, supernatural powers, Ascension-derived traits, or unusual biological enhancements.

His reputation was built conventionally.

Training.

Experience.

Discipline.

And years of surviving operations that steadily transformed an ambitious young recruit into one of the faction's experienced battlefield commanders.

Shadow currently commands the **Hitman Battalion**, the same formation in which he began much of his early Hell Host career.

His designation, **Hitman 0-7**, has remained with him long after he ceased being an ordinary member of the unit.

---

# Early Life

Ryan Taylor was born into a family already closely connected with the [[Hell Hosts]].

Both of his parents served the faction during his childhood.

Their military responsibilities frequently kept them away from home, meaning much of Ryan's upbringing was handled by a regular caretaker while his parents were deployed.

Despite their frequent absence, Ryan admired them enormously.

He grew up surrounded by stories of Hell Host personnel, military service, distant worlds, and the dangers his parents faced.

Rather than resenting their careers, Ryan became fascinated by them.

From a young age, he decided that he wanted to follow them into service.

---

# Childhood Ambition

Ryan's interest in the Hell Hosts developed beyond childhood admiration.

As he grew older, he began actively studying the faction.

He learned about its military structure.

Its history.

Its weapons.

Its operations.

Its enemies.

And the requirements expected of personnel seeking to join.

What began as imitation gradually became genuine ambition.

Ryan trained extensively before entering service, determined not to rely upon his parents' reputation to earn his position.

He wanted to become a Hell Host soldier because he was capable of being one.

Eventually, he succeeded.

---

# Joining the Hell Hosts

Ryan entered active Hell Host military service as a conventional human soldier.

He possessed no anomalous abilities.

He received no experimental transformation.

He had no supernatural advantage.

His early career was built upon weapons training, physical conditioning, battlefield awareness, and discipline.

Ryan eventually became associated with a specialist Doom Corps formation known as the **Hitman Battalion**.

There he received the operational designation:

**Hitman 0-7.**

The designation became closely associated with him during subsequent operations.

Even after Ryan adopted the callsign **Shadow**, many personnel continued referring to him as 0-7.

---

# Hitman Battalion

The **Hitman Battalion** is a Doom Corps formation specializing in targeted offensive operations against strategically important enemy personnel and assets.

Despite its name, the battalion is not composed exclusively of assassins.

Its responsibilities can include:

- High-value target operations
- Command disruption
- Precision assaults
- Hostile leadership capture
- Strategic asset destruction
- Deep infiltration
- Reconnaissance
- Sabotage
- Small-unit special operations
- Battlefield target elimination

Hitman personnel are frequently deployed ahead of larger conventional operations.

Their objective is often to weaken an enemy before the main assault begins.

Destroy communications.

Eliminate a field commander.

Disable defenses.

Identify targets.

Sabotage infrastructure.

Then disappear before the enemy can organize an effective response.

---

# Hitman 0-7

Ryan received the designation **Hitman 0-7** during his early service with the battalion.

The exact significance of the numerical designation is internal to Hitman operational organization.

Ryan became strongly associated with 0-7 after participating in numerous operations under the identifier.

Even after his promotion through the Hell Host rank structure, the designation remained.

Among veterans who served alongside him during his earlier career, **0-7** is sometimes used more frequently than Shadow.

Ryan answers to either.

---

# Rise Through the Ranks

Shadow's advancement was unusually rapid during portions of his career.

He eventually attained the rank of **Corporal**, gaining increasing responsibility within Hitman operations.

Approximately one year later, further battlefield experience and command performance resulted in another major advancement.

Over subsequent service, Ryan eventually became commander of the same battalion in which he had once served as Hitman 0-7.

Modern records therefore distinguish between his former conventional rank and his current command appointment.

**Corporal** represents an earlier stage of his career.

**Battalion Commander** represents what he eventually became.

His progression from ordinary Hitman operative to commanding the formation became a defining part of his reputation.

---

# The Callsign Shadow

Ryan eventually became known by the callsign **Shadow**.

The name reflects his preferred operational style.

Shadow does not interpret stealth as simply remaining unseen.

He considers it the ability to control when the enemy becomes aware of his presence.

Sometimes that means complete infiltration.

Sometimes it means allowing the enemy to notice one threat while Hitman personnel approach from another direction.

Sometimes it means attacking quickly enough that understanding what happened becomes irrelevant.

Shadow favors deliberate action over unnecessary spectacle.

His battalion is expected to accomplish its objective and disappear before hostile forces can effectively respond.

---

# No Anomalous Abilities

Shadow possesses no known anomalous powers.

This fact has become increasingly notable as the Hell Hosts encounter more enhanced soldiers, altered humans, artificial intelligences, Ascended beings, and reality-affecting entities.

Shadow remains human.

His effectiveness comes from conventional capabilities developed to an exceptional level:

- Physical conditioning
- Marksmanship
- Tactical awareness
- Battlefield experience
- Stealth
- Leadership
- Weapons proficiency
- Close-quarters combat
- Rapid decision-making
- Operational planning

Older personnel records occasionally rated virtually every aspect of Shadow's abilities at the highest possible level.

Modern assessments treat those ratings as enthusiastic exaggeration.

Shadow is extremely capable.

He is not physically perfect.

He can be injured.

He can make mistakes.

He can be overwhelmed.

And he can die.

The fact that he continues operating successfully despite those limitations is considered considerably more impressive than the old ratings.

---

# Combat Style

Shadow is a highly adaptable combatant.

Unlike personnel who specialize entirely in one engagement range, he is comfortable transitioning between conventional infantry combat, precision shooting, infiltration, and close-range fighting.

His preferred method is preparation.

Shadow studies the target before attacking whenever possible.

He considers:

- Enemy numbers
- Defensive positions
- Command structure
- Escape routes
- Reinforcement times
- Surveillance
- Terrain
- Communications
- Available cover
- Civilian presence

Once an engagement begins, he attempts to maintain control over its tempo.

Shadow would rather win before the enemy realizes how badly their position has deteriorated.

---

# 94CAR

Shadow commonly carries the [[94CAR]] as his primary general-purpose weapon.

Its reliability and versatility suit his broad operational requirements.

Rather than heavily modifying the weapon toward one specific combat role, Shadow prefers a configuration capable of remaining useful across rapidly changing conditions.

Hitman operations frequently transition from infiltration into conventional combat with little warning.

Shadow chooses equipment accordingly.

---

# O2 PR

Shadow's second documented weapon is an older specialist firearm designated the **O2 PR**.

Surviving records do not consistently identify the full meaning of the PR designation.

The weapon has nevertheless remained associated with Shadow throughout portions of his service history.

Rather than replacing the O2 PR with a modern Hell Host weapon, current historical records preserve the designation pending further documentation regarding its specifications and role.

Shadow has used the weapon alongside his standard 94CAR during specialist deployments.

---

# Personality

Shadow is generally kind and helpful toward personnel under his command.

He is also strict.

These traits are not contradictory.

Ryan believes that soldiers deserve commanders who genuinely care whether they survive.

He also believes that survival requires discipline.

During downtime, Shadow can be approachable and supportive.

He frequently assists less experienced personnel and takes training seriously.

During operations, his expectations become considerably harsher.

Repeated carelessness, refusal to follow orders, or behavior that unnecessarily endangers other personnel can provoke a much colder side of his personality.

Older descriptions occasionally characterize him simply as:

**Kind. Strict. Mean. Helpful.**

The description lacks elegance.

It is not entirely wrong.

---

# Leadership

Shadow's leadership style was heavily influenced by having risen through Hitman Battalion rather than entering it as an outside commander.

He understands the formation from the perspective of the personnel carrying out its operations.

He has performed the same work he now orders others to undertake.

This gives him considerable credibility among veteran Hitman personnel.

Shadow expects officers to understand the practical consequences of their orders.

If a plan looks excellent on a tactical display but is impossible for the soldiers physically executing it, he considers it a bad plan.

Likewise, he expects personnel to speak when they identify a genuine operational problem.

He does not consider blind obedience a substitute for competence.

Once the decision has been made, however, Shadow expects the battalion to move.

---

# Relationship with His Parents

Shadow's parents were among the earliest influences on his decision to join the Hell Hosts.

Their service meant they were frequently absent during his childhood.

Ryan was instead largely cared for by a babysitter or caretaker while they were deployed.

Despite this distance, he remained fascinated by their careers.

His eventual decision to join the Hell Hosts was partly inspired by a desire to follow the path they had taken.

However, Shadow has always resisted the suggestion that his career exists solely because of them.

His parents inspired him to begin.

Everything afterward was his responsibility.

The current status of Ryan's parents is undocumented.

---

# Relationship with the Hell Hosts

Unlike several Hell Host operatives whose loyalty developed through complicated alliances, coercion, or circumstance, Shadow actively wanted to join the faction.

The Hell Hosts were part of his life before he became a soldier.

They represented the world his parents belonged to and eventually the future he chose for himself.

This does not make him blindly loyal.

Years of service have given Ryan a far more complicated understanding of the faction than he possessed as a child.

He has seen victories.

Failures.

Good commanders.

Bad decisions.

Heroism.

Waste.

His loyalty is therefore no longer based upon childhood idealization.

It is based upon experience.

He knows what the Hell Hosts actually are.

He continues serving anyway.

---

# Reputation

Shadow is best known for the unusual path he took through the Hitman Battalion.

He entered as another soldier.

He became Hitman 0-7.

He became a Corporal.

He gained greater operational responsibility.

Eventually, he returned to the same formation as its commander.

For younger Hitman personnel, his career is sometimes used as an example of advancement through demonstrated capability rather than extraordinary powers.

Shadow has no Element 95 abilities.

No Ascension.

No Farlands alteration.

No experimental combat substance.

No artificial body.

No reality manipulation.

He is simply very good at his job.

---

# Legacy of 0-7

Shadow could have abandoned the designation **Hitman 0-7** after taking command.

He never did.

The designation reminds him that he was not always the person giving orders.

He once stood where his soldiers stand.

He once waited for someone else to decide the plan.

He once entered operations knowing that somebody above him had decided the risk was acceptable.

That memory influences how he commands.

Shadow knows every marker on a tactical display represents an actual person.

Hitman 0-7 taught him that.

Battalion Commander Ryan Taylor refuses to forget it.

> *"I was one of you before I commanded you. Don't let the rank make you forget that. I won't."*
