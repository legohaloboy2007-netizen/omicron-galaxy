# Ice Kingdom

The Ice Kingdom is a frozen region located within the outer reaches of the [[Omicron Galaxy]]. Covered in endless snowstorms, ancient ruins, glacial oceans, and abandoned structures, the region is considered one of the coldest and most isolated territories in known space.

For centuries, the Ice Kingdom remained largely dormant, with few confirmed signs of intelligent life outside scattered military outposts and mining operations. Strange myths and unexplained disappearances surrounding the region caused many explorers and civilians to avoid it entirely.

Following the Ascension of [[Calypso]], the region became increasingly active. Now known as the Ice Queen, Calypso rules over the Ice Kingdom as its central leader, commanding growing influence across the frozen systems. Since her ascension, unusual storms, frozen anomalies, and reports of hostile ice creatures have become far more common throughout the region.

Despite its harsh environment, the Ice Kingdom contains valuable mineral deposits, hidden ruins, and ancient structures buried beneath the ice.

## Known For
- Endless frozen landscapes
- Ancient buried ruins
- Violent blizzards and ice storms
- Isolated military and mining outposts
- Legends surrounding the Ice Queen
- Dangerous frozen wildlife and anomalies
