# Infinity

> *"Speed is silence. Silence is control."*

## Profile

**Real Name:** Evelyn Jordan  
**Callsign:** Infinity  
**Known As:** Hell Host Infinity  
**Age:** 24  
**Gender:** Female  
**Species:** Human  
**Status:** Active  
**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]  
**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Rank:** Sergeant  
**Role:** Rapid Disruption Operative  
**Relationship Status:** Single  
**Known Languages:** English / Spanish / Limited French  
**Primary Weapon:** Magma Shotgun  
**Secondary Weapons:** Shock Knives  
**Known Abilities:** Enhanced Speed / Danger Sense / Insanity / Infinite Rush  

Evelyn Jordan, better known as **Infinity**, is a Hell Host Doom Corps Sergeant specializing in rapid assault and battlefield disruption.

Infinity possesses enhanced movement speed, unusually sensitive danger perception, and an anomalous ability capable of severely disrupting the perception and neurological function of nearby targets.

Rather than relying upon overwhelming physical strength or heavy armor, Infinity fights through movement.

She closes distance quickly, attacks before opponents can properly respond, disrupts groups through sensory interference, and relocates before hostile forces can establish an effective firing solution.

Despite the intensity of her combat style, Infinity is generally relaxed and friendly outside battle.

She also takes tea considerably more seriously than most military personnel consider necessary.

---

# Background

Little is documented regarding Infinity's life before joining the [[Hell Hosts]].

Her abilities, early life, family history, and circumstances of recruitment have not been formally established.

By the time she entered active Doom Corps service, Infinity had already demonstrated exceptional mobility and unusual neurological abilities.

She eventually attained the rank of Sergeant and became primarily associated with rapid disruption operations.

Unlike many anomalous Hell Host personnel, Infinity's history is not defined by a known catastrophic experiment, ancient anomaly, or major transformation.

She is simply Evelyn Jordan.

Until further information becomes available, her earlier history remains largely private.

---

# Appearance

Infinity wears distinctive white, black, and red Doom Corps combat armor designed around mobility.

Compared with heavier Hell Host assault equipment, her armor prioritizes:

- Flexibility
- Impact protection
- High-speed stability
- Lightweight construction
- Rapid weapon handling
- Protection during sudden acceleration

Her helmet contains a fully enclosed dark visor and integrated combat systems.

The white armor makes Infinity unusually visually distinctive among Hell Host personnel, many of whom favor considerably darker combat equipment.

Outside active operations, Evelyn generally dresses casually and wears glasses.

She has a known burn scar on one arm.

The origin of the injury has not been documented.

# Abilities

## Enhanced Speed

Infinity possesses significantly enhanced movement speed.

Under normal conditions, she is capable of running somewhat faster than the fastest terrestrial animals such as the cheetah.

While comparatively restrained beside some extreme anomalous entities, this still gives Infinity extraordinary mobility for a human combatant.

More importantly, her nervous system and reflexes appear capable of keeping pace with her movement.

She can therefore:

- Rapidly close distance
- Reposition between cover
- Flank enemy formations
- Evade conventional attacks
- Enter and leave close combat quickly
- Traverse dangerous areas before opponents can properly react

Infinity is not infinitely fast despite her callsign.

She is frequently required to remind people of this.

---

## Danger Sense

Infinity possesses an enhanced ability to detect immediate danger in her surroundings.

The ability does not provide conventional future sight.

Instead, Infinity experiences an instinctive warning when an immediate threat is nearby or about to affect her.

This may alert her to:

- Incoming attacks
- Nearby ambushes
- Hidden threats
- Sudden environmental danger
- Hostile movement
- Immediate lethal intent

The information she receives is imprecise.

Infinity may know that something is wrong without immediately understanding what the threat actually is.

Her speed allows her to react to these warnings far more effectively than an ordinary human could.

---

# Insanity

Infinity possesses an unusual targeted neurological ability informally known as **Insanity**.

She can direct the effect toward nearby individuals, disrupting their perception and neurological stability.

Affected targets may experience:

- Severe headaches
- Dizziness
- Vertigo
- Confusion
- Hallucinations
- Sensory distortion
- Loss of concentration
- Difficulty distinguishing real threats from false perceptions

The name **Insanity** is somewhat misleading.

Infinity does not simply cause permanent mental illness at will.

Instead, the ability temporarily overwhelms the target's ability to accurately process their surroundings.

The effect can be devastating during combat.

A soldier experiencing hallucinations and severe vertigo may be physically unharmed yet completely incapable of fighting effectively.

The ability is strongest at relatively close range and becomes more difficult to maintain against multiple targets.

Prolonged or repeated use places increasing mental strain upon Infinity herself.

---

# Infinite Rush

**Infinite Rush** is Infinity's most extreme physical ability.

Originally referred to simply as her **Ultimate**, Infinite Rush temporarily pushes her body beyond its normal enhanced state.

During activation, Infinity experiences dramatic increases in:

- Speed
- Agility
- Reflexes
- Physical strength
- Offensive capability
- Reaction time

Her perception of surrounding movement also appears accelerated, allowing her to process events more effectively while travelling at extreme speed.

To outside observers, Infinity may appear to cross short distances almost instantaneously.

This is not genuine teleportation or complete time manipulation.

It is an extreme burst of enhanced movement and perception.

Infinite Rush cannot be maintained indefinitely.

Extended activation places severe strain upon Infinity's muscles, cardiovascular system, nervous system, and armor.

She therefore uses it primarily for brief decisive attacks, emergency repositioning, or rapid elimination of priority targets.

---

# Combat Style

Infinity specializes in **rapid disruption**.

She rarely remains stationary during active combat.

Her preferred approach is to destabilize an enemy formation before it can properly respond.

Infinity may use Insanity to compromise perception, rapidly enter the affected formation, eliminate or disable priority targets, and withdraw before remaining personnel can establish her position.

Her primary battlefield roles include:

- Rapid assault
- Flanking
- Close-quarters combat
- Enemy disruption
- Target elimination
- Emergency interception
- Hit-and-run attacks

Her greatest advantage is not simply speed.

It is the amount of information an opponent must process while fighting her.

Infinity moves quickly.

Danger Sense helps her avoid retaliation.

Insanity makes enemies less capable of interpreting what they see.

Infinite Rush can then overwhelm whatever resistance remains.

---

# Magma Shotgun

The **Magma Shotgun** is Infinity's signature firearm.

The weapon fires specialized high-temperature kinetic ammunition designed to deliver extreme thermal energy upon impact.

Its ammunition can burn through or severely damage many forms of armor and protective material.

The weapon's original description claimed its projectiles were **hotter than magma**.

Modern Hell Host documentation considers the phrase technically unhelpful but broadly accurate regarding the weapon's intended effect.

Infinity favors the Magma Shotgun because its close-range destructive power complements her speed.

Rather than engaging in prolonged firefights, she can rapidly close distance and deliver devastating shots before repositioning.

---

# Shock Knives

Infinity normally carries approximately **four Shock Knives**.

Each knife contains an electro-thermal edge capable of delivering a powerful electrical discharge on contact.

Infinity is highly skilled at using them both as conventional blades and throwing weapons.

The knives incorporate a return system allowing them to navigate back toward Infinity after being thrown.

This function is sometimes compared to the behavior of an ancient mythological weapon.

Infinity generally finds the comparison amusing.

The combination of returning weapons and extreme movement speed allows her to attack targets from rapidly changing angles without repeatedly stopping to recover equipment.

---

# Burn Scar

Infinity possesses a visible burn scar on one arm.

The injury predates portions of her current service record.

The exact circumstances surrounding the burn remain undocumented.

Infinity does not appear particularly concerned by it.

---

# Personality

Infinity is generally friendly, relaxed, and easy to interact with.

Personnel descriptions frequently characterize her as simply:

**Nice and cool.**

Despite her combat capabilities, Evelyn does not cultivate an intimidating personality outside operations.

She enjoys casual conversation and maintains a comparatively ordinary off-duty life.

Her relaxed demeanor can contrast sharply with her battlefield behavior.

Once combat begins, Infinity becomes considerably more focused and efficient.

She favors speed, control, and ending engagements before they can become prolonged.

---

# Coffee and Tea

Infinity enjoys both coffee and tea.

Tea wins.

This distinction is apparently important.

She frequently prepares her own drinks while off duty and has developed strong preferences regarding both.

Her reputation for drinking tea has become sufficiently well known among personnel that it is occasionally associated with her almost as strongly as her weapons.

Infinity considers this perfectly reasonable.

> **Tea first. Chaos second.**

# Relationship with [[Karma]]

Infinity is a known friend of [[Karma]].

Their friendship predates or extends beyond routine military cooperation.

Infinity appears considerably more relaxed around Karma than around personnel she knows exclusively through operations.

The exact history of their friendship remains undocumented.

Nevertheless, Karma is considered one of Infinity's established personal connections within the Hell Hosts.

# Limitations

Infinity's abilities provide exceptional mobility but do not make her invulnerable.

Her speed requires room to maneuver and becomes less useful in environments designed specifically to restrict movement.

Danger Sense warns of threats but does not automatically explain them.

Insanity requires concentration and becomes increasingly demanding when used against multiple targets.

Infinite Rush places substantial physical strain upon her body and cannot be maintained continuously.

Infinity is therefore most effective during short, decisive engagements.

Prolonged attritional combat reduces many of her advantages.

---

# Reputation

Infinity is known within the Doom Corps as an unusually fast and disruptive combatant.

Enemy personnel attempting to track her must contend simultaneously with her movement, unusual weapons, danger perception, and neurological interference.

Among Hell Host personnel, however, her reputation is considerably less dramatic.

Evelyn Jordan is a Sergeant.

A friend.

A soldier.

And someone who will almost certainly judge the quality of whatever tea is available aboard the ship.

The fact that she can subsequently cross a battlefield faster than most personnel can track her does not appear to have affected this priority.
