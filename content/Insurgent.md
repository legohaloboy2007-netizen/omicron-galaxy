# Insurgent

## Overview

**Real Name:** Classified

**Age:** Late 30s to Early 40s

**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]

**Unit:** [[The Red Hand]]

**Rank:** Platoon Captain / General

Insurgent serves as the acting field leader of:
[[The Red Hand]]

and is responsible for many of the Hell Hosts' most aggressive suppression and coercion operations.

Where other commanders seek compromise, Insurgent seeks resolution.

Direct, uncompromising, and brutally effective, he has built a reputation as one of the most feared operatives within the galaxy.

His methods are controversial.

His results are not.

Many enemy commanders have described Insurgent as:

> "The moment diplomacy dies."

---

## Appearance

Insurgent stands approximately:

**6'1" - 6'3"**

with a broad, heavily-built frame.

Years of warfare have left visible marks upon him.

### Notable Features

- Dark brown hair
- Grey beginning to form around the temples
- Brown eyes
- Extensive burn scars
- Weathered appearance

His face is rarely seen.

Outside a small number of senior personnel, most people know him only through his armored mask.

### Armor

Insurgent wears some of the heaviest combat armor within Hell Host special operations.

His equipment is built around:

- Durability
- Intimidation
- Shock assaults
- Frontline survivability

Key features include:

- Reinforced black armor plating
- Heavy combat systems
- Fur-lined combat collar
- Red Hand insignia
- Full facial protection

Many soldiers describe him as:

> "Looking more like a moving bunker than a man."

---

## Personality

Insurgent is:

- Direct
- Blunt
- Honest
- Protective
- Uncompromising

Despite his reputation, Insurgent rarely lies.

He prefers uncomfortable truths over comforting falsehoods.

This often makes him difficult to work with, but strangely dependable.

### The Reality Behind The Reputation

Many assume Insurgent is constantly angry.

The reality is more complicated.

He simply has little patience for:

- Excuses
- Politics
- Delays
- Empty promises

When a problem exists, his instinct is to solve it.

Immediately.

---

## Combat Style

Insurgent specializes in:

- Shock assaults
- Urban warfare
- Area denial
- Psychological warfare
- Counter-insurgency operations

Unlike many commanders, he prefers leading from the front.

His approach is simple:

Create overwhelming pressure.

Force mistakes.

Exploit weakness.

End resistance.

### Weapons

#### [[C74 Laser-Propelled Grenade Launcher]]

Insurgent's primary weapon.

Designed for devastating anti-structure and anti-personnel engagements.

The weapon reflects his overall philosophy:

If something is standing in the way, remove it.

---

#### Combat Knife

Used during close-quarters engagements and interrogations.

---

#### Interrogation Tools

Insurgent possesses extensive training in both physical and psychological interrogation methods.

This reputation alone is often enough to break resistance before questioning even begins.

---

## Leadership Style

Insurgent leads through presence rather than inspiration.

He expects discipline.

He expects competence.

He expects results.

While many soldiers find him intimidating, few question his commitment to those under his command.

He does not ask people to do things he would refuse to do himself.

---

## Relationships

### [[Hatchet]]

The person Insurgent trusts most.

Hatchet's reliability, consistency, and professionalism have earned a level of trust few people ever receive from Insurgent.

Although they rarely discuss it openly, their partnership forms the backbone of the Red Hand.

---

### [[Karma]]

Insurgent respects Karma greatly.

At the same time, he does not fully understand her.

Her fractured psychology and shifting personality remain difficult for him to interpret.

His view is simple:

> "I don't need to understand her.
>
> I need to trust her."

And he does.

---

### [[Predator]]

Predator values Insurgent's effectiveness and willingness to act decisively.

Although the two occasionally disagree on methods, Predator recognizes the importance of having someone willing to make difficult decisions.

---

## Off-Duty Life

When not deployed, Insurgent lives a surprisingly simple life.

Most of his time is spent:

- Working out
- Maintaining equipment
- Reading military reports
- Reviewing historical campaigns
- Sitting quietly with trusted people

He avoids:

- Formal events
- Political gatherings
- Large parties

Whenever possible, he chooses practicality over social obligations.

---

## Hobbies

### Combat Training

Insurgent constantly seeks improvement.

Even off-duty, he can often be found:

- Sparring
- Training
- Practicing drills

---

### Boxing

One of his favorite physical activities.

He appreciates the honesty of it.

No politics.

No excuses.

Just skill.

---

### Military History

Insurgent has a deep interest in historical warfare.

He frequently studies:

- Campaigns
- Commanders
- Uprisings
- Military failures

He believes history provides lessons most soldiers ignore.

---

### Barbecues

One of the few things capable of making Insurgent genuinely relax.

Many people find it difficult to reconcile:

The feared commander of the Red Hand.

And the man enthusiastically arguing over cooking techniques.

## Flaws

### Escalation

Insurgent sees most problems as things that need solving immediately.

This mindset often leads him toward aggressive solutions before considering alternatives.

Although effective, it can create unnecessary conflict.

---

### Stubbornness

Insurgent struggles to admit when he is wrong.

Even when evidence proves otherwise, changing his position can take considerable effort.

---

### Within Hell Hosts

Insurgent is respected as:

- Effective
- Reliable
- Dangerous
- Decisive

Many soldiers fear him.

Many more trust him.

The two are not mutually exclusive.

---

### Outside Hell Hosts

Enemy forces often describe Insurgent as:

- Brutal
- Relentless
- Uncompromising

His arrival is often interpreted as a sign that peaceful solutions have failed.
