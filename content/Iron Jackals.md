# Iron Jackals

The Iron Jackals are a heavily militarized salvage syndicate operating throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]], known for battlefield scavenging, illegal technology recovery, armored raids, and the reconstruction of stolen military hardware. Originally formed from scavengers, engineers, deserters, and survivalists following early galactic wars, the faction gradually evolved into one of the most dangerous industrial raider groups within known space.

The Iron Jackals specialize in recovering wreckage from destroyed battlefields, abandoned colonies, fleet graveyards, and catastrophic warzones. Using salvaged components from military vehicles, starships, Titans, and industrial machinery, the faction constructs heavily modified weapons and armored equipment unmatched by most criminal organizations.

Unlike sophisticated syndicates such as the [[Mirage Collective]] or large-scale cartels like the [[Black Vipers]], the Iron Jackals thrive in unstable regions devastated by war, industrial collapse, or abandonment. Entire scrapyard settlements and salvager outposts throughout the galaxy are rumored to operate under Jackal control.

The faction is infamous for stripping battlefields within hours of major conflicts ending, often clashing with military recovery teams, corporations, and rival scavenger groups over valuable technology. Rumors persist that the Iron Jackals possess partially reconstructed military prototypes and stolen experimental weapons recovered from classified operations.

Iron Jackal forces commonly utilize improvised heavy armor, industrial exosuits, modified war vehicles, and salvaged military technology fused together through crude but effective engineering. Their appearance is heavily industrial, featuring scrap-metal plating, hazard markings, exposed mechanical components, and reinforced combat machinery.

Despite their criminal status, many frontier worlds rely on the Iron Jackals for repaired equipment, salvaged resources, and protection within lawless sectors abandoned by larger governments.
