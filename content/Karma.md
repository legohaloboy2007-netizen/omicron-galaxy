# Karma

## Overview

**Real Name:** Unknown

**Age:** 25

**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]

**Unit:** [[The Red Hand]]

**Rank:** Colonel

Karma serves as the execution specialist of:
[[The Red Hand]]

and is widely regarded as one of the most dangerous marksmen within the:
[[Hell Hosts]].

Outside combat, she is known as a respectful, thoughtful, and highly professional officer.

During combat, something changes.

The shift is subtle.

The same face.

The same eyes.

The same voice.

Yet everyone notices it.

No one can fully explain it.

Military psychologists have attributed this phenomenon to severe combat trauma and compartmentalized identity fracture.

Karma herself is fully aware of it.

That awareness is what makes it frightening.

---

## Appearance

Karma stands approximately:

**5'6" - 5'9"**

with an athletic build developed through years of military service.

Every movement feels deliberate.

Controlled.

Efficient.

Nothing wasted.

### Facial Features

Karma possesses softer features than most people expect.

At first glance, she appears:

- Professional
- Intelligent
- Approachable

The contrast between her appearance and her reputation often leaves people unsettled.

### Hair

- Dark brown
- Usually worn neatly
- Long enough to tie back

Before operations, she automatically ties her hair into a tighter style.

A small ritual she performs without thinking.

### Eyes

Perhaps the most memorable part of her appearance.

Off duty, her eyes appear:

- Calm
- Focused
- Thoughtful

During combat, nothing visibly changes.

Yet people immediately sense that something is different.

The eyes remain the same.

The person behind them does not.

### Distinguishing Feature

Karma always seems slightly tired.

Not physically exhausted.

Simply someone who has not truly relaxed in a very long time.

---

## Personality

Karma is:

- Respectful
- Thoughtful
- Intelligent
- Self-aware
- Protective

Despite her reputation, she is one of the easiest officers in the Hell Hosts to work alongside.

She listens.

She pays attention.

She treats people with respect.

### Self-Awareness

Unlike many trauma survivors, Karma does not deny her condition.

She knows something is wrong.

She knows the other side exists.

She knows there are moments she struggles to fully understand.

This awareness creates a constant internal conflict.

One she rarely discusses.

---

### Protective Nature

Karma is particularly protective of younger soldiers.

Having witnessed the damage war can inflict on a person, she quietly watches over those she believes may be struggling.

Many soldiers never realize how closely she pays attention.

---

## Combat Style

Karma specializes in:

- Long-range elimination
- Precision strikes
- Counter-command operations
- Battlefield overwatch
- Target removal

Where Insurgent creates pressure and Hatchet creates stability, Karma delivers the final outcome.

### Weapons

#### [[M138G Sniper Energy Rifle]]

Karma's primary weapon.

Extremely accurate and devastating at long range.

Many operations have ended with a single shot from her rifle.

---

#### Sidearms & Combat Knives

Although known primarily as a sniper, Karma remains highly capable in close-range engagements.

---

## The Other Side

Military psychologists believe Karma suffers from a compartmentalized combat identity created through years of warfare and trauma.

When combat begins, a second persona emerges.

One that is:

- Colder
- More ruthless
- More detached
- More effective

Karma is aware of these changes.

In some situations, she can even influence them.

The exact nature of the phenomenon remains poorly understood.

### Her Greatest Fear

Not the other side.

Not combat.

Not death.

The possibility that there is no difference anymore.

---

## Relationships

### [[Insurgent]]

Insurgent is perhaps the closest thing Karma has to an older brother.

The relationship is not openly affectionate.

Neither would know what to do with that.

Yet trust exists between them.

Karma respects him because:

- He is honest
- He is predictable
- He says exactly what he believes

In a world where she often questions herself, that consistency matters.

---

### [[Hatchet]]

Perhaps Karma's strongest relationship.

Hatchet never attempts to fix her.

Never judges her.

Never analyzes her.

He is simply there.

And sometimes that is exactly what she needs.

A common interaction between them might consist of:

> Hatchet:
>
> "Tea."
>
> Karma:
>
> "Thank you."

Entire conversation.

Both understand perfectly.

---

### [[Predator]]

Karma respects Predator's leadership and commitment to the Hell Hosts.

While they rarely interact on a personal level, she recognizes the weight he carries.

---

## Off-Duty Life

Outside combat, Karma actively seeks peace.

Not because she is naturally peaceful.

Because she needs it.

Silence helps.

Routine helps.

Calm helps.

---

## Hobbies

Karma reads extensively.

Anything capable of briefly pulling her away from her own thoughts.

The movement helps organize thoughts she struggles to confront directly.

Predictably.

Unlike people.

Moments that might otherwise disappear.

## Flaws

### Self-Doubt

Karma does not fully trust herself.

Not because she lacks confidence.

Because she understands exactly what she is capable of.

Questions she rarely speaks aloud include:

> "Was that me?"

> "Was that the other side?"

> "Does it matter anymore?"

---

### Avoidance

Karma often avoids confronting her own trauma.

Instead, she continues functioning.

Working.

Serving.

Moving forward.

It works.

Until it doesn't.

---

## Reputation

### Within Hell Hosts

Karma is viewed as:

- Professional
- Dependable
- Respectful
- Extremely dangerous

Many personnel find her surprisingly approachable.

Others find her deeply unsettling.

Most eventually realize both impressions are true.

---

### Outside Hell Hosts

Enemy forces often know Karma only through stories.

The sniper who appears once.

Ends the battle.

Then disappears.

Numerous commanders have attributed battlefield collapses to her interventions.

Whether exaggerated or not, her reputation remains formidable.
