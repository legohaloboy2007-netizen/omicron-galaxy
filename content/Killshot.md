# Killshot

**Real Name:** Unknown  
**Codename:** Killshot  
**Species:** [[Humans|Human]]  
**Gender:** Male  
**Height:** 6'0"  
**Faction:** [[Free World Order]]  
**Role:** Marksman / Sniper  
**Status:** Active  

Killshot is an elite marksman serving within the [[Free World Order]] and is regarded as one of the faction's most accomplished long-range combatants.

Specialising in precision shooting, reconnaissance, assassination, and rapid target elimination, Killshot has developed a reputation for eliminating multiple hostile personnel within extremely short periods of time.

His real identity and much of his life before joining the Free World Order remain unknown.

## Appearance

Killshot stands approximately 6'0" and wears specialised Free World combat armour configured for reconnaissance and precision combat.

Unlike the heavier equipment used by personnel such as [[Slaughter]], Killshot's armour prioritises mobility, concealment, environmental monitoring, and minimal battlefield visibility.

His armour incorporates an active camouflage system capable of significantly reducing his visibility.

## Personality

Killshot is patient, focused, and highly disciplined.

He is comfortable spending extended periods observing a target or battlefield before firing and rarely allows pressure to force him into taking a shot before he considers it viable.

During active engagements, however, Killshot can transition rapidly from patient observation to extremely fast target acquisition.

His reputation is built not only upon accuracy, but upon his ability to eliminate several targets in rapid succession before hostile forces can determine his location.

## Free World Order

Killshot serves as one of the Free World Order's premier marksmen.

He is primarily deployed during operations requiring long-range reconnaissance, elimination of high-value targets, counter-sniper operations, or precision support for larger Free World units.

During conventional battles, Killshot may operate ahead of friendly positions or from concealed locations overlooking the primary battlefield.

His role allows him to disrupt enemy forces before or during an assault by eliminating officers, communications personnel, weapon operators, or other strategically important targets.

## Marksmanship

Killshot possesses exceptional accuracy with ballistic firearms.

Although particularly associated with sniper rifles, his skill extends to hand cannons, pistols, and other precision weapons.

He is capable of acquiring and engaging multiple targets within extremely short periods of time.

Rather than relying entirely upon advanced targeting systems, Killshot possesses extensive conventional marksmanship training and can continue operating effectively if electronic assistance becomes unavailable.

### The Three-Kill Shot

Killshot's most famous recorded feat involved the elimination of three hostile personnel using a single conventional bullet.

The projectile passed through the first target before continuing through two additional individuals positioned behind them.

No explosive, specialised, or anomalous ammunition was used.

The circumstances required an extremely precise alignment of all three targets and exceptionally accurate shot placement.

The incident became one of the most frequently repeated stories concerning Killshot within the Free World Order.

While later retellings have exaggerated various details surrounding the engagement, the three confirmed eliminations from a single conventional projectile remain part of his documented combat record.

## Active Camouflage

Killshot's armour incorporates an advanced active camouflage system.

Rather than rendering him literally invisible, the system uses adaptive materials, optical systems, and environmental processing to reproduce surrounding visual information across the exterior of his armour.

When stationary and properly concealed, Killshot can become extremely difficult to identify with the naked eye.

Movement, environmental interference, damage to the armour, certain scanning systems, thermal detection, and close observation can reveal his position.

The system therefore supplements Killshot's conventional concealment techniques rather than replacing them.

Killshot frequently uses active camouflage to establish firing positions in locations enemy forces would otherwise consider too exposed.

## Weapons

### Sniper Rifle

Killshot's primary weapon is a precision ballistic sniper rifle configured for extreme accuracy at long range.

Different ammunition types can be used depending upon the intended target.

### Hand Cannon

A powerful handgun provides Killshot with an effective secondary weapon when long-range combat is no longer practical.

Its stopping power makes it particularly useful against armoured opponents.

### Silenced Pistol

Killshot carries a suppressed sidearm for situations where maintaining concealment is more important than maximum firepower.

It is commonly used during infiltration and reconnaissance operations.

### Throwing Knives

Killshot carries several balanced combat knives.

Although primarily intended as conventional tools and close-range weapons, his exceptional accuracy allows him to use them effectively as thrown weapons when necessary.

## Equipment

In addition to his weapons, Killshot commonly carries medical supplies, reconnaissance equipment, ammunition, and specialised ammunition for his primary rifle.

Explosive ammunition can be carried during operations where additional anti-material capability is required.

His equipment loadout varies considerably depending upon whether he is operating independently, supporting conventional forces, or participating in an infiltration mission.

## Combat Style

Killshot attempts to control an engagement before his opponent understands that one has begun.

He studies enemy positions, identifies important personnel, establishes escape routes, and selects firing positions before engaging whenever circumstances permit.

Once firing begins, he prioritises targets according to their immediate importance rather than simply engaging whichever enemy is easiest to hit.

Officers, heavy weapon operators, communications personnel, enemy marksmen, and exposed specialists are frequent priorities.

Killshot will also relocate between firing positions when necessary to prevent enemy forces from identifying his location.

His active camouflage makes this particularly effective.

## Close-Range Combat

Although primarily a sniper, Killshot is not helpless at close range.

His hand cannon, suppressed pistol, and knives provide several options when an enemy reaches his position.

Nevertheless, close combat removes many of Killshot's greatest advantages.

Whenever possible, he attempts to disengage and restore distance rather than remaining in prolonged close-quarters combat.

## Reputation

Killshot is widely respected within the Free World Order for his precision and reliability.

Enemy personnel who become aware of his presence frequently alter movement patterns, avoid exposed positions, and increase surveillance of potential sniper locations.

This disruption can itself benefit Free World forces even when Killshot is not actively firing.

His reputation has consequently become another weapon.

A battlefield does not necessarily need to contain Killshot for soldiers to begin worrying that it does.
