# Kosander

Kosander is a powerful allied military civilization operating within the [[Outer Rim]], known for its disciplined armed forces, heavily fortified worlds, and long-standing alliance with the [[Angel Swords]]. Unlike the vast empires of the [[Hell Hosts]] or the mysterious powers of the [[Voidlands]], Kosander functions primarily as a highly militarized regional power focused on defense, survival, and strategic warfare.

The civilization is centered around the world of [[Joshua]], a heavily fortified military planet containing enormous training facilities, defense systems, and fleet production centers. Due to its strategic location within the Outer Rim, Joshua has become one of the most defended worlds under Kosander control.

Kosander forces are widely respected throughout the galaxy for their discipline, tactical coordination, and resilience during large-scale conflicts. Their military doctrine emphasizes defensive warfare, strategic positioning, and overwhelming coordinated assaults rather than reckless expansion.

Throughout galactic history, Kosander has fought alongside the [[Angel Swords]] during numerous wars and crusades, particularly during conflicts involving the [[Hell Hosts]]. While maintaining close ties with the Angel Swords, Kosander remains politically independent and retains its own military command structure.

The faction is led by [[Kosis]], one of the most respected military leaders within the Outer Rim. Under his leadership, Kosander strengthened its military infrastructure and expanded its influence across nearby systems.

Despite its military focus, Kosander is generally viewed more favorably than many larger galactic powers due to its defensive nature and comparatively stable governance.
