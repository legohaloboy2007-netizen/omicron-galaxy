# Korven Drahl

> "An army does not march on courage. It marches because someone made sure the fuel arrived."

## Overview

**Korven Drahl** is one of the highest-ranking military commanders within the [[Ashen Kingdom]] and serves as a principal military authority beneath [[Blood]], the Ashen Lord.

An experienced Ashen officer from [[Vexar Prime]], Korven is known for disciplined command, industrial-scale logistics, siege warfare, and the coordination of military operations across the kingdom's harsh volcanic territories.

Unlike commanders who build their reputations through dramatic battlefield victories, Korven believes wars are usually decided long before opposing armies meet.

Fuel.

Ammunition.

Rail access.

Repair capacity.

Food.

Replacement equipment.

If those systems fail, courage becomes irrelevant.

Korven's influence over Ashen military doctrine has therefore extended far beyond battlefield tactics.

He has helped shape an armed force designed to keep fighting in environments where infrastructure, terrain, and extreme heat can be as dangerous as the enemy.

## Appearance

Korven is a heavily built Ashen with dark charcoal-grey skin and pronounced mineral-like patterns across his arms, neck, and portions of his face.

Under normal conditions, these markings resemble dark branching veins beneath the skin.

During extreme heat or physical exertion, they become significantly more visible and may produce a faint ember-red glow.

His eyes are a deep copper colour.

Korven's hair was once black but has developed substantial grey with age.

He keeps it short.

Years of military service have left him with several visible scars, including a burn extending across one side of his neck.

Korven generally wears heavy Ashen military armour designed for extended operations in volcanic environments.

The armour is practical, reinforced, and noticeably less decorative than equipment worn by royal ceremonial units.

---

## Personality

Korven is practical, patient, disciplined, and extraordinarily difficult to impress.

He does not care how dramatic a plan sounds.

He cares whether it works.

Korven is particularly irritated by officers who focus entirely on combat while ignoring supply, maintenance, evacuation, or reinforcement.

To him, logistics are not secondary to warfare.

They are warfare.

Despite his severe professional manner, Korven is not cruel.

He has spent enough time commanding large formations to understand exactly what careless orders cost.

He dislikes unnecessary casualties and considers wasting trained personnel through poor planning one of the clearest signs of incompetent leadership.

Korven possesses a dry sense of humour that generally becomes more noticeable the worse a situation gets.

If he begins making jokes, something has probably gone very wrong.

# Role Within the Ashen Kingdom

Korven serves as one of the principal commanders of the [[Ashen Kingdom]]'s conventional armed forces.

His responsibilities include:

- Large-scale military deployments
- Defence of major Ashen worlds
- Siege operations
- Strategic logistics
- Military rail coordination
- Reinforcement planning
- Industrial mobilisation
- Supply-chain security
- Defensive infrastructure
- Advising [[Blood]] on conventional military matters

Korven is particularly important because Blood's personal power does not eliminate the need for ordinary armed forces.

An Ascended ruler may win battles.

Someone still has to defend cities, supply armies, occupy territory, evacuate civilians, and keep the kingdom functioning.

That is where Korven operates.

---

# Military Philosophy

Korven believes military power is ultimately industrial.

Weapons require factories.

Factories require resources.

Resources require transportation.

Transportation requires protection.

Everything connects.

Because of this, he teaches officers to think beyond the immediate battlefield.

A successful commander must understand:

- Supply routes
- Rail networks
- Fuel production
- Industrial capacity
- Repair infrastructure
- Medical evacuation
- Weather and volcanic activity
- Civilian population movement
- Replacement personnel
- Ammunition expenditure

Korven considers an army that cannot sustain itself to already be defeated.

---

# Ashen Rail Warfare

The Ashen Kingdom maintains extensive rail networks across several of its worlds, particularly for moving industrial materials and military equipment.

Korven became one of the strongest advocates for integrating these networks directly into military planning.

Armoured military trains are used to transport:

- Troops
- Heavy weapons
- Vehicles
- Ammunition
- Medical equipment
- Construction machinery
- Mobile command systems

During large campaigns, rail junctions become strategically important objectives.

Korven is known for designing layered defensive networks around these locations.

Destroying a single rail line rarely stops an Ashen deployment.

Korven assumes the enemy will try.

---

# Siege Warfare

Korven is particularly experienced in prolonged siege operations.

He prefers isolation and pressure over wasteful frontal assaults.

His methods frequently involve:

- Cutting supply routes
- Controlling transportation
- Disabling industrial infrastructure
- Establishing artillery positions
- Creating layered defensive lines
- Forcing enemies to expend resources
- Opening evacuation routes for civilians
- Negotiating surrender when possible

Korven does not believe a siege must end with the complete destruction of the target.

If an enemy can be forced to surrender without sacrificing thousands of soldiers, he considers that preferable.

---

# Relationship with Blood

Korven serves beneath [[Blood]], the Ashen Lord.

Their relationship is unusual.

Blood possesses levels of destructive capability no conventional commander can match.

Korven possesses something Blood does not:

The ability to manage an entire military system.

Korven does not attempt to compete with Blood's personal power.

Instead, he focuses on ensuring that the kingdom does not become dependent upon it.

This has occasionally caused disagreement between them.

Korven strongly opposes military planning based around the assumption that Blood will personally solve every major crisis.

His position is simple:

**A kingdom whose defence depends entirely upon one person does not have a defence system.**

Blood appears to respect Korven enough to tolerate this criticism.

Korven is one of relatively few officers willing to give it.

---

# Relationship with Ember

Korven respects [[Ember]] and considers them an important figure within the Ashen Kingdom.

Their relationship depends heavily upon Ember's current role within established lore, but Korven generally approaches them with the same expectation he applies to everyone else:

Power does not exempt someone from planning.

If Ember participates in a military operation, Korven expects them to understand the objective before fighting begins.

This has occasionally resulted in blunt conversations.

---

# Relationship with Soldiers

Korven has a strong reputation among ordinary Ashen military personnel.

He is demanding.

He is also known for refusing to spend soldiers unnecessarily.

Troops under Korven's command generally receive:

- Strong logistical support
- Redundant evacuation plans
- Reliable ammunition supply
- Proper environmental equipment
- Clear operational objectives

He considers these things basic responsibilities rather than privileges.

Korven has personally removed officers from command for neglecting troop welfare or treating preventable casualties as acceptable.

---

# Industrial Mobilisation

During major wars, Korven works closely with mining operations, factories, rail authorities, and resource administrators.

This gives him influence extending beyond traditional military command.

He has coordinated emergency industrial mobilisation on several occasions, converting civilian production capacity toward:

- Armour manufacture
- Vehicle repair
- Ammunition
- Emergency infrastructure
- Medical equipment
- Defensive construction

Korven understands that doing this too aggressively can damage the civilian economy.

A kingdom cannot remain permanently mobilised without consequences.

---

# Grimhold

Korven spends significant time overseeing military activity connected to [[Grimhold]].

The world serves as an important Ashen military outpost and staging location.

Korven has been involved in expanding its:

- Defensive infrastructure
- Supply capacity
- Training facilities
- Heavy weapons deployment
- Strategic transportation networks

He considers Grimhold essential to the kingdom's ability to respond quickly to external threats.

---

# Malgor

[[Malgor]] is particularly important to Korven because of its mining output.

Military campaigns consume extraordinary quantities of material.

Korven therefore treats the protection of Malgor's mines and transportation infrastructure as a strategic priority.

He has repeatedly argued that resource worlds deserve the same defensive attention as prestigious capital installations.

A weapon factory without raw material produces nothing.

Korven repeats this point often.

---

# Combat Ability

Although primarily a senior commander, Korven remains an experienced combatant.

He is physically strong even by Ashen standards and possesses extensive experience with heavy military equipment.

His preferred weapons include:

- Heavy battle rifles
- Large-calibre sidearms
- Ashen combat blades
- Heavy machine weapons when required

Korven fights methodically.

He does not rely upon speed or elaborate manoeuvres.

He uses cover, suppressive fire, environmental awareness, and overwhelming positional advantage.

---

# Ashen Physiology

Korven possesses the natural biological advantages of the [[Ashens]].

These include:

- High heat tolerance
- Resistance to smoke and particulates
- Strong respiratory filtration
- Dense physical build
- Increased tolerance for volcanic environments

His mineral-like skin patterns become more visible during combat or extreme environmental exposure.

Korven is not immune to fire.

He becomes irritated when outsiders assume otherwise.

# Strengths

## Logistics

Korven is exceptionally skilled at coordinating large-scale transportation and supply networks.

## Siege Warfare

He has extensive experience surrounding, isolating, and pressuring fortified positions.

## Industrial Strategy

Korven understands the relationship between warfare, manufacturing, mining, and infrastructure.

## Discipline

He remains calm even during rapidly deteriorating campaigns.

## Soldier Welfare

Korven understands that preserving trained personnel is strategically valuable as well as morally important.

---

# Weaknesses

## Methodical Planning

Korven prefers campaigns supported by clear logistics and preparation.

Highly unpredictable warfare can reduce his effectiveness.

## Limited Patience for Politics

He understands political reality but dislikes ceremonial court behaviour.

This occasionally creates unnecessary enemies.

## Dependence on Infrastructure

Many of Korven's preferred strategies rely upon functioning transportation and industrial systems.

Enemies capable of destroying infrastructure rapidly can force him into less comfortable forms of warfare.

# Early Life

Korven was born on [[Vexar Prime]] and grew up around heavy industry.

Members of his family worked in transportation, mining, and industrial maintenance rather than military service.

This background profoundly influenced how he later understood warfare.

Korven entered the Ashen military as a young adult.

His early career involved protecting industrial convoys and transportation networks rather than glamorous front-line assignments.

He gradually rose through command positions after repeatedly demonstrating an unusual ability to keep military formations supplied under difficult conditions.

Eventually, his logistical expertise became impossible to separate from his tactical ability.

By the time he reached senior command, Korven had become one of the architects of modern Ashen military doctrine.

---

# View of the Ashen Kingdom

Korven is deeply loyal to the Ashen Kingdom.

His loyalty is primarily directed toward its people rather than its symbols.

He respects tradition, monarchy, and military hierarchy.

He does not believe any of them matter if ordinary Ashens cannot live safely.

To Korven, the kingdom is not the palace on Vexar Prime.

It is every miner on Malgor.

Every soldier on Grimhold.

Every rail engineer maintaining a line through volcanic storms.

Every civilian city depending upon those systems continuing to function.

That is what he believes he is defending.
