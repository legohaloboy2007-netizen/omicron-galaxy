# Kron Vhaz’kar

## The Citadel of Eternal Dominion

Kron Vhaz’kar is the capital world of the:

[[Dominion Keepers]]

One of the oldest and most militarized powers operating within the [[Voidlands]].

The world is not a planet in the traditional sense.

Kron Vhaz’kar is an immense fortified citadel-world approximately the size of a small star, composed almost entirely of layered fortress structures, defensive megasystems, and colossal voidsteel architecture surrounding a stabilized artificial core.

Within the [[Voidlands]], it is known as:

The Citadel of Eternal Dominion.

## The Dominion Keepers

[[Dominion Keepers|The Dominion Keepers]] are a highly authoritarian [[Voidlands]] civilization dedicated to:
- order
- permanence
- military supremacy
- and resistance against total reality collapse

Unlike many [[Voidlands]] factions that embrace instability or adaptation, the Keepers believe:
the Void must be controlled through absolute structure.

Their philosophy states:

> “Chaos survives only where order hesitates.”

The Keepers maintain one of the largest standing military forces within the [[Voidlands]].

## Structure

Kron Vhaz’kar is constructed from immense concentric fortress layers surrounding a stabilized gravitational core.

The world consists of:
- colossal fortress sectors
- planetary-scale walls
- suspended military citadels
- endless battlements
- orbital defense rings
- industrial war sectors
- command spires
- dimensional anchor arrays

Entire regions of the structure are large enough to contain:
- oceans
- fleet shipyards
- military districts
- and fortress-cities within the walls themselves.

The world was designed not merely as a capital—

But as a weaponized civilization.

## Voidsteel Architecture

The architecture of Kron Vhaz’kar is dominated by:
- golden-black fortress structures
- angular megacitadels
- towering defensive spires
- abyssal halls
- massive war gates
- and layered defensive bastions

Nearly all major construction is formed from:

Voidsteel

An extraordinarily dense material resistant to:
- dimensional instability
- gravitational distortion
- Void corrosion
- and Element 95 contamination

Many fortress walls are kilometers thick.

## The Overlord Council

At the center of Kron Vhaz’kar lies:

The Throne Bastion

Seat of the:
Overlord Council

The ruling authority of [[Dominion Keepers|The Dominion Keepers]].

The Council governs through:
- military doctrine
- strategic law
- dimensional containment policy
- and strict hierarchical command

The Bastion itself is protected by:
- layered Void shields
- reality anchors
- autonomous war systems
- and elite Keeper Legions

Very few outsiders are permitted entry.

## The Black Legions

The Dominion Keepers maintain enormous military forces known as:

The Black Legions

These armies specialize in:
- Void warfare
- fortress defense
- dimensional containment
- anti-corruption operations
- large-scale fleet combat

Their soldiers are known for:
- absolute discipline
- emotional suppression
- unwavering loyalty
- and heavy Voidsteel war armor

The Legions are feared even among Voidlands civilizations.

## Dimensional Anchor Arrays

One of Kron Vhaz’kar’s greatest strengths is its immense network of:

Dimensional Anchor Arrays

These systems stabilize localized reality around the citadel-world and prevent:
- spatial collapse
- recursive distortion
- uncontrolled Void fractures
- and reality destabilization

Without the Arrays, the structure would likely collapse under the pressure of surrounding Void Laws.

## The Iron Halo

Surrounding Kron Vhaz’kar is a massive orbital defense network known as:

The Iron Halo

This system contains:
- fortress satellites
- anti-fleet cannons
- dimensional interceptors
- gravity disruption weapons
- and autonomous defense platforms

The Halo is considered one of the strongest defensive systems in known existence.

## Internal Districts

Despite its militarized nature, Kron Vhaz’kar contains enormous internal population sectors including:
- fortress-cities
- industrial hive districts
- military academies
- Voidsteel foundries
- archive sanctums
- and command cathedrals

Entire generations of Dominion Keepers live their entire lives within the citadel-world.

## Relationship to the Voidlands

The Dominion Keepers view many Voidlands civilizations as:
- unstable
- dangerous
- or ideologically weak

They maintain especially hostile relations toward:
- [[The Witness]]
- reality cults
- uncontrolled corruption groups
- and rogue Void entities

The Keepers consider themselves:
the final wall preventing total collapse.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Kron Vhaz’kar is known as:
- the capital of the Dominion Keepers
- one of the largest fortress structures in existence
- a citadel-world built against the Void itself
- the heart of military order within the Voidlands

Among the Dominion Keepers, one phrase is carved across countless fortress gates:

> “Reality breaks.  
> Dominion remains.”
