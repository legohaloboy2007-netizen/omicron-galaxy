# Kryon’s End

Kryon’s End is a frozen graveworld located at the farthest edge of known [[Ice Kingdom]] territory within the Outer Winter Expanse. Once a major military stronghold during an ancient war long before the modern era, the planet now stands as a shattered wasteland of ruined fortresses, frozen battlefields, and buried war relics consumed by endless winter.

Across the Ice Kingdom, Kryon’s End is remembered not as a world of victory—

But as a world where the war never truly ended.

## The Last Stand of High Marshal Kryon

Kryon’s End takes its name from:

High Marshal Kryon

One of the greatest military commanders in Ice Kingdom history.

During an ancient invasion of the outer reaches, Kryon refused to abandon the planet even after enemy forces overwhelmed the region. Alongside his fleets, soldiers, and defensive legions, he made a final stand against impossible odds.

The battle devastated:
- the land
- the oceans
- the skies
- and much of the planet’s infrastructure

In the aftermath, the enemy vanished, the war ended elsewhere, and Kryon himself disappeared within the storm.

His body was never recovered.

Within the Ice Kingdom, his sacrifice became legendary.

## Environment

Kryon’s End is classified as:
- Dead World
- Frozen Tundra Planet
- Ancient Conflict Zone

The planet is dominated by:
- perpetual blizzards
- subzero temperatures
- frozen wastelands
- ruined battlefields
- shattered fortresses
- collapsed military sectors
- ice-covered ship graveyards

Entire warships remain frozen within glaciers and snowfields thousands of years after the conflict ended.

The atmosphere is harsh, unstable, and filled with constant storm activity.

## Ancient Battlefields

Much of Kryon’s End remains scarred by ancient warfare.

Across the surface lie:
- destroyed fortifications
- abandoned trenches
- frozen armored convoys
- shattered defense cannons
- ruined military citadels

Some battlefields still contain dormant systems and unidentified energy signatures buried beneath the ice.

## Frozen Fleet Graveyards

Massive fleets destroyed during the final conflict remain preserved across the planet.

These graveyards contain:
- frozen warships
- buried carriers
- collapsed orbital debris
- ancient weapons systems
- inaccessible command vessels

Many Ice Kingdom expeditions seek to recover lost military technology from the wreckage.

## Buried Vaults & Forgotten Bunkers

Deep beneath the frozen surface are countless:
- sealed military vaults
- forgotten bunkers
- hidden storage facilities
- abandoned command centers

Many remain inaccessible due to:
- unstable ice structures
- radiation pockets
- dormant defense systems
- unknown internal activity

Some believe Kryon’s final command bunker still exists somewhere beneath the planet.

## Wildlife & Threats

Over centuries, the frozen ecosystem adapted to the dead world.

Known threats include:
- Iceborn predators
- rad-warped fauna
- subsurface tunneling creatures
- environmental collapse zones
- unstable radiation sectors
- unidentified ancient defense constructs

Several expeditions have disappeared without explanation in the deeper frozen regions.

## Reclamation Efforts

Under Queen [[Calypso]]’s command, elite Frostguard forces and archaeotech expeditions have begun limited reclamation operations across the planet.

Current objectives include:
- recovering ancient war assets
- securing surviving infrastructure
- locating buried military archives
- uncovering lost Ice Kingdom history
- investigating hidden vault systems

Mission priority across the planet remains classified as:

HIGH

## Reputation

Throughout the galaxy, Kryon’s End is known as:
- the Graveworld of the Ice Kingdom
- one of the deadliest frozen worlds in known space
- a planet haunted by ancient war
- a frozen military tomb preserved beneath endless storms

Among Ice Kingdom historians, one ancient phrase is often repeated:

> “The war ended everywhere else. On Kryon’s End, it simply froze.”
