# Leviticus

Leviticus is one of the most feared worlds controlled by the [[Angel Swords]], known across the galaxy by many names including:

- Hell
- The Eye
- Terror

The planet is considered extremely dangerous, both physically and psychologically, and very few individuals are ever permitted to travel there.

According to Angel Sword doctrine:

> Those who enter Leviticus may never leave.

Unless they satisfy a strict and largely unknown set of criteria defined by the Angel Sword Code of Honor.

The exact meaning of these conditions remains heavily restricted within Angel Sword archives.

## Environment

Leviticus is a world consumed almost entirely by fire.

The planet’s surface is dominated by:
- oceans of magma
- burning mountain ranges
- collapsing volcanic landscapes
- endless ash storms
- blackened wastelands
- rivers of molten rock
- constant seismic instability

Large portions of the atmosphere glow red and orange due to continuous volcanic activity and atmospheric ignition.

The skies of Leviticus are frequently obscured by:
- ash clouds
- burning debris
- volcanic lightning
- dense smoke storms

From orbit, the planet resembles a massive burning eye within space.

This appearance is believed to be one of the origins of the name:
The Eye.

## Dangerous Entities

Leviticus is inhabited by numerous hostile and poorly understood entities adapted to the planet’s extreme conditions.

Very little verified information exists regarding these lifeforms due to the extremely low survival rate of expeditions.

Survivor accounts describe:
- massive shadow-like creatures within volcanic storms
- humanoid entities emerging from ash fields
- voices heard within firestorms
- figures appearing in regions previously thought uninhabited

Many reports contradict one another completely.

Some believe portions of the planet may possess anomalous properties beyond natural explanation.

## The Rule of Names

One of the oldest warnings associated with Leviticus states:

> Names can be deadly.

The exact meaning of this phrase remains unknown.

Angel Sword records surrounding the subject are highly restricted, though many believe:
- speaking certain names on the planet is dangerous
- some entities respond to recognition
- names may possess anomalous influence within certain regions of Leviticus

Very few outside the Angel Swords fully understand the warning.

## Purpose Within the Angel Swords

The true purpose of Leviticus remains unclear.

Some theories suggest it functions as:
- a sacred trial world
- a containment zone
- a prison planet
- a spiritual proving ground
- an ancient punishment site
- a location tied to forgotten Angel Sword history

Despite its dangers, the Angel Swords continue to maintain hidden facilities and restricted operations on the planet.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Leviticus is viewed as:
- one of the most terrifying worlds in known space
- a cursed planet tied to Angel Sword mythology
- a place few return from
- a world surrounded by secrecy and fear

Even among the Angel Swords themselves, Leviticus is rarely discussed openly.
