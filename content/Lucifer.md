# Lucifer

**Real Name:** Unknown  
**Known Name:** Lucifer  
**Species:** Unknown  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** Unknown; believed to be several thousand years old  
**Faction:** [[Angel Swords]]  
**Affiliation:** Rogue / Formerly Angel Swords  
**Former Rank:** Master General  
**Status:** Active  

Lucifer is an ancient anomalous humanoid and former high-ranking member of the [[Angel Swords]].

Believed to have existed for several thousand years, Lucifer's true origin remains unknown. Historical Angel Sword records frequently describe him as an **angel**, although whether this represents a literal species classification, religious terminology, or an interpretation of his anomalous nature remains disputed.

Lucifer once held the rank of Master General within the Angel Swords before eventually separating from the faction.

He is particularly notable for the anomalous properties of his blood.

When introduced into the body of another living organism, Lucifer's blood can temporarily produce extreme biological and anomalous changes.

The results are powerful.

They are not always survivable.

## Personality

Lucifer is calm, patient, and exceptionally difficult to provoke.

Despite possessing considerable combat experience, he rarely seeks ordinary confrontation.

Lucifer has lived long enough that most conventional opponents provide little interest to him.

He is fascinated by individuals who continue developing beyond their apparent limitations and frequently observes promising warriors rather than immediately confronting them.

Lucifer does not necessarily spare these individuals because of compassion.

He wants to know what they might become.

His greatest obsession is finding an opponent capable of genuinely challenging him.

This desire has shaped much of his behaviour across centuries.

Lucifer may allow an enemy to escape, refuse an opportunity to kill a developing warrior, or deliberately restrict himself during combat simply because eliminating the individual would destroy the possibility of a more interesting confrontation later.

This philosophy has made his behaviour extremely unpredictable.

## Origins

Lucifer's origins remain unknown.

Records attributed to him predate many modern political powers within the Omicron Galaxy.

Some Angel Sword archives describe encounters with Lucifer thousands of years before the modern era, although the reliability and interpretation of these records remain disputed.

The name **Lucifer** may not be his original name.

His exact connection to the early history of the Angel Swords remains similarly unclear.

At some point, Lucifer became closely associated with the faction and eventually achieved the rank of Master General.

How an individual of uncertain origin gained such authority remains an important subject within Angel Sword history.

## The Angel

Historical records frequently refer to Lucifer as an **angel**.

The meaning of this classification is unclear.

Lucifer is humanoid but possesses biological and anomalous characteristics inconsistent with baseline humans and several other known species.

Whether he belongs to an undocumented species, represents an altered organism, or exists as something considerably stranger remains unknown.

Lucifer himself has provided little clarification.

## Angel Swords

Lucifer formerly served as one of the highest-ranking military figures within the Angel Swords.

His extraordinary lifespan means that his relationship with the faction extends across periods of history experienced by few living individuals.

Eventually, Lucifer separated from the Angel Swords and became a rogue operative.

The precise circumstances surrounding his departure remain poorly documented.

Although no longer reliably controlled by Angel Sword leadership, Lucifer retains extensive knowledge of the faction, its history, military doctrine, and older traditions.

His existence remains an uncomfortable part of Angel Sword history.

## Blood

Lucifer's blood possesses anomalous properties.

When consumed, injected, transfused, or otherwise introduced into another organism, small quantities can produce dramatic temporary changes.

Recipients have demonstrated enhanced physical performance and other anomalous capabilities.

The exact result varies significantly between individuals.

Two recipients exposed to identical quantities may develop completely different effects.

Some experience enhanced strength or reflexes.

Others demonstrate unusual sensory, biological, or anomalous phenomena.

The process is highly unpredictable.

## Blood Transfer

Exposure to Lucifer's blood does not permanently grant another individual all of his abilities.

Instead, the substance appears to interact with the recipient's existing biology.

The resulting transformation can produce significant increases in capability, but also places enormous strain upon the body.

Possible consequences include:

- Extreme fever
- Organ damage
- Neurological instability
- Abnormal tissue growth
- Temporary mutation
- Psychological disturbance
- Systemic biological failure
- Death

Repeated exposure can produce increasingly unpredictable consequences.

Lucifer's blood is consequently regarded as both an extraordinarily valuable biological resource and an extreme medical hazard.

## Combat

Lucifer is an exceptionally experienced combatant.

Thousands of years of survival have exposed him to numerous fighting styles, weapons, technologies, and military doctrines.

His greatest advantage is therefore not raw physical strength.

It is experience.

Lucifer frequently recognises techniques, tactical patterns, and mistakes because he has encountered variations of them before.

He rarely commits completely to a battle immediately.

Instead, he observes.

If an opponent interests him, Lucifer may deliberately prolong the engagement in order to understand their abilities.

## Anomalous Abilities

Lucifer possesses several anomalous capabilities, although historical accounts frequently exaggerate their extent.

Confirmed reports suggest that he can manipulate matter within a limited area around himself.

This allows Lucifer to alter existing material and, under certain circumstances, form temporary weapons from matter available within his surroundings.

He has also demonstrated unusual control over local gravitational forces.

The full limits of these abilities remain unknown.

Ancient accounts attribute numerous additional powers to Lucifer, including teleportation, invisibility, telepathy, restoration, and the projection of destructive energy.

Many of these reports cannot be independently verified.

Some may represent misunderstood applications of his confirmed abilities.

Others may simply be mythology accumulated around an individual who has existed for thousands of years.

## Weapons

Lucifer rarely carries a conventional permanent arsenal.

His control over matter allows him to reshape suitable material into simple weapons when necessary.

Blades are particularly common.

Lucifer is nevertheless familiar with conventional weapons and will use them when circumstances require it.

He appears to consider dependence upon a specific weapon unnecessarily restrictive.

## Search for an Equal

Lucifer's primary personal objective is deceptively simple.

He wants to be challenged.

After thousands of years of conflict, ordinary victories provide little satisfaction.

Lucifer is consequently fascinated by individuals whose abilities continue developing.

He has occasionally refused to kill promising opponents specifically because he believes they may eventually become powerful enough to provide the confrontation he seeks.

This behaviour should not be mistaken for mercy.

Lucifer has killed countless individuals throughout his existence.

He simply considers killing someone with unrealised potential wasteful.

## Historical Accounts

Lucifer appears in numerous fragmented records attributed to different periods of Angel Sword history.

These accounts frequently contradict one another.

Some describe him as a military commander.

Others describe an angelic warrior.

Some portray him as a traitor.

Others treat him almost as a religious figure.

Several descriptions attribute abilities to Lucifer that have never been observed in the modern era.

Separating the historical individual from the mythology surrounding him has become extremely difficult.

Lucifer appears to prefer it that way.

## Unknowns

Much about Lucifer remains unresolved.

His original name is unknown.

His birthplace is unknown.

His species is unknown.

The mechanism behind his longevity is unknown.

The origin of his anomalous blood remains unexplained.

It is unclear whether he was already ancient when he first encountered the Angel Swords or whether his transformation occurred afterward.

It is similarly uncertain how many individuals throughout history have received his blood.

Lucifer has survived long enough to become partially indistinguishable from the legends surrounding him.
