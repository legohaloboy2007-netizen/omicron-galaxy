# Maelis Vorn

> "Ash does not care whether you are strong. It enters the lungs all the same."

## Overview

**Dr. Maelis Vorn** is a senior Ashen physician and researcher within the [[Ashen Kingdom]], specializing in industrial medicine, environmental toxicology, heat trauma, and Ashen physiology.

Her work focuses on keeping civilians, miners, soldiers, and industrial personnel alive in environments defined by extreme heat, volcanic gases, ash exposure, and heavy machinery.

Maelis is particularly well known for her research into the long-term effects of mineral contamination and repeated exposure to volcanic particulates.

Unlike many researchers whose work remains confined to laboratories, Maelis spends significant time in mines, factories, rail depots, military installations, and disaster zones.

She believes medicine designed for perfect clinical conditions is useless if most people actually need treatment somewhere covered in smoke.

## Appearance

Maelis has dark ash-brown skin with subtle copper-grey undertones.

Her mineral-like branching patterns are especially visible across her forearms and upper chest.

Under intense heat or physical stress, these markings can become faintly amber-red.

Her eyes are dark gold with a reddish tint.

Her hair is black and usually worn short for practical reasons.

Maelis typically wears medical clothing reinforced for industrial environments, including:

- Heat-resistant outer layers
- Respiratory filtration systems
- Chemical protection
- Portable diagnostic equipment
- Emergency trauma supplies

She frequently complains that ceremonial medical uniforms are designed by people who have never treated anyone near an active refinery.

---

## Personality

Maelis is blunt, intelligent, practical, and deeply protective of patients.

She has little patience for bravado around injury.

A soldier claiming they are "fine" while actively bleeding irritates her more than the injury itself.

Maelis is also extremely skeptical of industrial administrators who treat worker casualties as unavoidable.

She understands that dangerous work cannot always be made safe.

She does not accept that as an excuse to stop trying.

Her humour is dry and frequently appears at inappropriate moments.

She believes this helps.

Not everyone agrees.

# Role Within the Ashen Kingdom

Maelis serves within the kingdom's senior medical and scientific institutions.

Her responsibilities include:

- Treating severe industrial injuries
- Studying Ashen physiology
- Developing heat-resistant medical equipment
- Researching volcanic gas exposure
- Investigating mineral contamination
- Supporting mining operations
- Advising military medical units
- Improving emergency care in remote settlements
- Studying long-term occupational disease
- Training field physicians

Her work frequently overlaps with both civilian industry and the military.

---

# Ashen Physiology Research

Maelis has contributed significantly to modern understanding of Ashen biology.

Her research includes:

- Heat tolerance
- Respiratory filtration
- Mineral absorption
- Blood chemistry
- Skin adaptation
- Temperature regulation
- Particulate resistance
- Cold vulnerability

She is particularly interested in the mineral-like branching patterns found beneath Ashen skin.

These structures appear to play a role in heat distribution and environmental adaptation, though their exact biological function remains incompletely understood.

Maelis strongly opposes simplistic descriptions of Ashens as "fireproof."

They are not.

---

# Industrial Medicine

A large portion of Maelis's career has focused on injuries associated with heavy industry.

Common cases include:

- Crush injuries
- Severe burns
- Toxic inhalation
- Chemical exposure
- Machinery accidents
- Mining collapse
- Thermal shock
- Metal contamination

Maelis helped develop several field-treatment protocols now used across the Ashen Kingdom.

She believes the first minutes after an industrial accident are often more important than the first hour in a hospital.

---

# Volcanic Toxicology

Ashen worlds contain numerous naturally occurring gases and mineral contaminants.

Even species adapted to these environments can suffer from prolonged exposure.

Maelis studies:

- Sulfur compounds
- Heavy metals
- Ash particulates
- Volcanic aerosols
- Industrial fumes
- Reactive mineral dust

Her research has improved respiratory protection and long-term health monitoring among miners and factory workers.

---

# Heat Trauma

Ashens possess far greater heat tolerance than humans and many other species.

This has occasionally created a dangerous cultural assumption that heat-related injury is not serious.

Maelis strongly disagrees.

She has spent years treating:

- Thermal exhaustion
- Severe dehydration
- Internal overheating
- Burn trauma
- Heat-induced organ stress

Ashens can survive more heat.

That does not mean they can survive unlimited heat.

---

# Relationship with Blood

Maelis has treated personnel affected by incidents involving [[Blood]]'s Ascended abilities.

This gives her a complicated professional relationship with the Ashen Lord.

She respects his authority.

She is also entirely willing to tell him when his presence has created a medical disaster.

Blood's extreme heat and destructive capability can make battlefield treatment exceptionally difficult.

Maelis has on multiple occasions demanded that he leave an active medical zone because his ambient effects were interfering with patient care.

He did.

Eventually.

---

# Relationship with Korven Drahl

Maelis works closely with [[Korven Drahl]] during military operations.

Korven values her because she provides realistic assessments of how long forces can remain operational under environmental stress.

Maelis values Korven because he understands that exhausted soldiers eventually stop being useful soldiers.

Their cooperation has improved medical evacuation and heat-management protocols across several Ashen military formations.

---

# Relationship with Seraphine Kael

[[Seraphine Kael]] frequently consults Maelis when industrial or medical problems become political.

Worker illness, unsafe factories, mine collapses, and toxic exposure can rapidly create unrest.

Seraphine handles the political consequences.

Maelis handles the medical reality.

The two work well together, although Maelis has little patience for attempts to soften language in official reports.

If people were poisoned, she wants the report to say they were poisoned.

---

# Relationship with Damar Veyk

Maelis and [[Damar Veyk]] have worked together extensively on industrial safety.

Damar designs systems intended to prevent accidents.

Maelis treats people when those systems fail.

Their relationship is built around mutual respect and frequent arguments about acceptable risk.

Maelis believes Damar is too willing to personally enter dangerous repair zones.

Damar believes Maelis worries professionally.

Both are correct.

---

# Worker Health

Maelis is particularly focused on the health of miners, rail workers, factory crews, and industrial personnel.

She believes dangerous professions often normalize symptoms that should never be considered normal.

Coughing blood after a shift is not "part of the job."

Neither is chronic nerve damage.

Neither is heat collapse.

Maelis has pushed aggressively for:

- Better filtration systems
- Mandatory health screening
- Improved protective equipment
- Exposure limits
- Emergency medical training
- Worker compensation

This has made her unpopular with some industrial organizations.

She does not care.

---

# Field Work

Maelis regularly travels to:

- Mines
- Smelting complexes
- Rail yards
- Military bases
- Volcanic settlements
- Disaster sites

She prefers treating conditions where they occur rather than relying solely on hospital data.

This has repeatedly placed her in dangerous environments.

Maelis claims that this is necessary.

Other people claim she is stubborn.

These statements are compatible.

---

# Scientific Philosophy

Maelis believes science should remain connected to practical outcomes.

Research is valuable.

Treatment is better.

She has little interest in publishing impressive findings if they never improve anyone's life.

Her approach emphasizes:

1. Identify the hazard.
2. Understand the mechanism.
3. Reduce exposure.
4. Improve treatment.
5. Prevent recurrence.

She becomes deeply frustrated when organizations skip step three because prevention is expensive.

---

# Combat and Emergency Capability

Maelis is not a combat specialist.

She is, however, highly capable in emergency situations.

She carries:

- Medical sidearm
- Trauma kit
- Respiratory equipment
- Portable scanner
- Chemical detection tools
- Emergency injectors

When operating in active danger zones, she relies on military or security personnel for protection.

Maelis considers this efficient.

She has no interest in pretending to be a soldier.
