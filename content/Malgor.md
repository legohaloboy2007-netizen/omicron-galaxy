# Malgor

Malgor is a volcanic industrial world and one of the primary resource extraction planets of the [[Ashen Kingdom]]. Known throughout surrounding systems as:

The Furnace World

Malgor exists as the industrial backbone of the Ashen war machine, supplying enormous quantities of ore, refined metals, reactor materials, and volcanic alloys essential to the kingdom’s survival and expansion.

The planet is brutal, toxic, and unforgiving.

But within the Ashen Kingdom, endurance on Malgor is considered a mark of strength.

## Role Within the Ashen Kingdom

Malgor functions as:
- resource extraction world
- refining hub
- heavy industrial planet
- logistics center
- prison labor sector
- weapons production supplier

Much of the Ashen Kingdom’s:
- military industry
- forge infrastructure
- armored production
- heavy machinery
- industrial fuel systems

depend directly on resources harvested from Malgor.

## Environment

Malgor is a harsh volcanic world dominated by:
- toxic ash storms
- endless mining basins
- industrial wastelands
- molten fissures
- cracked volcanic plains
- heavy-metal dust storms
- seismic instability

The atmosphere is:
- toxic
- ash-filled
- heavily polluted
- dense with industrial emissions

The planet suffers near-constant environmental upheaval caused by:
- volcanic eruptions
- tectonic fractures
- industrial overexploitation
- chemical contamination

Life on Malgor is harsh even by Ashen standards.

## The Scarred Basins

Massive open-pit mining regions known as:

The Scarred Basins

stretch across entire continents.

These colossal excavation sites contain:
- rare ores
- reactor materials
- volcanic alloys
- geothermal deposits
- ancient mineral reserves

Entire industrial populations work within the basins continuously.

## The Iron Railways

The Iron Railways are enormous armored transport systems connecting Malgor’s industrial sectors.

These heavily defended rail networks transport:
- ore
- refined metals
- fuel
- industrial machinery
- military resources

across the dangerous volcanic wastelands.

The rail convoys are constant targets for:
- raiders
- smugglers
- escaped prisoners
- industrial sabotage groups

## The Smoke Cities

The Smoke Cities are vast industrial fortress-settlements built around:
- refineries
- smelters
- weapons foundries
- processing plants
- industrial reactors

The air within these cities is nearly unbreathable without filtration systems.

Production never stops.

Entire districts operate continuously under permanent red illumination from industrial fire and volcanic glow.

## The Ash Storms

One of Malgor’s greatest dangers is the planet’s massive ash storms.

These storms contain:
- volcanic ash
- toxic gas
- metal dust
- electrical instability
- burning debris

Entire convoys and settlements have vanished within major storm systems.

Even heavily armored outposts can be destroyed by severe ash cyclones.

## Labor Camps

Malgor contains countless prison labor camps supplying workers to the kingdom’s industrial sectors.

Condemned prisoners and debt-bound laborers are forced into:
- mining operations
- refinery maintenance
- hazardous excavation
- deep industrial work

Escape from the camps is considered nearly impossible.

Within the Ashen Kingdom, obedience is treated as currency.

## The Forgotten Depths

Deep beneath Malgor’s crust lie ancient cavern systems known as:

The Forgotten Depths

These subterranean regions contain:
- unstable tunnels
- abandoned mining sectors
- ancient buried structures
- geothermal caverns
- unexplored ruins

Very few who descend into the deepest sectors ever return.

Rumors persist that something older than the Ashen Kingdom itself lies hidden beneath the planet.

## Society & Culture

Malgor’s culture revolves around:
- labor
- endurance
- sacrifice
- production
- industrial survival

The people of Malgor are respected across the Ashen Kingdom for surviving conditions few others could endure.

One common miners’ creed states:

> “We do not fear the suffering.  
> We mine it.  
> We smelt it.  
> We weaponize it.”

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Malgor is known as:
- the Furnace World
- the industrial backbone of the [[Ashen Kingdom]]
- one of the harshest mining worlds in known space
- a planet consumed by industry and extraction

To outsiders, Malgor is a nightmare of endless labor and toxic fire.

To the Ashen Kingdom, it is indispensable.
