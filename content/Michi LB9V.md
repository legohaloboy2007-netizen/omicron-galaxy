# Michi LB9V

Michi LB9V is a heavily industrialized trade world located within the [[Prioxis System]], widely known throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]] as one of the largest weapons trading hubs in known space. The planet serves as a major center for arms manufacturing, military supply contracts, black market weapon distribution, and corporate defense production.

Unlike many heavily populated civilian worlds, Michi LB9V developed around industrial expansion and military commerce. Massive factory cities, orbital ship-loading platforms, refinery zones, and weapon foundries dominate much of the planet’s landscape. The skies are permanently crowded with cargo traffic, industrial smoke, and transport fleets moving military hardware across the galaxy.

The planet maintains a complex and often dangerous balance between legitimate corporate industry and large-scale black market activity. Governments, mercenary groups, corporations, syndicates, and military factions all operate within Michi LB9V’s economy, making the world one of the most politically volatile trade centers in the galaxy.

Due to the sheer volume of weapons moving through the planet, organizations such as the [[Black Vipers]], [[The Brotherhood]], and the [[Mirage Collective]] are known to maintain hidden operations throughout the world’s lower industrial sectors and underground trade districts.

Major corporations including [[Valkyrian Interstellar]] and independent military contractors also maintain manufacturing facilities and logistical operations on the planet.

Despite its economic importance, Michi LB9V suffers from heavy pollution, overcrowded industrial sectors, labor exploitation, and constant criminal conflict surrounding trade routes and weapons distribution.
