# Necromancer

## Overview

Real Name: Classified

Necromancer is the Admiral of the:
[[Fourth Fleet]]

within the:
[[Hell Hosts]]

and one of the most feared individuals in the Omicron Galaxy.

Little is known about his origins, and even less is known about the experiment that transformed him.

Official records regarding his past are heavily restricted, leading to decades of rumors, speculation, and conspiracy theories throughout both military and civilian circles.

Despite his unsettling reputation, Necromancer remains fiercely loyal to the Hell Hosts and serves directly under:
[[Predator]]

as one of his most trusted operatives.

To most soldiers, Necromancer is not viewed as a hero.

He is viewed as a warning.

---

# Appearance

Necromancer stands approximately:

6'3"

with a lean build and an unnaturally still posture.

His appearance is often described as:

- Pale
- Exhausted
- Gaunt
- Unsettling

Notable Features

- Pale skin
- Sharp jawline
- Hollow cheeks
- Long black hair
- One completely black damaged eye
- Constantly tired appearance

His face is rarely fully visible.

Most sightings involve:

- Shadows
- High collars
- Damaged visors
- Partial masks
- Poor lighting

As a result, very few individuals can confidently describe what he actually looks like.

Clothing & Armor

Necromancer's uniform consists of:

- Long black military coat
- Dark grey armor plating
- High reinforced collar
- Fourth Fleet insignia
- Black combat gloves

One of the most frequently discussed details about Necromancer is:

The Gloves

No verified record exists of Necromancer removing them.

No one knows why.

No one has ever asked twice.

---

# Personality

Necromancer is:

- Cold
- Quiet
- Focused
- Emotionally distant

Unlike many commanders, he rarely engages in casual conversation.

He does not appear hostile.

He simply appears disconnected.

Many soldiers describe interactions with him as:

«"Talking to someone who is present physically but somewhere else mentally."»

Despite his unsettling demeanor, Necromancer remains completely human.

At least, as far as anyone knows.

---

# The Experiment

The event responsible for creating Necromancer remains classified.

Even among high-ranking personnel, information is extremely limited.

Several theories exist.

Common Rumors

- Exposure to [[Element 95]]
- Experimental military augmentation
- Contact with an unknown anomaly
- Exposure to an undiscovered element
- A classified Fourth Fleet project

None of these theories have ever been confirmed.

The truth remains known only to a handful of individuals.

If any.

---

# Powers & Abilities

Glitching

Necromancer possesses an anomalous ability commonly referred to as:

Glitching

During combat, he appears capable of briefly shifting out of sync with reality.

Observers often describe the phenomenon as:

- Frame skipping
- Reality distortion
- Visual desynchronization

Rather than moving conventionally, Necromancer appears to simply:

Not be where he was a moment ago.

This allows him to avoid a significant number of incoming attacks.

Unnatural Movement

One of the most disturbing aspects of Necromancer's behavior occurs when he is standing still.

Witnesses frequently report:

- Complete lack of movement
- No visible adjustments in posture
- No fidgeting
- No unnecessary motion

Then suddenly:

He is somewhere else.

No footsteps.

No transition.

No warning.

---

# Combat Style

Necromancer fights with calculated efficiency.

His combat style revolves around:

- Precision
- Positioning
- Psychological pressure
- Exploiting confusion

Rather than overwhelming opponents with force, he forces them to question what they are seeing.

Many engagements involving Necromancer end before enemies fully understand what happened.

---

# History

Very little is known about Necromancer's life prior to joining the:
[[Hell Hosts]]

Most official records have been classified.

At some point in his early adulthood, he became involved in a military experiment that permanently altered him.

Following the incident, he eventually entered service within the Hell Hosts and rose through the ranks to become:

Admiral of Fourth Fleet

His effectiveness in combat and strategic operations quickly gained the attention of:
[[Predator]]

who began assigning him increasingly important missions.

Over time, Necromancer became one of Predator's most trusted field operatives.

---

# Fourth Fleet

Necromancer commands:

[[Fourth Fleet]]

One of the most feared fleets within the Hell Hosts military.

The fleet possesses a dark reputation among soldiers and civilians alike.

A common saying states:

«"If Fourth Fleet has arrived, something has already gone wrong."»

Whether deserved or not, the fleet's association with Necromancer has made it the subject of countless stories and rumors.

---

# Relationships

[[Predator]]

Predator views Necromancer as something of an apprentice.

The two share a level of trust rarely seen within Hell Host leadership.

Predator frequently assigns Necromancer highly sensitive operations and appears more informed about his condition than most.

The exact nature of their relationship remains unclear.

---

# [[Destiny]]

Destiny strongly dislikes Necromancer.

Part of this resentment stems from her belief that Predator often gives Necromancer more attention than his own daughter.

Whether this perception is true or not remains debatable.

Necromancer himself appears largely unaware of the tension.

---

# [[Red Wind]]

Red Wind finds Necromancer deeply unsettling.

While she acknowledges his value as a commander, she struggles to feel comfortable around him.

Like many others, she cannot fully explain why.

---

# Reputation

Within Hell Hosts

Necromancer is respected.

He is feared.

He is trusted.

These are not always the same thing.

Most soldiers avoid interacting with him unless necessary.

Not because he is cruel.

Because something about him feels wrong.

Outside Hell Hosts

Enemy factions often view Necromancer as:

- An anomaly
- A myth
- A psychological weapon

Numerous reports regarding his abilities are considered unreliable due to their contradictory nature.

---

# Rumors

Numerous stories surround Necromancer.

Among them:

- He has died multiple times.
- He does not blink.
- He cannot be photographed correctly.
- His black eye is looking somewhere else.
- Fourth Fleet soldiers return changed after serving under him.
- He knows the outcome of battles before they begin.

None have been verified.

---

# Psychological Theme

Necromancer embodies:

Terror

Not the terror of overwhelming force.

Not the terror of destruction.

The terror of uncertainty.

The feeling that something is wrong.

The feeling of seeing something you cannot explain.

The feeling that reality itself has briefly stopped making sense.
