# Nerevos

Nerevos is a mysterious oceanic world located within the [[Aegimius System]], known for its endless deep-blue oceans, ancient abandoned civilizations, and unexplained disappearances connected to the planet’s forgotten history. The world is considered one of the most enigmatic planets within the [[Omicron Galaxy]], attracting explorers, researchers, treasure hunters, and anomaly investigators seeking answers buried beneath its waters and ruined continents.

The planet is divided between four major continents:
- [[Kandar]]
- [[Leesar]]
- [[Novter]]
- [[Perona]]

Thousands of years ago, all four continents were home to advanced civilizations whose origins and cultures remain largely unknown. Massive ruined cities, abandoned harbors, collapsed monuments, and ancient structures still remain scattered across the continents, preserved by time and isolation.

At some point in the distant past, all four civilizations disappeared entirely.

No confirmed records explain what happened.

Despite countless expeditions and investigations, no evidence of war, planetary collapse, or natural extinction has ever been fully verified. Entire populations simply vanished, leaving their cities frozen in time.

The oceans surrounding the continents are exceptionally deep and poorly mapped, with strange energy readings, submerged ruins, and unexplained signals frequently detected beneath the surface. Several expeditions sent into the abyssal regions of Nerevos have disappeared without trace.

The planet later became heavily associated with [[Robert Anderson]], whose investigation into the abandoned civilizations and hidden anomaly activity eventually led to his Ascension Event. Although much of the incident remains classified or fragmented, the events on Nerevos permanently altered Anderson and connected him to forces beyond conventional understanding.

The true nature of Nerevos — and what happened to its ancient civilizations — remains unknown.
