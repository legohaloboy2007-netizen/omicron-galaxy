# Nexus

> "You don't see Nexus. You feel him when everything works."

## Overview

**Samuel Ardyn**, known by the callsign **Nexus**, is a Hell Host battlefield systems controller and Sergeant serving within the [[Doom Corps]].

Assigned to the [[305th Telemetry Recon Battalion]], commonly known as the **Long Eyes**, Nexus specializes in battlefield telemetry, drone coordination, tactical data integration, target tracking, and real-time communication between Hell Host units.

Samuel rarely operates as the most visible person on a battlefield.

That is precisely the point.

His role is to connect the battlefield together.

Reconnaissance feeds.

Drone surveillance.

Sniper observations.

Aircraft movements.

Infantry positions.

Enemy activity.

Command directives.

Nexus receives these individual streams and transforms them into information that soldiers can actually use.

Many Hell Host personnel have completed missions without ever knowing Samuel was involved.

They knew where the enemy was.

Their maps updated correctly.

Their support arrived on time.

Their extraction route remained open.

That was enough.

## Appearance

Samuel typically wears specialized Hell Host telemetry armour built around battlefield communication and data processing.

His equipment includes extensive electronic integration across the helmet, forearms, and control rig.

Unlike conventional Doom Corps armour, Nexus's equipment prioritizes:

- Communications
- Sensor integration
- Data processing
- Drone control
- Electronic protection
- Tactical networking

His helmet contains multiple visual and communications systems, allowing Samuel to process information from several sources without relying entirely upon external displays.

During major operations, Nexus is often surrounded by tactical monitors, drone feeds, holographic maps, and command-network interfaces.

---

## Personality

Samuel is analytical, methodical, calm, and highly focused.

He is comfortable processing large quantities of information under extreme pressure and rarely allows rapidly changing battlefield conditions to overwhelm him.

Nexus prefers precision over improvisation.

He dislikes vague reports.

"Enemies nearby" is not useful.

"Seven hostiles, east service corridor, moving north" is useful.

Samuel has developed a reputation for asking extremely specific questions during operations.

This occasionally irritates soldiers in immediate danger.

Those soldiers usually appreciate the resulting artillery coordinates shortly afterward.

Off duty, Samuel is considerably less intense.

He possesses a dry sense of humour and is surprisingly patient with younger personnel.

His professional standards remain high.

His tolerance for badly configured equipment does not.

Nexus serves within the **305th Telemetry Recon Battalion**, commonly known as the **Long Eyes**.

The unit specializes in gathering, processing, and distributing battlefield intelligence.

Its responsibilities include:

- Drone surveillance
- Tactical telemetry
- Battlefield mapping
- Electronic observation
- Target tracking
- Communications relay
- Reconnaissance integration
- Movement analysis
- Unit networking

The Long Eyes rarely receive the same attention as frontline assault formations.

Their information can nevertheless determine whether those formations succeed.

# Battlefield Systems Controller

Samuel's role as a **Battlefield Systems Controller** places him between reconnaissance assets, command personnel, and frontline units.

During large operations, Nexus may simultaneously monitor:

- Friendly infantry positions
- Enemy movement
- Drone feeds
- Aircraft
- Sniper observations
- Vehicle formations
- Communication traffic
- Sensor networks
- Orbital reconnaissance
- Extraction corridors

His job is not simply collecting information.

It is deciding **what matters right now**.

Too much information can be as dangerous as too little.

Nexus filters battlefield data and ensures relevant information reaches the personnel who need it before circumstances change.

---

# Drone Swarm Coordination

One of Nexus's primary responsibilities is coordinating multiple reconnaissance drones simultaneously.

His drone networks can provide:

- Aerial surveillance
- Target identification
- Thermal imaging
- Terrain mapping
- Movement tracking
- Communication relays
- Route reconnaissance

Samuel may operate several drones directly while allowing automated systems to handle others.

He constantly changes drone positioning to reduce predictable coverage patterns and minimize losses.

Destroying one drone rarely blinds Nexus.

It simply creates a gap he needs to fill.

---

# Real-Time Battlefield Mapping

Nexus maintains constantly updated tactical maps during active operations.

These maps may include:

- Friendly forces
- Confirmed hostile positions
- Probable enemy movement
- Structural damage
- Collapsed routes
- Civilian locations
- Known hazards
- Landing zones
- Extraction corridors

Information can change within seconds.

Samuel's responsibility is ensuring the map changes with it.

He considers outdated intelligence more dangerous than missing intelligence because personnel may trust it without realizing it is wrong.

---

# Enemy Movement Prediction

Nexus is highly skilled at recognizing movement patterns.

He uses available information to estimate where enemy forces are likely to move next.

This involves analyzing:

- Current position
- Terrain
- Available cover
- Known objectives
- Supply routes
- Previous behaviour
- Reinforcement possibilities
- Friendly pressure

These predictions are probabilistic.

Samuel cannot see the future.

He can simply make highly informed estimates.

He is careful to distinguish between:

**Confirmed.**

**Probable.**

And:

**Guessing.**

# Squad HUD Integration

Nexus can distribute tactical information directly into compatible Hell Host helmet and HUD systems.

This allows soldiers to receive:

- Target markers
- Route updates
- Hazard warnings
- Friendly positions
- Drone observations
- Extraction directions

Samuel deliberately avoids overwhelming personnel with unnecessary information.

A soldier under fire does not need twelve pages of reconnaissance data.

They need to know:

**Three enemies are entering through the door behind them.**

# Target Designation

Nexus regularly designates targets for:

- Infantry
- Snipers
- Aircraft
- Artillery
- Drones
- Armoured units

He prioritizes threats based upon their immediate influence on the battlefield.

A seemingly minor communications operator may receive higher priority than a heavily armed infantry soldier if removing them disrupts an entire enemy network.

This makes Nexus particularly effective at identifying vulnerabilities that frontline personnel cannot immediately see.

# Communication Relay

Battlefield communication is rarely clean.

Buildings interfere with signals.

Enemy forces jam frequencies.

Equipment is destroyed.

Units move outside expected coverage.

Nexus can use drones and relay systems to maintain communication between separated Hell Host formations.

In some operations, the 305th effectively becomes the nervous system connecting otherwise isolated forces.

Vantage provides:

Nexus takes that information and integrates it into the wider tactical network.

Victor may see something from several kilometres away.

Their roles complement one another extremely well.

Vantage sees.

Nexus connects.

Nexus acts as an important link between strategic command and tactical execution.

# Relationship with Frontline Units

# Indirect Threat

Nexus carries a **High (Indirect)** threat classification.

This reflects the unusual nature of his battlefield impact.

Samuel himself is not among the Hell Hosts' most dangerous individual combatants.

Eliminating him in a direct duel would be considerably easier than confronting many elite Doom Corps soldiers.

Reaching him is the problem.

And while an enemy attempts to do so, Nexus may be:

- Tracking their movement
- Informing nearby units
- Redirecting patrols
- Deploying drones
- Marking targets
- Coordinating aerial support

Fighting Nexus means fighting the network around him.

---

# Information Discipline

Samuel understands that information can become dangerous when distributed carelessly.

He follows strict rules regarding:

- Classified intelligence
- Civilian information
- Friendly positions
- Command communications
- Reconnaissance sources

Not every soldier needs access to everything Nexus knows.

Compartmentalization prevents both confusion and enemy exploitation.

# System Failure

Nexus trains extensively for situations where technology fails.

He maintains contingency procedures for:

- Drone loss
- Communications blackout
- Sensor failure
- Electronic warfare
- Network compromise
- Power loss

Samuel strongly rejects the assumption that advanced technology removes the need for conventional military skills.

Networks fail.

Maps become outdated.

People still need to think.
