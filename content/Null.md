# Null

> "The armor doesn't protect him. It protects everything else from him."

## Overview

**Kael Virek**, known by the callsign **Null**, is a Hell Host experimental combat operative assigned to the [[999th Experimental Weapons Battalion]], commonly referred to as **Shoved Under the Rug**.

A member of [[House Virek]], Kael became the subject of a classified weapons program known as **Project Null**, which involved bonding a synthetic adaptive combat substance directly to his body.

The experiment succeeded.

Technically.

The substance dramatically enhanced Kael's physical capabilities and granted him access to adaptive armour formation, accelerated movement, partial regeneration, and limited form distortion.

It also left him unstable.

Null is simultaneously a soldier, experimental platform, and containment risk.

He remains active only because his effectiveness in extreme operations is considered too valuable to discard.

## Appearance

Kael's natural appearance is difficult to observe while deployed.

The synthetic adaptive substance bonded to his body frequently spreads across the outer surface of his armour and equipment, forming black, fluid-like structures between conventional armour plating.

His containment armour is predominantly white, black, and dark grey, though much of the original surface has become stained or partially covered by the adaptive material.

The substance can visibly move across:

- Armour seams
- Arms
- Torso
- Weapons
- Damaged equipment

During periods of instability, the material may extend beyond its normal containment boundaries.

Kael's face is almost always concealed during deployment.

This is partly tactical.

It is also procedural.

# Personality

Null is quiet, withdrawn, emotionally conflicted, and highly focused during operations.

He avoids forming unnecessary connections with other personnel.

This behaviour developed significantly after Project Null.

Kael is not emotionless.

If anything, the opposite is true.

He has simply become increasingly uncomfortable with allowing people to become close to him while his condition remains unstable.

He understands what he is capable of.

More importantly, he understands what could happen if control fails.

Kael follows orders reliably and rarely complains about dangerous deployments.

This is not because he considers himself expendable.

He has simply become accustomed to being treated as though he might be.

One question appears repeatedly throughout his psychological evaluations:

**Does Kael still consider himself a soldier, or has he begun believing he is only the weapon?**

Kael has not answered.

Within House Virek, his condition is not treated as a source of prestige.

It is treated as something that happened to one of their own.

# Project Null

**Project Null** was a classified Hell Host experimental weapons program investigating the military potential of a synthetic adaptive combat substance.

The material was designed to bond with a biological subject and respond dynamically to combat conditions.

Kael became one of its primary test subjects.

The bonding process was permanent.

Once fully integrated, the substance began interfacing directly with his nervous system, armour, and biological processes.

Its capabilities exceeded expectations.

So did the risks.

Project Null produced a soldier capable of adapting physically to combat conditions at extraordinary speed.

It also created a system whose full behaviour remains difficult to predict.

---

# The Adaptive Substance

The substance bonded to Kael behaves somewhere between:

- Synthetic armour
- Programmable material
- Reactive biological coating
- Autonomous combat system

It responds both to Kael's conscious intent and involuntary neurological signals.

This distinction is extremely important.

Fear.

Pain.

Stress.

Adrenaline.

Anger.

All can influence the substance.

Under normal conditions, Kael maintains control.

During severe trauma or emotional overload, the material may begin responding faster than conscious decision-making allows.

This is the primary reason he requires containment protocols.

---

# Adaptive Armour Formation

Null can direct the synthetic material across his body to reinforce vulnerable areas.

The substance can temporarily form:

- Additional armour plating
- Shields
- Reinforced limbs
- Blunt defensive structures
- Basic tools
- Weapon reinforcement

These formations are temporary.

The more material concentrated into one area, the less remains available elsewhere.

Kael cannot simply cover himself in unlimited armour.

Every adaptation is a trade-off.

---

# Weapon Adaptation

The adaptive substance can spread across compatible weapons.

This allows Kael to:

- Reinforce damaged components
- Alter grips and stabilization
- Create temporary weapon supports
- Improve armour integration
- Modify weapon handling

The substance does not magically transform one firearm into another.

It adapts existing equipment.

This makes Kael exceptionally effective with weapons specifically designed to interface with Project Null systems.

---

# Enhanced Speed

Null can use the adaptive system to temporarily reinforce muscles and joints, allowing extreme bursts of movement.

During these bursts, Kael becomes substantially faster than normal human personnel.

The effect is short-lived.

Extended use dramatically increases strain on both his body and the synthetic substance.

Repeated overuse may cause:

- Muscular tearing
- Nervous-system overload
- Loss of balance
- Substance instability
- Temporary system shutdown

Null therefore uses enhanced speed primarily for sudden repositioning rather than sustained movement.

---

# Partial Regeneration

The substance can assist with physical recovery.

It can:

- Seal wounds
- Reduce blood loss
- Stabilize fractures
- Support damaged tissue
- Accelerate some healing processes

The effect is not instantaneous.

Severe trauma can overwhelm it.

Null can still die.

The regeneration system is designed primarily to keep him functional long enough to survive or reach medical support.

It is not immortality.

---

# Form Distortion

Kael can temporarily alter his physical outline by redistributing the adaptive substance across his armour and body.

This allows limited changes to:

- Silhouette
- Thermal signature
- Apparent body shape
- Armour profile

This does not amount to full shapeshifting.

Null cannot perfectly imitate another individual.

The ability is primarily useful for:

- Concealment
- Breaking visual identification
- Confusing thermal optics
- Short-duration infiltration

---

# Instability

The greatest problem with Project Null is control.

The substance does not always respond predictably.

Variability may be triggered by:

- Extreme stress
- Severe injury
- Electrical interference
- High anomalous exposure
- Emotional instability
- System damage

When unstable, the substance may begin forming structures without deliberate input.

Containment teams refer to these periods as **override events**.

During an override event, Kael may remain conscious but lose partial control over the material surrounding him.

This is considered a critical emergency.

---

# Containment Exo-Suit Mk-IV

Null wears a specialized containment exo-suit designed specifically around Project Null.

The suit is not primarily intended to protect Kael from enemy fire.

Its most important function is limiting the adaptive substance.

The Mk-IV contains:

- Reinforced physical barriers
- Substance isolation channels
- Emergency suppressant systems
- Neural monitoring
- Remote shutdown systems
- Armour segmentation

The suit allows controlled portions of the material to deploy while attempting to prevent uncontrolled spread.

This led to the saying:

> **"The armor doesn't protect him. It protects everything else from him."**

# 999th Experimental Weapons Battalion

Null serves within the **999th Experimental Weapons Battalion**.

The unit is informally known as:

**Shoved Under the Rug.**

The nickname reflects its role within the Hell Hosts.

The 999th handles:

- Unstable experimental weapons
- Classified battlefield prototypes
- Dangerous augmentation programs
- Containment-dependent personnel
- Technologies unsuitable for conventional deployment

Personnel assigned to the 999th are frequently involved in systems that senior command would prefer the wider military never needs to understand.

Project Null is one of them.

---

# Deployment Protocol

Null is not deployed casually.

Recommended missions include:

- Critical-threat operations
- Extreme casualty environments
- Experimental weapons testing
- Enemy positions considered unsuitable for conventional forces
- Situations where adaptive capabilities provide exceptional advantage

Typical deployment procedure follows:

1. Deployment authorization
2. System integrity check
3. Combat engagement
4. Continuous monitoring
5. Post-mission containment
6. Medical and neurological evaluation

Containment procedures are reviewed after every deployment.

Null dislikes this process.

He understands why it exists.

---

# Termination Protocol

A classified termination protocol exists for Project Null.

The protocol may be authorized if:

- Kael permanently loses control
- The adaptive substance overrides containment
- The material begins uncontrolled replication or spread
- Recovery becomes impossible

The existence of the protocol is not hidden from Kael.

He knows.

This has significantly affected his relationship with the program.

The people deploying him are also the people authorized to kill him if the experiment fails.

---

# Combat Style

Null fights aggressively and adaptively.

Unlike conventional personnel who enter combat with a fixed loadout and doctrine, Kael constantly changes how his equipment and body respond.

His engagements may shift rapidly between:

- Rifle combat
- Close-range assault
- Defensive armour formation
- Rapid movement
- Improvised shielding
- Weapon reinforcement

This makes him highly unpredictable.

It also makes prolonged combat dangerous.

The longer Kael pushes the substance, the greater the chance of instability.

# Relationship with Haze

Null and [[Haze|Nyra Voss]] have a personal history predating or overlapping his current condition.

The two were previously involved romantically.

Their relationship ended badly.

The exact details remain private.

What is known is that Haze represented control and discipline during a period when Kael increasingly became defined by instability.

Their personalities clashed heavily.

Nyra plans.

Kael reacts.

Nyra controls environments.

Null increasingly struggled to control himself.

The breakup was not clean.

They can still operate together when required.

Neither particularly enjoys it.

---

# Relationship with Hammerfall

[[Hammerfall|Kassandra Virek]] treats Kael as family first and an experimental asset second.

This occasionally places her in direct disagreement with personnel responsible for Project Null.

Kassandra understands that containment may become necessary.

She also refuses to allow Kael to be treated as disposable equipment.

Her position is straightforward:

She will protect him if possible.

If he permanently loses control, she will also be one of the people willing to stop him.

Kael understands this.

Strangely, he trusts her more because of it.

---

# Relationship with Dreadnought

[[Dreadnought|Adrian Virek]] views Null primarily through operational terms.

Project Null is an extraordinary battlefield asset.

It is also a strategic liability.

Adrian has little patience for pretending otherwise.

He supports Kael's continued deployment while control remains acceptable.

Emotional considerations do not heavily influence his assessment.

This can make their interactions tense.

Kael believes Adrian sees him as a weapon.

Adrian would argue that during a military operation, that distinction is irrelevant.

# Operational Record

Null's success rate is considered unusually high.

His missions frequently involve scenarios where conventional forces are expected to suffer severe casualties.

Project Null's adaptive capabilities allow Kael to survive circumstances that would disable ordinary soldiers.

However, success does not necessarily mean the deployment was safe.

Several completed missions have resulted in:

- Major suit damage
- Medical stabilization
- Extended containment
- Substance instability
- Revised deployment protocols

Project Null command therefore evaluates operations through two separate measures:

**Mission success.**

And:

**Subject stability.**

The first remains excellent.

The second remains concerning.

## Physical Enhancement

Project Null grants significant improvements to strength, speed, and survivability.

## Regeneration

The bonded substance can stabilize injuries and accelerate recovery.

## Versatility

---

# Limitations

## Instability

The adaptive substance may become increasingly difficult to control under severe stress.

## System Strain

Enhanced movement and regeneration consume significant physical resources.

## Containment Dependence

Damage to the Mk-IV suit can dramatically increase operational risk.

## Psychological Pressure

Kael's awareness of his own termination protocol and unstable status affects his mental health.

## Friendly-Fire Risk

During severe override events, the system may no longer distinguish tactical necessity as carefully as Kael would.

# Reputation

Null occupies an uncomfortable place within the Hell Hosts.

To some soldiers, he is a highly effective experimental operative.

To others, he is evidence that Project Null should never have happened.

To the 999th, he is simultaneously their greatest success and their most dangerous responsibility.

Kael's own interpretation is far simpler.

He is still alive.

He is still serving.

For now, that is enough.
