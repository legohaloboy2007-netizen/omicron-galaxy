# Nullstone

## Overview

Nullstone is a rare Void-associated mineral found primarily within the deeper regions of the [[Voidlands]]. The material is infamous for its ability to weaken or disrupt conventional physical laws, making it one of the most dangerous naturally occurring substances ever discovered.

Unlike most minerals, Nullstone appears partially disconnected from normal reality.

The substance is heavily associated with:
- dimensional instability
- Void distortion
- reality suppression
- and spatial interference

Because of its extreme anomalous properties, Nullstone is utilized only by civilizations capable of surviving prolonged exposure to unstable environments, particularly:
- [[Reality Weavers]]
- [[Dominion Keepers]]
- and certain [[The Anchorless Caste|Anchorless]] factions

Most conventional civilizations prohibit all handling of the material.

## Nature

Nullstone is a dense geometric mineral that appears to absorb:
- energy
- light
- sound
- and occasionally spatial coherence itself

The material demonstrates highly abnormal interactions with surrounding reality.

Known effects include:
- weakened gravitational consistency
- localized physics disruption
- energy suppression
- dimensional instability
- and temporary breakdown of conventional spatial laws

Researchers studying Nullstone often describe nearby environments as:
“less real.”

## Physical Appearance

Nullstone commonly appears as:
- matte black crystalline formations
- angular geometric ore
- or perfectly smooth dark mineral masses

The surface absorbs most visible light, making the material appear unnaturally flat or depthless.

Under certain conditions, observers report:
- shifting geometry
- distorted reflections
- impossible angles
- and movement occurring within the stone itself

Some samples appear different depending on the observer.

## Discovery

Ancient records suggest Nullstone was first encountered during early expeditions into:
the [[Voidlands]]

Exploration teams reported regions where:
- gravity weakened
- navigation failed
- distances changed unpredictably
- and sections of reality appeared incomplete

Large Nullstone deposits were eventually discovered near:
- deep Void fractures
- collapsed dimensional zones
- and unstable anomaly regions

Many early expeditions disappeared entirely.

## Properties

Nullstone possesses several highly anomalous characteristics.

### Reality Suppression
The material weakens local physical consistency, reducing the stability of surrounding space.

---

### Energy Absorption
Certain forms of energy rapidly dissipate near concentrated Nullstone deposits.

---

### Dimensional Distortion
Extended exposure may destabilize dimensional boundaries and interfere with portal systems.

---

### Perceptual Interference
Observers frequently experience:
- disorientation
- altered depth perception
- hallucinations
- and inconsistent spatial awareness

---

### Void Resonance
The material reacts strongly to:
- Void anomalies
- dimensional instability
- and high concentrations of anomalous energy

## Applications

Despite the danger, Nullstone has limited use in advanced Void technologies.

Known applications include:
- dimensional dampening systems
- anomaly containment structures
- anti-energy shielding
- stealth architecture
- and reality suppression fields

Some [[Reality Weavers]] utilize refined Nullstone within:
- geometric stabilizers
- spatial restructuring devices
- and recursive architecture systems

The [[Dominion Keepers]] occasionally incorporate the material into:
- fortress defenses
- dimensional anchor systems
- and anti-anomaly containment sectors

## Risks

Nullstone is considered highly dangerous due to prolonged exposure effects.

Known side effects include:
- cognitive instability
- perception loss
- spatial confusion
- memory degradation
- emotional detachment
- and partial dissociation from reality

In severe cases, individuals exposed to concentrated Nullstone deposits report:
- forgetting physical locations
- perceiving impossible geometry
- or temporarily losing awareness of their own existence

Some victims disappear entirely under unexplained circumstances.

## Mining & Extraction

Mining Nullstone is extraordinarily hazardous.

Deposits are often located within:
- unstable Void zones
- recursive environments
- collapsed dimensional sectors
- and regions affected by Void Laws

Extraction crews commonly experience:
- navigation failure
- communication blackout
- hallucinations
- and temporal inconsistency

Many mining operations are abandoned shortly after discovery.

## Relationship to the Voidlands

Nullstone is considered one of the purest naturally occurring materials associated with:
the [[Voidlands]]

Some theorists believe the substance forms where:
normal reality begins losing structural integrity.

Others argue the material may originate from:
outside conventional existence entirely.

No theory has been confirmed.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Nullstone is known as:
- the mineral of weakened reality
- the black stone of the Void
- one of the most unstable materials ever discovered
- and a substance capable of disrupting physics itself

Among Voidlands explorers, one warning is frequently repeated:

> “If the walls stop feeling real,  
> you’ve already found Nullstone.”
