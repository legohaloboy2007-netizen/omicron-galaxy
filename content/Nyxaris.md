# Nyxaris

## The Hallucination Sphere

Nyxaris is a corrupted research world located deep within the [[Farlands]], infamous for its severe psychological effects and unstable atmospheric anomalies caused by prolonged [[Element 95]] exposure.

Once a major scientific and neurological research colony, the planet is now consumed by hallucination-inducing neurotoxins saturating the atmosphere itself.

Very few who enter Nyxaris leave with their sanity intact.

The planet has become known across the galaxy as:

The Hallucination Sphere

## Collapse of the Research Colonies

Before its corruption, Nyxaris served as an advanced research and experimentation world focused on:
- neural sciences
- perception studies
- consciousness mapping
- Element 95 neurological interaction
- cognitive enhancement technologies

Following multiple large-scale Element 95 incidents, the atmosphere became permanently contaminated by airborne neurotoxic compounds linked directly to the corruption.

Entire research sectors collapsed into chaos.

Personnel began reporting:
- shared hallucinations
- time distortion
- impossible sightings
- memory fragmentation
- voices originating from nowhere

Within weeks, communication with the planet failed almost entirely.

## Environment

Nyxaris is covered by:
- abandoned laboratory complexes
- warped forests
- spectral mirage zones
- decaying research cities
- corrupted atmospheric regions
- shifting visual anomalies

The atmosphere itself is highly dangerous.

Exposure commonly causes:
- hallucinations
- paranoia
- time perception distortion
- false memories
- sensory confusion
- auditory phenomena
- identity instability

Many explorers report experiencing:
- conversations that never occurred
- people appearing in impossible locations
- events repeating incorrectly
- distorted versions of reality
- visions of dead individuals

No two expedition reports fully align.

## The Mirage Zones

Large regions of Nyxaris are classified as:

Spectral Mirage Zones

Within these sectors, reality becomes visually inconsistent.

Travelers report:
- landscapes changing while observed
- structures appearing and disappearing
- duplicated personnel
- impossible weather transitions
- distorted shadows
- pathways leading back to themselves

Some scientists believe the corruption on Nyxaris affects:
not only perception—
but localized reality itself.

## Corrupted Scientists

Many of the original researchers stationed on Nyxaris survived the collapse physically.

Mentally, however, most became severely altered by prolonged neurotoxin exposure and Element 95 contamination.

Corrupted scientists encountered across the planet display:
- unstable behavior
- fragmented speech
- violent paranoia
- obsession with perception and consciousness
- severe bodily corruption

Some continue attempting research centuries after the planet’s fall.

## Illusion-Walking Predators

Nyxaris is inhabited by predatory entities capable of exploiting hallucinations and sensory distortion.

These creatures are commonly referred to as:

Illusion-Walkers

Reported abilities include:
- mimicking voices
- disguising themselves as humans
- appearing within hallucinations
- distorting visual perception
- stalking targets through mirage zones

Very little verified biological data exists regarding the species.

## The Echo Array

One of the most infamous structures on Nyxaris is:

The Echo Array

A partially functioning orbital communication and research station still broadcasting distorted transmissions across surrounding systems.

The station continuously emits:
- fragmented distress calls
- corrupted research logs
- distorted voices
- impossible coordinates
- repeated warnings
- unidentified signals

Some broadcasts appear current.

Others originate from individuals confirmed dead centuries ago.

The Array occasionally transmits:
messages seemingly directed personally at nearby listeners.

## Temporal Distortion

Time perception on Nyxaris is considered highly unreliable.

Expeditions frequently report:
- missing hours or days
- repeated conversations
- inconsistent timelines
- memories of events that never occurred
- watches and clocks malfunctioning
- conflicting mission records

Some survivors claim they spent only minutes on the planet despite years passing externally.

Others report the opposite.

## Scientific Importance

Despite the extreme danger, Nyxaris remains a high-interest target for:
- [[Obsidian Veil]]
- black-market researchers
- rogue scientists
- consciousness cults
- experimental military divisions

The planet is believed to contain:
- lost neurological research
- advanced perception technology
- classified Element 95 archives
- early experiments tied to consciousness-linked Ascension

Most retrieval operations fail.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Nyxaris is known as:
- the Hallucination Sphere
- one of the most psychologically dangerous worlds in existence
- a planet where perception itself cannot be trusted
- a graveyard of forbidden research

Among veteran Farlands explorers, one warning is repeated constantly:

> “If Nyxaris shows you something familiar—run.”
