# Omicron Galaxy

The **Omicron Galaxy** is a large spiral galaxy surrounding the supermassive black hole known as [[Graveyard V19]]. It serves as the primary setting of the Hell Host Universe and contains thousands of inhabited systems, fractured empires, hostile anomalies, ancient civilizations, and largely unexplored regions.
