# Parallax Theta

## The Training World of the Weavers

Parallax Theta is a hostile training world controlled by the [[Reality Weavers]] deep within the [[Voidlands]]. Designed not for habitation but for transformation, the planet functions as a massive reality-engineering proving ground where Weavers learn to manipulate unstable geometry, dimensional structure, and Void Laws themselves.

To outsiders, Parallax Theta appears less like a planet—

And more like a living paradox.

The world constantly shifts between configurations, reshaping itself through recursive spatial patterns and impossible environmental changes.

## Purpose

Parallax Theta serves as the primary training world of the Reality Weavers.

Here, initiates and advanced Weavers are trained in:
- offensive geometry warping
- defensive spatial manipulation
- dimensional restructuring
- Void Law adaptation
- perception-based combat
- reality destabilization techniques

Failure on Parallax Theta is often fatal.

The Reality Weavers consider survival within its environments proof that an individual can withstand the instability of higher Void existence.

## Environment

The surface of Parallax Theta is composed almost entirely of:
- impossible obstacle structures
- recursive architecture
- reality mazes
- fractal storms
- shifting gravity zones
- collapsing dimensional pathways
- unstable geometric landscapes

The world rearranges itself continuously.

Entire regions may:
- invert
- duplicate
- disappear
- overlap
- or fold into impossible configurations

No stable global map of the planet exists.

## Reality Mazes

One of the most infamous features of Parallax Theta is its enormous:

Reality Mazes

These structures are designed specifically to:
- disorient perception
- destabilize spatial awareness
- force adaptive thinking
- test dimensional manipulation skills

Within the mazes:
- corridors loop infinitely
- distances become inconsistent
- exits relocate
- rooms intersect impossibly
- and geometry changes while observed

Many initiates are permanently lost within the deeper sectors.

## Living Fractal Storms

Massive atmospheric anomalies known as:

Living Fractal Storms

roam across the planet.

These storms appear partially sentient and consist of:
- geometric lightning
- recursive energy patterns
- spatial distortion fields
- fragmented matter
- collapsing dimensional waves

Exposure to a fractal storm may result in:
- temporary duplication
- partial erasure
- dimensional displacement
- or severe perceptual damage

Some storms actively pursue movement.

## Clipping Zones

One of the planet’s greatest dangers are areas known as:

Clipping Zones

Within these unstable regions:
pieces of reality simply cease to exist.

Affected zones may suddenly:
- lose gravity
- lose physical matter
- lose sound
- lose light
- or erase portions of space entirely

Individuals entering a Clipping Zone may:
- disappear instantly
- become partially phased
- fall through spatial layers
- or cease existing from certain perspectives

The phenomenon remains poorly understood even by the Weavers themselves.

## The Spiral Arenas

The Spiral Arenas are massive combat-training sectors where Weavers engage in:
- geometry-based warfare
- dimensional dueling
- spatial manipulation exercises
- tactical perception combat

Battles within the arenas often involve:
- collapsing terrain
- altered gravity
- weaponized geometry
- recursive attack structures
- localized reality fractures

Traditional combat doctrine becomes nearly meaningless within these environments.

## Inhabitants

Parallax Theta is populated primarily by:
- Weaver initiates
- combat instructors
- Pattern Scribes
- Null Architects
- dimensional overseers

The planet contains few permanent settlements, as most structures continuously relocate or restructure themselves.

## Psychological Effects

Extended exposure to Parallax Theta commonly causes:
- geometric hallucinations
- directional confusion
- recursive thought patterns
- altered spatial perception
- dissociation
- inability to process normal architecture

Some survivors permanently lose the ability to perceive straight lines correctly after leaving the world.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Parallax Theta is known as:
- the Training World of the Reality Weavers
- one of the most spatially unstable worlds in existence
- a planet where geometry itself becomes hostile
- a proving ground for Void-adapted minds

Among the Reality Weavers, one phrase is taught to every initiate:

> “Reality is not broken.  
> You were simply taught the wrong shape.”
