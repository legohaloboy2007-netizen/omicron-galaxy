# Era 3 — Power Unbound

**Power Unbound** marked a major turning point in the history of the [[Omicron Galaxy]]. While conventional military conflict remained a major force throughout the era, the search for anomalous power, ancient technology, and forbidden knowledge became increasingly important to the factions and individuals shaping the galaxy.

Following the [[Act 8 — Hell Hosts-Free World War|Hell Host–Free World War]] and the disappearance of [[Onyx]], [[Ultimatum]] assumed temporary leadership of the [[Free World Order]]. The growing threat of the [[Angel Swords]] eventually pushed the Free World Order and [[Hell Hosts]] into an unlikely temporary alliance despite their recent war.

At the same time, the Hell Hosts expanded their presence within the [[Prioxis System]], bringing them into greater contact with civilian populations, corporations, criminal organizations, and the [[Protection and Crime Enforcement]].

Within the [[Aegimius System]], PCE detective [[Robert Anderson]] discovered an ancient [[The Elysarian Dominion|Elysarian]] Ascension Chamber while investigating the disappearance of [[Mick Holder]]. Anderson survived its activation and became one of the first major modern examples of an [[Ascension Events|Ascension Event]].

The era also saw the return of [[Destiny]] after nine months of captivity by the [[Obsidian Veil]]. Shortly afterwards, [[Blood]] disappeared during an operation within the [[Ashen Kingdom]] and underwent a catastrophic Ascension, eventually becoming the [[Blood|Ashen Lord]].

Tensions surrounding [[Element 95]] and the search for increasingly powerful weapons eventually escalated into the [[Act 16 — Hell Hosts vs Angel Swords|Hell Host–Angel Sword War]]. The Hell Hosts and Free World Order fought together against the Angel Swords in a massive conflict spanning numerous systems, with major battles taking place around [[Joshua]] and [[Genesis]]. The war ended with the Angel Swords overwhelmed and their surviving forces scattering throughout the galaxy.

Following the war, personnel from the Hell Hosts and Free World Order gathered together at a remote cabin to celebrate their victory, providing a rare moment of peace between soldiers who had once fought against one another.

The era concluded with the Ascension of [[Calypso|Ellie Parsons]], a relatively new Hell Host soldier who discovered the ancient [[Chamber of Ogygia]] during a reconnaissance mission within the [[Ice Kingdom]]. The chamber transformed Ellie into [[Calypso]], the Ice Queen, awakening a kingdom that had remained dormant for centuries.

By the end of Power Unbound, the balance of power within the Omicron Galaxy had begun to change.

Ancient technologies were awakening.

Ascension had returned.

And ordinary people were beginning to become something far greater.
