# Predator

## Overview

**Real Name:** William Grant

Predator is the Supreme Commander of the:
[[Hell Hosts]]

and one of the most feared military leaders in the Omicron Galaxy.

Known for personally leading frontline operations rather than commanding safely from behind the lines, Predator built the Hell Hosts into one of the most powerful military factions in known space through relentless warfare, strategic brilliance, and uncompromising leadership.

To his enemies, he is:
- a warlord
- a nightmare
- and a symbol of inevitable conflict.

To his soldiers, he is:
- respected
- feared
- and trusted.

Despite the brutality of his methods, Predator genuinely believes the galaxy is collapsing into chaos, and that the Hell Hosts are one of the few forces capable of restoring order.

He does not seek conquest for power alone.

He seeks survival.

## Appearance

Predator stands approximately:
6’1"

and possesses a strong, battle-worn build shaped by decades of warfare.

His true appearance is largely unknown outside of:
Hell Host High Command.

Most soldiers only ever see him wearing his iconic:
black-and-red command armor.

### Notable Features
- Scar across his right eye
- Scar running along the right side of his neck
- Brown eyes
- Deep raspy voice

His voice is often described as:
calm,
cold,
and commanding.

### Armor

Predator’s armor is among the most recognizable within the:
[[Hell Hosts]]

The suit features:
- black tactical plating
- red illuminated systems
- reinforced combat armor
- and a long black command cape lined with deep crimson.

The helmet itself became infamous throughout the galaxy due to its:
- emotionless design
- glowing red optics
- and intimidating silhouette.

Many enemy soldiers recognize the helmet before they recognize the battlefield itself.

## Personality

Predator is:
- cold
- disciplined
- tactical
- and emotionally restrained.

However, beneath the armor and reputation exists an exhausted man carrying immense psychological burden.

Unlike many military leaders, Predator leads from the front lines personally, believing respect must be earned through action rather than rank alone.

This leadership style earned him deep loyalty among veteran Hell Host soldiers.

New recruits often fear him.
Veterans respect him.

Despite his ruthless methods, Predator does not view himself as evil.

He understands many of his actions are morally wrong, but believes the long-term survival of civilization outweighs temporary morality.

He does not attempt to justify every action to himself.

He simply believes:
it will be worth it in the end.

### Flaws
- Short temper
- Emotionally repressed
- Can act impulsively under emotional stress
- Struggles to separate personal attachment from command decisions
- Carries severe guilt beneath his composed exterior

## Leadership Style

Predator is famous for:
leading by example.

He frequently participates directly in:
- military operations
- assaults
- rescue missions
- and planetary warfare.

Unlike many commanders, Predator willingly places himself in danger beside his soldiers.

This created a culture within the Hell Hosts where:
respect is earned through action.

Rooms often fall silent when he enters.
Not always from fear—
but from respect.

## Combat Style

Predator primarily fights using:
strategy,
precision,
and overwhelming battlefield control.

However, in close combat he becomes:
extremely aggressive and highly dangerous.

He demonstrates exceptional proficiency with:
- knives
- pistols
- and close-quarters combat.

### Signature Weapon — “Judgement”

Predator’s personalized revolver:
[[Judgement]]

is one of the most iconic weapons within the Hell Hosts.

The weapon was custom-built to his specifications and is capable of firing:
specialized explosive ammunition.

Predator rarely uses explosive rounds unless absolutely necessary.

Each shot from Judgement is treated with deliberate precision.

A common phrase associated with the weapon states:

> “I do not miss.  
> I do not warn.  
> I justify.”

## History

Much of Predator’s early life remains classified.

Known records indicate:
both of his parents died during military conflict when he was young.

Following their deaths, William Grant entered a life involving:
- mercenary work
- illegal ship theft
- and survival-based criminal activity.

Eventually, he joined the:
[[Terrorism Control Agency|TCA]]

before later becoming involved with the early:
[[Hell Hosts]]

following the conclusion of the:
TCA-F.O.R War.

Predator quickly rose through the ranks during:
the first Hell Host-Free World conflicts.

After:
- George Wilson died in battle
- and Jordan Thompson disappeared

Predator assumed leadership of the Hell Hosts.

Under his command, the faction expanded massively in:
- territory
- military strength
- influence
- and technological advancement.

## Defining Events

### The Rainfall Execution
One of the most controversial moments in Predator’s history.

During a rain-soaked operation against:
[[Free World Order]]

Predator personally executed captured enemy soldiers.

The event became infamous across the galaxy and permanently damaged his reputation among several factions.

---

### The Hell Host-Angel Sword War
Predator personally killed the leader of the:
[[Angel Swords]]

during the conflict.

The act became legendary within Hell Host military history.

---

### The Destiny Rescue Operation
Following Destiny’s kidnapping and eventual transformation, Predator launched multiple operations attempting to recover her.

The failure of these missions deeply affected him psychologically.

## Relationships

### [[Destiny]]
Predator’s daughter.

Their relationship is deeply complicated.

During Destiny’s childhood, Predator was rarely present due to:
- military service
- mercenary contracts
- and warfare.

Although the two eventually grew closer within the Hell Hosts, Destiny refused special treatment and insisted on earning her rank independently.

Following her kidnapping and Ascension, their relationship became distant and emotionally fractured.

Destiny understands her fate was not directly his fault—
but part of her still blames him.

Predator carries immense guilt over failing to save her.

---

### [[Azure Gale]]
Predator’s second-in-command and one of the few individuals capable of openly challenging him.

Their personalities differ heavily.

Where Predator is cold and restrained,
Azure Gale is:
- sarcastic
- theatrical
- and excessively brutal.

Despite frequent disagreements, the two deeply trust one another.

---

### [[Blood]]
Predator viewed Blood almost like a son.

Blood and Destiny maintained a close relationship for many years, causing Predator to grow personally attached to him.

Following Blood’s Ascension and rise within the:
[[Ashen Kingdom]]

Predator became obsessed with attempting to recover him.

Much of this obsession stems from guilt.

---

### [[Ranger]]
One of Predator’s oldest and most trusted companions.

The two have known each other since the earliest days of the:
[[Hell Hosts]]

Predator consistently entrusts Ranger with:
- high-risk operations
- critical missions
- and frontline leadership.

---

### [[Red Wind]]
Predator treated Red Wind with unusual protectiveness.

He frequently attempted to help her uncover memories of her forgotten past.

However, their relationship always carried a strange tension—
as though Predator knew more about her history than he admitted.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Predator is viewed differently depending on perspective.

### Within the Hell Hosts
He is respected as:
- a legendary commander
- frontline leader
- and the architect of Hell Host power.

---

### Outside Factions
Most external civilizations view Predator with:
- fear
- distrust
- or outright hatred.

His rapid military expansion and ruthless tactics caused many factions to consider the Hell Hosts one of the galaxy’s greatest threats.

Among enemy soldiers, reports of:
Hell Host fleets entering orbit
often trigger panic.

## Personal Life

Outside combat, Predator lives an isolated and emotionally distant existence.

He spends little time outside his armor and rarely engages in recreational activity.

### Common Habits
- Drinks whiskey with water or ice
- Smokes only while off duty
- Frequently remains awake for extended periods
- Spends large amounts of time alone

### Off-Duty Appearance
When not wearing armor, Predator commonly wears:
- black leather jackets
- cargo trousers
- combat boots
- military dog tags
- and a specialized Hell Host wrist-device used for communication and tactical systems.

## Psychological State

Despite his composed public image, Predator suffers from:
- exhaustion
- depression
- unresolved guilt
- and emotional isolation.

However, he refuses to allow personal suffering to interfere with his responsibilities as commander.

At his core, Predator genuinely believes:
he is doing what must be done to preserve the galaxy—
even if history eventually condemns him for it.
