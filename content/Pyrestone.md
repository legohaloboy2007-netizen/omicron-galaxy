# Pyrestone

## Overview

Pyrestone is a volatile volcanic crystalline material native to the:

[[Ashen Kingdom]]

and is regarded as one of the most powerful naturally occurring thermal-energy resources in known space.

The material is heavily associated with:
- volcanic industry
- forge civilizations
- thermal weaponry
- and magma-reactive engineering

Unlike stable energy sources such as [[Axionium]], Pyrestone is highly aggressive and naturally unstable, capable of storing and releasing immense quantities of thermal energy.

The substance became foundational to the industrial and military expansion of the Ashen Kingdom, powering:
- forge-temples
- volcanic reactors
- siege weapons
- and colossal industrial foundries

Among the Ashen peoples, Pyrestone symbolizes:
strength through destruction.

## Nature

Pyrestone is a highly compressed thermo-reactive crystal formed deep within:
- volcanic fault systems
- magma oceans
- and extreme geothermal environments

The crystal continuously absorbs and stores:
- heat
- pressure
- seismic energy
- and thermal radiation

Under stress, the material releases this energy violently.

Researchers describe Pyrestone as:
“a crystal constantly attempting to erupt.”

## Physical Appearance

Pyrestone commonly appears as:
- dark volcanic crystal
- glowing orange-red mineral veins
- or fractured obsidian-like formations containing molten internal light

The material emits:
- intense heat
- low seismic vibration
- and unstable thermal pulses

Large deposits often cause:
- localized temperature spikes
- volcanic instability
- and atmospheric ash activity

Under darkness, Pyrestone glows like:
contained magma beneath fractured stone.

## Discovery

Ancient records suggest Pyrestone was first discovered within the volcanic regions surrounding:
[[Vexar Prime]]

Early Ashen civilizations quickly realized the material’s potential for:
- forging
- industrial energy
- and warfare

As the Ashen Kingdom expanded, Pyrestone became central to nearly every aspect of its civilization.

Entire fortress-cities and industrial sectors were eventually constructed around massive Pyrestone reactor cores.

## Properties

Pyrestone possesses several highly dangerous characteristics.

### Thermal Storage
The crystal stores extraordinary quantities of heat and pressure.

---

### Energy Release
Under activation or destabilization, Pyrestone can release devastating thermal output.

---

### Seismic Resonance
Large deposits influence surrounding tectonic activity and volcanic systems.

---

### Forge Compatibility
Highly effective for:
- heavy metallurgy
- industrial furnaces
- and volcanic engineering

---

### Volatile Instability
Improper containment may result in:
- thermal detonation
- magma eruption
- or catastrophic reactor failure

## Industrial Applications

Pyrestone forms the backbone of:
Ashen industrial civilization.

The material is used in:
- volcanic reactors
- forge-temples
- industrial furnaces
- mining systems
- magma extraction
- and planetary power systems

Massive Pyrestone furnace cores power many of the Ashen Kingdom’s:
- war foundries
- industrial megastructures
- and fortress-cities

## Military Applications

The Ashen Kingdom heavily utilizes Pyrestone within:
- thermal artillery
- magma weapons
- incendiary systems
- siege reactors
- volcanic bombardment platforms
- and heavy war machinery

Some Pyrestone weapons are capable of:
melting armored vehicles or destabilizing defensive structures through sustained thermal output.

Elite Ashen siege units frequently carry:
Pyrestone-core weapon systems.

## Relationship to the Ashen Kingdom

Within the:
[[Ashen Kingdom]]

Pyrestone is viewed as:
- sacred fire
- industrial strength
- and proof of survival through hardship

Many Ashen rituals involve:
- forge-fire ceremonies
- volcanic offerings
- and Pyrestone ignition rites

The kingdom’s philosophy teaches:
power must be forged under pressure.

## Environmental Effects

Regions containing large Pyrestone deposits frequently experience:
- volcanic storms
- magma rivers
- seismic instability
- atmospheric ash clouds
- and extreme environmental heat

Some abandoned mining sectors continue burning centuries after collapse.

## Corruption Interaction

Pyrestone reacts unpredictably when exposed to:
[[Element 95]]
or [[Deatherium]]

Known effects include:
- unstable crystal mutation
- living magma growths
- thermal corruption storms
- and catastrophic energy surges

Several Ashen containment disasters reportedly originated from corrupted Pyrestone reactors.

## Modern Rarity

While still actively mined within the Ashen Kingdom, high-purity Pyrestone is difficult and dangerous to extract.

Major deposits are commonly found near:
- volcanic trench systems
- magma oceans
- and unstable tectonic fault regions

Mining operations experience extremely high casualty rates.

## Common Theories

The true origin of Pyrestone remains uncertain.

Popular theories suggest:
- it formed naturally under extreme planetary pressure
- it is partially connected to planetary cores
- or it developed through long-term exposure to abyssal geothermal energy

Some Ashen myths claim:
the crystal contains fragments of a dead world’s fire.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Pyrestone is known as:
- the volcanic crystal of the Ashen Kingdom
- one of the most dangerous thermal materials ever discovered
- and the fuel behind Ashen industry and warfare

Among Ashen forge-workers, one phrase is commonly repeated:

> “Fire does not destroy the worthy.  
> It reveals them.”
