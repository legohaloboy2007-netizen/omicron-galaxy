# Ranger

## Overview

**Real Name:** Elijah Miller

**Callsign:** Ranger

**Age:** 38

**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]

**Rank:** Lieutenant General

**Origin:** 7th Division

**Role:** Frontier Warfare Commander

**Threat Level:** Extreme

**Primary Weapon:** Custom Lever Action Rifle

Elijah Miller, known throughout the galaxy as **Ranger**, is one of the most recognizable and unconventional officers in Hell Hosts history.

Known for his frontier mentality, fearless aggression, and complete disregard for impossible odds, Ranger has become both a battlefield asset and a legend among troops.

He rose through the ranks through raw performance rather than political influence, earning his position through victories, survival, and a willingness to take risks few others would consider.

To allies, he is a hero.

To enemies, he is a recurring problem.

To high command, he is a headache that somehow keeps working.

---

## Appearance

Ranger stands approximately:

**6'2"**

with a broad, athletic build developed through years of combat and life on frontier worlds.

### Features

- Dark brown hair
- Hazel eyes
- Scar across his chin
- Slightly crooked nose
- Constantly looks like he knows something you don't

### The Grin

Ranger's most recognizable feature.

Many soldiers claim they can identify him by his smile alone.

Unfortunately, that smile usually means something reckless is about to happen.

### Armor

Ranger's equipment reflects his frontier origins.

His gear prioritizes:

- Mobility
- Durability
- Practicality

His iconic poncho and custom rifle have become almost as famous as the man carrying them.

---

## Personality

Ranger embodies the old-world frontier warrior mentality.

He is:

- Bold
- Outspoken
- Loyal
- Competitive
- Confident
- Surprisingly charismatic

He often treats danger as entertainment.

This tendency concerns nearly everyone around him.

### Loyalty

Despite his reputation, Ranger is deeply loyal to both his allies and the Hell Hosts.

He will fight fiercely for the people he trusts.

### Rules Of War

Ranger is brutal in combat.

He is not cruel.

Enemy combatants willing to surrender, defect, or switch sides are frequently given the opportunity.

Those who refuse usually regret the decision shortly afterward.

---

## Leadership Style

Ranger leads from the front.

He believes soldiers are more likely to follow someone willing to face danger alongside them.

Many of his most famous victories occurred because Ranger personally placed himself in situations most commanders would avoid.

His troops trust him because they know:

> Ranger would never ask them to do something he wouldn't do himself.

---

## Combat Style

Ranger specializes in:

- Mobile warfare
- Precision shooting
- Shock assaults
- Frontier operations
- High-risk engagements

### Aggressive Problem Solving

Most officers create plans.

Ranger creates opportunities.

His approach often appears reckless from the outside.

The frustrating reality is that it usually works.

### Precision Shooting

Despite his reputation as a brawler and frontline commander, Ranger is considered one of the finest marksmen within Hell Hosts.

Numerous targets previously considered unreachable have been eliminated by Ranger under combat conditions.

---

## Weapons

### Custom Lever Action Rifle

Ranger's signature weapon.

An unusual choice in an era dominated by advanced military technology.

To Ranger, however, the rifle represents home.

Reliable.

Accurate.

Simple.

The weapon has become inseparable from his reputation.

### Combat Knife

Used frequently in close quarters.

Maintained carefully and trusted completely.

## Origin

Ranger was born on a sparsely populated frontier colony world.

Unlike the heavily urbanized core systems, his home valued:

- Independence
- Survival
- Self-reliance
- Hunting
- Practical skill

While others grew up around military academies and politics, Ranger grew up around open land, dangerous wildlife, and old rifles.

His frontier mentality remains visible in nearly everything he does.

## Relationships

### [[Predator]]

Predator trusts Ranger because results speak louder than methods.

The two have known each other since the early years of Hell Hosts.

Despite Ranger's unconventional approach to warfare, Predator continues assigning him critical missions.

That trust has been earned repeatedly.

---

### [[Champion]]

One of Ranger's closest friends.

The two share a brotherly relationship built through years of combat experience.

Both lead from the front.

Both care deeply about their troops.

The difference is that Champion thinks before acting.

Ranger thinks while acting.

Their debates are legendary.

---

### [[Destiny]]

Ranger treats Destiny almost like a younger sibling.

He respects her abilities and often attempts to pull her out of her shell.

He genuinely wants to see her succeed.

---

### [[Red Wind]]

Ranger often tries to include Red Wind in conversations and gatherings.

Not because he pities her.

Because he notices when people isolate themselves.

His efforts rarely succeed.

He keeps trying anyway.

---

### [[Azure Gale]]

Mutual respect.

Azure Gale plans.

Ranger executes.

Their approaches differ dramatically, but both understand the value of the other.

---

### [[Forsaken]]

Forsaken tolerates Ranger.

Ranger finds this incredibly funny.

He occasionally attempts to get reactions out of him.

Results vary.

---

### [[Necromancer]]

Unlike most people, Ranger is not intimidated by Necromancer.

This confuses everyone involved.

Including Necromancer.

## The Bounty

Ranger possesses one of the largest active bounty totals placed on any Hell Hosts officer.

The first bounty was issued by:

- [[Free World Order]]

Followed by:

- [[Angel Swords]]
- [[Kosander]]

Eventually numerous factions began contributing their own rewards.

### Ranger's Response

Most people would find a multi-million-credit bounty concerning.

Ranger treated it like a scoreboard.

Whenever intelligence reports arrived detailing bounty increases, Ranger celebrated.

One famous incident involved several enemy factions combining their bounties into a single reward pool.

The total exceeded:

**231,000,000 Credits**

Ranger's response:

> "Only 231 million?"
>
> "I've been working hard this year."

### Military Assessment

One enemy intelligence report allegedly contained the recommendation:

> "Do not publicly increase Ranger's bounty.
>
> Previous increases appear to improve target morale."
