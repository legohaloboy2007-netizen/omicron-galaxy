# Rania

Rania is a fertile agricultural and trade world located within the [[Prioxis System]], known across the [[Omicron Galaxy]] as one of the largest food production and export planets in the region. Unlike the industrialized atmosphere of worlds such as [[Michi LB9V]], Rania is defined by vast farmland continents, oceanic trade routes, massive agricultural cities, and sprawling bio-engineered ecosystems designed to sustain billions across nearby systems.

The planet plays a critical role in supplying food, livestock, synthetic nutrients, and agricultural resources to major civilian populations, military powers, and industrial worlds throughout the galaxy. Due to its economic importance, Rania maintains extensive trade agreements with corporations, governments, and transport organizations.

Large portions of the planet are covered by genetically optimized farmland, climate-controlled crop regions, floating oceanic harvest facilities, and enormous automated agricultural complexes. Advanced farming technology developed by corporations such as [[Helix Dynamics]] has dramatically increased the planet’s productivity over the centuries.

Despite its peaceful appearance, Rania remains politically and economically vulnerable due to its importance within galactic supply chains. Criminal syndicates, smugglers, and black market groups frequently target trade routes connected to the planet, while larger factions compete for influence over its exports and transport systems.

During periods of galactic war, Rania became strategically valuable due to its ability to sustain military populations and supply frontline systems with critical resources.
