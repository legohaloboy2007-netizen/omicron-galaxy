# Hell Hosts Military Rank Structure

The Hell Hosts military is divided into **Ranks**, **Positions**, and **Appointments**.

- **Rank** determines a soldier's authority and seniority.
- **Position** determines their current command or job.
- **Appointments** are unique leadership roles held by only one individual at a time.

# Notes

- Rank determines authority.
- Position determines responsibility.
- Appointments are prestigious roles awarded by High Command.
- A soldier's **rank** and **position** are not always the same.
