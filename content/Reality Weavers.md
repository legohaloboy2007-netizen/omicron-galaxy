# Reality Weavers

The Reality Weavers are one of the four dominant Void Civilizations inhabiting the [[Voidlands]], feared throughout the galaxy for their mastery over geometry manipulation, dimensional architecture, and reality distortion. Unlike traditional civilizations built upon physical infrastructure and conventional science, the Reality Weavers reshape existence itself through highly advanced Void-based knowledge.

The civilization is centered around the world of [[Aeonhollow]], a planet of impossible architecture where space folds infinitely inward and structures rearrange themselves in response to thought, movement, and intention. To outsiders, their worlds appear incomprehensible and physically impossible, though Reality Weaver inhabitants navigate them naturally.

The Reality Weavers believe reality is not fixed, but instead a flawed structure capable of being rewritten, refined, and perfected. Through their understanding of Void laws, they manipulate matter, gravity, geometry, and dimensional space with extraordinary precision.

Their civilization is governed by the Nexus Conclave, a ruling body composed of immensely powerful Void Architects, High Weavers, and Pattern Scribes. These individuals oversee the shaping of cities, pocket realms, and dimensional constructs throughout the Voidlands.

Reality Weaver society is highly intellectual and deeply hierarchical. Knowledge is treated as sacred, and those who fail to comprehend higher Void principles are considered fundamentally lesser beings.

The faction is also heavily associated with “Shaped Spaces,” artificial pocket dimensions created through Void manipulation. Some of these spaces are believed to contain entire isolated civilizations, weapons, or forgotten realities hidden from the rest of existence.

Although not openly expansionist like the [[Dominion Keepers]], the Reality Weavers are extremely hostile toward outsiders attempting to access Void knowledge or ancient dimensional technology.
