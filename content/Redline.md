# Redline

> "We don't chase. We close. We end it."

## Overview

**Alec Vandrix**, known by the callsign **Redline**, is a Hell Host pursuit and elimination specialist serving within the [[Doom Corps]] as a member of the [[612th Hunters]].

A member of [[House Vandrix]], Alec is known for operating at the extreme edge of mission parameters, frequently pursuing targets beyond distances or boundaries where most soldiers would be ordered to disengage.

He specializes in rapid pursuit, infiltration, reconnaissance, and close-range target elimination.

Where other Hunters may locate or observe a target, Redline is often responsible for ensuring they do not escape.

His methods are quiet.

His results generally are not.

## Appearance

Alec is a human male with dark hair, a lean athletic build, and an appearance shaped by years of field operations.

He commonly wears lightweight black Doom Corps armour designed for pursuit, infiltration, and rapid movement.

Red markings identify both his Hell Host allegiance and connection to House Vandrix.

His equipment prioritizes mobility over heavy protection.

Alec frequently wears a lower-face covering during operations and carries minimal exposed equipment beyond what is necessary for the mission.

His armour is usually worn, scratched, and repaired rather than pristine.

Redline has little interest in ceremonial presentation.

---

## Personality

Redline is cold, quiet, focused, and intensely mission-oriented.

He speaks little during operations.

When he does speak, his communication is usually brief and directly relevant to the objective.

Alec has little patience for unnecessary hesitation.

Once a target has been designated and the pursuit has begun, he believes every delay increases the chance of failure.

He can be ruthless when necessary, particularly when dealing with hostile targets that have repeatedly escaped capture.

Despite this, Redline is not indiscriminately violent.

His aggression is highly focused.

He does not enjoy chaos.

He eliminates obstacles.

Alec is also notably uninterested in internal politics.

He is loyal to the mission, his unit, and the Hell Hosts personnel operating beside him.

He has considerably less patience for political arguments surrounding how those missions are assigned.

---

# House Vandrix

Alec was raised within [[House Vandrix]], the same military family associated with several notable Hell Host personnel.

The Vandrix name carries a strong reputation within the faction.

Unlike younger members such as [[Striker|Rowan Vandrix]], Alec appears relatively comfortable with the family legacy.

He neither rejects it nor relies upon it.

To Redline, the name is simply another expectation to meet.

His exact relationship to the other documented members of House Vandrix remains unclear.

---

# The 612th Hunters

Redline serves within the **612th Hunters**, a rapid reconnaissance, pursuit, and elimination unit operating beneath the [[Doom Corps]].

The Hunters specialize in finding targets that believe distance, terrain, or hostile territory will protect them.

Their operations commonly involve:

- High-speed pursuit
- Reconnaissance
- Infiltration
- Target elimination
- Recovery missions
- Tracking hostile personnel
- Operations beyond conventional front lines

Redline is particularly suited to the unit's aggressive pursuit role.

A common 612th Hunters saying is:

> **"We track. We adapt. We eliminate.  
> We are movement.  
> We are the end of the line."**

---

# Background

Alec was raised within House Vandrix and enlisted in the [[Doom Corps]] at nineteen.

He initially served in conventional reconnaissance roles.

His ability to move quickly through hostile territory and eliminate threats at close range eventually attracted the attention of the 612th Hunters.

Alec transferred into the unit after repeatedly demonstrating exceptional performance during:

- Reconnaissance operations
- Close-quarters engagements
- Pursuit missions
- Infiltration exercises

His later service record is heavily classified.

What is known is that Redline has survived several operations assessed beforehand as having effectively no expected survival probability.

Alec does not discuss these missions.

His superiors rarely do either.

---

# Callsign

Alec earned the callsign **Redline** during a pursuit operation in which a hostile target crossed the designated maximum engagement boundary.

Doctrine required disengagement.

Alec continued.

He crossed beyond the operational limit, maintained pursuit, and eliminated the target.

The incident produced significant debate among his superiors.

It also gave him his name.

The **red line** represented the point where pursuit was supposed to end.

Alec crossed it anyway.

---

# Pursuit Doctrine

Redline approaches pursuit differently from [[Longstride|Kira Rhenford]].

Longstride specializes in prolonged tracking and patient observation.

Redline specializes in **closure**.

Once the target's location has been established, Alec attempts to reduce the distance as quickly as tactical conditions allow.

His priorities are:

1. Prevent escape.
2. Deny alternate routes.
3. Maintain contact.
4. Force the target into predictable movement.
5. End the pursuit.

Redline dislikes allowing targets time to reorganize.

Momentum is one of his primary weapons.

---

# Combat Style

Alec specializes in fast, controlled aggression.

He performs best during:

- Close-to-medium-range combat
- Rapid infiltration
- Urban pursuit
- Hostile structure clearing
- Target interception
- Short-duration firefights

Redline frequently uses speed and positioning to force opponents into disadvantageous engagements.

He prefers ending combat quickly rather than allowing a prolonged exchange.

His fighting style relies heavily upon efficient movement, accurate fire, and close-range finishing techniques.

# Loadout

## 94CAR Carbine

Redline carries a **94CAR Carbine** as his primary weapon.

The weapon offers a balance between mobility, accuracy, and stopping power.

Alec values it because it remains effective during both pursuit and direct combat.

---

## PX-9 Sidearm

Alec carries a compact **PX-9 Sidearm** fitted for suppressed operation.

The weapon is primarily used during infiltration, covert engagements, and situations where rifle fire would attract unnecessary attention.

---

## Hidden Blade

Redline possesses a retractable blade integrated into his forearm equipment.

The weapon is primarily intended for close-quarters situations and silent eliminations.

Alec uses it sparingly.

If the hidden blade has become necessary, the target is usually already extremely close.

---

## Combat Knife

Redline also carries a conventional tactical knife.

He considers redundancy useful.

---

# Additional Equipment

Alec's field equipment commonly includes:

- Smoke grenades
- Tracking beacons
- Variable optics
- Tactical sensors
- Navigation equipment
- Compact survival equipment

His loadout is deliberately kept light enough to preserve mobility.

---

# Operating Beyond Limits

One of Redline's greatest strengths is also one of his most persistent problems.

He has a habit of operating beyond standard boundaries when he believes the mission can still be completed.

These boundaries may involve:

- Distance
- Time
- Supply availability
- Enemy territory
- Extraction windows
- Physical exhaustion

Alec does not ignore limits because he believes himself invincible.

He ignores them because he considers some limits situational rather than absolute.

His superiors do not always agree.

---

# Survival Record

Redline has survived several operations where the probability of successful extraction was considered effectively nonexistent.

These events have contributed significantly to his reputation.

Alec does not believe survival proves that the original risk was acceptable.

He simply believes someone had to complete the objective.

This attitude has made him both highly valuable and occasionally infuriating to command staff.

---

# Relationship with Longstride

Redline and [[Longstride|Kira Rhenford]] serve within the same unit but fulfill different roles.

Kira is a tracker.

Alec is a closer.

Longstride may follow a target for hours or days while gathering information and waiting for the correct moment.

Once the target is located and movement becomes predictable, Redline can be brought forward to rapidly end the pursuit.

Their personalities are similarly quiet, but their operational styles differ considerably.

Kira possesses patience.

Alec possesses urgency.

Together, they can be extremely difficult to escape.

---

# Relationship with Striker

Alec and [[Striker|Rowan Vandrix]] are both members of [[House Vandrix]].

Their exact family relationship remains undocumented.

Alec is older and significantly more experienced than Rowan.

Where Striker is still attempting to prove himself worthy of the Vandrix reputation, Redline appears largely uninterested in proving anything publicly.

This difference in attitude may make Alec an interesting point of comparison for the younger soldier.

# Reputation

Within the 612th Hunters, Redline has earned a reputation as one of the unit's most reliable pursuit specialists.

Targets who escape initial contact are not necessarily safe.

Sometimes escape simply means Redline has started following.

Among other Doom Corps personnel, his reputation is summarized by a simple warning:

**If Redline is still moving, the pursuit is not over.**
