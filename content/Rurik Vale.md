# Rurik Vale

> "If the storm sounds quiet, that's when you worry."

## Overview

**Rurik Vale** is a Glacian frontier ranger, explorer, and survival specialist operating across the remote territories of the [[Ice Kingdom]].

Where figures such as [[Vael Taryn]], [[Seren Vahl]], and [[Elara Senn]] operate within the kingdom's military, political, and scientific institutions, Rurik spends most of his time far from major settlements.

His work takes him into frozen wilderness, abandoned outposts, deep glacial regions, unexplored cave networks, and worlds where rescue may be days or weeks away.

Rurik is best known for operating on dangerous frontier worlds such as [[Kyrons End]] and [[Glacerion]].

He has guided expeditions, tracked missing personnel, investigated vanished settlements, and survived environments considered effectively uninhabitable by most species.

Rurik prefers the frontier.

Cities are too loud.

Politics is worse.

## Appearance

Rurik possesses the dense, cold-adapted build common among Glacians.

His skin is pale grey with a faint blue undertone, and his eyes are a deep icy blue with a pronounced crystalline iris structure.

His hair is dark silver rather than white and is usually kept short enough to remain practical beneath survival gear.

Rurik's face and hands show numerous scars from years of field work.

Most are minor.

He remembers where all of them came from.

He usually wears layered frontier armour designed for:

- Extreme cold
- High winds
- Ice storms
- Long-distance travel
- Creature attacks
- Low-visibility conditions

His equipment is visibly worn and repaired rather than ceremonial.

Rurik trusts gear that has already survived something.

---

## Personality

Rurik is independent, observant, dry-humoured, and extremely difficult to panic.

Years spent working in isolation have made him comfortable with silence.

He does not feel the need to fill every moment with conversation.

Rurik is cautious without being fearful.

He respects dangerous environments and becomes irritated by people who confuse recklessness with courage.

He is especially impatient with inexperienced expedition members who ignore safety procedures because they believe advanced equipment will compensate.

It usually does not.

Despite his rough manner, Rurik is protective of people under his responsibility.

If he accepts someone onto an expedition, he considers bringing them back alive part of the job.

He does not abandon people easily.

# Role Within the Ice Kingdom

Rurik operates within the Ice Kingdom's frontier and exploration services.

His assignments commonly include:

- Guiding expeditions
- Searching for missing personnel
- Mapping unexplored territory
- Investigating damaged outposts
- Tracking dangerous wildlife
- Surveying glacial regions
- Supporting scientific teams
- Recovering lost equipment
- Inspecting remote settlements
- Investigating unusual environmental activity

He often operates with small teams rather than large military formations.

In some cases, Rurik works alone.

He prefers it that way more often than he admits.

---

# Frontier Ranger

The role of a frontier ranger within the Ice Kingdom is broader than simple exploration.

Remote settlements frequently exist far beyond immediate military or emergency support.

Rangers act as a combination of:

- Scout
- Survival expert
- Search-and-rescue operative
- Tracker
- Environmental observer
- Expedition guide
- Emergency responder

Rurik is considered particularly experienced in situations where communication has failed and nobody knows what happened.

These assignments are rarely simple.

---

# Kyrons End

Rurik has spent considerable time operating on [[Kyrons End]].

The frozen world is considered one of the most dangerous territories associated with the Ice Kingdom.

Its environment is hostile.

Its settlements are limited.

Its wildlife is worse.

Rurik has guided multiple expeditions across the planet and participated in search operations involving personnel lost during storms, creature attacks, and equipment failures.

He maintains extensive personal notes regarding local wildlife and terrain.

Some official maps include corrections originally written in Rurik's field journals.

He dislikes discussing how many expeditions ignored those corrections before they became official.

---

# Glacerion

Rurik also operates frequently on [[Glacerion]], a mining world known for enormous ice formations and hazardous terrain.

Mining activity creates additional risks including:

- Structural collapse
- Deep ice fractures
- Equipment failure
- Subsurface exposure
- Industrial accidents
- Unstable excavation zones

Rurik frequently assists mining teams operating beyond established settlements.

He is particularly experienced in navigating the planet's massive ice-spike regions.

He considers them beautiful.

He also considers anyone attempting to climb them recreationally an idiot.

---

# Tracking

Rurik is an exceptionally skilled tracker.

His abilities are entirely conventional.

He does not possess supernatural tracking powers.

Instead, he relies upon:

- Footprints
- Ice displacement
- Broken surfaces
- Heat traces
- Equipment marks
- Wildlife behaviour
- Wind patterns
- Snow accumulation
- Environmental disturbance

Frozen environments preserve evidence well.

They also bury it quickly.

Rurik understands the difference.

He can often determine how long ago someone passed through an area based on how snow and ice have changed since.

---

# Survival Skills

Rurik is trained to survive extended periods with minimal support.

His expertise includes:

- Shelter construction
- Emergency heating
- Food rationing
- Ice navigation
- Storm prediction
- Field medicine
- Equipment repair
- Creature avoidance
- Emergency signalling
- Long-range travel

He strongly believes that survival begins before anything goes wrong.

Most disasters, in his experience, begin with somebody assuming they have more time than they actually do.

---

# Combat Ability

Rurik is not a conventional soldier, but frontier work has made him highly capable in combat.

He primarily fights when:

- Protecting expedition members
- Defending remote settlements
- Responding to wildlife attacks
- Escaping hostile territory

He favours practical weapons with strong reliability in extreme cold.

Typical equipment includes:

- Long-range rifle
- Heavy sidearm
- Survival knife
- Signal equipment
- Emergency explosives
- Environmental sensors

Rurik prefers avoiding unnecessary fights.

A dead predator is still dangerous if killing it causes an avalanche.

---

# Wildlife Expertise

Rurik possesses extensive knowledge of Ice Kingdom wildlife.

He studies:

- Migration patterns
- Hunting behaviour
- Territorial markers
- Breeding seasons
- Environmental responses
- Signs of anomalous behaviour

He believes most animal attacks are predictable.

The problem is usually that people ignore the warning signs.

Rurik is considerably more concerned when wildlife begins behaving incorrectly.

Animals running away from something are useful information.

Animals disappearing completely are worse.

---

# Relationship with Calypso

Rurik respects [[Calypso]] as ruler of the Ice Kingdom but has little involvement with royal court life.

He has met her on several occasions, usually after major frontier incidents.

Rurik speaks to Calypso respectfully.

He does not become particularly formal.

This has surprised several court officials.

Calypso does not appear bothered.

Rurik's value comes from providing information that rarely reaches the throne through conventional administration.

If an isolated region is deteriorating, he is often one of the first people to know.

---

# Relationship with Vael Taryn

Rurik has worked with [[Vael Taryn]] during military and rescue operations.

The two share a similar respect for preparation and practical decision-making.

Vael trusts Rurik's judgment regarding terrain and environmental risk.

Rurik trusts Vael because the General does not treat frontier personnel as disposable scouts.

Their disagreements usually concern timing.

Vael thinks in campaigns.

Rurik thinks in storms.

Sometimes the storm wins.

---

# Relationship with Elara Senn

Rurik frequently serves as a guide for [[Elara Senn]] and other scientific teams entering dangerous frozen regions.

Their working relationship is built largely around mutual irritation and respect.

Elara dislikes Rurik referring to ancient research sites as:

**"Probably haunted."**

Rurik continues doing it.

Rurik, in turn, dislikes scientists attempting to remain at dangerous sites after conditions begin deteriorating.

Elara occasionally does this.

They have argued about it more than once.

Rurik has still brought her back alive every time.

---

# Relationship with Seren Vahl

Rurik has far less patience for politics than [[Seren Vahl]].

Seren understands this.

She occasionally requests his presence during discussions involving frontier settlements because she considers direct testimony more useful than sanitized reports.

Rurik hates these meetings.

He attends anyway.

Usually in field clothing.

---

# Field Philosophy

Rurik follows several basic rules.

Do not travel without telling someone where you are going.

Do not trust weather because it was calm an hour ago.

Do not approach unknown wildlife.

Do not activate ancient machinery because it "looks intact."

Do not assume communications will work when needed.

And never ignore the person who lives there because a map says something different.

Rurik considers these principles obvious.

Experience suggests otherwise.

---

# Reputation

Rurik is highly respected among:

- Frontier settlers
- Miners
- Rangers
- Expedition teams
- Remote military personnel
- Search-and-rescue units

He has a reputation for finding people who should have been impossible to recover.

Not all searches succeed.

Rurik remembers the unsuccessful ones more clearly than the successful ones.

He keeps a record of every person officially listed as missing during one of his operations.

He does not consider someone dead until enough evidence exists to justify saying so.

# Strengths

## Environmental Awareness

Rurik notices small environmental changes extremely quickly.

## Survival Experience

He has spent years operating in conditions that can kill unprepared personnel within minutes.

## Tracking

Rurik is highly skilled at interpreting evidence left in snow, ice, and hostile terrain.

## Independence

He functions effectively without constant communication or support.

---

# Weaknesses

## Distrust of Bureaucracy

Rurik sometimes delays reporting issues because he believes official processes will slow the response.

This has caused problems.

## Self-Reliance

He occasionally attempts tasks alone that would be safer with additional personnel.

## Poor Political Patience

Rurik is useful in diplomatic meetings only when someone wants completely unfiltered information.
