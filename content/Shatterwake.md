# Shatterwake

## The Fractured World of the Riftborn

Shatterwake is one of the most unstable worlds within the [[Voidlands]] and serves as the primary domain of:

[[Riftborn]]

A powerful subculture within [[The Anchorless Caste]].

The planet no longer exists as a single unified celestial body.

Instead, Shatterwake has been shattered into thousands of drifting planetary fragments suspended across a vast spatial anomaly field. Each shard exists within its own localized reality state, possessing:
- unique environments
- independent gravity
- altered physics
- distorted time flow
- and differing dimensional stability

No two shards behave exactly alike.

To outsiders, the planet appears less like a destroyed world—

And more like reality itself fractured apart.

## The Great Fracture

Ancient Anchorless oral records claim Shatterwake was once a normal world long before:
- the deep expansion into the Voidlands
- the rise of the Reality Weavers
- and the modern understanding of Void Laws

At some unknown point, the planet suffered an event known only as:

The Great Fracture

The catastrophe shattered the world physically and causally, splitting not only the planet—
but aspects of local reality itself.

To this day, the cause remains unknown.

Some theories suggest:
- a failed Void experiment
- a dimensional war
- collapse of a planetary-scale Lattice structure
- or the activation of an ancient weapon.

## Environment

Shatterwake consists of countless drifting shards ranging from:
- mountain-sized fragments
- floating continents
- city remnants
- fractured oceans
- isolated forests
- crystalline debris fields
- recursive anomaly zones

Each shard possesses unique laws.

Examples include:
- reversed gravity
- frozen time progression
- accelerated decay
- endless storms
- non-linear geography
- atmospheric inversion
- unstable dimensional overlap

Travel between shards is extremely dangerous and unpredictable.

## The Riftborn

The Riftborn are a Void-adapted people native to Shatterwake and descendants of [[The Anchorless Caste]].

Unlike conventional beings, [[Riftborn]] physiology partially synchronizes with local reality conditions.

As they move between shards, their forms subtly reshape to adapt to the environmental laws of each fragment.

Changes may include:
- altered skeletal structure
- shifting skin texture
- gravity adaptation
- sensory mutation
- dimensional phase variance
- changes in perception or memory

Some [[Riftborn]] no longer possess a permanently stable physical form.

The [[Riftborn]] consider this adaptation sacred rather than horrifying.

## Shard Navigation

The [[Riftborn]] travel across Shatterwake using:
- tether bridges
- dimensional sails
- gravity currents
- Void tide pathways
- and phase-linked transit systems

Many travel routes only exist temporarily before collapsing or restructuring.

Entire settlements may relocate unintentionally when local shard physics shift.

## The Broken Skies

The skies above Shatterwake are filled with:
- drifting planetary fragments
- dimensional lightning
- fractured stars
- unstable auroras
- shattered moons
- and floating debris fields suspended impossibly in space

In some regions, observers can see:
different versions of the same shard simultaneously.

## Temporal Shards

Certain fragments of Shatterwake experience altered time flow.

Known examples include:
- shards where time barely moves
- regions trapped in repeating moments
- accelerated decay sectors
- and fragments where cause and effect occur out of order

Riftborn explorers often age inconsistently after long expeditions.

## The Hollow Fragment

One of the most feared regions is:

The Hollow Fragment

A completely silent shard where:
- sound does not exist
- movement leaves delayed echoes
- and physical shadows behave independently from their owners

Many [[Riftborn]] refuse to enter the fragment.

## The Planet-Cutter Rumor

A persistent [[Voidlands]] rumor claims one hidden shard contains:
an ancient weapon capable of slicing a planet apart.

Descriptions vary dramatically.

Some believe the weapon caused:
The Great Fracture itself.

Others claim the shard is guarded by:
- impossible entities
- autonomous Void defenses
- or a surviving pre-fracture intelligence

No confirmed expedition has recovered the weapon.

## Relationship to the Anchorless

Unlike the drifting philosophy of the greater [[The Anchorless Caste|Anchorless]], the [[Riftborn]] embrace instability itself.

They believe:
identity is not fixed—
only temporary.

This ideology has made them both respected and feared throughout the [[Voidlands]].

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Shatterwake is known as:
- the fractured world of the [[Riftborn]]
- one of the most spatially unstable planets in existence
- a world where reality shattered into pieces
- a place where identity changes with the terrain itself

Among the Riftborn, one saying is spoken often:

> “The universe broke.  
> We simply learned how to live between the cracks.”
