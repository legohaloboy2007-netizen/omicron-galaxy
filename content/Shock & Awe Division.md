# Shock and Awe Division

> *"Protect. Serve. Ensure."*

The **Shock and Awe Division** is an elite protective formation of the [[Fleet Guard]] responsible for safeguarding the [[Hell Hosts]]' highest-ranking personnel, strategically critical assets, and individuals whose survival is considered essential to military operations.

Unlike conventional Fleet Guard formations, Shock and Awe is not primarily intended to capture territory or engage enemy forces in prolonged combat.

Its mission is considerably simpler:

**Keep the objective alive. Keep the objective secure. Get the objective home.**

Shock and Awe personnel are selected from some of the most capable soldiers within the Hell Hosts and receive extensive training in close protection, combat extraction, defensive operations, convoy security, counter-ambush tactics, and emergency response.

The division is most famously associated with the protection of Hell Host admirals and senior military leadership.

---

## Responsibilities

Shock and Awe operations include:

- VIP protection
- Senior officer security
- Admiral protection
- Strategic asset security
- Diplomatic escorts
- Emergency extraction
- High-value convoy protection
- Secure transport operations
- Counter-ambush operations
- Evacuation of critical personnel
- Protection of classified material
- Recovery of isolated VIPs
- Security during negotiations and diplomatic events
- Reinforcement of compromised command facilities

The division may operate aboard warships, military installations, planetary facilities, civilian environments, active battlefields, or anywhere else a protected individual or asset is required to travel.

---

## Protected Personnel

Shock and Awe is most closely associated with protecting senior Hell Host command personnel.

Historically, permanent or mission-specific protection has commonly been assigned to:

- Fleet Admirals
- Admirals
- Senior Fleet Command personnel
- Officers above Major General or equivalent command authority
- High-ranking Hell Host leadership
- Critical scientific personnel
- Strategic intelligence personnel
- Diplomats and political representatives
- Other individuals designated strategically irreplaceable

Protection is determined by operational importance rather than prestige alone.

A relatively low-ranking scientist carrying knowledge critical to an ongoing crisis may receive a larger Shock and Awe detail than a senior officer operating inside a secure Hell Host facility.

Likewise, senior commanders do not necessarily travel with large protective units when circumstances do not require them.

Threat assessment determines deployment.

---

## Strategic Asset Protection

Personnel are not the division's only responsibility.

Shock and Awe may be assigned to protect strategic assets considered too important to risk conventional security alone.

These may include:

- Classified prototypes
- Experimental technology
- Sensitive intelligence
- Anomalous artifacts
- Critical data systems
- Command equipment
- High-value military cargo
- Irreplaceable research material

In these situations, the protected asset is treated similarly to a VIP.

If the asset can be evacuated, Shock and Awe extracts it.

If it cannot be moved, the division establishes a defensive perimeter and holds until reinforcement or recovery arrives.

If capture would represent an unacceptable strategic risk, contingency protocols may authorize destruction of the asset.

# Selection

Shock and Awe recruits from experienced Hell Host personnel rather than inexperienced soldiers.

Candidates are expected to already possess exceptional combat records before being considered.

Selection emphasizes more than marksmanship or physical ability.

A successful Shock and Awe operative requires:

- Discipline
- Situational awareness
- Rapid threat recognition
- Independent judgement
- Close-quarters combat ability
- Defensive planning
- Weapons proficiency
- Emotional control
- Extraction experience
- Team coordination
- Loyalty under extreme pressure

The division has little use for soldiers interested primarily in personal glory.

A protective operative may successfully complete an entire mission without firing a weapon.

That is considered a success.

The objective surviving is more important than the operative appearing impressive.

---

# Protective Doctrine

Shock and Awe operates according to a doctrine of **overwhelming local superiority**.

The name does not refer simply to aggressive firepower.

When a protected individual comes under threat, the division attempts to seize control of the immediate environment faster than the attacker can exploit it.

Personnel may rapidly establish:

- Defensive positions
- Suppressive fire
- Armored evacuation routes
- Electronic countermeasures
- Air support
- Emergency transport
- Alternate extraction corridors
- Reinforcement zones

The objective is not necessarily to destroy every hostile force present.

It is to create enough control over the immediate battlefield to move the protected individual or asset to safety.

Shock and Awe therefore distinguishes between **winning the engagement** and **completing the protection mission**.

If the VIP is safely extracted while hostile forces remain operational, the mission can still be considered successful.

If every attacker is killed but the VIP dies, the mission has failed.

---

# Extraction Doctrine

Every Shock and Awe protection plan includes multiple contingencies.

Primary extraction routes are supported by secondary and emergency alternatives whenever circumstances permit.

Personnel are trained to assume that:

- Vehicles may be destroyed
- Communications may fail
- Landing zones may become compromised
- Friendly forces may become unavailable
- Routes may become blocked
- Enemy intelligence may know the original evacuation plan

Protection teams are therefore expected to improvise without losing sight of their primary responsibility.

The protected individual does not dictate extraction once an emergency protocol has been activated.

Even extremely senior officers can temporarily find themselves being physically removed from danger by personnel considerably below them in rank.

Shock and Awe considers embarrassment preferable to a funeral.

---

# Codenames and Identity Protection

One of the division's oldest traditions is the use of **operational codenames**.

Every full Shock and Awe operative selects or receives a codename used throughout sensitive assignments.

Historically, the division went considerably further.

Original Shock and Awe doctrine required the real identities of accepted personnel to be erased from accessible Hell Host databases, leaving their operational codenames as their primary recorded identities.

The purpose was counterintelligence.

Shock and Awe personnel routinely operate near admirals, command staff, classified facilities, strategic assets, and sensitive movements. Compromising the identity of a protective operative could allow hostile intelligence services to investigate their history, target relatives, impersonate contacts, or prepare specifically for their deployment.

Modern Hell Host systems no longer literally destroy every record associated with an operative.

Instead, their identities are placed behind heavily restricted security partitions.

To most personnel and ordinary military databases, however, the practical result remains similar:

**Shock and Awe personnel are known by their codenames.**

Their legal identities exist.

Very few people are authorized to access them.

---

# Fleet Guard

Shock and Awe operates under the [[Fleet Guard]].

Its position reflects the division's historical role protecting admirals, command personnel, warships, and strategic naval assets.

However, the division is not restricted to fleet environments.

Shock and Awe teams frequently deploy planetside alongside [[Doom Corps]], aviation units, diplomatic missions, intelligence personnel, and other Hell Host formations.

Where the protected objective goes, Shock and Awe can follow.

---

# Standard Weapons

Shock and Awe personnel have access to the wider [[Hell Host Arsenal]], although several weapons have traditionally been associated with the division.

### [[CR-556]]

The CR-556 is commonly used for general protective operations due to its versatility and relatively manageable profile.

It is particularly suited to escorts where personnel may transition rapidly between vehicles, interiors, and open environments.

### [[MR-102E]]

The MR-102E is used by designated marksmen and overwatch personnel.

Protective teams may position marksmen away from the immediate VIP detail to identify and engage threats before they reach the protected formation.

### [[Y205]]

The suppressed Y205 is frequently used during covert escorts, interior security operations, and situations where compact weaponry is preferable.

### [[333 HCS]]

The 333 HCS provides Shock and Awe teams with heavy long-range precision capability.

Its high-caliber ammunition offers exceptional effectiveness against armored targets and hardened positions, although its slower handling and reload requirements make it primarily an overwatch weapon rather than a conventional protective firearm.

Additional weapons may be issued according to mission requirements.

---

# Emergency Response

Shock and Awe teams are occasionally dispatched even when no protection detail was originally assigned.

If a senior commander, diplomat, scientist, or other strategically important individual becomes trapped behind hostile lines, Shock and Awe may be deployed specifically to recover them.

These missions can involve penetrating active combat zones, establishing temporary defensive corridors, locating the objective, and extracting them before hostile forces can respond.

Such operations are among the division's most dangerous assignments.

They are also among the reasons Shock and Awe maintains personnel capable of functioning as conventional elite infantry when required.

---

# Command Philosophy

Shock and Awe personnel are taught that protection is not passive.

Standing beside an important person and waiting for someone to attack them is considered inadequate security.

Operatives continuously evaluate:

- Nearby personnel
- Terrain
- Entrances and exits
- Vehicle positioning
- Possible firing positions
- Crowd movement
- Electronic activity
- Unusual behavior
- Potential escape routes
- Changes in the operational environment

A successful protective detail often prevents an attack from becoming possible in the first place.

---

# [[Shepherd]]

[[Shepherd]] is one of the division's most experienced protective operations commanders.

Holding the rank of Captain, Shepherd specializes in combat extraction and the recovery of high-value personnel from active combat environments.

His command philosophy strongly reflects Shock and Awe doctrine.

The protected objective survives.

Everything else is secondary.

Shepherd has become particularly associated with operations in which conventional evacuation plans have already failed and a new extraction method must be established under fire.

He is trusted with enough operational authority that even senior personnel are expected to follow his instructions once an emergency extraction begins.

---

# Reputation

Shock and Awe enjoys considerable respect throughout the Hell Hosts.

Its personnel are not famous because they win spectacular battles.

They are famous because important people have survived battles they should not have survived.

Among senior command personnel, the arrival of Shock and Awe can carry two very different meanings.

Either someone has decided they are extremely important—

or intelligence believes something extremely unpleasant is about to happen.

Sometimes both.

## Motto

**Protect. Serve. Ensure.**

Protect the objective.

Serve the mission.

Ensure they return.
