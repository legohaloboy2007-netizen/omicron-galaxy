# Shooter

> *"The weapons changed. The war didn't."*

## Profile

**Real Name:** Wei Chen  
**Callsign:** Shooter  
**Age:** 68  
**Nationality:** Chinese  
**Status:** Active  
**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]  
**Former Affiliation:** [[Free Order Rebellion]]  
**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Rank:** Lieutenant  
**Unit:** Independent Attachment  
**Special Abilities:** None  
**Primary Weapon:** VX-12 Marksman Rifle  
**Secondary Weapon:** PX-9 Service Pistol

Wei Chen, better known by his callsign **Shooter**, is a veteran Hell Host soldier and former operative of the [[Free Order Rebellion]].

Unlike many notable Hell Host personnel, Shooter possesses no anomalous abilities, experimental augmentations, advanced combat armor, or unusual technology.

He is simply an exceptionally experienced soldier.

Having survived decades of warfare, Shooter has developed an extensive understanding of reconnaissance, marksmanship, fieldcraft, survival, and small-unit combat.

He has fought with equipment now considered obsolete, watched military doctrines repeatedly change, and survived conflicts involving technologies that did not exist when he first entered combat.

Very little surprises him anymore.

---

## Appearance

Shooter is an older man with short dark-grey hair and a heavily weathered appearance.

He generally wears practical combat equipment rather than newer specialized armor systems.

Much of his personal equipment is older than the equipment issued to modern Hell Host infantry, although it has been extensively maintained and modified throughout his service.

Shooter sees little reason to replace equipment simply because something newer exists.

If it continues to function, he continues to use it.

---

## Free Order Rebellion

Before joining the Hell Hosts, Shooter served as an operative within the [[Free Order Rebellion]].

His service with the rebellion accounts for much of his early combat experience and established the methods he continues to use decades later.

Operating with limited resources taught Shooter to function without reliable logistical support, advanced battlefield networks, or technological superiority.

He became accustomed to extended operations where equipment failure, ammunition shortages, communication loss, and isolation were expected rather than exceptional.

The exact circumstances surrounding his departure from the Free Order Rebellion and eventual integration into the Hell Hosts are rarely discussed by Shooter himself.

He does not appear particularly interested in revisiting them.

He retained his callsign, much of his equipment, and many of his methods after joining the Hell Hosts.

---

## Hell Host Service

Shooter currently serves as a Lieutenant within the [[Doom Corps]], although he operates primarily through independent attachment rather than remaining permanently assigned to a conventional formation.

This allows him to be attached to units requiring his particular experience.

He is commonly used for:

- Long-range reconnaissance
- Extended field operations
- Precision shooting
- Tracking
- Forward observation
- Counter-sniper operations
- Survival in isolated environments
- Small-team operations
- Independent assignments

Shooter is capable of working alongside conventional squads but is equally comfortable operating alone for extended periods.

His survival rate during long-duration operations has made him particularly valuable for missions where extraction or resupply cannot be guaranteed.

---

## Combat Style

Shooter's combat ability comes almost entirely from experience.

He is neither exceptionally fast nor unusually strong compared with younger Hell Host soldiers.

Instead, Shooter attempts to ensure that physical advantages never become relevant.

He studies terrain, predicts enemy movement, selects firing positions carefully, and avoids unnecessary engagements.

Years of field experience allow him to notice details inexperienced soldiers may overlook: disturbed terrain, unnatural silence, poor concealment, unusual movement, changes in local wildlife, predictable defensive positions, and other indications of nearby threats.

Shooter rarely reacts faster than everyone around him.

He simply recognizes what is about to happen earlier.

This approach becomes particularly valuable when advanced technology fails.

Electronic warfare, damaged communications, sensor interference, environmental conditions, and anomalous phenomena can render sophisticated battlefield systems unreliable.

Shooter spent much of his life fighting without them.

---

## Equipment

Shooter has a strong preference for older, proven military equipment.

His primary weapon is a **VX-12 Marksman Rifle**, which he maintains personally and has extensively modified throughout his service.

He also carries a **PX-9 Service Pistol**, combat knife, smoke grenades, field equipment, and a dedicated range finder.

Shooter is fully capable of using modern Hell Host weaponry but generally refuses to replace equipment he already understands unless a mission specifically requires it.

This is not caused by an opposition to technology.

Shooter simply places considerably more value on reliability and familiarity than novelty.

---

## Personality

Shooter is quiet, blunt, patient, and emotionally reserved.

He rarely participates in arguments that do not directly affect an operation and has little interest in military prestige or personal recognition.

Political disagreements similarly receive little attention from him.

Shooter has lived through enough changing governments, organizations, commanders, enemies, weapons, and doctrines that he has become deeply skeptical of claims that warfare itself has fundamentally changed.

He judges personnel primarily through their actions.

Competence earns his respect considerably faster than rank.

Failure does not necessarily lose it.

Refusing to learn from failure does.

---

## Age and Experience

At 68, Shooter is considerably older than most active frontline Doom Corps personnel.

Modern medicine allows him to remain physically capable of active service, but age has nevertheless changed how he fights.

Shooter does not attempt to compete with younger soldiers through raw speed or endurance.

Instead, he conserves energy, minimizes unnecessary movement, and relies heavily on positioning and anticipation.

He is fully aware that increasingly advanced autonomous systems, targeting technology, reconnaissance networks, and augmented personnel can perform many tasks faster than he can.

This does not appear to bother him.

Shooter considers technology another tool.

When those tools work, he uses them.

When they stop working, he keeps going.

## Known Relationships

### [[Hammerfall]]

Shooter respects Hammerfall's direct approach to command and her willingness to make difficult battlefield decisions.

His assessment of her is characteristically brief:

> *"Old dog. Gets the job done."*

---

### [[Aeon Unit]]

Shooter recognizes the enormous tactical capabilities of [[Aeon Unit]] but remains unconvinced that every battlefield problem requires machine analysis.

Aeon's assessment of Shooter's methods has occasionally described them as inefficient.

Shooter's response:

> *"Outcome successful."*

Aeon reportedly accepted the correction.

---

### [[Breach]]

Shooter and [[Breach]] communicate surprisingly well despite neither being particularly conversational.

Shooter appreciates Breach's ability to provide fire support without requiring unnecessary explanation.

Neither considers silence uncomfortable.

---

### [[Drifter]]

Shooter respects [[Drifter]] as an experienced independent operator.

Both are comfortable with extended periods of isolation and generally require little supervision in the field.

Shooter has described Drifter simply as:

> *"Respect. That's all I've got."*

---

### [[Nexus]]

Shooter acknowledges [[Nexus]] as an exceptionally capable battlefield strategist and systems operator.

However, Shooter occasionally finds Nexus' dependence on large quantities of battlefield information excessive.

He has remarked that Nexus is:

> *"Hard to predict. Harder to replace."*

---

### [[Null]]

Shooter has little interest in understanding exactly what [[Null]] is capable of.

When asked for his tactical assessment of Null, Shooter reportedly answered:

> *"Irrelevant variable."*

Whether this was an actual tactical judgment or Shooter refusing to participate in the conversation is unclear.

---

## Reputation

Shooter is not among the most famous soldiers in the Hell Hosts.

He has never attempted to become one.

Among personnel who have actually served beside him, however, his reputation is considerably stronger.

Younger soldiers often initially regard his preference for older equipment and conventional fieldcraft as outdated.

This opinion tends to disappear after the first operation where communications fail, targeting systems stop functioning, or environmental interference renders advanced sensors unreliable.

Shooter has spent decades watching military technology change.

His methods have changed with it.

The fundamentals have not.

> *"New weapons. Old war. Same outcome."*

---

## Notes

- Shooter possesses no known supernatural or anomalous abilities.
- He maintains much of his equipment personally.
- He prefers proven equipment over newer systems without clear advantages.
- He is highly experienced in long-duration missions.
- He is comfortable operating independently.
- Shooter generally ignores praise directed toward him.
- His former service with the Free Order Rebellion predates his Hell Host career.
