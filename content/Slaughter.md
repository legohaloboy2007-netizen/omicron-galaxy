# Slaughter

**Real Name:** Unknown  
**Codename:** Slaughter  
**Species:** Human (Unconfirmed)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Height:** 6'0"  
**Faction:** [[Free World Order]]  
**Role:** Heavy Weapons Specialist  
**Status:** Active  

Slaughter is a high-ranking heavy weapons specialist serving within the [[Free World Order]].

Known for his extensive use of heavy weaponry, exceptional battlefield resilience, and complete refusal to speak, Slaughter has developed a formidable reputation among both Free World personnel and opposing forces.

Despite serving alongside the Free World Order for an extended period, remarkably little is known about the individual beneath his armour.

His real name, age, birthplace, background, and even species remain uncertain.

He has never been reliably heard speaking.

## Appearance

Slaughter stands approximately 6'0" and is typically equipped with extremely heavy combat armour designed to withstand sustained frontline engagements.

His equipment is considerably heavier than that carried by ordinary Free World infantry and is designed around protection, ammunition capacity, and the operation of heavy weapons.

His face is rarely, if ever, exposed.

Although Slaughter is generally assumed to be human, this has never been definitively confirmed.

## Silence

One of Slaughter's most distinctive characteristics is his complete silence.

He does not speak during combat.

He does not speak casually with other Free World personnel.

He has not been reliably recorded speaking to senior officers or faction leadership.

Whether Slaughter is physically incapable of speech or simply refuses to speak is unknown.

Despite this, he remains capable of operating effectively alongside other personnel.

Slaughter communicates when necessary through gestures, battlefield movements, tactical markers, written communication, and other non-verbal methods.

Personnel who regularly operate alongside him have reportedly become extremely proficient at interpreting his intentions without requiring verbal communication.

## Personality

Understanding Slaughter's personality is difficult due to his silence and highly private nature.

His actions nevertheless demonstrate considerable courage and commitment to the Free World Order.

He has repeatedly entered extremely dangerous combat situations to protect allied personnel, hold defensive positions, or complete objectives despite overwhelming hostile resistance.

His battlefield record suggests an individual considerably more disciplined than his codename and reputation might initially imply.

Slaughter rarely appears interested in recognition for his actions.

## Free World Order

Slaughter is considered one of the Free World Order's more experienced and respected heavy combat personnel.

His position was earned primarily through battlefield performance, bravery, and continued service during major conflicts.

Although he holds significant standing within the organisation, Slaughter is primarily associated with direct combat rather than administrative or political authority.

He is frequently deployed when Free World forces expect heavy resistance.

## Combat

Slaughter specialises in heavy weapons and sustained frontline combat.

His physical conditioning and armour allow him to carry equipment that would significantly restrict the mobility of ordinary infantry.

His potential loadout includes machine guns, heavy automatic weapons, rocket launchers, flamethrowers, explosives, and other weapons intended for use against fortified positions, vehicles, large concentrations of infantry, or heavily armoured targets.

He does not carry every available weapon simultaneously.

Equipment is selected according to the requirements of an operation, although Slaughter frequently carries considerably more ammunition and weaponry than standard personnel.

## Heavy Shield

One of Slaughter's signature pieces of equipment is a heavy combat shield.

Designed for frontline operations, the shield provides substantial protection against ballistic weapons, explosives, energy weapons, and battlefield debris.

Slaughter can use the shield while advancing through hostile fire, protecting nearby personnel, or establishing temporary cover in exposed environments.

Its substantial weight makes prolonged use difficult for ordinary infantry.

Slaughter's armour and physical conditioning allow him to operate it considerably more effectively.

The shield is particularly useful when combined with other Free World personnel, allowing Slaughter to absorb or redirect incoming fire while allied soldiers advance behind him.

## Armour

Slaughter wears heavily reinforced Free World combat armour.

Protection is prioritised over mobility.

Additional reinforcement surrounds critical areas of the body, while the suit incorporates systems designed to assist with the weight of his weapons, ammunition, shield, and armour.

The suit's powered assistance does not provide extraordinary superhuman strength.

Instead, it reduces the physical burden created by Slaughter's unusually heavy equipment.

This allows him to remain mobile while carrying weapon systems normally operated from fixed positions or by multiple personnel.

## Battlefield Role

Slaughter is most effective in environments where subtlety is no longer required.

He can provide suppressive fire, destroy fortified positions, engage vehicles, protect advancing infantry, and hold narrow defensive positions against substantially larger enemy forces.

During assaults, Slaughter can operate as a mobile anchor for nearby infantry.

During defensive operations, he is capable of turning otherwise vulnerable positions into extremely difficult obstacles for attacking forces.

His willingness to remain under sustained enemy fire has contributed heavily to his reputation for bravery.

## Reputation

Slaughter's codename and appearance have contributed to an intimidating reputation.

Enemy accounts frequently describe an almost completely silent armoured soldier advancing through incoming fire while carrying weapons normally associated with dedicated heavy infantry teams.

Within the Free World Order, however, his reputation is not based solely upon intimidation.

Many personnel respect Slaughter because of his repeated willingness to place himself between hostile forces and allied soldiers.

His silence has also produced countless rumours.

Some claim Slaughter suffered injuries that permanently destroyed his ability to speak.

Others believe his identity is deliberately classified.

More unusual theories question whether he is human at all.

None have been confirmed.

## Relationship with Free World Personnel

Despite rarely communicating conventionally, Slaughter operates effectively within Free World units.

Personnel who have fought alongside him repeatedly tend to develop an understanding of his movements and non-verbal communication.

He has demonstrated particular willingness to protect wounded personnel and soldiers trapped under enemy fire.

Whatever his reasons for remaining silent, Slaughter's actions have earned considerable trust among those accustomed to fighting beside him.

## Unknown Identity

Very little reliable information exists concerning Slaughter before his service with the Free World Order.

His real name remains unknown.

No confirmed civilian records have been publicly associated with him.

His age is unknown.

His place of birth is unknown.

His species is officially uncertain, although human remains the most common assumption.

Slaughter has made no apparent effort to clarify any of these questions.
