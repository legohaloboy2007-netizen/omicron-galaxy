Spectre Division

«[!info] Division Profile
Faction: [[Hell Hosts]]
Type: Military Police / Internal Security Division
Status: Active
Primary Role: Internal security, military policing, investigations, detention
Secondary Roles: Counter-intelligence, riot control, classified-site security, prisoner transport, threat detection
Composition: Human personnel and repurposed / modernised robotic enforcement units
Motto: “We Are the Law.”
Doctrine Phrase: “Order. Control. Compliance.”
Core Principle: “Your rank does not place you above the law.”
Command Title: Provost Marshal
Primary Equipment: [[SP-9 Enforcer Carbine]], [[SP-28 Compliance Shotgun]], [[SP-7 Stun Baton]], [[SP-91 Restraint Cuffs]]»

---

Overview

The Spectre Division is the primary military police, internal security, detention, and counter-intelligence branch of the [[Hell Hosts]].

Where other divisions fight external wars, Spectre protects the military from threats within.

Its responsibilities include:

- Military policing
- Internal investigations
- Base security
- Prison security
- Counter-intelligence
- Sabotage investigation
- Classified facility protection
- Riot control
- Mutiny response
- Desertion cases
- Smuggling investigations
- Espionage detection
- Prisoner transport
- Internal threat assessment

Spectre personnel are not ordinary civilian police.

Local authorities remain responsible for most civilian law enforcement across Hell Host-controlled worlds.

Spectre becomes involved when a case concerns:

- Military personnel
- Military installations
- Classified information
- Strategic assets
- Internal security
- National or faction-level threats

Their reputation is simple.

If Spectre is asking questions, something has already gone wrong.

---

Origins

The Spectre Division traces its origins directly to the [[TCA-FOR War]].

Both the [[Free Order Rebellion]] and [[Terrorism Control Agency]] used robotic enforcement units for:

- Facility security
- Prison control
- Military discipline
- Battlefield policing
- Restricted-zone enforcement

Following the creation of the Hell Hosts, large numbers of these machines were recovered, repaired, reprogrammed, and reorganised under a unified security structure.

That structure eventually became the modern Spectre Division.

---

Early Spectre

The earliest Spectre formations were almost entirely robotic.

This made them particularly effective for internal security.

Machines:

- Did not require sleep
- Could patrol indefinitely
- Could not be bribed easily
- Were difficult to intimidate
- Could guard prisoners for extreme periods
- Could maintain detailed records
- Could operate in hazardous locations

However, Hell Host Command eventually recognised a major limitation.

Robots were excellent at enforcing rules.

Humans were often better at understanding:

«Why the rules had been broken.»

As a result, human personnel were gradually introduced into the division.

Modern Spectre is now a hybrid organisation.

The modern Spectre Division consists of:

- Human Military Police
- Human investigators
- Counter-intelligence officers
- Riot-control personnel
- Detention officers
- Security specialists
- Robotic enforcement units
- Intelligence analysts
- Prison administrators
- Technical specialists

Human and robotic personnel frequently operate together.

A typical Spectre enforcement team may include:

- Human lead officer
- Human investigator
- Robotic enforcers
- Restraint specialist
- Evidence technician

---

FOR Enforcer Units

«[!warning] Legacy Platform
Origin: [[Free Order Rebellion]]
Role: Patrol / pursuit / arrest support
Status: Modernised and still active»

Former FOR enforcement machines remain in limited Spectre service.

These units were historically designed for:

- Rapid patrol
- Pursuit
- Crowd control
- Arrest operations
- Mobile enforcement

Compared with old TCA models, FOR units are generally:

- Lighter
- Faster
- More agile
- Less heavily armoured

Modern variants retain their original chassis design but have been extensively rebuilt using Hell Host technology.

Internally, little of the original system may remain.

Spectre technicians still commonly refer to them simply as:

«FORs»

---

TCA Enforcer Units

«[!warning] Legacy Platform
Origin: [[Terrorism Control Agency]]
Role: Heavy enforcement / detention security
Status: Modernised and still active»

TCA enforcement units were originally designed for more heavily secured environments.

They commonly guarded:

- Government sites
- Detention centres
- Restricted laboratories
- Major military installations

TCA units are typically:

- Heavier
- More durable
- Slower
- More physically imposing

They are especially common in:

- Military prisons
- High-security facilities
- Research compounds
- Restricted bases

Like FOR units, modern TCA enforcers have been repeatedly upgraded.

Their exterior may be old.

Their internal systems are not.

---

Human Military Police

Human MPs form the most visible part of modern Spectre.

They handle:

- Patrols
- Arrests
- Interviews
- Discipline cases
- Evidence collection
- Prisoner processing
- Base security
- Military traffic control
- Soldier misconduct

They are the Spectre personnel most ordinary Hell Host soldiers encounter.

This has contributed significantly to the division's reputation.

---

Internal Investigations

Internal Investigations handles serious cases involving Hell Host personnel or facilities.

Typical investigations include:

- Corruption
- Suspicious deaths
- Classified information leaks
- Sabotage
- Missing weapons
- Compromised officers
- Illegal experimentation
- Disappearances
- Evidence tampering

Investigators may operate in:

- Standard Spectre armour
- Low-profile tactical equipment
- Plain clothes

The arrival of Internal Investigations is generally considered significantly more serious than a routine MP visit.

Common Saying

«“If Spectre knows your name, hope it's because you reported something.”»

Counter-Intelligence

Spectre Counter-Intelligence specialises in identifying threats attempting to penetrate Hell Host infrastructure.

Targets may include:

- [[Angel Sword]] infiltrators
- [[Free World Order]] spies
- [[Obsidian Veil]] agents
- Corporate intelligence personnel
- Compromised military staff
- Rogue artificial intelligence
- Internal extremist networks

Counter-Intelligence works closely with:

- Fleet intelligence
- [[Research Division]]
- Senior command
- Military communications personnel

Obsidian Veil infiltration efforts are considered particularly difficult due to the organisation's experience with covert operations and internal manipulation.

---

Detention Command

Detention Command operates Hell Host military prisons and high-security holding facilities.

Its responsibilities include:

- Prisoner processing
- Military detention
- Interrogation support
- High-risk prisoner transport
- Facility security
- Escape prevention
- Classified prisoner management

Prisoners may include:

- Enemy commanders
- Spies
- Saboteurs
- Mutineers
- Dangerous soldiers
- Rogue specialists
- Anomalous detainees

Some specialised facilities are designed to contain individuals ordinary prisons could not safely hold.

---

High-Security Detention

Spectre maintains several facilities whose locations and prisoner lists are classified.

These may contain:

- High-ranking enemy officers
- Rogue Ascended individuals
- Dangerous researchers
- Artificial intelligences
- Anomalous prisoners
- Personnel involved in highly classified incidents

Access to such facilities is tightly restricted.

Even senior officers may not possess automatic clearance.

---

Riot Response Units

Spectre Riot Response personnel are deployed when unrest becomes violent but full military intervention would be excessive.

They specialise in:

- Crowd control
- Military riots
- Prison disturbances
- Base unrest
- Violent arrests
- Barricaded personnel

Equipment commonly includes:

- Riot shields
- Non-lethal ammunition
- Stun weapons
- Gas systems
- Heavy armour
- Breaching equipment

Their objective is usually:

«Control first. Kill only if control fails.»

---

Authority

Spectre authority operates differently from conventional battlefield rank.

A Spectre officer does not automatically outrank every soldier they encounter.

However, during a legitimate investigation or enforcement action, Spectre jurisdiction may apply regardless of the subject's division.

This means Spectre can investigate personnel from:

- [[Doom Corps]]
- [[Aviation Division]]
- [[Fire Division]]
- [[Research Division]]
- [[Eradication Division]]
- Fleet personnel
- Senior officers

Rank does not automatically prevent investigation.

---

Core Principle

«“Your rank does not place you above the law.”»

This phrase is one of the defining principles of modern Spectre doctrine.

The division exists specifically to prevent:

- Rank
- Reputation
- Political influence
- Military status

from automatically shielding personnel from accountability.

Higher-ranking suspects generally require more extensive authorisation before arrest.

They are not immune.

---

Provost Marshal

The overall commander of the Spectre Division holds the title:

«Provost Marshal»

The Provost Marshal oversees:

- Division-wide policing policy
- Internal security operations
- Major investigations
- Military detention
- Counter-intelligence
- High-level warrants
- Security standards

The identity of the current Provost Marshal may vary depending on era.

---

Weapons Doctrine

Spectre differs significantly from frontline divisions in its approach to weaponry.

The division prioritises:

- Control
- Arrest
- Incapacitation
- Escalation management

Lethal force remains authorised when necessary.

It is not always the first option.

Spectre equipment is therefore designed around both:

- Lethal
- Non-lethal

engagement.

Recording Doctrine

One of the most important features of Spectre enforcement is documentation.

Robotic and human enforcement personnel record:

- Audio
- Visual footage
- Weapon discharge
- Restraint use
- Biometric information
- Arrest timing
- Officer identification

This information is automatically logged.

The system exists for:

- Evidence
- Intelligence
- Investigation review
- Accountability

Tampering with Spectre operational recordings is considered a major offence.

---

Evidence Control

Spectre maintains strict chain-of-custody procedures.

Evidence collected during investigations may include:

- Weapons
- Digital records
- Biological samples
- Classified materials
- Surveillance footage
- Artificial intelligence cores
- Anomalous objects

Sensitive evidence is frequently stored in dedicated security facilities.

Base Security

Spectre maintains a major presence at:

- Military bases
- Fleet installations
- Restricted facilities
- Prisons
- Strategic research locations

Duties include:

- Checkpoints
- Patrols
- Access control
- Restricted-zone monitoring
- Intrusion response

Spectre robotic units are particularly common at fixed installations because of their ability to remain on patrol indefinitely.

---

Relationship with Doom Corps

The relationship between Spectre and the [[Doom Corps]] is famously complicated.

Doom Corps personnel often describe Spectre as:

«“Humourless bastards.”»

Spectre personnel tend to respond by reminding Doom Corps soldiers to stop:

- Fighting in mess halls
- Breaking base equipment
- Ignoring restricted areas
- Driving military vehicles irresponsibly
- “Borrowing” things that were not theirs

Despite constant complaints, both divisions maintain genuine respect.

When serious incidents occur inside Hell Host facilities, Doom Corps personnel are usually happy Spectre exists.

---

Relationship with Aviation Division

The [[Aviation Division]] provides Spectre with a constant stream of administrative misery.

Common problems include:

- Unauthorised aircraft modifications
- Hangar pranks
- Reckless ground vehicle use
- Restricted flight behaviour
- Questionable nose art
- Equipment theft disguised as “borrowing”

Some Spectre offices maintain unusually large files on individual pilots.

Personnel such as [[Icarus]] have not improved the situation.

---

Relationship with Fire Division

The [[Fire Division]] is generally considered easier to police than Doom Corps or Aviation.

Generally.

Common incidents still include:

- Unauthorised artillery modifications
- Stolen road signs
- Improvised gun decorations
- Ammunition accounting discrepancies
- Old FOR/TCA arguments escalating into fights

Spectre personnel have occasionally been forced to explain that welding civilian infrastructure onto artillery does not make it military property.

---

Relationship with Eradication Division

The relationship between Spectre and the [[Eradication Division]] is extremely important.

Their doctrines are fundamentally different.

Spectre:

«Arrest. Investigate. Contain.»

Eradication:

«Destroy.»

Spectre is often involved in confirming:

- Target identity
- Legal status
- Civilian evacuation
- Detention requirements
- Capture priority

before Eradication receives final authorisation.

Once eradication status is confirmed, the possibility of arrest may disappear quickly.

---

Relationship with Research Division

The [[Research Division]] is one of Spectre's most demanding assignments.

Spectre guards:

- Classified laboratories
- Prototype facilities
- Element 95 research
- Axionium research
- Ascension projects
- Experimental weapons
- Restricted archives

Common investigations include:

- Unauthorised experimentation
- Missing research materials
- Security violations
- Forged access permissions
- Classified information leaks

A stereotypical interaction is:

«Researcher: “Technically, I was authorised.”
Spectre Officer: “You forged the authorisation.”»

Classified Facility Security

Spectre units assigned to highly sensitive research facilities receive additional training.

These units may encounter:

- Element 95 contamination
- Artificial intelligence incidents
- Prototype weapons
- Anomalous materials
- Experimental organisms
- Ascension-related events

Some security teams carry specialised equipment not used by ordinary MPs.

---

Reputation Among Soldiers

Spectre is rarely the division soldiers are excited to see approaching them.

This is not necessarily because they are hated.

It is because Spectre generally arrives when:

- Someone is in trouble
- Something has been stolen
- Someone has died
- Security has failed
- Someone knows more than they should

Most Hell Host personnel complain about Spectre.

They stop complaining when they need Spectre.

---

Reputation Among Officers

Senior officers generally respect Spectre's independence.

They may not enjoy it.

A division commander accustomed to unquestioned authority may find it deeply uncomfortable when an investigator asks for:

- Records
- Access logs
- Weapons reports
- Personal testimony

Spectre culture strongly discourages investigators from being intimidated by rank.

---

Civilian Jurisdiction

Spectre is not intended to replace ordinary civilian law enforcement.

Local police generally handle:

- Civilian crime
- Traffic law
- Property disputes
- Ordinary public disturbances

Spectre becomes involved if:

- Military personnel are implicated
- Classified assets are involved
- National security is threatened
- Military installations are targeted
- Civilian activity creates a direct military security risk

This separation prevents Spectre from functioning as a universal civilian secret police force.

Notable Operation: Glass House

«[!success] Counter-Intelligence Operation
Codename: Operation Glass House
Threat: [[Obsidian Veil]] infiltration network
Result: Network dismantled»

[[Operation Glass House]] was a major Spectre counter-intelligence operation targeting an Obsidian Veil infiltration network operating across multiple Hell Host installations.

The investigation began after minor irregularities were discovered in:

- Security logs
- Supply movements
- Personnel access records

Individually, none appeared significant.

Together, they formed a pattern.

Spectre investigators eventually uncovered a coordinated network involving:

- Stolen credentials
- Compromised technicians
- Hidden surveillance
- Data theft
- Embedded operatives

The operation lasted for months.

Spectre deliberately avoided early arrests to prevent the wider network from disappearing.

When the final arrests began, multiple teams acted simultaneously across several locations.

The network collapsed before its members could warn one another.

---

Legacy

Glass House became a famous example of Spectre doctrine.

No massive battle occurred.

No artillery fired.

No fleet arrived.

The enemy simply realised too late that Spectre had been watching them for months.

---

Notable Operation: Quiet Barracks

«[!success] Internal Security Operation
Threat: Armed military unrest
Result: Resolved without lethal force»

[[Operation Quiet Barracks]] involved a serious armed disturbance among Hell Host personnel at a major military installation.

Command initially considered deploying Doom Corps security forces.

Spectre requested control of the situation instead.

Rather than immediately assaulting the occupied area, Spectre:

- Isolated communications
- Cut weapon access
- Negotiated with ringleaders
- Identified uninvolved personnel
- Used non-lethal teams to separate hostile groups

The incident was resolved without a major firefight.

Most personnel surrendered.

The operation became an important case study demonstrating why Spectre exists separately from conventional combat divisions.

Not every military problem needs to become a battle.

Insignia

«A skull beneath a vertical blade, framed by angular gold wings.»

- Authority

- Military order

Division Culture

Spectre culture values:

- Discipline
- Patience
- Procedure
- Evidence
- Professional distance
- Accountability

Spectre personnel are often stereotyped as humourless.

This is not entirely true.

Their humour is simply frequently inappropriate for public consumption.

Investigators and prison personnel in particular develop extremely dry senses of humour after years of exposure to military stupidity.

---

The Law and the Soldier

Spectre doctrine does not assume Hell Host personnel are criminals.

It assumes large militaries require accountability.

The Division exists because:

- Soldiers make mistakes
- Officers can abuse power
- Security can fail
- Enemies infiltrate
- Secrets are stolen
- Loyalty can break

The Hell Hosts cannot defend the galaxy if they cannot control themselves.

Spectre exists to make sure they do.

«“We Are the Law.”»

Its operational doctrine is frequently summarised as:

«“Order. Control. Compliance.”»
