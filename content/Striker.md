# Striker

> "I don't need glory. I need proof."

## Overview

**Rowan Vandrix**, known by the callsign **Striker**, is a young Hell Host soldier serving within the [[Doom Corps]].

A member of the well-known [[House Vandrix]], Rowan grew up surrounded by relatives who had already established formidable reputations within the Hell Hosts.

Rather than relying upon his family name, Rowan has become determined to build a reputation entirely his own.

He enlisted in the Doom Corps at eighteen and quickly demonstrated considerable talent as an aggressive assault soldier.

His combat ability is undeniable.

His patience is less reliable.

At only twenty-one, Striker remains relatively early in his career, but his potential has already attracted considerable attention from his superiors.

## Appearance

Rowan is a young human male with dark hair, an athletic build, and the heavily worn appearance common among active Doom Corps personnel.

He typically wears black Hell Host combat armour marked with red identification details and Vandrix family insignia.

Unlike more ceremonial members of House Vandrix, Striker's equipment is primarily practical.

His armour frequently shows evidence of field use, including scoring, impact marks, ash, and repaired damage.

Rowan appears to take considerably more pride in equipment that has survived combat than equipment that looks untouched.

---

## Personality

Striker is quiet, intense, competitive, and emotionally guarded.

He rarely discusses personal doubts openly and strongly dislikes appearing weak in front of other soldiers.

Much of this behaviour comes from the expectations surrounding the Vandrix name.

Rowan has spent most of his life surrounded by siblings whose military reputations developed before his own.

This created a powerful need to prove that he belongs among them.

Striker is aggressive during operations and rarely backs away from dangerous assignments.

However, this aggression should not be mistaken for poor combat discipline.

Once fighting begins, Rowan becomes focused and controlled.

His primary weaknesses appear before and after combat rather than during it.

He volunteers too quickly.

Pushes himself unnecessarily.

Takes criticism personally.

And sometimes treats caution as evidence that he has not yet proven himself.

---

# House Vandrix

Rowan belongs to [[House Vandrix]], a family with multiple members serving within the Hell Hosts.

The Vandrix name carries considerable military recognition.

Its traditional motto is:

> **"Victory is earned. Respect is taken."**

Growing up beneath that reputation heavily influenced Rowan.

He does not dislike his family name.

He dislikes the possibility that others may believe it is the only reason he succeeded.

Because of this, Rowan deliberately avoids requesting preferential treatment and reacts poorly when others imply that his career has been assisted by his relatives.

His goal is simple.

When somebody hears the name **Striker**, he wants them to think of Rowan before they think of Vandrix.

# Doom Corps

Rowan enlisted in the [[Doom Corps]] at eighteen.

He deliberately chose a demanding combat branch rather than pursuing a safer position through family influence.

The decision was partly ideological.

It was also personal.

Rowan wanted an environment where performance mattered more than surname.

His early career quickly established him as a capable assault soldier.

He became known for volunteering for dangerous operations and pushing aggressively through hostile positions.

His superiors have repeatedly noted that his potential is substantial.

They have also repeatedly reminded him that potential means very little if impatience gets him killed.

---

# Combat Style

Striker specializes in aggressive infantry combat.

He performs best during:

- Assault operations
- Urban combat
- Rapid advances
- Defensive breakthroughs
- Close-to-medium-range engagements
- High-pressure infantry actions

Rowan prefers maintaining momentum once an engagement begins.

He dislikes allowing an enemy time to reorganize.

His aggression is generally controlled through strong Doom Corps training, but situations involving personal stakes can make him more reckless.

---

# Loadout

## 94-CAR Assault Rifle

Striker's primary weapon is the **94-CAR Assault Rifle**.

The weapon is a reliable Hell Host service rifle designed for sustained combat across multiple engagement ranges.

Rowan prefers it because of its versatility and durability.

He has resisted suggestions to replace it with more specialized weaponry.

---

## P2 Particle Systematic Pistol

The **P2 PSP** serves as Striker's standard sidearm.

It is commonly carried by Doom Corps personnel and provides a reliable secondary weapon during close-range engagements.

---

## Combat Knife

Rowan carries a Vandrix-issued combat knife for close-quarters fighting, utility work, and emergency use.

Unlike his firearms, the knife carries greater personal significance because of its association with House Vandrix.

---

# Need to Prove Himself

Striker's defining internal conflict is not cowardice.

It is insecurity disguised as ambition.

Rowan knows he is talented.

What he does not yet know is whether he would have been noticed without the Vandrix name.

This uncertainty drives much of his behaviour.

He accepts dangerous assignments because success provides evidence.

He trains constantly because improvement provides evidence.

He rejects praise he believes comes too easily because it does not feel earned.

For Rowan, military achievement is not primarily about fame.

It is about proof.

Proof that he belongs.

Proof that he earned his position.

Proof that **Striker** is more than another Vandrix callsign.

Rowan excels during aggressive infantry operations.

## Discipline

Despite his personality, he becomes highly focused once combat begins.

Striker is extremely difficult to discourage once committed to an objective.

## Loyalty

He forms trust slowly but is fiercely loyal once it has been earned.

---

# Weaknesses

## Impatience

Rowan frequently wants results faster than experience allows.

## Pride

Criticism can become personal when it touches on his family reputation.

## Overcommitment

He has a tendency to volunteer for dangerous assignments unnecessarily.

## Emotional Guarding

Rowan rarely admits when pressure is affecting him.

This makes it difficult for others to intervene before problems worsen.

---

# Reputation

Within the Doom Corps, Striker is increasingly viewed as a soldier worth watching.

He has not yet achieved the reputation of older Hell Host figures.

That is precisely the point.

His career is still being written.

Some personnel see him as another talented Vandrix.

Others have begun viewing him as Striker first.

Rowan considers the second group evidence that he is making progress.
