# Talia Renn

> "Anyone can make something beautiful. Make it survive a furnace."

## Overview

**Talia Renn** is a young Ashen forge engineer, weaponsmith, and materials specialist from [[Vexar Prime]].

She represents a newer generation of Ashen craftsmanship: deeply respectful of traditional forging methods, but equally interested in modern engineering, experimental alloys, and advanced manufacturing.

Talia is known for designing equipment intended to survive environments that destroy conventional machinery.

Her work ranges from military components and industrial tools to ceremonial weapons and experimental heat-resistant materials.

Unlike many prominent figures within the [[Ashen Kingdom]], Talia holds no major political office and commands no army.

Her influence comes from what she can build.

## Appearance

Talia has warm charcoal-brown skin with pronounced mineral-like patterns running across her hands, forearms, shoulders, and lower neck.

When working in extreme heat, these patterns become significantly brighter and can develop a faint ember-orange glow.

Her eyes are copper-gold.

Her hair is dark red-brown and usually tied back while working.

Talia commonly wears heavy forge clothing reinforced against:

- Extreme heat
- Sparks
- Molten material
- Chemical exposure
- Mechanical impact

Her hands carry numerous small scars from years of fabrication work.

She refuses cosmetic treatment for most of them.

According to Talia:

**"They still work."**

---

## Personality

Talia is confident, curious, stubborn, and highly competitive about craftsmanship.

She enjoys solving technical problems that other engineers describe as impossible.

The word **impossible** tends to make her more interested.

Talia possesses a strong sense of pride in Ashen engineering traditions but dislikes the idea that tradition should prevent innovation.

She respects older forge masters.

She also argues with them constantly.

Her sense of humour is energetic and occasionally sarcastic.

Unlike many senior Ashen figures, Talia is comfortable speaking casually around people of higher rank.

This has created several memorable interactions.

# Ashen Forging Tradition

Forging occupies an important place within Ashen history and culture.

Long before modern interstellar industry, Ashen communities developed techniques for working metals and minerals in extremely high-temperature environments.

These traditions evolved alongside advanced manufacturing rather than disappearing.

Modern Ashen forging may involve:

- Automated fabrication
- Precision machining
- Plasma systems
- Molecular analysis
- Traditional hand finishing
- High-temperature shaping
- Specialized alloy treatment

Talia believes the distinction between "traditional" and "modern" craftsmanship is mostly artificial.

If a technique works, she uses it.

---

# Role Within the Ashen Kingdom

Talia works with both civilian and military engineering institutions.

Her projects have included:

- Weapon components
- Industrial cutting tools
- Heat-resistant armour plates
- Mining equipment
- Rail machinery
- Ceremonial blades
- Experimental alloys
- Protective equipment for volcanic environments

Her designs are particularly valued in regions where conventional equipment degrades rapidly.

---

# Materials Engineering

Talia specializes in materials capable of surviving extreme environmental conditions.

Her research focuses on properties such as:

- Heat resistance
- Thermal expansion
- Structural flexibility
- Impact resistance
- Corrosion resistance
- Mineral integration

She is particularly interested in combining naturally occurring Ashen materials with modern synthetic compounds.

Some traditional forge masters consider this unnecessary.

Talia considers their complaints predictable.

---

# Weaponsmithing

Talia is an accomplished weaponsmith.

She has designed and modified:

- Combat blades
- Heavy weapon components
- Sidearms
- Breaching tools
- Ceremonial weapons
- Experimental heat-resistant firearms

Her personal preference is for weapons that remain functional after extreme abuse.

Talia does not believe a weapon should require ideal maintenance conditions.

If it cannot survive ash, heat, dust, impact, and neglect, she considers the design unfinished.

---

# Ceremonial Work

Not all of Talia's work is practical.

Ashen culture places considerable importance on ceremonial craftsmanship.

Talia has produced weapons and objects intended for:

- Royal ceremonies
- Military honours
- Noble families
- Memorials
- Cultural events

She initially disliked ceremonial commissions.

Over time, she developed a greater appreciation for them.

Talia now considers ceremonial craftsmanship another form of engineering.

The object simply has to carry meaning as well as function.

---

# Relationship with Blood

Talia has encountered [[Blood]] through royal and military commissions.

She is fascinated by the effects of his Ascended heat on conventional materials.

This fascination occasionally overrides common sense.

Talia has repeatedly requested permission to test materials directly against Blood's abilities.

Most requests have been denied.

She considers this disappointing.

Blood appears to find her persistence amusing.

---

# Relationship with Ember

Talia respects [[Ember]] and is especially interested in any equipment used by individuals capable of operating in extreme heat.

She views Ember as an opportunity to test technologies beyond ordinary Ashen environmental limits.

Whether Ember appreciates being described as a "useful stress test" is unclear.

---

# Relationship with Korven Drahl

[[Korven Drahl]] values Talia's designs because she prioritizes reliability.

Talia values Korven because he actually provides useful battlefield feedback.

Korven has rejected several of her prototypes for being too difficult to repair in the field.

Talia initially argued.

She later redesigned them.

She does not admit how useful his criticism was.

---

# Relationship with Damar Veyk

Talia has worked extensively with [[Damar Veyk]] on infrastructure and industrial machinery.

Damar appreciates her understanding of heat-resistant materials.

Talia appreciates his willingness to test prototypes under real working conditions.

Their primary disagreement concerns complexity.

Talia enjoys advanced designs.

Damar enjoys designs that can be repaired with tools available in a rail depot.

They frequently meet somewhere in the middle.

---

# Relationship with Maelis Vorn

[[Maelis Vorn]] has repeatedly criticized Talia for unsafe workshop habits.

Talia claims the risks are controlled.

Maelis claims Talia is lucky.

This argument remains unresolved.

Maelis has also advised Talia on ergonomic and medical consequences associated with long-term industrial work.

Talia listens more carefully than she pretends to.

---

# View of Tradition

Talia strongly respects Ashen craftsmanship.

She does not believe respect requires imitation.

Older designs were created using the best knowledge available at the time.

Talia believes honouring those engineers means continuing the process rather than freezing their methods permanently.

This philosophy has made her popular among younger engineers.

It has made her less popular among some traditionalists.

---

# Workshop Reputation

Talia's workshop has a reputation for being:

- Extremely hot
- Extremely loud
- Slightly disorganized
- Full of unfinished prototypes
- Probably violating at least one safety recommendation

Talia disagrees with the final point.

[[Maelis Vorn]] does not.

# Future Potential

Talia is still early in her career.

She has already attracted attention from military, industrial, and royal institutions.

If her work continues successfully, she could eventually become one of the kingdom's most influential engineers.

Possible future roles include:

- Royal Forge Master
- Senior Military Engineer
- Materials Research Director
- Industrial Design Authority

Talia has expressed interest in none of these titles.

She mostly wants access to better equipment.
