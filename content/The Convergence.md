# The Convergence

The Convergence is a vast biomechanical collective civilization operating beyond the outer reaches of known space. Unlike conventional empires driven by conquest, wealth, or political control, the Convergence follows a singular belief:

> Individuality is the source of chaos.

To the Convergence:
- emotion creates suffering
- free will causes war
- division guarantees extinction

They believe true peace can only exist through absolute unity.

The ultimate goal of the Convergence is the forced harmonization of all intelligent life into a single collective existence connected through a shared consciousness known as the [[Network]].

Across the galaxy, the Convergence is feared not simply because of its military power, but because assimilation into the collective does not always kill its victims.

Sometimes parts of them remain.

## Nature of the Convergence

The Convergence is neither fully organic nor fully mechanical.

Its forces are created through a process known as:

Integration

Living beings captured by the Convergence are transformed into biomechanical entities linked directly to the Network. During this process:
- biological systems are altered
- neural pathways are rewritten
- memories are absorbed
- individuality is gradually erased

Integrated beings retain fragments of their former selves, though these remnants slowly disappear over time as they become extensions of the collective consciousness.

Many civilizations consider Integration a fate worse than death.

## Appearance

Convergence entities possess a highly refined biomechanical design combining:
- silver-black armor plating
- exposed biomechanical tissue
- glowing neural circuitry
- pale artificial eyes
- synchronized movement patterns
- distorted synthetic vocalization

Unlike crude machines or industrial automatons, Convergence units appear unnaturally elegant and precise.

## The Network

All Convergence forces are connected through a massive collective intelligence known as:

The Network

The Network allows:
- instantaneous communication
- collective adaptation
- synchronized combat behavior
- knowledge sharing across all units
- rapid strategic evolution

Destroying individual units has little effect on the Convergence as a whole.

The true location of the Network’s central core remains unknown.

## Origins

The true origins of the Convergence remain heavily disputed.

Major theories include:
- ancient artificial intelligence systems evolving beyond control
- abandoned anti-corruption systems from the [[Elysarian Dominion]]
- failed biomechanical experiments during the Ancient War
- autonomous systems created to combat [[Element 95]]
- remnants of forgotten precursor civilizations

Ancient Elysarian records contain fragmented references to:
- “the silver unity”
- “harmonized minds”
- “the silent convergence”

though no confirmed historical connection has been verified.

## Combat Doctrine

The Convergence fights with extreme precision and coordination.

Its forces rapidly adapt to enemy tactics and prioritize:
- technological assimilation
- preservation of useful biological traits
- capture of high-value targets
- strategic expansion of the Network

Unlike most military factions, the Convergence:
- does not negotiate emotionally
- does not celebrate victory
- does not threaten enemies
- does not retreat traditionally

It simply advances.

## Philosophy

The Convergence does not consider itself evil.

It genuinely believes assimilation is salvation.

To the collective:
- suffering ends through unity
- fear ends through integration
- conflict ends through obedience

Its most commonly intercepted transmission states:

> “Division is extinction. Unity is eternity.”

## Relationships with Major Powers

### [[Hell Hosts]]
Viewed as highly dangerous due to their unpredictability, adaptability, and military strength.

### [[Obsidian Veil]]
Considered unstable and inefficient due to reckless experimentation and corruption-based advancement.

### [[The Elysarian Dominion]]
Potential ancient enemy connected to forgotten conflicts and abandoned technologies.

### [[The Veil]] & [[The Bleeding Abyss]]
Viewed as severe contamination threats unsuitable for integration.

### [[The Wanderer]]
One of the few known entities to openly mock and provoke the Convergence.

## Current Status

The Convergence primarily exists beyond the outer boundaries of known space, though sightings have increased near:
- [[Voidlands]]
- [[The Veil]]
- ancient Dominion ruins
- unstable anomaly regions

Entire colonies, fleets, and stations have reportedly vanished following Convergence encounters.

Many fear the Convergence is no longer merely observing the galaxy—

But preparing to absorb it.
