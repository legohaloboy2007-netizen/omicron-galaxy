# Crimson Order

The Crimson Order is an ancient and highly mysterious organization surrounded by myth, fear, and fragmented historical records throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]]. To most civilizations, the Order exists only as folklore — a whispered legend associated with impossible warriors, forgotten rituals, and unexplained massacres across the galaxy’s history.

Very little verified information exists regarding the Crimson Order’s origins. Ancient records suggest the organization predates many modern galactic powers, with some theories claiming the Order existed long before the rise of the [[Hell Hosts]], the [[Angel Swords]], or even the early colonial systems.

Unlike conventional factions, the Crimson Order operates almost entirely in secrecy. Its members rarely appear openly, and sightings are often followed by disappearances, destroyed settlements, or heavily classified reports. Because of this, many civilians believe the Order is either extinct or never truly existed at all.

The organization is believed to possess knowledge of ancient cosmic forces, forbidden technologies, and early Ascension-related phenomena. Numerous legends describe Crimson Order members as warriors capable of unnatural speed, strength, and near-impossible combat abilities.

Throughout frontier worlds and isolated colonies, stories of the Crimson Order are commonly used as warnings or myths. Some believe the Order protects humanity from hidden cosmic threats, while others view them as ruthless executioners operating beyond morality.

The Crimson Order later re-emerged during the era of galactic collapse, becoming increasingly associated with events surrounding [[The Bleeding Abyss]], ancient anomalies, and the return of forgotten powers.

## Known For
- Ancient and highly secretive origins
- Mythological status across the galaxy
- Unexplained disappearances and massacres
- Exceptional combat capabilities
- Knowledge of forbidden cosmic phenomena
- Connections to Ascension-related events
- Reappearance during major galactic crises

## Common Beliefs and Rumors
- The Order predates modern galactic civilization
- Members undergo unknown rituals or ascensions
- The organization possesses hidden archives and relics
- Crimson Order operatives cannot truly die
- The Order has fought hidden wars throughout history

## Activities
- Elimination of high-value targets
- Recovery of ancient artifacts
- Containment of forbidden entities
- Operations involving cosmic anomalies
- Intervention during extinction-level threats
