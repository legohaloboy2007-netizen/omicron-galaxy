# The Forge

The Forge is an ancient and colossal megastructure discovered during deep-space expeditions beyond the known regions of the [[Omicron Galaxy]]. Considered one of the most important discoveries in modern galactic history, the Forge contains technologies, power systems, and engineering capabilities far beyond the understanding of contemporary civilization.

Very little is known about the true origins of the Forge. No confirmed records exist identifying its creators, purpose, or age, though many researchers believe the structure predates modern galactic civilization by millions — potentially billions — of years. Its design and internal systems appear fundamentally incompatible with conventional physics and engineering principles.

The Forge is not simply a factory or military installation. Instead, it functions as a massive self-sustaining construct capable of generating advanced materials, energy systems, weapons, and technologies previously believed impossible. Entire sections of the structure remain dormant, inaccessible, or incomprehensible even to the galaxy’s greatest scientists.

The discovery of the Forge permanently changed the technological development of the galaxy. Following initial exploration efforts, numerous factions — particularly the [[Hell Hosts]] — began reverse-engineering Forge technology to dramatically expand military production, ship engineering, energy weapon systems, and advanced material sciences.

The spread of Forge-derived technology accelerated the militarization of the galaxy and marked the beginning of a new era of power escalation. Many historians consider the discovery of the Forge to be one of the primary reasons later conflicts evolved beyond conventional warfare into reality-altering cosmic conflict.

The Forge is also heavily associated with dimensional instability, impossible energy signatures, and anomalous spatial phenomena. Some theories suggest the structure may possess connections to the [[Voidlands]], precursor civilizations, or realities beyond the known universe itself.

Despite decades of exploration, vast regions of the Forge remain unexplored, sealed, or actively hostile toward intruders.

- Modern civilizations were never meant to access its technology
