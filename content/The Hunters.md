# The Hunters

The Hunters are a highly dangerous and enigmatic faction operating throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]], feared for their relentless pursuit of powerful targets, anomalous entities, and ancient cosmic artifacts. Unlike conventional military organizations, the Hunters are not bound by territory, ideology, or political allegiance. Their only true loyalty is to the hunt itself.

Very little is known about the origins of the Hunters. Records describing their existence date back centuries, often appearing during periods of major galactic instability, extinction-level events, or large-scale anomaly outbreaks. Because of this, many civilizations consider the Hunters to be more myth than reality.

Hunter operatives are known for their extreme combat skill, advanced equipment, and ability to survive environments considered impossible for normal humans. Reports suggest they possess technology and weapons far beyond modern galactic standards, leading to theories that the faction has access to ancient or cross-dimensional knowledge.

Unlike the secretive manipulation of the [[Obsidian Veil]] or the military domination of the [[Hell Hosts]], the Hunters function more like apex predators within the galaxy. They appear without warning, eliminate their targets with overwhelming efficiency, and disappear just as quickly.

Throughout history, the Hunters have been linked to operations involving the [[Farlands]], [[Voidlands]], [[The Veil]], and ancient Ascension-related phenomena. Some theories even suggest the Hunters possess knowledge of realities beyond the [[Omicron Galaxy]] itself.

The return of the Hunters during the era of galactic collapse marked the beginning of several catastrophic conflicts and cosmic-scale events.

## Known For
- Hunting high-value targets and anomalies
- Advanced unknown technology
- Exceptional combat skill and survivability
- Operations across hostile cosmic regions
- Ancient and mysterious origins
- Cross-dimensional rumors and sightings
- Appearing during major galactic crises

## Common Theories
- The Hunters are older than modern civilization
- Their technology originates outside the galaxy
- Hunter operatives are heavily augmented or ascended
- The faction operates across multiple realities
- They answer to an unknown higher authority

## Activities
- Elimination of powerful entities
- Artifact retrieval operations
- Anomaly hunting
- Cosmic region expeditions
- Assassination and containment missions
