# Reapers Association

The Reapers Association is a highly feared contract organization operating throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]], specializing in assassination, infiltration, sabotage, intelligence gathering, and high-risk black operations. Despite their enormous reputation across the galaxy, the organization maintains an extremely small membership, consisting of only twelve known operatives at any given time.

Each Reaper is considered exceptionally dangerous, often possessing years of combat experience, specialized training, and heavily customized equipment. Unlike conventional mercenary groups, the Reapers Association values precision, professionalism, and secrecy above all else.

The organization operates under a strict internal code, with contracts treated as absolute obligations once accepted. Because of this, the Reapers have developed a reputation for reliability and efficiency unmatched by most criminal or military groups within known space.

During the war between the [[Terrorism Control Agency]] and the [[Free Order Rebellion]], the Reapers Association notoriously operated on both sides of the conflict simultaneously, accepting contracts from TCA commanders, FOR leaders, corporations, and independent clients alike. Their actions during the war contributed heavily to the chaos and instability surrounding the conflict.

Following the formation and rise of the [[Hell Hosts]], the Reapers Association gradually became more closely aligned with the faction, eventually serving as one of its primary black operations and contract specialist groups. Despite this relationship, the Association still retains a level of independence and secrecy separate from standard Hell Host military command.

The identities of most Reaper operatives remain unknown to the public, with many becoming legendary figures throughout criminal networks, military circles, and frontier worlds.
