# The Red Hand

## Overview

**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]

**Status:** Active

**Threat Level:** Severe

**Primary Roles:**
- Coercion
- Suppression
- Decisive Strike Operations
- Counter-Insurgency
- System Elimination

The Red Hand is an elite three-member operational cell deployed when diplomacy has failed, resistance has spread beyond containment, or command requires absolute compliance.

Unlike:
[[Wolf Unit]]

which specializes in precision tracking and targeted eliminations, the Red Hand exists to dismantle entire systems.

Where the Wolves remove individuals quietly,

the Red Hand breaks organizations loudly.

Their deployments are often associated with:

- Collapsing insurgencies
- Destroyed command structures
- Public military defeats
- Forced compliance operations
- High-profile suppression campaigns

When the Red Hand arrives, negotiations are usually already over.

---

## Purpose

The Red Hand serves as one of the Hell Hosts' most aggressive special operations units.

Their assignments often involve situations where conventional military action has proven ineffective or politically impossible.

Typical missions include:

- Eliminating insurgent leadership
- Crushing resistance movements
- Capturing strategic targets
- Interrogation operations
- Counter-command warfare
- Shock deployments

The unit is designed to create rapid collapse within hostile organizations.

Rather than fighting armies directly, they attack:

- Leadership
- Morale
- Logistics
- Communication
- Coordination

Until resistance can no longer function.

---

## Reputation

The Red Hand possesses one of the most feared reputations in the galaxy.

Among enemy forces, their appearance is often viewed as confirmation that a conflict has entered its final stage.

Military intelligence reports frequently associate the unit with:

- Sudden command collapses
- Missing officers
- Internal panic
- Rapid territorial loss
- Psychological breakdowns within enemy leadership

A common saying among enemy soldiers states:

> "If the Wolves are hunting you, run.
>
> If the Red Hand is coming, it's already too late."

---

## Operational Doctrine

Each member fulfills a distinct purpose.

Together they create a highly effective cycle of escalation, control, and elimination.

### [[Insurgent]]

The Fire.

The Escalation.

The Pressure.

Insurgent serves as the acting field leader and primary force behind Red Hand operations.

He specializes in creating collapse through fear, violence, and overwhelming pressure.

---

### [[Hatchet]]

The Control.

The Stability.

The Anchor.

Hatchet ensures operations remain focused and effective.

Where others lose control, Hatchet remains steady.

---

### [[Karma]]

The Consequence.

The Execution.

The End.

Karma serves as the unit's final measure.

When deployed, she ensures targets do not recover.

---

## Unit Dynamic

Personnel have summarized the Red Hand using a single phrase:

> Insurgent starts the fire.
>
> Hatchet keeps it controlled.
>
> Karma ends it.

This dynamic has become synonymous with the unit itself.

---

## Internal Structure

Although all three members operate as equals in many situations, their responsibilities naturally differ.

### Insurgent Leads

Creates pressure.

Breaks resistance.

Forces action.

---

### Hatchet Stabilizes

Maintains discipline.

Controls escalation.

Ensures objectives are achieved.

---

### Karma Finishes

Removes remaining threats.

Delivers decisive outcomes.

Leaves no loose ends.

---

## Comparison To The Wolf Unit

While both groups operate within the:
[[Hell Hosts]]

their purposes differ significantly.

### Wolf Unit

- Precision
- Tracking
- Silent elimination
- Long-term hunts

---

### Red Hand

- Suppression
- Coercion
- Organizational collapse
- Direct action

A target hunted by the Wolves may never know they were being watched.

A target facing the Red Hand knows exactly what is happening.

The fear comes from realizing it cannot be stopped.

---

## Reputation Within Hell Hosts

The Red Hand is respected for its effectiveness and feared for its methods.

Many personnel acknowledge the necessity of the unit.

Few actively seek to work alongside them.

Even among Hell Host forces, the Red Hand occupies an uncomfortable position.

They perform the tasks others would rather avoid.
