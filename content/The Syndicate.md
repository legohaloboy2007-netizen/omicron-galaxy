# The Syndicate

**Classification:** Independent contract organisation  
**Status:** Active  
**Leadership:** Unknown  
**Headquarters:** Unknown  
**Territory:** None known  
**Estimated Strength:** Unknown  
**Primary Activities:** Contract operations, assassination, retrieval, sabotage, infiltration, high-risk operations  

The Syndicate is a secretive independent organisation operating throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]], known for accepting dangerous contracts on behalf of governments, corporations, military factions, criminal organisations, and private clients.

Unlike conventional mercenary groups, the Syndicate possesses no publicly known leadership, headquarters, territory, membership registry, or organisational structure.

The number of individuals operating within the Syndicate is unknown.

This uncertainty is deliberate.

Every known Syndicate operative wears effectively identical armour, uses the same artificially produced voice, and refuses to reveal any identifying information. Operatives are typically deployed alone, with multiple Syndicate personnel appearing together only when a contract is considered exceptionally dangerous or complex.

To most clients, there is no individual behind the armour.

There is only **the Syndicate**.

## Identity

The identities of Syndicate operatives are among the organisation's most closely protected secrets.

Members do not publicly use names, ranks, callsigns, or personal designations.

Their armour is intentionally standardised and possesses no visible rank insignia, unit markings, personal decoration, or identifying symbols beyond those associated with the Syndicate itself.

Their voices are similarly concealed.

Every known operative communicates using the same synthetic voice profile, making identification through speech effectively impossible.

As a result, witnesses encountering separate Syndicate operatives frequently believe they have encountered the same individual.

This has made determining the organisation's true size extremely difficult.

Estimates range from a small group of highly trained specialists to a considerably larger organisation operating throughout the galaxy.

No estimate has been verified.

## Physical Differences

Despite efforts to maintain a uniform identity, Syndicate operatives are not physically identical.

Height remains the most obvious difference between members.

Minor differences in movement, posture, handedness, weapon preference, and repeated behavioural habits have also been documented.

Experienced individuals who have encountered the Syndicate multiple times have occasionally claimed to recognise specific operatives based entirely upon these habits.

The Syndicate itself never acknowledges such distinctions.

Whether operatives possess internal names or designations is unknown.

## Contracts

The Syndicate operates almost exclusively through contracts.

Who is permitted to contact the organisation, how contracts are submitted, and how clients are selected are poorly understood.

The Syndicate has accepted work from organisations on opposing sides of major political and military conflicts, suggesting that it maintains little permanent allegiance to any particular government or faction.

Payment varies depending upon the contract.

Currency is accepted, but reports suggest that information, technology, rare materials, access to restricted locations, and other forms of compensation may also be requested.

The Syndicate appears willing to reject contracts without explanation.

Once a contract is accepted, however, its operatives display an extreme commitment to completing the agreed objective.

## Deployment

Most Syndicate contracts are assigned to a single operative.

One operative may be responsible for infiltrating a military installation, assassinating a protected target, retrieving an artefact, sabotaging infrastructure, or eliminating an entire hostile group.

The deployment of multiple operatives is considerably rarer.

Consequently, the appearance of two or more Syndicate members during the same operation is generally interpreted as evidence that the contract is exceptionally difficult, valuable, or important.

The largest number of Syndicate operatives ever deployed simultaneously is unknown.

## Combat Doctrine

Syndicate operatives are known for highly unconventional combat methods.

Rather than relying solely upon conventional firearms and military tactics, they frequently manipulate the surrounding environment itself as a weapon.

Industrial machinery, transportation networks, power systems, construction equipment, cargo infrastructure, automated systems, and explosive materials have all been repurposed during documented operations.

A Syndicate operative entering an industrial facility is therefore considered significantly more dangerous than an ordinary combatant entering the same environment.

Anything capable of moving, accelerating, detonating, collapsing, launching, crushing, or carrying significant energy may potentially become part of the operative's arsenal.

## Engineering

One of the defining characteristics of Syndicate personnel is their exceptional understanding of mechanical and electronic systems.

Operatives have demonstrated the ability to rapidly construct improvised traps, modify existing machinery, redirect automated systems, and convert civilian infrastructure into weapons.

Rail and cargo systems are particularly notorious.

Several incidents have involved explosive cargo or heavily loaded transport platforms being accelerated toward defensive positions at extreme speeds.

Other documented tactics include remotely triggered environmental traps, improvised launch systems, automated projectile mechanisms, manipulated doors and pressure systems, and deliberate structural collapse.

These techniques make Syndicate operatives particularly unpredictable in developed environments.

Destroying or confiscating an operative's conventional weapons does not necessarily render them unarmed.

## Weapons

Syndicate operatives carry equipment appropriate to individual contracts, although several weapon categories appear repeatedly.

Heavy close-quarters weapons are common, particularly weapons capable of exploiting momentum and kinetic force.

Explosives are also frequently employed, both conventionally and as components within improvised mechanisms.

Firearms vary considerably between operations.

Syndicate personnel have demonstrated proficiency with pistols, rifles, shotguns, precision weapons, explosives, melee weapons, and captured enemy equipment.

Operatives show little attachment to any particular weapon.

Their environment is frequently their most dangerous weapon.

## Armour

All known Syndicate operatives wear visually identical armour.

The armour completely conceals the wearer's identity and is designed to prevent conventional visual identification.

No personal modifications are permitted to remain visible during operations.

The armour also incorporates the voice modulation system responsible for producing the standardised Syndicate voice.

Whether every suit is genuinely identical internally is unknown.

Differences in equipment observed between operations suggest that individual suits may contain specialised systems while maintaining the same external appearance.

## Behaviour

Although the Syndicate attempts to erase individual identity, personality occasionally becomes visible through behaviour.

Some operatives favour direct confrontation.

Others demonstrate greater patience.

Some repeatedly inspect their weapons.

Others remain almost completely motionless while waiting.

Certain operatives favour particular methods of entry, specific types of traps, unusual combat movements, or recurring gestures.

These habits have become the primary method by which intelligence analysts attempt to distinguish individual Syndicate members.

This practice is highly unreliable.

The Syndicate has never confirmed whether analysts have successfully identified the same operative across multiple incidents.

## Organisation

Almost nothing is known about the Syndicate's internal structure.

It is unclear whether the organisation possesses a conventional commander, governing council, distributed leadership system, or another form of authority.

Likewise, there are no confirmed ranks.

The absence of visible hierarchy may itself be intentional.

From the perspective of outsiders, every operative represents the organisation equally.

Killing or capturing one member therefore provides little indication of the Syndicate's wider structure.

## Recruitment

No confirmed method of joining the Syndicate is publicly known.

There are no recruitment offices, military academies, public applications, or known initiation sites.

The backgrounds of individual operatives are equally uncertain.

Various theories suggest that Syndicate personnel may be recruited from special forces units, intelligence organisations, criminal groups, engineering professions, mercenary organisations, or other highly specialised backgrounds.

None have been confirmed.

## Reputation

The Syndicate has developed a significant reputation among military forces, criminals, mercenaries, corporations, and intelligence organisations.

Unlike [[Requiem]], the Syndicate's existence is not generally disputed.

People know they exist.

The mystery concerns who they are.

Their reputation is strengthened by the organisation's preference for deploying individual operatives. A single figure wearing Syndicate armour can therefore cause considerable alarm among personnel familiar with their operations.

The appearance of multiple operatives is considerably more concerning.

An informal saying among mercenaries claims:

**"One means a contract. Two means a problem."**

The origin of the phrase is unknown.

## Relationship with Other Factions

The Syndicate maintains no known permanent alliances.

Contracts may place its operatives alongside or against virtually any faction within the Omicron Galaxy.

The organisation's willingness to work for competing powers has made establishing long-term diplomatic relations with the Syndicate extremely difficult.

A faction employing a Syndicate operative during one conflict has no guarantee that another operative will not be contracted against them later.

The Syndicate appears to consider the completion of individual contracts more important than political loyalty.

## Rumours

The secrecy surrounding the Syndicate has produced numerous theories regarding its true nature.

Some believe the organisation contains only a handful of operatives whose identical appearance creates the illusion of a much larger force.

Others believe hundreds or thousands of Syndicate members operate throughout the galaxy.

Another theory proposes that the identical voice and armour exist specifically to prevent outsiders from realising how frequently individual members are killed and replaced.

More extreme theories suggest that the Syndicate deliberately encourages contradictory estimates of its membership.

No reliable count has ever been established.

It is entirely possible that nobody outside the Syndicate has ever known how many of them exist.
