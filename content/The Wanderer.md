# The Wanderer

The Wanderer is a mysterious god-like entity whose true origins, species, and age remain completely unknown. Unlike cosmic rulers such as [[The Witness]] or [[The Forsaken Monarch]], the Wanderer does not appear motivated by conquest, domination, or destruction.

Instead, it behaves as an unpredictable observer of galactic events, frequently appearing during moments of great significance for reasons known only to itself.

Some civilizations consider the Wanderer harmless.

Others believe it may be one of the most dangerous beings in existence.

No known faction has ever successfully explained its true motives.

## Appearance

The Wanderer most commonly appears in humanoid form, though many scholars and ancient records suggest this form is merely a projection chosen for the comfort of lesser beings.

Descriptions vary slightly between encounters, but most reports include:
- elegant or refined clothing
- calm body language
- glowing or unnatural eyes
- relaxed posture
- subtle distortions surrounding its presence
- unsettlingly casual behavior during catastrophic situations

Witnesses often report feeling strangely uncomfortable around the Wanderer even when it appears friendly.

Some survivors claim its appearance changes subtly whenever directly observed.

## Personality

The Wanderer is known for:
- cryptic humor
- philosophical conversation
- casual reality manipulation
- unpredictable appearances
- speaking as though future events are already known

It frequently treats wars, cosmic disasters, and political conflicts with strange amusement, often behaving with complete emotional detachment regardless of the situation.

Despite its occasionally comedic demeanor, interactions with the Wanderer often leave individuals deeply unsettled.

Many describe the experience as feeling:
- observed
- studied
- or subtly manipulated

The Wanderer rarely answers questions directly.

Instead, it often responds through:
- riddles
- vague warnings
- contradictions
- impossible statements
- references to events that have not yet occurred

## Known Abilities

The Wanderer appears capable of manipulating reality itself with little visible effort.

Reported abilities include:
- teleportation
- time distortion
- matter manipulation
- dimensional travel
- gravity alteration
- illusion creation
- localized stasis
- appearing in impossible locations
- observing or referencing future events

The full extent of its abilities remains unknown.

No confirmed limit to its power has ever been observed.

## Historical Presence

References resembling the Wanderer appear throughout history across multiple civilizations, including:
- ancient Elysarian archives
- fragmented Voidlands records
- lost anomaly reports
- early Dominion star charts

Some records suggest the Wanderer predates:
- [[The Elysarian Dominion]]
- the Ancient War against [[Element 95]]
- possibly even portions of modern reality itself

No civilization has successfully determined:
- where it came from
- what species it belongs to
- whether it truly exists physically
- or what it ultimately wants

## Relationships to Major Powers

### [[The Nameless God]]
The Wanderer appears unusually knowledgeable regarding the Shroud and the entity known as the Nameless God.

Some theories suggest it has encountered the Shroud entity directly.

### [[Queen Aeloria]]
Ancient Dominion records imply the Wanderer may have interacted with Aeloria personally before her disappearance.

The nature of their relationship remains unknown.

### [[The Witness]]
The Wanderer appears aware of the Witness and Veil consciousness, though it has never openly aligned itself with the Veil.

### [[The Founder]]
Some believe the Wanderer possesses knowledge regarding the Founder’s true nature and long-term goals.

### [[The Convergence]]
One of the few entities known to openly mock and provoke the Convergence collective.

## Encounters

The Wanderer has been known to:
- appear moments before catastrophic events
- warn individuals cryptically
- interfere unpredictably in galactic affairs
- observe major battles without participating
- disappear instantly without explanation

Even powerful anomalous beings appear cautious around it.

Many survivors claim the Wanderer behaves as though it already knows how events will unfold.

## Philosophy & Interpretation

No confirmed ideology or goal has ever been identified.

Major theories suggest:
- it is studying civilization
- it exists outside linear time
- it is observing the galaxy for unknown reasons
- it is waiting for a future event
- it understands truths reality itself cannot contain

Some believe the Wanderer is harmless.

Others fear it already knows how the story ends.

## Current Status

The Wanderer possesses:
- no known territory
- no military forces
- no political allegiance
- no confirmed species or civilization

It simply appears when and where it chooses.

Attempts to track, imprison, or study the Wanderer have universally failed.

Its current whereabouts are unknown.
