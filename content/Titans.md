# Titans

Titans are colossal strategic war machines operated by the [[Hell Hosts]], designed to fight across both planetary and orbital battlefields.

Among the largest mobile weapons ever fielded by Hell Host ground forces, Titans combine the characteristics of a combat walker, mobile fortress, and spacecraft into a single platform.

They are extraordinarily rare.

The immense cost, infrastructure, personnel, and resources required to construct and maintain a Titan prevent their deployment on anything approaching the scale of conventional armored vehicles. As a result, the appearance of even a single Titan on a battlefield represents a major escalation.

Despite their rarity, Titans have developed a fearsome reputation throughout the [[Omicron Galaxy]].

Destroying one is rarely a matter of simply possessing a sufficiently large weapon.

It is an operation.

---

# Design

Titans are enormous humanoid war machines towering over conventional vehicles and battlefield structures.

Although exact dimensions vary between individual designs, Titans can reach hundreds of metres in height.

Their bodies contain systems normally distributed across numerous military vehicles and installations, including:

- Heavy weapon batteries
- Point-defence systems
- Energy shielding
- Advanced targeting arrays
- Communications systems
- Multiple power systems
- Ammunition storage
- Engineering compartments
- Thruster assemblies
- Internal maintenance spaces
- Redundant control systems

Despite their humanoid appearance, Titans are not intended to replicate human movement perfectly.

Their shape exists primarily to provide extreme terrain mobility, wide weapon coverage, independent movement in zero gravity, and the ability to operate across environments that would restrict conventional warships or armored formations.

A Titan is effectively a mobile military installation capable of walking.

---

# Survivability

Titan survivability is based around several layers of protection rather than any single defensive system.

Their outer hulls consist of extremely heavy armor capable of surviving weapons that would destroy conventional armored vehicles.

Beneath this protection are compartmentalized internal systems designed to isolate catastrophic damage.

Titans also possess powerful energy shields and extensive point-defence networks capable of intercepting missiles, aircraft, drones, and other incoming threats.

Critical systems are heavily redundant.

A Titan may continue operating after suffering damage that would completely disable most military platforms.

Loss of individual weapons, sensor arrays, sections of armor, or even entire limbs does not necessarily result in destruction.

Because of this, attacks against Titans frequently focus on gradually disabling individual systems before attempting to destroy the central structure.

Confirmed Titan kills have historically required concentrated attacks involving combinations of heavy armor, artillery, aviation, orbital support, other Titans, or similarly powerful weapons.

Titans are extremely difficult to destroy.

They are not invulnerable.

# Ocular Lance

Perhaps the most recognizable feature of a Hell Host Titan is the enormous single optic located within its armored head.

The structure functions as part of the Titan's primary sensor array but also houses one of its most powerful weapons.

Officially designated the **Ocular Lance**, the weapon is more commonly referred to by Hell Host personnel as the **Eye Laser**.

The Ocular Lance concentrates an enormous amount of energy into a directed beam capable of devastating heavily armored targets.

Potential targets include:

- Armored vehicles
- Fortifications
- Enemy combat walkers
- Hardened military installations
- Shielded positions
- Spacecraft
- Sections of larger warships

Firing the weapon requires a significant charge period.

During this process, the Titan's optic becomes increasingly luminous while surrounding systems begin venting accumulated heat.

Power may also be temporarily diverted from other systems to support the weapon.

This creates one of the most recognizable warnings on a battlefield.

When a Titan's eye begins to glow, Hell Host forces are trained to clear its projected firing corridor immediately.

Enemy forces generally require little encouragement to do the same.

Repeated use of the Ocular Lance generates immense thermal and power-management demands, preventing the weapon from being fired continuously.

# Planetary Warfare

Titans are primarily strategic breakthrough and siege platforms.

They are deployed against threats or defensive positions considered beyond the practical capabilities of conventional armored formations.

Common Titan operations include:

- Breaking heavily fortified defensive lines
- Destroying major enemy fortifications
- Assaulting shielded installations
- Supporting large-scale planetary invasions
- Engaging enemy super-heavy vehicles
- Providing mobile heavy fire support
- Fighting other Titan-scale threats

Their enormous size allows Titans to cross many battlefield obstacles through brute force alone.

Smaller structures, defensive walls, trenches, and conventional battlefield obstacles may present little meaningful restriction to their movement.

However, Titans are not deployed without support.

Infantry, armored vehicles, aircraft, artillery, engineers, and air-defence systems commonly operate alongside them.

This prevents enemy forces from exploiting the Titan's size or overwhelming individual defensive systems through concentrated attacks.

---

# Orbital and Space Operations

Unlike conventional combat walkers, Hell Host Titans are capable of independent operation in space.

Large propulsion systems distributed throughout the body allow a Titan to maneuver in zero gravity, reposition between orbital locations, and participate directly in space combat.

Additional maneuvering thrusters positioned around the torso and limbs provide control over orientation.

Titans can also perform planetary deployment from orbit.

During atmospheric entry, their propulsion and stabilization systems control descent before the Titan transitions into conventional surface operation.

The sight of a Titan descending from orbit has become one of the most recognizable displays of Hell Host military power.

Despite their capabilities, Titans are not replacements for conventional warships.

Dedicated naval vessels possess greater strategic range, more efficient propulsion, larger weapon reserves, and superior capabilities during prolonged fleet engagements.

Titans instead occupy an unusual position between planetary and naval warfare.

They can engage smaller spacecraft directly, provide fire support during orbital engagements, and threaten larger vessels when circumstances allow.

Titans are particularly dangerous at extremely close range.

Their maneuverability in zero gravity and reinforced structure allow them to physically interact with hostile spacecraft, including attaching themselves to or landing upon sufficiently large vessels.

Few warship crews consider this a desirable development.

---

# Deployment

Titans possess sufficient propulsion to move between planetary surfaces and orbital space but are not normally expected to travel independently between star systems.

For interstellar deployments, Titans are transported by specialized Hell Host naval vessels and logistical formations.

A Titan deployment therefore requires substantial preparation.

Fuel, ammunition, replacement components, engineering equipment, specialized recovery systems, and enormous maintenance crews must accompany the machine.

This logistical burden is one of the primary reasons Titans remain so rare.

The Hell Hosts could construct enormous numbers of conventional vehicles and weapons for the resources required to build and maintain even a small Titan force.

Consequently, Titans are reserved for situations where conventional military power is considered insufficient.

# 950th Titan Walker Battalion

**Designation:** 950th Titan Walker Battalion  
**Nickname:** Mountain Breakers  
**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]  
**Primary Role:** Titan Warfare / Strategic Assault

The 950th Titan Walker Battalion, commonly known as the **Mountain Breakers**, is the principal Hell Host formation responsible for Titan operations.

Despite its designation as a battalion, the majority of its personnel do not directly operate Titans.

The formation contains the enormous support structure required to maintain them, including:

- Titan crews
- Engineers
- Reactor specialists
- Weapons technicians
- Logistics personnel
- Recovery teams
- Ground security
- Battlefield coordinators
- Transport personnel
- Maintenance crews

Only a relatively small number of Titans may participate in an individual operation.

Even a single deployment can require thousands of personnel working behind the battlefield to maintain combat readiness.

The Mountain Breakers have historically been deployed against some of the most heavily fortified enemy positions encountered by the Hell Hosts, including major defensive complexes operated by the [[Free World Order]].

Their nickname originates from their role as fortress breakers.

Whether any member of the battalion has literally broken a mountain remains a matter of soldier debate.

# Strategic Limitations

The extraordinary combat capability of Titans comes with equally extraordinary limitations.

They are expensive to manufacture, difficult to transport, demanding to maintain, and impossible to conceal once deployed.

Their enormous profiles make them obvious targets for artillery, aviation, orbital weapons, and enemy fleets.

Damage that would be considered routine on a conventional vehicle can require enormous industrial resources to repair when inflicted upon a Titan.

A destroyed Titan represents not only the loss of a strategic weapon but potentially years of construction, specialized components, and experienced personnel.

Hell Host commanders therefore do not deploy Titans simply because they are available.

Their appearance usually indicates that conventional methods have failed, the enemy possesses comparable strategic weapons, or the objective is considered important enough to justify the risk.

---

# Reputation

To Hell Host soldiers, Titans represent overwhelming reinforcement.

To hostile forces, their arrival can fundamentally alter the nature of an engagement.

Their immense silhouettes can be visible from extraordinary distances, while the movement of a Titan across a battlefield can be felt through the ground long before it reaches nearby positions.

The distinctive glow of an Ocular Lance charging has become particularly infamous among forces that have encountered them.

Despite their reputation, Titans are neither supernatural nor indestructible.

They are machines.

Extremely large, heavily armed, spacefaring machines that are exceptionally difficult to kill.

But machines nonetheless.
