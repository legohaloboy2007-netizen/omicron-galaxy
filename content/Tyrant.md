# Tyrant

> *"You don't have to like the man giving the order. You just have to decide whether the order is worth following."*

## Profile

**Real Name:** Thomas Powell  
**Callsign:** Tyrant  
**Known As:** Hell Host Tyrant  
**Age:** Unknown  
**Gender:** Male  
**Species:** Human  
**Place of Birth:** [[Earth]]  
**Nationality:** South African  
**Status:** Active  
**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]  
**Branch:** [[Doom Corps]]  
**Formation:** 43rd Heavy Assault Battalion  
**Rank:** Battalion Commander  
**Role:** Heavy Assault / Battlefield Command  
**Height:** 6'0" / 183 cm  
**Known Ability:** Active Camouflage  
**Primary Weapons:** [[A12 Heavy Shotgun]] / [[71 HC MG]]  
**Sidearm:** [[P2 Particle Service Pistol]]  
**Additional Equipment:** Combat Knife  

Thomas Powell, better known as **Tyrant**, is a Hell Host Doom Corps commander and commanding officer of the **43rd Heavy Assault Battalion**.

Tyrant is known for aggressive battlefield leadership, extensive experience with heavy infantry operations, and a complicated history with the faction he now serves.

Before becoming a respected Hell Host commander, Powell spent part of his life actively opposed to Hell Host interests.

For a period, his hostility extended directly toward [[Predator]].

Today, Tyrant commands thousands of personnel in Predator's military.

Neither man has forgotten the irony.

---

# Early Life

Thomas Powell was born on [[Earth]].

Much of his early civilian history remains undocumented.

Powell is believed to be South African and retains a distinctive South African accent.

He entered military service before his association with the [[Hell Hosts]], developing considerable experience with infantry weapons, small-unit tactics, and battlefield command.

Even before becoming Tyrant, Powell demonstrated the traits that would later define his command style.

He was aggressive without being reckless.

Blunt without being careless.

And exceptionally difficult to intimidate.

These qualities eventually placed him in direct opposition to the Hell Hosts.

---

# Conflict with the Hell Hosts

Before joining the faction, Powell served alongside a military force whose objectives eventually came into conflict with Hell Host operations.

The resulting confrontation ended catastrophically for Powell's side.

His unit suffered severe losses.

Powell survived.

In the aftermath, he placed much of the responsibility upon [[Predator]].

Predator had been involved in the decisions that resulted in Powell's force being defeated, and Powell viewed those decisions not as distant battlefield calculations but as the reason people under his command had died.

His resentment became deeply personal.

For a period, Thomas Powell genuinely hated Predator.

Some accounts suggest that his anger became severe enough that Powell contemplated killing him.

Whether he ever made a serious attempt remains disputed.

What is certain is that the two were not always allies.

---

# Reluctant Alliance

Powell's relationship with the Hell Hosts began changing when circumstances forced him to fight alongside them.

Faced with a threat neither side could effectively confront alone, Powell temporarily cooperated with Hell Host forces.

The alliance was intended to be practical.

It became something more permanent.

During the operation, Powell demonstrated exceptional battlefield leadership and repeatedly coordinated successfully with Hell Host units despite his personal hostility toward their command.

Predator noticed.

More importantly, Powell began seeing the Hell Hosts from inside their own lines rather than exclusively through the consequences of fighting against them.

His opinion did not change overnight.

He still disagreed with decisions Predator had made.

He still remembered the people he had lost.

But hatred gradually became something considerably more complicated.

Understanding.

---

# Joining the Hell Hosts

Powell eventually accepted a position within the [[Hell Hosts]].

The decision surprised personnel familiar with his previous history.

Tyrant never claimed that joining meant he suddenly agreed with everything the faction had done.

Instead, he believed the Hell Hosts were capable of accomplishing something worthwhile.

His willingness to criticize Hell Host command did not disappear after enlistment.

If anything, it became one of the reasons he eventually proved valuable as an officer.

Tyrant does not tell superior officers what he believes they want to hear.

He tells them what he believes is true.

That habit has caused arguments.

It has also prevented disasters.

---

# The 43rd Heavy Assault Battalion

Tyrant eventually rose to command the **43rd Heavy Assault Battalion**, a Doom Corps formation specializing in sustained frontline combat and heavily contested offensive operations.

The 43rd is commonly deployed when conventional infantry formations require additional firepower to break through entrenched resistance.

Its operational roles include:

- Heavy infantry assault
- Urban warfare
- Defensive line breaking
- Fortified position clearance
- Close-range shock operations
- Heavy weapons deployment
- Infantry support
- Sustained frontline combat

Tyrant frequently operates close to the front rather than directing every engagement from a distant command center.

He believes commanders should understand the conditions experienced by the soldiers carrying out their orders.

This does not mean he personally leads every charge.

Tyrant considers commanders who repeatedly expose themselves for dramatic effect to be liabilities.

He fights when his presence is useful.

He commands when command is more useful.

---

# Command Style

Tyrant is direct, demanding, and exceptionally difficult to pressure.

His orders tend to be short.

He dislikes unnecessary military theater and has little patience for officers more concerned with appearances than battlefield conditions.

Despite his callsign, Tyrant is not known for abusing personnel under his command.

The name instead reflects the overwhelming force with which he prefers to control a battlefield.

Once the 43rd establishes itself in an area, Tyrant attempts to deny the enemy any opportunity to recover.

Pressure remains constant.

Heavy weapons suppress defensive positions.

Infantry advances.

Escape routes are restricted.

Resistance is isolated and destroyed.

Tyrant prefers victories that leave the enemy with progressively fewer options until surrender or destruction becomes inevitable.

---

# Active Camouflage

Tyrant uses an integrated active camouflage system capable of significantly reducing his visibility.

The system alters the appearance of his armor to approximate surrounding terrain and lighting conditions.

It does not make him completely invisible.

Movement, environmental interference, thermal sensors, specialist detection systems, and close observation can reveal his position.

Tyrant primarily uses camouflage for:

- Ambushes
- Repositioning
- Reconnaissance
- Concealed observation
- Breaking enemy line of sight
- Preparing close-range attacks

His use of camouflage can appear unusual for a heavy assault commander.

Tyrant considers this an advantage.

Enemies expecting a heavily armed officer to announce himself from several hundred metres away frequently discover him considerably closer.

---

# A12 Heavy Shotgun

Tyrant frequently carries the [[A12 Heavy Shotgun]] during close-range operations.

The weapon is particularly effective during urban combat, boarding actions, fortified position clearance, and other environments where engagements occur at short distances.

Tyrant's aggressive fighting style makes the A12 a natural choice.

He does not use it because it is elegant.

He uses it because at close range, elegance is usually unnecessary.

---

# 71 HC MG

When greater sustained firepower is required, Tyrant may instead deploy with the [[71 HC MG]].

The heavy machine gun is designed for sustained suppression and devastating anti-infantry fire.

Among Hell Host soldiers, the weapon has acquired the unofficial nickname:

**The Bloody Lawnmower.**

Tyrant approves of the weapon.

He refuses to confirm whether he approves of the nickname.

This has generally been interpreted as approval of both.

The 71 HC MG is used when Tyrant expects prolonged engagements where suppression and area control are more important than the mobility provided by his shotgun.

He does not normally carry both primary weapons simultaneously.

---

# P2 Particle Service Pistol

Tyrant carries a [[P2 Particle Service Pistol]] as his standard sidearm.

Unlike his primary weapons, the P2 is comparatively conventional by Hell Host standards.

Its reliability and compact size make it useful when heavier weapons are unavailable or impractical.

He additionally carries a combat knife.

---

# Personality

Tyrant is confident, blunt, and difficult to impress.

He possesses a dry sense of humor and frequently responds to tense situations with understated sarcasm.

His South African accent is distinctive and becomes noticeably stronger when he is irritated.

Powell is generally more relaxed around enlisted personnel than many expect from someone commanding a major combat formation.

He has little interest in maintaining an artificial aura of superiority.

However, once an operation begins, his demeanor changes considerably.

Orders become concise.

Distractions disappear.

Arguments can wait.

Tyrant expects competence from those around him and reacts poorly to avoidable stupidity.

Genuine mistakes can be corrected.

Carelessness that gets other people killed is considerably harder for him to forgive.

---

# Relationship with [[Predator]]

Tyrant and [[Predator]] possess one of the more unusual command relationships within the Hell Hosts.

They were once enemies.

Tyrant blamed Predator for the destruction of personnel he had served beside and carried genuine hatred toward him for years.

Their later cooperation did not erase that history.

Instead, repeated operations gradually changed their understanding of one another.

Tyrant came to recognize that the decisions he once interpreted entirely as personal cruelty had been made within circumstances more complicated than he initially understood.

Predator, meanwhile, never demanded that Tyrant forgive him.

Today, the two share a difficult but genuine professional respect.

Tyrant is willing to challenge Predator when he believes a decision is wrong.

Predator allows him to.

Neither mistakes disagreement for disloyalty.

Occasionally, personnel unfamiliar with their history are horrified by the way Tyrant speaks to the founder of the Hell Hosts.

Veterans generally continue eating.

---

# Reputation

Tyrant is well known throughout the Doom Corps because of both his position and his history.

To members of the 43rd, he is a commander willing to fight close enough to understand what his soldiers are experiencing.

To other officers, he is competent, argumentative, and often irritatingly difficult to prove wrong.

To personnel familiar with his past, he represents something stranger.

A man who once wanted nothing to do with the Hell Hosts eventually became responsible for leading thousands of their soldiers.

Tyrant does not consider this hypocrisy.

He considers it evidence that people are allowed to change their minds.

---

# Legacy

Older records concerning Tyrant occasionally contain the phrase:

> **"He is about to make history."**

At the time, the meaning was unclear.

Years later, personnel began interpreting it differently.

Tyrant's history was not defined by destroying the Hell Hosts.

It was defined by becoming part of them without pretending the conflict that came before had never happened.

He never became blindly loyal.

He never rewrote his own past.

He simply chose what he believed was worth fighting for now.

For Tyrant, that was enough.
