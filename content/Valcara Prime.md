# Valcara Prime Incident

The Valcara Prime Incident was one of the most catastrophic biological disasters in the history of the [[Omicron Galaxy]], transforming the industrial world of [[Valcara Prime]] into a quarantined nightmare zone overrun by mutation, infection, and uncontrolled bio-organic horrors.

Originally a densely populated industrial and commercial planet, Valcara Prime served as a major manufacturing and trade hub prior to the incident. The catastrophe began following a series of classified experiments and containment failures secretly linked to the [[Obsidian Veil]] and anomalous biological research operations.

Within days of the initial outbreak, large portions of the planet descended into chaos as civilians, military personnel, and industrial workers became violently mutated by an unknown bio-corruptive agent. Entire city sectors collapsed into panic, communication systems failed, and evacuation efforts rapidly deteriorated.

The outbreak produced numerous hostile bio-organic entities, including heavily mutated humanoids, unstable infection clusters, and massive engineered monstrosities later classified as [[Revenants]]. These creatures displayed extreme resilience, rapid regeneration, and aggressive adaptation during combat.

As the situation worsened, the [[Hell Hosts]] deployed specialized containment and biohazard response divisions to prevent the outbreak from spreading beyond the planet. The disaster ultimately led to the formal rise and expansion of [[Gravewatch]], a specialized battalion within Doom Corps dedicated to biological containment, corruption response, and anomalous warfare.

Despite large-scale quarantine efforts, orbital bombardments, and military cleansing operations, significant portions of [[Valcara Prime]] were permanently lost. Entire districts remain abandoned, sealed, or overrun by biological growths and hostile entities to this day.

The Valcara Prime Incident is considered one of the earliest major warnings regarding the dangers of uncontrolled anomaly experimentation and biological weaponization within the galaxy.
