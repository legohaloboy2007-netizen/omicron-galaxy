# Valkyrian Interstellar

Valkyrian Interstellar is one of the largest aerospace, shipbuilding, and industrial engineering corporations within the [[Omicron Galaxy]], known for manufacturing advanced starships, orbital infrastructure, fleet systems, and large-scale industrial technology. The corporation maintains enormous influence across both civilian and military sectors due to its role in supplying fleets and engineering support to governments, corporations, and major factions throughout the galaxy.

Founded during the rapid expansion of interstellar civilization, Valkyrian Interstellar quickly became a dominant force in ship production through its massive orbital shipyards, advanced engine systems, and high-capacity manufacturing networks. Their vessels are widely respected for durability, reliability, and engineering quality.

The corporation produces a wide range of technology, including military cruisers, transport vessels, industrial carriers, orbital platforms, and specialized exploration ships designed for dangerous regions such as the [[Farlands]]. Due to its influence and manufacturing capabilities, Valkyrian contracts are considered highly valuable throughout the galaxy.

Although publicly presenting itself as a neutral industrial corporation, Valkyrian Interstellar has frequently faced criticism for supplying military powers, private armies, and questionable organizations during periods of galactic conflict. The company prioritizes expansion, innovation, and profit above political ideology, often maintaining relationships with multiple opposing factions simultaneously.

The corporation maintains major shipyards and engineering facilities across numerous systems, particularly within heavily industrialized and economically powerful regions such as the [[Prioxis System]] and [[Andromeda System]].
