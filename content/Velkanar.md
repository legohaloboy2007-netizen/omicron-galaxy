# Velkanar

## The Cult World of the Far Taken

Velkanar is the primary stronghold and temple-world of the cult known as:

[[The Far Taken]]

Located deep within the corrupted regions of the [[Farlands]], Velkanar is one of the few worlds where [[Element 95]] corruption has reached a strange state of semi-stability rather than complete planetary collapse.

Unlike other corrupted worlds consumed by uncontrolled mutation and environmental destruction, Velkanar has become something far more disturbing:

A functioning civilization built within the corruption itself.

The Far Taken believe the planet is sacred.

## The Far Taken

The Far Taken are one of the oldest and most fanatical cults associated with the [[Farlands]] and the expanding influence of [[The Veil]].

Unlike conventional survivalist groups or scavengers operating within corrupted space, the Far Taken actively embrace mutation, transformation, and communion with the corruption.

Their core belief states:

> “Perfection is found through surrender.”

Members willingly expose themselves to controlled corruption rituals in pursuit of what they call:
Ascendant Unity.

Many Far Taken members display:
- physical mutation
- crystalline growths
- altered perception
- partially shared consciousness
- severe psychological instability

Despite this, the cult remains highly organized.

## Environment

Velkanar is covered by:
- corrupted temples
- molten crystal rivers
- mutated forests
- unstable ritual grounds
- blackened mountain ranges
- massive crystalline formations
- partially living structures

Unlike many corrupted worlds, the planet’s environment appears strangely synchronized with the Far Taken’s presence.

Entire regions pulse rhythmically with energy tied to:
- ritual activity
- corruption surges
- collective consciousness events

The skies above Velkanar are permanently fractured by:
- glowing atmospheric tears
- crystalline lightning
- Veil distortions
- shifting auroras of corrupted light

## Corruption Stability

Velkanar represents one of the few known examples of:
semi-stable corruption.

Rather than endlessly mutating uncontrollably, the planet’s corruption appears:
- patterned
- reactive
- partially self-regulating

This stability allows the Far Taken to:
- build permanent structures
- maintain settlements
- conduct rituals
- survive long-term exposure
- create corrupted constructs

Many researchers consider this deeply alarming.

Some believe the world itself may now function as a living extension of the cult.

## The Fractured Star Monastery

At the center of Velkanar stands:

The Fractured Star Monastery

A colossal temple complex constructed around a massive partially exposed Element 95 crystalline structure.

The monastery serves as:
- the spiritual center of the Far Taken
- site of major rituals
- gathering place of cult leadership
- location of Ascendant ceremonies
- home of the cult’s supreme leader

The structure itself appears partially fused with the corruption.

Its architecture constantly shifts subtly over time.

Interior reports describe:
- impossible geometry
- moving corridors
- living walls
- harmonic chanting with no visible source
- and crystalline growths responding to thought

## The Shepherd of the Fractured Star

The ruler of the Far Taken is known only as:

The Shepherd of the Fractured Star

Very little verified information exists regarding the entity.

Descriptions vary dramatically between encounters.

Some claim the Shepherd is:
- an Ascended individual
- a former human prophet
- a consciousness merged with Element 95
- a servant of [[The Witness]]
- or something no longer entirely biological

The Shepherd rarely appears publicly, communicating primarily through:
- visions
- psychic broadcasts
- ritual manifestations
- corrupted transmissions

Within the Far Taken, the Shepherd is worshipped almost as a divine figure.

## Semi-Sapient Constructs

Velkanar is protected by corrupted entities known as:

Semi-Sapient Constructs

These beings appear to be:
- fused biological-organic masses
- partially mechanical corruption forms
- ritual-created guardians
- or mutated remnants of former followers

Unlike mindless corrupted creatures, these constructs display:
- coordinated behavior
- tactical intelligence
- protective instincts
- reaction to ritual commands

Many appear permanently bound to temple grounds.

## Ritual Activity

Large-scale rituals occur constantly across Velkanar.

These ceremonies involve:
- synchronized chanting
- controlled corruption exposure
- consciousness merging
- Ascendant rites
- collective psychic events

During major rituals, atmospheric anomalies across the planet intensify dramatically.

Some off-world observers report hearing whispers transmitted through communications systems during these events.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Velkanar is known as:
- the cult world of the Far Taken
- one of the most dangerous inhabited worlds in the [[Farlands]]
- a planet where corruption became organized
- a sacred site of Element 95 worship

Among Farlands explorers, one phrase is often repeated:

> “Vraelith was where corruption began.  
> Velkanar is where it learned to pray.”
