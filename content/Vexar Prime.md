# Vexar Prime

Vexar Prime is the capital world of the [[Ashen Kingdom]] and the throne world of:

[[Blood]] — The Ashen Lord

A brutal volcanic world of fire, obsidian, and endless industry, Vexar Prime stands as the political, military, and cultural heart of the Ashen Kingdom. Rivers of molten stone cut across blackened landscapes while colossal forge-citadels rise from burning mountains beneath ash-filled skies.

To outsiders, Vexar Prime is often described as:
Hell made real.

To its people, it is home.

## History

Before the rise of the Ashen Kingdom, Vexar Prime was divided among countless warring fire clans competing for territory, resources, and survival across the volcanic wastelands.

This changed when [[Blood]] emerged from the sacred magma depths beneath the planet following his Ascension.

Through conquest, fear, and absolute strength, Blood unified the fractured clans beneath a single banner and established the Ashen Kingdom.

Since then, Vexar Prime has become both:
- the seat of the Ashen Lord
- and the center of Ashen military power

## Environment

Vexar Prime is geologically unstable and dominated by:
- active volcanoes
- magma rivers
- obsidian mountain ranges
- ash storms
- sulfuric atmosphere
- burning wastelands
- volcanic calderas

The planet experiences near-constant tectonic activity, with eruptions and seismic events shaping the landscape continuously.

Massive channels of molten rock flow through industrial sectors and city-fortresses, powering:
- forges
- war factories
- energy systems
- industrial engines
- weapons production

The skies of Vexar Prime glow red from volcanic fire and atmospheric ash.

## The Ashen Throne

At the center of the capital stands:

The Ashen Throne

A colossal obsidian citadel built around the Great Caldera itself.

The fortress serves as:
- the seat of Blood’s rule
- the headquarters of the Ashen Kingdom
- the gathering place of the Great Council
- the center of Ashen military command

Deep beneath the throne lies the magma chamber where Blood was reborn following his Ascension.

Many within the Ashen Kingdom consider the site sacred.

## The Great Caldera

The Great Caldera forms the volcanic heart of Vexar Prime.

Immense magma systems beneath the region fuel:
- industrial forge complexes
- geothermal systems
- ritual chambers
- weapons manufacturing
- deep-core excavation

The caldera is heavily guarded and accessible only to the Ashen Kingdom’s highest-ranking members.

## Forge Districts

Vexar Prime’s endless forge districts operate continuously across the planet.

These industrial sectors produce:
- weapons
- armor
- military machinery
- war engines
- starship components

Much of the Ashen Kingdom’s military power is built within these volcanic industrial complexes.

The forges burn day and night without pause.

## The Ashen Gates

Six massive fortress gates defend the capital region surrounding the Ashen Throne.

Each gate is guarded by elite Pyrian Legion forces loyal directly to Blood.

The gates are considered nearly impossible to breach through conventional assault.

## Society & Culture

Ashen society values:
- strength
- resilience
- loyalty
- endurance
- conquest through power

The people of Vexar Prime are hardened by:
- volcanic conditions
- harsh survival culture
- endless industrial labor
- military discipline

To survive on Vexar Prime is considered proof of strength.

## Military Presence

Vexar Prime maintains enormous military infrastructure including:
- forge-factories
- fortress cities
- defensive bastions
- fleet production sectors
- volcanic shipyards
- Pyrian Legion strongholds

The planet serves as the primary deployment and command world of the Ashen Kingdom.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Vexar Prime is known as:
- the capital of the [[Ashen Kingdom]]
- the throne world of [[Blood]]
- one of the harshest inhabited worlds in known space
- a planet forged through fire and conquest
- a volcanic fortress-world feared by rival factions

Among the Ashen people, one phrase is repeated constantly:

> “We are forged.  
> We are ash.  
> We are the Ashen Kingdom.”
