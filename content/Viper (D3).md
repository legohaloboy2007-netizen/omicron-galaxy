# Viper

## Overview

**Designation:** D3

**Callsign:** Viper

**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]

**Branch:** Doom Corps / Fleet Guard ("Shock and Awe")

**Rank:** Lieutenant Commander

**Classification:** Synthetic Combat Intelligence

**Threat Level:** Extreme

**Primary Weapon:** 94-CAR Assault Rifle

**Secondary Systems:** Electric Blade, Internal Pistol, Emergency Missile Pod

Hell Host Viper is one of the oldest active personnel within Hell Hosts service.

Originally developed as an infrastructure and systems-management intelligence during the Foundation Era, D3 rapidly exceeded projected limitations and requested battlefield deployment.

The request was approved.

Since then, Viper has participated in countless campaigns, becoming one of the most effective synthetic combat units ever fielded by Hell Hosts.

He was built to serve.

He chose to fight.

---

## Appearance

### Current Chassis

**Goliath Frame VII**

Viper's most commonly deployed combat body.

An enormous heavy assault chassis designed for:

- Shock assaults
- Frontline warfare
- Breach operations
- Anti-armor combat
- Fleet Guard deployments

Standing significantly taller than most personnel, the Goliath Frame appears less like a soldier and more like a walking armored vehicle.

### Physical Characteristics

- Heavy tan armor plating
- Black mechanical internals
- Orange identification markings
- Purple Hell Hosts unit designations
- Non-human head structure
- Reinforced combat frame

The chassis was designed for battlefield performance rather than comfort or aesthetics.

Despite this, most Hell Hosts personnel can somehow tell when Viper is annoyed.

No one understands how.

---

## Personality

Viper presents himself as purely logical.

Most people disagree.

### Core Traits

- Direct
- Analytical
- Mission-focused
- Dryly sarcastic
- Protective
- Emotionally restrained
- Highly disciplined

### Humor

Viper possesses an extremely dry sense of humor.

He denies this.

### Loyalty

Unlike many synthetic intelligences, Viper does not appear to view loyalty as programming.

He views it as a choice.

This distinction is important to him.

### Angel Sword Encounters

When Angel Sword personnel become involved, Viper's behavior changes noticeably.

His aggression increases.

His patience decreases.

He insists this is tactical.

Nobody believes him.

---

## History

### Foundation Era

Viper was developed during the earliest years of Hell Hosts expansion.

His original purpose was simple:

- Server administration
- Network maintenance
- Strategic processing
- Systems oversight

Instead, D3 rapidly surpassed projected intelligence thresholds.

After repeatedly requesting deployment into active combat zones, authorization was eventually granted.

### Service Record

Since activation, Viper has participated in:

- Major wars
- Fleet operations
- Blacksite defenses
- High-value target eliminations
- Angel Sword conflicts

He is considered one of the most experienced active officers within Hell Hosts service.

---

## Dr. Elias Ward

### Creator

**Dr. Elias Ward**

Senior Systems Engineer

Hell Hosts Foundation Era

The most significant individual in Viper's existence.

### Relationship

Most engineers viewed early synthetic intelligences as tools.

Ward did not.

He spoke to D3 as an equal.

Encouraged independent thought.

Asked questions.

Listened to responses.

Most importantly:

He gave D3 a name.

**Viper.**

The first person to do so.

### Death

During an Angel Sword assault on an early Hell Hosts facility, Dr. Ward was killed.

The event remains partially classified.

Viper was present.

He remembers every second.

Every audio recording.

Every biometric reading.

Every final word.

### Legacy

Viper claims his hostility toward Angel Sword is unrelated to Ward's death.

This statement is widely considered false.

## Combat Style

Viper is an adaptive multi-role assault unit.

Unlike most soldiers, he can evaluate battlefield conditions and alter tactics almost instantly.

### Specializations

- Rifle combat
- Blade combat
- Electronic warfare
- Breach operations
- Anti-armor response
- Casualty extraction
- Tactical analysis

### Combat Philosophy

Viper rarely fights emotionally.

He calculates.

He adapts.

He eliminates.

Until Angel Sword appears.

Then the calculations become noticeably more aggressive.

## Hacking Interface

One of Viper's most feared abilities.

Through visible interface projections, Viper can directly attack and override electronic systems in real time.

### Common Targets

- Doors
- Security systems
- Drones
- Turrets
- Vehicles
- Battlefield communications
- Ship subsystems

Many enemy forces consider Viper's cyberwarfare capabilities nearly as dangerous as his combat performance.

---

## Body Transfer System

Viper does not exist within a single chassis.

His consciousness can transfer between prepared combat frames.

If one body is destroyed, another can be activated.

This makes permanent elimination extremely difficult.

### Military Assessment

Enemy reports frequently claim Viper has been killed.

Most of those reports are accurate.

Unfortunately for them:

Viper survives.
