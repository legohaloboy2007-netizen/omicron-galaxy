# Virellon Prime

Virellon Prime is a harsh high-gravity frontier world located within the [[Aegimius System]], known for its abundant heavy-metal deposits, ancient volcanic plateaus, and brutal industrial environments. The planet is considered one of the most dangerous yet economically valuable worlds within the largely underdeveloped Aegimius frontier.

The surface of Virellon Prime is dominated by enormous basalt plateaus, ancient volcanic mountain chains, ash-filled valleys, and deep mineral-rich caverns formed over millions of years of tectonic instability. Vast quantities of dense heavy metals and rare industrial materials lie beneath the planet’s crust, attracting mining corporations, industrial contractors, and frontier prospectors from across the [[Omicron Galaxy]].

Due to the planet’s extreme gravity, infrastructure and settlements require heavily reinforced construction. Most cities are built low into the terrain or directly into volcanic rock formations to withstand both gravitational pressure and frequent seismic activity.

The environment of Virellon Prime is exceptionally unforgiving. Violent ashstorms, unstable volcanic zones, toxic atmospheric pockets, and collapsing excavation sites are common hazards faced by workers and explorers alike. Despite these dangers, the planet remains a major source of high-density industrial metals used in shipbuilding, reactor systems, heavy armor production, and advanced engineering projects.

Because of its isolation within the [[Aegimius System]], Virellon Prime developed a rugged frontier culture centered around survival, industrial labor, and resource extraction. Many crews operating on the planet are heavily armed due to frequent conflicts over mining territory, salvage rights, and smuggling activity.

Rumors also persist regarding ancient structures buried deep beneath the volcanic plateaus, though most expeditions searching for them never return.
