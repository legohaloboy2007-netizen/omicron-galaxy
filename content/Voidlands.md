# Voidlands

The Voidlands are an ancient and highly unstable cosmic region located beyond the [[Farlands]], where corrupted space gives way to complete reality distortion. Unlike the violent mutation and biological corruption seen within the Farlands, the Voidlands are defined by impossible physics, dimensional instability, and the influence of ancient Void Civilizations that predate the known laws of the universe.

The region is considered one of the most dangerous locations within the [[Omicron Galaxy]]. Gravity behaves selectively, time fractures unpredictably, sound can travel without source, and space itself folds inward without warning. Despite these anomalies, the Voidlands possess an eerie and unnatural stillness, as though the region itself is conscious and observing those who enter it.

The Voidlands are believed to have existed before the universe stabilized into its current physical laws. Vast non-Euclidean megastructures, impossible geometries, and “shaped spaces” — artificial pocket realms created by Void Architects — are scattered throughout the region.

Travel through the Voidlands is nearly impossible using conventional technology. Standard engines and faster-than-light systems fail within the region, requiring teleportation systems, Void-tier navigation, or cosmically augmented vessels to safely traverse its space.

The region contains immeasurable wealth, ancient artifacts, limitless energy sources, and forgotten technologies. However, these are fiercely protected by the Void Civilizations, powerful factions capable of manipulating matter, geometry, and reality itself.

The Voidlands are also believed to share deep connections with [[The Veil]], dimensional anomalies, and ancient pre-cosmic events hidden from most known civilizations.

## Major Worlds

### [[Vor-Na’Thul]]
The capital of the Voidlands, known as “The Throne Where Nothing Begins.” A moon-sized non-Euclidean megastructure built before the known universe stabilized into its current laws.

### [[Aeonhollow]]
Capital world of the [[Reality Weavers]], containing impossible architecture and geometric singularities known as Lattice Wells.

### [[Parallax Theta]]
Training world of the Weavers, filled with reality mazes, fractal storms, and dangerous Clipping Zones where reality ceases to exist.

### [[Astral Shear]]
An endless black ocean world inhabited by the [[The Anchorless Caste]].

### [[Shatterwake]]
Homeworld of the [[Riftborn]], split into thousands of drifting shards with differing gravity and time-flow.

### [[Kron Vhaz'kar]]
Fortified citadel-world and capital of the Dominion Keepers, constructed from voidsteel.

### [[Dreadmarch Bastion]]
Military foundry world of the Dominion Keepers, containing cloning facilities, war-beast forges, and mass-production systems for Void armies.

## Major Factions

### [[Reality Weavers]]
A Void Civilization focused on geometry manipulation, dimensional architecture, and reality alteration.

### [[Riftborn]]
A civilization adapted to unstable environments and fractured dimensions.

### [[Dominion Keepers]]
A heavily militarized Void Civilization seeking control and preservation of Void territory.

## Notable Locations

### [[The Umbral Archive]]
A library-world preserving echoes of erased timelines and collapsed realities.

### [[Gate of Sundering]]
An ancient broken portal capable of transporting entire fleets between universes.

### [[Gate of the Edge]]
The silent boundary separating the Farlands from the Voidlands.

## Travel Conditions
