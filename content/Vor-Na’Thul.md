# Vor-Na’Thul

## The Throne Where Nothing Begins

Vor-Na’Thul is the capital of the [[Voidlands]] and one of the most incomprehensible structures in known existence.

It is not a city in any conventional sense.

Vor-Na’Thul is a colossal non-Euclidean megastructure approximately the size of a small moon, suspended deep within the core regions of the Voidlands where normal reality begins to fail completely.

Ancient records suggest the structure predates:
- modern civilizations
- [[The Elysarian Dominion]]
- the formation of current physical laws
- and possibly even the stabilized universe itself

Many believe Vor-Na’Thul was not built within reality—

But before reality fully solidified into its current form.

Within the Voidlands, it is known as:

The Throne Where Nothing Begins.

## The Deep Void

Vor-Na’Thul exists at the deepest known point of the [[Voidlands]], a region where:
- physics weakens
- dimensional laws collapse
- causality becomes unstable
- and reality is gradually replaced by what many scholars call:

Void Laws.

Within these regions:
- distance behaves inconsistently
- geometry loses meaning
- time flows unpredictably
- perception becomes unreliable
- and thought itself may influence physical space

Conventional navigation is almost impossible.

Most vessels attempting direct approach never return.

## Structure

Vor-Na’Thul’s architecture cannot be fully understood by normal perception.

The structure appears to contain:
- impossible angles
- recursive corridors
- infinite interior spaces
- shifting geometry
- suspended platforms with no visible support
- living void-material
- structures existing simultaneously in multiple states

Observers frequently report:
- rooms larger internally than externally possible
- pathways leading into themselves
- staircases descending upward
- entire sectors disappearing while observed
- architecture reacting to thought or emotion

Many who study visual recordings of the structure experience:
- migraines
- hallucinations
- memory loss
- perceptual instability
- severe psychological distress

## Void Laws

The region surrounding Vor-Na’Thul operates under principles entirely separate from normal physics.

Known anomalies include:
- gravity inversion
- localized time collapse
- spontaneous spatial recursion
- matter instability
- consciousness bleed
- non-linear causality
- perception-linked environmental change

Some researchers believe the Voidlands are not corrupted space—

But remnants of reality before existence stabilized.

## The Throne Core

At the center of Vor-Na’Thul lies an inaccessible region known only as:

The Throne Core

No confirmed expedition has ever reached it.

The Core is believed to contain:
- the origin of Void activity
- an ancient intelligence
- a gateway beyond conventional reality
- or the true seat of [[The Witness]]

Some cults claim the Throne Core is not a location—
but a state of existence.

## Relationship to The Witness

Many believe Vor-Na’Thul serves as the primary nexus of influence for:

[[The Witness]]

Across the Voidlands, psychic transmissions and Veil-linked manifestations become dramatically stronger closer to the structure.

Some theories suggest:
- the Witness resides within Vor-Na’Thul
- the structure itself is alive
- the city acts as a consciousness amplifier
- or Vor-Na’Thul is the Witness.

No theory has ever been confirmed.

## The Voidbound

The regions surrounding Vor-Na’Thul are inhabited by entities collectively referred to as:

The Voidbound

These beings display:
- distorted anatomy
- unstable forms
- fragmented consciousness
- partial synchronization with Void Laws

Some appear humanoid.

Others do not resemble known life at all.

Many entities encountered near Vor-Na’Thul seem only partially physical, flickering between states of existence.

## Exploration Attempts

Very few factions have attempted direct exploration of Vor-Na’Thul.

Known expeditions involving:
- [[Obsidian Veil]]
- rogue Farlands cults
- forbidden research groups
- deep anomaly expeditions

have almost universally ended in:
- disappearance
- madness
- temporal inconsistency
- or complete communication loss

Recovered recordings are often physically impossible.

## Elysarian Knowledge

Ancient Elysarian Dominion archives contain fragmented warnings regarding Vor-Na’Thul.

Unlike their campaigns against [[Element 95]], the Dominion never attempted direct assault or containment of the structure.

Several surviving records simply state:

> “Do not descend beneath the last threshold.”

Much of the remaining information was intentionally erased.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Vor-Na’Thul is regarded as:
- the heart of the [[Voidlands]]
- the deepest known anomaly in existence
- a place where reality no longer applies
- the possible throne of [[The Witness]]
- one of the most feared locations in the universe

Among Voidlands survivors, one warning is repeated more than any other:

> “The deeper you go, the less the universe remembers you.”
