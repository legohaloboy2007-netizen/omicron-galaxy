# Vraelith 95

## The First Corrupted World

Vraelith 95 is widely recognized as the origin point of the first confirmed large-scale [[Element 95]] containment breach within the [[Farlands]]. Once believed to be a conventional research and industrial world, the planet was completely transformed following catastrophic exposure to uncontrolled Element 95 corruption.

Today, Vraelith 95 no longer behaves like a normal planet.

The world itself appears alive.

Entire continents shift without warning while enormous crystalline growths spread endlessly across the surface. Geological stability has completely collapsed, and reality throughout the planet behaves inconsistently.

Vraelith 95 is considered one of the most dangerous worlds in known space.

## The Collapse

The original containment disaster remains only partially understood.

Most surviving records indicate:
- large-scale Element 95 experimentation
- containment instability
- dimensional rupture events
- mass biological corruption
- catastrophic environmental mutation

Within days of the breach, the planet underwent rapid transformation.

The surface became consumed by:
- living crystalline growths
- biological corruption
- shifting landmasses
- unstable terrain
- reality distortion zones

Entire populations disappeared.

Very few evacuation vessels escaped the system.

## Environment

The surface of Vraelith 95 is in a constant state of mutation.

Known planetary features include:
- moving landmasses
- crystal forests formed from Element 95 growths
- flesh-like plains
- unstable gravity regions
- pulsating terrain
- living organic structures
- corrupted atmospheric storms

The ground itself often appears partially organic, with sections of the planet:
- breathing
- shifting
- reacting to movement
- or growing over abandoned structures

Massive veins of glowing Element 95 crystal spread beneath and across the surface like infected roots.

## The Cathedral

One of the most infamous locations on the planet is:

The Cathedral

A colossal structure almost entirely consumed by Element 95 corruption.

The building’s original purpose remains unknown, though many believe it once served as:
- a research facility
- containment center
- ritual complex
- or early experimentation site

Over time, the Cathedral became physically fused with the corruption itself.

The interior constantly changes shape and appears partially alive.

Exploration attempts report:
- impossible architecture
- living walls
- distorted sound
- biological growths
- and sections larger internally than externally possible

Very few expeditions return intact.

## The Cathedral Beast

Deep within the Cathedral resides an entity known only as:

The Cathedral Beast

Very little confirmed information exists regarding the creature.

Survivor accounts describe:
- colossal malformed anatomy
- shifting biological structures
- crystal growth embedded into flesh
- distorted vocalizations
- and immense intelligence

Some believe the Beast is:
- the result of early Element 95 experimentation
- a fused mass of multiple organisms
- a failed Ascension attempt
- or something created directly by the corruption itself

Even fragmented manifestations known as:
Cathedral Beast Echoes

have wiped out entire expedition teams.

## The Singularity Pit

At the center of Vraelith 95 lies:

The Singularity Pit

A colossal planetary chasm where raw Element 95 continues erupting upward from beneath the surface like a reverse waterfall.

The Pit emits:
- gravitational distortion
- dimensional instability
- energy storms
- corrupted atmospheric phenomena

Long-range observation confirms the Element 95 within the Pit behaves unlike any other known sample.

Some researchers believe the Singularity Pit may connect directly to:
- a dimensional rupture
- the origin point of the contamination
- or something deeper beneath reality itself.

## Native Threats

Known hostile entities on Vraelith 95 include:
- Thickskins
- Corruption Swarms
- Cathedral Beast Echoes
- crystal-mutated organisms
- malformed biological masses
- anomalous predatory entities

Many lifeforms on the planet appear capable of rapid adaptation and mutation.

## Scientific Importance

Despite the danger, Vraelith 95 remains one of the most heavily studied worlds associated with [[Element 95]].

Multiple factions have attempted covert operations on the planet including:
- [[Hell Hosts]]
- [[Obsidian Veil]]
- independent research groups
- black-market anomaly collectors

Most operations end in catastrophic losses.

## Reputation

Across the galaxy, Vraelith 95 is known as:
- the First Corrupted World
- the birthplace of modern Element 95 catastrophe
- one of the deadliest planets in known space
- a world where reality itself became infected

Many believe Vraelith 95 represents the moment the galaxy first realized:

Element 95 was not simply a resource.

It was something far worse.
