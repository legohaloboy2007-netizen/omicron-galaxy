# War Between Hell Hosts and Free World

The War Between the [[Hell Hosts]] and the [[Free World Order]] was one of the most personal and devastating conflicts in the history of the [[Omicron Galaxy]], fought between former brothers turned bitter enemies: [[Predator]] and [[Onyx]].

The conflict began after years of growing hostility between the rapidly expanding [[Hell Hosts]] and the increasingly aggressive operations of the [[Free World Order]]. Political tension, territorial disputes, black market conflicts, and personal hatred between both leaders eventually escalated into full-scale war.

Unlike the large ideological conflict between the [[Hell Hosts]] and [[Angel Swords]], this war was fueled heavily by betrayal, revenge, and personal history. Many within both factions had once fought alongside each other during the aftermath of the [[War of Virex Prime]], making the conflict especially brutal and emotionally charged.

The war spread across numerous systems, involving fleet battles, planetary invasions, sabotage campaigns, black operations, and large-scale urban warfare. The [[Hell Hosts]] utilized overwhelming military force, industrial expansion, and fleet superiority, while the [[Free World Order]] relied on rapid strike tactics, sabotage, infiltration, and guerrilla-style operations.

Several major operations carried out by Free World forces targeted Hell Host infrastructure, military convoys, and supply networks, forcing the Hell Hosts into prolonged retaliatory campaigns across contested territories.

The conflict eventually culminated during the infamous [[Rainfall Execution]], where [[Predator]] finally captured and psychologically broke [[Onyx]] following the collapse of major Free World resistance forces. The event became one of the most infamous moments in modern galactic history.

Despite the defeat of the [[Free World Order]], remnants of the faction survived under new leadership structures and later temporarily allied with the [[Hell Hosts]] against larger existential threats involving the [[Farlands]], [[Obsidian Veil]], and cosmic anomaly regions.
