# War between Hell Hosts and Angel Swords

The War between the [[Hell Hosts]] and the [[Angel Swords]] was one of the largest and most destructive military conflicts in the history of the [[Omicron Galaxy]], fought between two rapidly expanding superpowers with fundamentally opposing ideologies.

The conflict began following the aggressive expansion of the [[Hell Hosts]] into territories and systems long protected or influenced by the [[Angel Swords]]. Rising political tension, military escalation, and ideological hostility between the two factions eventually erupted into full-scale war across multiple systems.

Unlike the earlier conflicts of the TCA/FOR era, the war between the Hell Hosts and Angel Swords was fought on a truly galactic scale. Massive fleet battles, planetary invasions, orbital bombardments, and prolonged military campaigns devastated large portions of the [[Outer Rim]] and nearby systems.

The [[Hell Hosts]] relied heavily on overwhelming industrial warfare, fleet superiority, mechanized assaults, and rapid expansion tactics under the leadership of [[Predator]] and [[Azure Gale]]. In contrast, the [[Angel Swords]] utilized highly disciplined crusader armies, elite soldiers, fortified holy worlds, and advanced energy weaponry during their campaigns.

Several sacred Angel Sword worlds, including regions surrounding [[Genesis]], became major battlegrounds throughout the conflict. Meanwhile, Hell Host military production expanded dramatically during the war, solidifying the faction as one of the most dominant military powers in the galaxy.

Despite suffering enormous losses, the [[Angel Swords]] ultimately survived the conflict, though much of their territory and influence was weakened. The war ended with a major Hell Host victory, permanently shifting the balance of power within the galaxy.

The aftermath of the conflict left deep hatred between both factions, shaping political and military tensions for years afterward.
