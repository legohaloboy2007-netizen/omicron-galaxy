# White Wolf

## Overview

**Real Name:** Unknown

**Age:** 28

**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]

**Unit:** [[Wolf Unit|The Wolf Unit]]

**Rank:** Commander

White Wolf is one-third of the:
[[Wolf Unit]]

and serves as the unit's primary marksman, overwatch specialist, and precision eliminator.

Known for her silence, patience, and impossible accuracy, White Wolf has become one of the most feared snipers within the:
[[Hell Hosts]]

Few people can claim to know her well.

Even fewer realize that behind her cold exterior lies someone far more observant, thoughtful, and quietly caring than her reputation suggests.

Many assume she is the calmer half of the Wolf twins.

Those assumptions are often fatal.

---

## Appearance

White Wolf stands approximately:

**5'7"**

with a lean, athletic build.

### Notable Features

- Black hair
- Two white streaks running through her hair
- Ice-grey eyes
- Pale complexion
- Controlled posture
- Minimal visible emotion

One of White Wolf's most unsettling traits is how rarely she appears to blink.

Whether this is intentional or simply a habit remains unknown.

### Equipment

White Wolf typically wears:

- Dark tactical combat gear
- Wolf Unit markings
- Lightweight combat armor
- Precision targeting systems
- Multiple concealed knives

Her equipment is designed for mobility, stealth, and precision rather than direct confrontation.

---

## Personality

White Wolf is:

- Quiet
- Observant
- Intelligent
- Patient
- Dryly humorous

To strangers, she often appears emotionless.

The reality is far more complicated.

White Wolf feels deeply.

She simply struggles to communicate those feelings openly.

### Hidden Kindness

Although she rarely expresses affection directly, White Wolf cares deeply about those she trusts.

Unfortunately, she often assumes people already know how she feels.

This leads to frequent misunderstandings.

From her perspective:

> "Of course I care."

Everyone else remains unconvinced.

---

## Combat Style

White Wolf specializes in:

- Long-range elimination
- Overwatch operations
- Precision targeting
- Silent infiltration
- Counter-sniper warfare

Where Black Wolf creates pressure, White Wolf provides the finishing blow.

Many targets never realize they have been engaged until it is too late.

### Weapons

#### [[333 HCS High-Calibre Sniper]]

White Wolf's primary weapon.

Capable of extreme-range engagements and renowned for its devastating stopping power.

---

#### Combat Knives

Like her brother, White Wolf is highly proficient with knives.

She frequently carries multiple blades and demonstrates exceptional precision when using them.

---

## Relationships

### [[Black Wolf]]

White Wolf's twin brother.

The two possess an almost instinctive understanding of one another.

They communicate through:

- Glances
- Gestures
- Body language
- Unspoken cues

Some personnel believe the twins possess their own language.

Whether true or not, their level of synchronization is extraordinary.

### Loyalty

White Wolf is every bit as protective of Black Wolf as he is of her.

Although less outwardly aggressive, she is no less dangerous when someone threatens him.

---

### [[Grey Wolf]]

White Wolf views Grey Wolf as family.

His guidance played a major role in shaping the hunter she eventually became.

Of all people outside her brother, Grey Wolf is one of the few whose opinions she genuinely values.

---

### [[Red Wind]]

Although unaware of it, White Wolf has become a source of fascination for Red Wind.

Several of Red Wind's private sketches feature White Wolf.

White Wolf herself remains completely oblivious.

---

### [[Destiny]]

White Wolf respects Destiny's capabilities and discipline.

Although they rarely interact directly, professional respect exists between them.

---

## Habits

- Tilts her head instead of asking questions
- Watches people slightly longer than normal
- Sharpens knives while listening
- Spins blades absentmindedly when thinking
- Fidgets constantly when sitting still
- Remembers embarrassing moments for years

---

## Off-Duty Life

### Around Strangers

White Wolf remains:

- Quiet
- Reserved
- Difficult to read

She rarely speaks unless necessary.

---

### Around Trusted People

The difference is dramatic.

White Wolf becomes significantly more talkative and relaxed.

While still quiet compared to most people, she actively participates in conversations and occasionally reveals a surprisingly dry sense of humor.

Without Black Wolf present, people often discover:

> She can speak perfectly well.
>
> She simply chooses not to most of the time.

## Flaws

### Emotional Communication

White Wolf struggles to express how she feels.

She often assumes others already understand her intentions and emotions.

This habit creates misunderstandings and occasionally causes distance between herself and others.

---

## Reputation

### Within Hell Hosts

White Wolf is viewed as:

- Professional
- Reliable
- Extremely dangerous

Many soldiers find her intimidating despite her calm demeanor.

Others simply find her impossible to read.

---

### Outside Hell Hosts

Enemy forces know White Wolf as one of the most lethal marksmen operating within the galaxy.

Numerous battlefield reports describe impossible shots attributed to her.

Whether exaggerated or not, her reputation alone is often enough to create panic.
