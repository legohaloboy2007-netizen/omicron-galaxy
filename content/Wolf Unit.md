# The Wolf Unit

## Overview

**Faction:** [[Hell Hosts]]

**Status:** Active

**Branch Links:** Doom Corps / Black Operations Attachments

**Threat Level:** Severe

**Primary Roles:**
- Tracking
- Elimination
- Infiltration
- Counter-Command Warfare
- High-Value Target Hunting

The Wolf Unit is an elite three-person hunter cell operating within the:
[[Hell Hosts]]

Composed of:
- [[Black Wolf]]
- [[White Wolf]]
- [[Grey Wolf]]

the unit specializes in operations where conventional battalions are considered too visible, too slow, or too predictable.

Their deployments are rare.

Their successes are not.

Across the galaxy, countless assassinations, disappearances, and command-level eliminations have been unofficially attributed to the Wolves.

Whether those reports are true is often impossible to verify.

By the time a target realizes they are being hunted, the operation is usually already over.

---

## Purpose

The Wolf Unit exists to solve problems that cannot be solved through conventional military force.

Typical assignments include:

- High-value target elimination
- Deep reconnaissance
- Enemy leadership disruption
- Intelligence recovery
- Counter-assassination operations
- Long-term tracking assignments

Unlike larger military formations, the Wolves are capable of operating independently for extended periods without support.

---

## Reputation

The Wolves possess one of the most feared reputations within the:
[[Hell Hosts]]

and beyond.

Among enemy forces, their presence is often associated with:

- Vanishing officers
- Missing commanders
- Failed evacuations
- Sudden intelligence leaks
- Unexplained deaths

Many military organizations consider being assigned a Wolf Unit target priority equivalent to a death sentence.

A common saying among intelligence circles states:

> "If the Wolves know your name, they already know where you'll die."

---

## Combat Doctrine

Each member serves a distinct role.

Together they form a highly coordinated hunting team.

### Black Wolf

Acts as the pressure point.

Forces enemies into mistakes.

Creates openings.

Controls engagements through direct action and intimidation.

---

### White Wolf

Acts as the execution point.

Provides overwatch.

Removes targets with precision.

Eliminates escape routes before they exist.

---

### Grey Wolf

Acts as the hunter.

Tracks targets.

Predicts movement.

Observes from a distance.

Ensures no target remains hidden.

---

## Internal Dynamics

Despite their reputation, the Wolves function less like soldiers and more like a family.

### Black Wolf Protects White Wolf

Although rarely acknowledged openly, Black Wolf possesses a fiercely protective attitude toward his twin sister.

---

### White Wolf Protects Black Wolf

White Wolf's loyalty is absolute.

Many of her most dangerous actions have occurred while protecting her brother.

---

### Grey Wolf Protects Them Both

Grey Wolf is not biologically related to either twin.

However, he is widely regarded as family by both.

Many personnel believe he played a major role in shaping the hunters they eventually became.

---

## Shared Traits

Personnel observations regarding the Wolves include:

- Move with near-identical timing
- Can predict each other's actions instinctively
- Rarely operate separately
- Communicate through gestures and subtle cues
- Highly proficient with knives
- Demonstrate unusually synchronized combat behavior

One report described them as:

> "Three hunters. One instinct."

Another described Black and White Wolf specifically as:

> "Two bodies using one plan."

---

## Deployment History

Many Wolf Unit operations remain classified.

Known assignments include:

- Leadership eliminations
- Counter-command warfare
- Strategic disappearances
- Long-range reconnaissance missions
- High-priority pursuit operations

Official records indicate:

**The Wolf Unit has never failed an assigned hunt.**
